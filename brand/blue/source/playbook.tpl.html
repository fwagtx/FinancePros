The formula

Every video: news + one money lesson

People come for the news and stay because they learn something. Every episode ties the day's story to one basic money idea, so watching every day adds up to a real financial education.

1 · The newsWhat happened

One story, told fast, with the key number on screen.

2 · The whyWhy it matters to you

Loans, prices, jobs, savings. Never just "stocks went up".

3 · The lessonMoney 101 moment

Dot explains the one money term hiding in the story, in about 10 seconds.

4 · What's nextWhat to watch

The next date or number, so people come back.

Example: "Fed holds rates" → why your credit card rate stays high → Money 101: what "APR" means → next Fed meeting date.

Breaking

FP Flash: only news that actually matters

Most "breaking" finance news is noise. FP Flash fires only when something affects a lot of people's money, so followers learn that when Ticker's screen flashes red, it's worth watching.

The ruleThis matters to you because…

If the system can't finish that sentence in plain words, there's no Flash. The story goes into the next scheduled show, or it's skipped.

Tier 1Always · ~15 min
  • Fed rate decisions and emergency moves
  • Jobs, CPI inflation and GDP reports, especially big surprises
  • S&P 500 moving ±2% or more in a day, or market-wide trading halts
  • A major bank failing
  • Government shutdown, debt-ceiling deal, or a tax or stimulus law signed
  • A world event that clearly moves markets (oil ±5% or more)
Tier 2If it scores 18+
  • A top-10 company moving ±5% or more on earnings
  • Layoffs of 1,000+ people (or 5%+ of staff) at a household name
  • A $20B+ deal involving a brand people know
  • Bitcoin moving ±8% or more in a day
  • Mortgage rates or gas prices at a multi-year high or low
Tier 3Never a Flash
  • Analyst upgrades and price targets
  • Small or obscure stocks
  • Rumors, "sources say" and social media posts
  • Opinions and predictions
  • Anything that sounds like a stock tip

The importance score (out of 25)

  • Reach, ×2 (up to 10): does it touch anyone with a loan, a job or savings?
  • Size (up to 5): how big is the move or the number?
  • Surprise (up to 5): how far from what experts expected?
  • Confirmed (up to 5): an official source. Zero means no post.

18+ Flash · 12–17 next scheduled show · under 12 skip

Guardrails

  • Official source first (Fed, government data, SEC filings, company press release). Otherwise, two independent outlets.
  • At most 2 Flash videos a day, 90 minutes apart. Tier 1 can break this.
  • Overnight news waits for the Morning Brief unless it's Tier 1.
  • One video per story, even if many alerts fire.
  • Mistakes are corrected or deleted within an hour, and the cause is fixed.

How the engine works

Watch

Free feeds: Fed press releases, government data releases, SEC filings, news headlines, prices every minute.

Score

Claude reads each alert, assigns a tier and runs the "so what" test.

Write

Ticker's script: facts, why it matters, one money lesson.

Check

Every number is compared with the source. Mismatch = blocked.

Make

Free AI voice, Ticker lip-sync, LED graphics, captions.

Post

Metricool publishes to all four platforms at once.

For scheduled data days (jobs, CPI, Fed), the Flash is built the night before and only the result is filled in when the number comes out. Free prices delayed by ~15 minutes are fine: we're a news channel, not a trading app.

Guardrails

Rules that never change

Education, not advice

We explain how money works. We never tell anyone to buy, sell or hold, and never predict prices. Every video says "Not financial advice."

Confirmed only

Official sources, or two independent outlets. No rumors, ever.

No politics

Report what a policy does to money. Never take sides.

Always cite

The source is on screen and in the caption.

Label AI

Use each platform's AI-content label. Ticker is an animated host with an AI voice.

Own the visuals

Our templates, our charts, licensed music. No clips copied from TV networks.
