Mascot

Meet Ticker

Ticker is the anchor of every video. His face is an LED screen, so it can show any expression, turn his eyes into green ▲ on up days and red ▼ on down days, and flash red for breaking news. His sidekick Dot, a single glowing pixel, pops up to explain money terms.

Personality

  • Calm, quick, a little dry. A friend at the news desk, never a hype man.
  • Catchphrase: "That's the money."
  • Tell: the green light on his right antenna means he's live.
  • Teaches: every episode, Ticker or Dot explains one money basic in plain words.

Face rules

  • Green ▲ eyes only on real up moves, red ▼ eyes only on real down moves.
  • Shocked face and red screen only for FP Flash.
  • Never shows a price target, a "buy" or a "sell" on his screen.
  • Same suit, same cobalt LED tie, same FP pin, every time.

Motion & sound

How it moves and sounds

  • LED scan wipe350 ms

    Between scenes, a wave of dots lights up across the screen and clears it. The signature transition.

  • Dots light up600 ms

    LED numbers and the logo switch on dot by dot, left to right.

  • Ticker's static blink120 ms

    His face flickers with TV static when his expression changes.

  • Charts draw800 ms

    Lines draw left to right, the end dot pops, and the number rolls in.

  • New shot every 3–5 sPace

    Cut on the narrator's pauses. Ticker reacts between facts.

Sound

  • Sound logo: three quick rising LED beeps with a soft TV click. Opens and closes every video.
  • Scan wipe: a short electric sweep.
  • Music bed: light electronic pulse, 110–120 BPM, well below the voice.
  • FP Flash: a two-tone alert, then only the voice.
  • Loudness: −14 LUFS for every platform.

All free: royalty-free sound libraries, plus the free ffmpeg tool for loudness.

Writing rules

  • Say "about 4 percent". The exact figure goes on screen.
  • Explain one term per video in five words or fewer.
  • Name the source for every number.
  • Never say buy, sell or "you should". Education, not advice.
  • No hype words and no politics.
  • End with the thing to watch next.

Disclaimer, spoken and written: "FinancePros TV is news and education, not financial advice."
