Logo

The logo system

The wordmark is "FINANCE" in bold italic, "PROS" in a red slanted box, and a small TV tag. The FP monogram is the short version for profile pictures and watermarks.

Spacing and size

  • Clear spaceHeight of the PROS box, on every side
  • Smallest wordmark160 px wide
  • Smallest FP bug32 px wide
  • Video bugTop right, 90 px wide on 1080 px video
  • SlantAlways 12°, leaning right

Never

  • Straighten the italic or change the slant angle.
  • Put the red PROS box on a red background. Switch it to black.
  • Add outlines, shadows, glows or gradients.
  • Stretch it, or retype it in a different font.
  • Write the name as "Finance Pros" or "FPTV" in text. It's FinancePros TV.
Signature

The 12° slash

One angle ties the whole brand together. Every tag, bar, ticker edge and transition leans right at 12°, like the italic type. It reads as forward motion: markets moving and news breaking.

Where it shows up

  • Show tags, the PROS box and the TV tag
  • The red wipe between scenes
  • The ticker strip's leading edge
  • The background stripes on banners and covers

Why it matters

When the channel is automated, the slash is what makes a template feel like a network. Someone scrolling should recognize a FinancePros TV video from the shapes alone, before reading a word.

Up and down

Up is ▲ green. Down is ▼ red. Because the brand color is also red, a falling number must always carry the ▼ arrow, so viewers never confuse "down" with "brand".

Motion & sound

How it moves and sounds

  • Slash wipe300 ms

    A red 12° bar sweeps left to right between scenes. It's the channel's signature transition.

  • Tags slide in200 ms · ease-out

    Show tags and lower thirds slide in from the left, along the slant.

  • Headlines stamp150 ms per word

    Headline words land one at a time with a tiny scale-down, like a stamp.

  • Charts draw800 ms

    Lines draw left to right, then the end dot pops and the number appears.

  • New shot every 3–5 sPace

    Cut on the narrator's pauses, never in the middle of a word.

Sound

  • News sting: two sharp hits with the opening slash wipe, under 1 second. It's the sound logo.
  • Whoosh: a short, soft whoosh on every slash wipe.
  • Music bed: tight, low electronic pulse, 110–120 BPM, kept well below the voice.
  • Flash: no music. Just the sting, a low hum and the voice. The silence makes it feel urgent.
  • Loudness: −14 LUFS for every platform.

All of this can be made free: royalty-free music and effects libraries, plus the free ffmpeg tool for loudness.

Writing rules

  • Say "about 4 percent", not "4.13 percent". The exact number goes on screen.
  • Explain jargon in five words or fewer, or leave it out.
  • Name the source for every number.
  • Never say buy, sell, or "you should".
  • No hype words: mooning, crashing (unless it truly is), insane.
  • Neutral on politics. Report what a policy does to money.

Spoken and written disclaimer: "FinancePros TV is news and education, not financial advice."
