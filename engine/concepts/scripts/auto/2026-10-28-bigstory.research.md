- Decision (Oct 28, 2026, 2:00 PM ET): FOMC kept the federal funds target range unchanged at 3-3/4 to 4 percent (3.75%-4.00%). Source: Fed release monetary20261028a (via search text); CNBC; NBC News; CNN; Fox Business; FXStreet; CoinDesk.
- Vote: 10-2. Dissenters: Governor Lisa D. Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth M. Hammack, both preferring a quarter-point (25 bp) increase. Source: Fed release; CNBC; NBC News. (NBC: Cook's first-ever dissent.)
- Statement language: economic activity expanding "at a strong pace" (CNBC: upgraded from "solid" in September); inflation "remains elevated". Exact full sentences not retrieved -> paraphrase only, no on-screen quote.

## What it means (mechanics, no predictions)
- Most credit card APRs are variable and tied to the prime rate, which banks typically set at the top of the Fed's range plus 3 points; no Fed change = no new prime-rate move from this meeting.
- Savings/money-market yields track the same short-term benchmark; no fresh change from this decision.
- 30-year mortgage rates follow longer-term Treasury yields, not the fed funds rate directly.
