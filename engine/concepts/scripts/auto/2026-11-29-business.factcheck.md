## Issues

1. **"Seller" is not accurate (lines 1, 3, 4, 5, cover/post) — must fix.** Amway's own figure says 38% of IBOs had *no sales*. Many IBOs only buy for themselves. Calling them all "sellers" misstates the population and turns the 38% stat into a contradiction ("sellers" with no sales). Use "IBO" (Amway's term). The averages also cover only IBOs at **Founders Platinum and below**. Put that in the post text.
2. **The FTC rule line (line 8) leaves out its status — must fix.** On its own, "the FTC proposed requiring proof" suggests the rule is moving forward or already applies. It was never published in the Federal Register, comment was closed, and it is not in effect. Say it stalled. The fix states only the fact, with no political framing.
3. **The compare scene mixes populations (line 5).** The left side ("Top 10% paid", $15,691) comes from the 59% who got paid. The right side ("All sellers", $750) covers all IBOs at Founders Platinum and below. That's fine if both are labeled correctly. Rename the right side to "All IBOs". Both values are 7 characters or fewer (OK).
4. **The worked example is unlabeled and its assumption isn't stated (line 6).** The math is right. But the first year's registration is free, so not every IBO pays $55. Say it is a *renewing* IBO at the average. Spoken and on-screen values match ($695, about $58 vs $57.92).
5. **The 38% stat is slightly simplified (line 4).** The disclosure says these IBOs had no sales and no payment. Note that 41% did not receive a payment, since 59% did. Say "no sales and no pay" so the stat stays exact and doesn't understate.
6. **The hook (line 1) is weak and slow.** It has 11 spoken words, with "seven hundred fifty dollars in twenty twenty-five" taking up most of the time. That runs about 4 s, not under 3 s, and it uses "seller". The fix is a question hook that leaves the year and population on screen, so the spoken part is shorter and more urgent.
7. **Awkward narration.** Line 3, "stayed under nine hundred dollars since twenty twenty-one", needs "has stayed". Line 7, "most people made", should be "most participants" (the FTC's wording).
8. **The header doesn't quite pay off.** "Where MLM money really goes" promises a money-flow story, but the video is about what members earn. Suggested: "What MLM members\nreally make".
9. **Line 9 mood "happy"** doesn't fit "many earn $0". Use "think". (Minor.)
10. **Line 7 text is 110 characters.** Tighten it (see fix).
11. **Post text:** change "seller" to "IBO", add "Founders Platinum and below", label the $55 example as applying to a renewing IBO, and fix the FTC rule wording in the sources line.

Other checks:
- **Defamation:** no "scam" or "illegal" language, and the Amway figures are Amway's own. The neutral framing is OK. "It's not just Amway" is acceptable, but the fix below drops it so the script doesn't imply Amway was among the FTC's 70.
- **Time words:** "in 2025", "Sept 2024" and "January 2025" are all still correct on Nov 29, 2026.
- **Stale news:** the FTC report is 2 years old, but it is dated on screen, so it's acceptable.
- **No advice or price predictions, and no political opinion.**
- **No keep:true flags.**
- **The cover pays off.**

## Why it fails

The script's numbers are all correct. It fails because "seller" misdescribes a population in which 38% made no sales, and because the line about the FTC proposal omits that it stalled and is not in effect. Both are factual-framing errors. They are fixed by the replacements above.
