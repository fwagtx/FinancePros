# Research brief: How Walmart beat everyone on price (lf-business, posts Nov 17, 2026)

Made Oct 2026. Walmart's fiscal year ends Jan 31. Fiscal 2026 (FY2026) = year ended Jan 31, 2026 (10-K filed Mar 13, 2026).
Q3 FY2027 results are expected around Nov 18-19, 2026, AFTER this video posts, so they are NOT included. Latest quarter used: Q2 FY2027 (three months ended July 31, 2026, reported Aug 20, 2026).

## News angle (timely, dated)
- Q2 FY2027 (qtr ended July 31, 2026; reported Aug 20, 2026): revenue $187.9B, up 5.9% (5.1% constant currency). Operating income $9.4B, up 28.8% (from $7.3B), including tariff refunds, partly offset by price investments. (Walmart Q2 FY27 release / 10-Q; WWD, Yahoo Finance)
- Walmart received about $2.9B in tariff refunds in that quarter, through the CBP refund process, after the US Supreme Court ruled in Feb 2026 that tariffs imposed under IEEPA were unlawful. Recorded as a reduction to cost of sales. Walmart said it is prioritizing the money into price investment and customer experience. (Walmart 10-Q for qtr ended July 31, 2026; CNN Aug 20, 2026; The Hill; Fox Business)
- More than 11,000 rollbacks across US business in Q2 FY2027 (company-stated, via news coverage).
- Q2 FY2027: global eCommerce +23%; Walmart U.S. eCommerce +24%; global advertising +38%; Walmart Connect U.S. +43% ex-Vizio; companywide membership fee revenue +17%. Sam's Club U.S. operating income $678M.
- Q1 FY2027 (qtr ended Apr 30, 2026): revenue $177.8B, up 7.3%; global eCommerce +26%; global advertising +37%.
- Guidance (Aug 2026, company): FY27 net sales +4.0% to 5.0% cc; adj. operating income +7.0% to 8.5% cc. (Not a prediction by us; attribute if used.)

## FY2026 10-K numbers (year ended Jan 31, 2026)
- Total revenues: $713.163B (up 4.7%, +$32.2B). Net sales $706.413B. Membership and other income $6.750B (FY2025: $6.447B).
- Gross profit: $171.018B; gross profit rate 24.2% of net sales (FY2025: 24.1%).
- Operating, selling, general and administrative expenses: 20.9% of net sales (FY2025: 20.7%). Derived dollars: 171.018 + 6.750 - 29.825 = $147.943B (our calculation).
- Operating income: $29.825B. Operating margin on revenue = 29.825/713.163 = 4.18% (our calc).
- Consolidated net income: $22.270B (includes noncontrolling interest). Per $1 of revenue = 22.270/713.163 = 3.12 cents (our calc).
- Inventories Jan 31, 2026: $58.851B (Jan 31, 2025: $56.435B).
- Inventory turnover (our calc): cost of sales = 706.413 - 171.018 = $535.395B; average inventory = (56.435 + 58.851)/2 = $57.643B; turnover = 9.29x; 365/9.29 = about 39 days. AlphaQuery lists 9.10 (different method).
- Segments FY2026: Walmart U.S. net sales $482.975B (op income $25.158B); Walmart International $130.423B (op inc $5.103B); Sam's Club U.S. $93.015B (op inc $2.442B).
- Sam's Club U.S. membership and other income FY2026: $2.525B; membership fee revenue +6.1%. Note: this exceeds the segment's $2.442B operating income (surprising fact; membership and other income includes some non-fee items).
- Global advertising FY2026: up 46% to nearly $6.4B (includes Vizio, acquired Dec 2024); Walmart Connect U.S. +41%. (Q4 FY26 release)
- eCommerce sales FY2026: about $150.4B (10-K disaggregated revenue); global eCommerce grew about 24% (per earnings coverage; weaker source). Sam's Club eCommerce about $15.0B.
- eCommerce became profitable globally and in the US for the first time in Q1 FY2026 (qtr ended Apr 30, 2025), per company (CFO John David Rainey).
- Scale: 10,955 retail units in 19 countries as of Jan 31, 2026 (Walmart U.S. 4,611; Sam's Club 601; International 5,743); about 2.1 million associates; about 280 million customers a week (company-stated in 10-K).
- 10-K Competition section defines EDLP: pricing items at everyday low prices so customers trust prices won't change under frequent promotional activity; EDLC: everyday low cost, commitment to control expenses so savings can be passed to customers. (paraphrase; language in FY2018-FY2024 10-Ks)

## History
- 1950: Sam Walton opens Walton's 5&10 (Ben Franklin franchise) in Bentonville, Arkansas.
- July 2, 1962: first Walmart opens at 719 W. Walnut St., Rogers, Arkansas (16,000 sq ft). Walton was 44.
- 1970: Walmart goes public; first distribution center and Home Office in Bentonville (some sources say 1971; we say 1970 per Walmart timeline).
- April 1983: first Sam's Club, Midwest City, Oklahoma.
- 1988: first Supercenter, Washington, Missouri.
- Early 1990s: Retail Link shares point-of-sale data (item, store, day) with suppliers; moved to web in 1997.
- Cross-docking: goods move from inbound supplier trucks to outbound store trucks at the DC with little or no storage time.
- Quote (Mar 17, 1992, Presidential Medal of Freedom): "If we work together, we'll lower the cost of living for everyone. We'll give the world an opportunity to see what it's like to save and to have a better life." (Walmart corporate; SPS Commerce)

## Worked examples
1. Per $100 of FY2026 net sales (real ratios): gross profit $24.20; operating expenses $20.90; difference $3.30, plus membership and other income adds about $0.96 per $100 of net sales -> about $4.26 per $100 net sales (vs $4.18 per $100 of total revenue). Use: "about $4.18 of operating profit per $100 of revenue."
2. HYPOTHETICAL turnover: two stores each hold $10,000 of inventory (at cost) and both want $10,000 gross profit a year. Store A turns inventory 9 times = $90,000 of goods at cost sold -> needs markup 10,000/90,000 = 11.1%. Store B turns 4 times = $40,000 -> needs 25%. Faster turns allow lower markups.
3. HYPOTHETICAL membership: 1,000 members x $60 = $60,000 in fees before selling anything.

## Surprising fact
Walmart's FY2026 consolidated net income was about 3.1 cents per dollar of revenue, yet $22.3B in total. And Sam's Club's membership and other income ($2.525B) was bigger than the whole segment's operating income ($2.442B).
