# Research brief: Guarantors, when you need someone to back your lease
Researched Oct 2, 2026 for posting Nov 21, 2026 (evergreen).

## Definitions
- A lease guarantor signs a separate guaranty promising to pay what the tenant owes (rent, other charges, damage under the lease, often the landlord's enforcement/legal costs) if the tenant does not. Guaranties are commonly "joint and several": the landlord can pursue the tenant, the guarantor, or both for the full amount. (Brick Underground sample guaranty riders; tenant-rights.com NY guarantor guide)
- A guarantor usually does not live in the unit or appear on the lease as a tenant.

## Income rules (industry practice, not law)
- NYC practice: tenant must earn 40x the monthly rent per year; guarantor usually 80x (double). StreetEasy: "$3,000 a month apartment ... $120,000 tenant ... $240,000 guarantor." (StreetEasy, "What Is a Guarantor")
- Outside NYC a common screen is income of 3x monthly rent (gross); some use 2.5x-3.5x. Guarantors are often held to a higher multiple (e.g., 4x-5x monthly). (rent-to-income guides, AAOA)
- 40x annual = 3.33x monthly (40/12). 80x annual = 6.67x monthly.

## Worked example (hypothetical $2,000/month rent)
- Tenant at 40x: 2,000 x 40 = $80,000/yr
- Guarantor at 80x: 2,000 x 80 = $160,000/yr
- Tenant at 3x monthly: 2,000 x 3 = $6,000/mo; x 12 = $72,000/yr
- NOTE: the brief suggested "guarantor $80,000 at 40x"; under NYC practice 40x is the TENANT bar, the guarantor bar is 80x = $160,000. Script uses the corrected figures.

## Institutional guarantor companies (fees, as reported)
- Insurent: fee approximates 70%-90% of one month's rent for a one-year lease guaranty for US applicants (higher for non-US; 2-year lease ~85% higher). (Insurent; Brick Underground)
- TheGuarantors: reported roughly 40%-130% of one month's rent depending on risk; often cited 4%-8% of annual rent. (LegalClarity; Panda Guarantee - secondary sources)
- Hypothetical: 70%-90% of $2,000 = $1,400-$1,800 for a 1-year lease.

## Context data
- Harvard JCHS State of the Nation's Housing 2026 (released June 17, 2026): record 22.7 million renter households, 49%, were cost-burdened (30%+ of income on housing) in 2024; 12.1 million severely burdened (50%+). 83% of renters earning under $30,000 cost-burdened.
- Apartment List national median rent, 2026 (chartable series):
  Feb $1,357; Mar $1,363; Apr $1,370; Jun $1,385; Jul $1,388; Aug $1,390; Sep $1,388 (Jan, May not confirmed). Sep 2026 down 0.4% year over year.
- At Sep 2026 median $1,388: 40x = $55,520; 80x = $111,040.

## Surprising fact
- In NYC, the guarantor typically needs to earn DOUBLE the tenant's income bar (80x vs 40x the monthly rent).
