## Picked: Anthropic files publicly for its IPO (Monday, October 26, 2026)

- Anthropic, PBC (maker of the Claude AI models/chatbot) publicly filed a Form S-1 with the SEC on Monday, Oct 26, 2026, before the opening bell (Forbes, CNN, Anthropic news release "Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed initial public offering").

## Context
- An S-1 is the registration statement a company files with the SEC to sell shares to the public; it is the first time the public sees audited financials.
- The huge accounting loss is mostly "paper": when a company's own value rises, instruments that convert into its shares become worth more, and that increase is booked as a loss.
