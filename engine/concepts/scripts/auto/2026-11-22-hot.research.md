# Research: Why is a wedding so expensive? (2026-11-22-hot)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, released Feb 18, 2026; 10,474 US couples married in 2025)
- Average wedding cost $34,200 (excludes engagement ring, avg ring $4,600).
- Average guest count 117; average cost per guest $292 (up $8 from 2024).
- Reception venue average $12,900; catering average $80 per person; photographer average $3,000.
- Medians are much lower than averages (averages skewed by big weddings).

## Chartable series (The Knot average wedding cost, by wedding year)
2019 $28,000; 2020 $19,000; 2021 $28,000; 2022 $30,000; 2023 $35,000; 2024 $33,000; 2025 $34,200.

## Worked example
Catering: $80 x 117 = $9,360. Venue + catering: $12,900 + $9,360 = $22,260 = 65.1% of $34,200.
Per guest: $292 x 117 = $34,164 (~$34,200). 20 fewer guests x $292 = $5,840.

## Surprising fact
About two-thirds of the average budget is the room and the food, not the dress or flowers.
