# Face-off: index fund vs individual stocks (research brief)
- Bessembinder, "Shareholder wealth enhancement" update (29,078 US common stocks, 1926-2023): 51.6% had negative lifetime compound returns; about 4% of stocks account for all net wealth creation above one-month Treasury bills.
- Bessembinder (2018, JFE, 1926-2016): 57.4% of stocks had lifetime buy-and-hold returns below one-month T-bills; top 4% explain net market gain.
- First Trust, "The S&P 500 Index 2025 Recap" (Jan 8, 2026): S&P 500 total return 17.9% in 2025; 30.5% of members beat the index (26.9% in 2023, 27.7% in 2024). So ~69.5% trailed.
- ICI Trends in Expenses and Fees of Funds, 2025: index equity mutual funds averaged 0.05%, index equity ETFs 0.14%, active equity mutual funds 0.64%.
- S&P 500 = about 500 large US companies (503 share classes).
- Worked math: a random 2025 S&P 500 pick: 100% - 30.5% = 69.5% chance it trailed the index.
- Chartable (short): share of S&P 500 stocks beating index: 2023 26.9%, 2024 27.7%, 2025 30.5%.

SOURCES:
- Hendrik Bessembinder, SSRN / ASU (1926-2023 update); Journal of Financial Economics (2018)
- First Trust Economics, S&P 500 Index 2025 Recap (Jan 8, 2026)
- ICI, Trends in the Expenses and Fees of Funds, 2025 (2026)
