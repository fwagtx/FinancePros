# Research brief: The history of the world's biggest financial crises
Slot: 2026-10-15-lf-world (World Money, long-form). Researched Sept 30 - Oct 1, 2026. Posts Thursday Oct 15, 2026 (evergreen: date every number).

## Angle / hook
No single breaking news hook; October is the anniversary month of the 1929 crash (late Oct 1929) and Black Monday (Oct 19, 1987). Use the evergreen hook:
"Stocks once fell 89 percent. It took 25 years to recover." Payoff twist (the surprising fact): counting dividends and deflation, an investor in the average stock at the 1929 high was back to break-even by late 1936 (Mark Hulbert, NYT, via History News Network).
Context (2026): IMF April 2026 GFSR lists a disorderly global market repricing as a key risk; global public debt near 100% of GDP (IMF). Not used as a prediction.

## Facts by era
### 1907
- Panic of 1907 began Oct 1907 after failed corner of United Copper; Knickerbocker Trust collapsed. No central bank; J.P. Morgan organized bankers to supply cash. (NY Fed Liberty Street Economics 2016; FDIC history)
- Federal Reserve Act passed/signed Dec 23, 1913. (US Senate history)
### 1929 / Great Depression
- Dow closed 381.17 on Sept 3, 1929; low 41.22 on July 8, 1932 = 89% below peak; did not regain 1929 high until Nov 23, 1954 (~25 yrs). (Federal Reserve History "Stock Market Crash of 1929")
- More than 9,000 US banks failed 1930-1933, ~30% of banks at end-1929 (Encyclopedia.com / Richmond Fed); depositors lost >$1.3 billion.
- Money supply fell by about 30%+ 1929-33, prices fell >25% (Federal Reserve History / Minneapolis Fed).
- Banking Act of 1933 created FDIC, signed June 16, 1933; insurance began 1934 at $2,500 per depositor. (FDIC)
- Bernanke quote, Nov 8, 2002 (Friedman 90th birthday): "Regarding the Great Depression. You're right, we did it. We're very sorry. But thanks to you, we won't do it again." (federalreserve.gov speech 20021108)
- Hulbert: break-even by late 1936 for lump sum in the average stock, counting dividends and deflation; CPI late 1936 >18% below fall 1929; dividend yield near 14% at July 8, 1932 low. (History News Network reprint of Mark Hulbert, NYT 2009)
### 1987
- Oct 19, 1987: Dow -508 points, -22.6%, largest one-day % drop in its history. (Library of Congress; Fed History)
- Dow first recovered its Black Monday losses Jan 24, 1989 (~15 months); market had recovered by Sept 1989. (CNBC / HNN)
- Today: market-wide circuit breakers halt trading at S&P 500 drops of 7% (15 min), 13% (15 min), 20% (rest of day). (NYSE MWCB FAQ; Nasdaq)
### Japan bubble
- BOJ discount rate 2.5% Feb 1987-May 1989; raised to 3.25% May 1989; 6% by Aug 1990. (BOJ IMES paper)
- Nikkei 225 record 38,915.87 on Dec 29, 1989; closed 7,054.98 on Mar 10, 2009 (lowest since Oct 1982; about 82% below peak). (Nikkei Indexes; Yahoo Finance)
- Closed 39,098.68 on Feb 22, 2024, first close above 1989 record, 34 years later. (Washington Times/AP, Bloomberg)
- 2025 year-end close 50,339.48, record year-end. (Nippon.com/Jiji Dec 30, 2025)
### Asia 1997
- July 2, 1997: Thailand floated the baht. ~25 per dollar before; low 56.50 per dollar on Jan 12, 1998. (Bank of Thailand; Thai PBS World)
- IMF-led packages: Thailand $17.2B (Aug 1997), Indonesia ~$40B (Nov 1997), South Korea $57B (Dec 3, 1997), largest at the time. (CRS report; PBS Frontline)
### 2008
- Lehman Brothers bankruptcy Sept 15, 2008: $639B assets, largest US bankruptcy. (Britannica; History.com)
- Household net worth ~$69T (2007) to ~$55T (2009), ~$14T lost; home prices fell ~30% mid-2006 to mid-2009; S&P 500 -57% Oct 2007-Mar 2009; unemployment peaked 10% Oct 2009. (Federal Reserve History "The Great Recession")
- Fed set funds target 0 to 1/4% on Dec 16, 2008, first time. (Federal Reserve press release)
- EESA raised FDIC limit $100,000 -> $250,000 effective Oct 3, 2008; Dodd-Frank (July 2010) made permanent. Still $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category in 2026; bills to raise it introduced (Semafor, Mar 25, 2026), not law. (FDIC; Bankrate)
- S&P 500 closed 1,569.19 on Mar 28, 2013, passing Oct 9, 2007 record 1,565.15. (CNBC/NPR)
### 2023
- Silicon Valley Bank closed Mar 10, 2023; $42B withdrawn Mar 9; second-largest US bank failure after WaMu 2008; >$200B assets. (Fed review Apr 28, 2023; FDIC)

## WORKED EXAMPLE (exact math, hypothetical)
$10,000 in the Dow (price only, no dividends) at Sept 3, 1929 close 381.17:
value at July 8, 1932 = 10,000 x 41.22 / 381.17 = $1,081.41 (loss 89.2%).
Gain needed to get back: 381.17 / 41.22 = 9.247 -> +825%.
Nikkei: 7,054.98 / 38,915.87 = 0.1813 -> -81.9%.

## Surprising fact
The famous "25 years to recover" from 1929 is the Dow price only. With dividends (yield near 14% at the bottom) and 1930s deflation, the average stock was back to break-even by late 1936 (Hulbert).
