# Research: How Trader Joe's keeps prices low with store brands

Trader Joe's is private (no 10-K); official info is from its own website/podcast. Industry numbers from PLMA/Circana.

## Company facts
- Founded by Joe Coulombe; first Trader Joe's in Pasadena, CA, 1967. Bought in 1979 by Theo Albrecht (Aldi Nord family); private; HQ Monrovia, CA. (Wikipedia; TheStreet)
- 661 US locations as of Aug 2026 (Wikipedia).
- About 80% of products sold under the Trader Joe's label (widely reported; e.g., Today, Acquired, Stratrix). Roughly 4,000 products vs tens of thousands at a typical supermarket.
- Trader Joe's About Us: "'Sale' is a four-letter word to us"; "NO coupons. NO membership cards. NO discounts... We offer the best everyday values, every day." (traderjoes.com via Tasting Table/Yahoo)
- How: buys directly from suppliers (no middlemen), buys in volume, store-brand labels (no national-brand marketing costs), small curated selection, small stores. (Trader Joe's; Today)

## Industry data (PLMA citing Circana Unify+, 2025, released 2026)
- US private-label (store brand) sales record $282.8B in 2025 (+$9B+ vs 2024); store-brand dollar sales +3.3% vs national brands +1.2%.
- Store-brand dollar share record 21.3% (unit share 23.5%) in 2025.
- 2021 -> 2025: store-brand dollar sales +$64.8B (+30%); dollar share 19.1% -> 21.3%.
- (Earlier PLMA releases used different data bases, e.g., 2023 dollar share 18.9%; not mixed into one chart.)

## Chartable series
- Store-brand dollar share (Circana Unify+, same 2026 release): 2021 19.1%, 2025 21.3% (two points; shown as compare, not a line chart).
- Store-brand dollar sales: 2021 ~$218.0B (282.8 - 64.8), 2025 $282.8B.

## Worked example
- 4,000 products vs a typical supermarket's ~30,000+: about 1/8 the items -> fewer products, bigger orders per item, more buying power per product (illustrative).
- 80% of 4,000 = 3,200 store-brand products.

## Surprising fact
- No sales, no coupons, no loyalty card: Trader Joe's says "'Sale' is a four-letter word."
