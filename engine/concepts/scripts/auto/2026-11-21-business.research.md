# Research: How game streaming platforms pay streamers (2026-11-21-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026. Twitch is owned by Amazon; Amazon's 10-K does not break out Twitch, so platform terms come from Twitch's own help/blog pages.

## Facts
- Twitch default subscription split: 50/50 of net subscription revenue for Affiliates and Partners (Twitch Partner Program Overview / Plus Program help page). "Net" is after taxes and certain fees.
- Twitch Plus Program (Twitch blog, Jan 24, 2024 "An Update to Several Streamer Payout Programs"; Twitch Plus Program help page): Level 1 = 60/40 at 100+ Plus Points for 3 consecutive months; Level 2 = 70/30 at 300+ Plus Points for 3 consecutive months. Plus Points: Tier 1 recurring sub = 1, Tier 2 = 2, Tier 3 = 6. Gifted and Prime subs do not count toward points. Enhanced share lasts 12 months from qualifying.
- US Tier 1 sub price: $5.99 since July 11, 2024 (was $4.99; first US increase). Twitch said creators keep the same % share, so they earn more per sub. (Tom's Guide, Dexerto, Destructoid, June-July 2024.) Mobile in-app price higher ($7.99).
- Bits: streamer gets 1 cent per Bit cheered ($1.00 per 100 Bits). Viewers pay about $1.40 for 100 Bits in the US on web (Twitch Bits help; Dexerto).
- Ads: 55% net ad revenue share when the streamer runs at least 3 minutes of ads per hour (Twitch blog, Mar 9, 2023 "A More Flexible Ads Incentive Program"; Twitch Support June 2022). Fewer ads: 30%.
- YouTube: creators keep 70% of channel memberships, Super Chat and Super Stickers (YouTube Help).
- Kick: 95/5 subscription split, creators keep 95% (Kick; Streams Charts 2026 Kick guide; Castr).
- Twitch hours watched: 19.2 billion in 2025, market share down 8.3 points over the year (Stream Hatchet 2025 Live Streaming Trends Report). 2024 roughly 20.8-20.9B (sources vary).

## Worked example (hypothetical, gross before taxes/fees)
100 recurring Tier 1 subs x $5.99 = $599.00/month.
- 50/50: $599 x 0.50 = $299.50
- 60/40: $599 x 0.60 = $359.40
- 70/30: $599 x 0.70 = $419.30
Difference 70/30 vs 50/50 = $119.80/month; x12 = $1,437.60/year.
Bits: viewer pays $1.40 for 100 Bits, streamer gets $1.00 -> $0.40 (about 29% of what the viewer paid) stays with Twitch.

## Surprising fact
The sub price tag isn't what the streamer earns: the same $5.99 sub can pay about $3.00 or about $4.19 depending on the streamer's Plus tier, and gifted subs don't count toward the better tier.
