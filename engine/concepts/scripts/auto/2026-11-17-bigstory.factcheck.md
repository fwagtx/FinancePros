## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 1 (awkward narration):** "a builder survey out today, where their confidence index fell..." was a long run-on sentence. Split into two sentences: "That's from a builder survey out today. Their confidence index fell to 25, the lowest since 2012." (text and say updated)
2. **Line 3 (wording):** NAHB measures "traffic of prospective buyers", but the script said "Foot traffic". Changed to "Buyer traffic" so it matches the on-screen label. (text and say updated)
3. **Line 5 (misleading "both"):** In "46% cut prices, the average cut is 6%, and 68% offer incentives, both post-Covid highs", "both" could be read as covering the 6% average cut, which is unchanged and not a high. Reordered to: "46% cut prices and 68% offer incentives, both post-Covid highs. The average cut is 6%." The list items were reordered to match (46% cut prices / 68% add perks / Avg cut: 6%).
4. **Line 6 (text and say differed):** The text said "The takeaway:" while the narration said "The takeaway is simple." Changed the text to match what is spoken.
