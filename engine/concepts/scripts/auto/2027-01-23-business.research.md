# Research brief: How self-storage makes money on stuff people forget they own
Made Nov 24, 2026 for posting Sat Jan 23, 2027 (evergreen). Show: How They Make Money.
Note: direct fetch of sec.gov / businesswire / q4cdn was blocked in this session; figures below were confirmed via web search results that quote the filings and the company's own release pages. Firecrawl unavailable (out of credits).

## Company: Public Storage (NYSE: PSA), largest US self-storage operator. Fiscal year 2025 (ended Dec 31, 2025).
- Scale: at Dec 31, 2025, owned and/or operated 3,533 self-storage facilities in 40 states, ~258 million net rentable sq ft (US); also 35% of Shurgard (Europe). Source: PSA release boilerplate (Feb 17, 2026) / FY2025 10-K.
- Total revenues (company-wide, $ millions):
  - 2021: 3,416  | 2022: 4,182 (FY2022 annual report/10-K)
  - 2023: 4,517.7 | 2024: 4,695.6 | 2025: 4,824.1 (FY2025 10-K)
- Ancillary operations revenue (mostly tenant reinsurance, i.e., insurance on stored goods): 2023 $258.1M; 2024 $299.6M; 2025 $334.7M. Tenant reinsurance premiums alone: 2023 $203.5M; 2024 $226.6M; 2025 $250.7M (+10.6%). (FY2025 10-K)
- Same-store pool (2,565 facilities, 175.3M sq ft, 76.4% of sq ft, 85.4% of NOI):
  - Same-store revenue 2025 $3,764.8M vs 2024 $3,763.6M (about flat).
  - Same-store NOI 2025 $2,828.9M vs $2,844.2M in 2024 (-0.5%).
  - Gross margin before indirect costs (full year 2025) 78.2% (78.5% in 2024); Q4 2025 same-store NOI margin 78.4%.
  - OUR MATH: NOI / revenue = 2,828.9 / 3,764.8 = 0.751 -> about 75 cents of each same-store revenue dollar was NOI in 2025.
  - Realized annual rent per occupied sq ft: $22.54 in 2025 (+0.5% from $22.43).
  - Weighted average sq ft occupancy: 92.0% in 2025 (92.4% in 2024). Q4 2025: 91.6%.
- The model ("low move-in rate, then existing customer rent increases", ECRI):
  - FY2025 10-K: "More than half of our tenants have rented their space for longer than a year at December 31, 2025, which supported our revenue growth from existing long-term tenants." Same statement for Dec 31, 2024 in FY2024 10-K.
  - FY2025 10-K: company lowered move-in rental rates in 2025 vs 2024 to stimulate move-ins; realized rent rose due to cumulative rate increases to existing long-term tenants, partly offset by lower rates to new tenants.
  - FY2024 10-K (via summary): average rates charged to new tenants fell 11.6% over the prior 12 months, partially offset by cumulative rate increases to existing long-term tenants. (secondary; not used on screen)
  - Q4 2024 call: management assumed 2025 move-in rents down ~5% at midpoint; early 2025 move-in rates down ~8%. (transcript, not used on screen)

## Worked example (OUR MATH)
A $100/month unit kept for 3 years: $100 x 12 x 3 = $3,600 (before any rent increases or fees).

## Surprising fact
More than half of Public Storage's tenants had been renting for over a year (Dec 31, 2025), and the company cut move-in rates in 2025 while revenue growth came from existing long-term tenants.
