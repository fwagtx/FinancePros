## Issues and fixes (0-based line indexes)

1. **Line 3: overstated inference.** The charge-off rate is dollar-based: written-off balances as a share of loans. It is not a count of borrowers, and card loans grew from $234.4B to $250.1B. "Fewer borrowers are defaulting" goes beyond the data.
   - text: `In other words, a smaller share of card balances is going bad.`
   - say: `In other words, a smaller share of card balances is going bad.` (12 words, was 10)

2. **Line 7: one company's numbers generalized to "banks".** The only data in the script is JPMorgan's.
   - text: `JPMorgan sees most cardholders still paying, and it's watching rates and prices.`
   - say: `So JPMorgan sees most cardholders still paying, while it watches rates and prices.` (13 words, was 16)
   - scene.title: `What JPM is watching`
   - scene.items: `["Rising rates", "Sticky inflation", "Fiscal deficits"]`. Dimon listed deficits, not card charge-offs, and this change also lines up with yt_desc.

3. **Line 4: rounding, and on-screen vs spoken mismatch.** The actual figure is +9.6%. The on-screen text also says "debit and credit cards" while the spoken line says "card purchases".
   - say: `They're spending too, with card purchases up about ten percent from a year earlier.` (14 words, was 13)
   - text: `Spending on its debit and credit cards grew about 10% from a year earlier.`
   - The stat value "+10%" can stay.

4. **post.tiktok (minor):** "Card defaults fell" makes the same overstatement as issue 1. Change it to `JPMorgan made $15.3B in 3 months. Card charge-offs fell. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #banks #creditcards #financeprostv` (142 characters).

5. **Line 6 narration (minor):** "adding: these forces..." makes the TTS run the quote straight on without a pause. Suggested say: `Jamie Dimon flagged sticky inflation and rising rates, adding, these forces may be manageable, but we remain prepared for a wide range of outcomes.` The word count is unchanged.
