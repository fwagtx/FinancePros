# Research brief: How sneaker resale platforms make money (posts Wed Jan 6, 2027)

Checked: November 7, 2026 (web search; stockx.com could not be fetched directly from this session, so StockX rates are from StockX help/seller pages as shown in search results plus third-party guides).
No timely news hook used; evergreen. Note: StockX and GOAT are private (no 10-K). Company numbers come from company press releases.

## The model in one line
Resale apps (StockX, GOAT, eBay's sneaker program) are middlemen: they hold no inventory of their own in the core marketplace, match buyers and sellers, inspect/authenticate the pair, and charge fees on both sides. What they sell is trust that the shoe is real.

## Key facts (with dates)
- StockX 2020 results (StockX "annual snapshot" press release, early 2021): more than 7.5 million trades, $1.8 billion gross merchandise value (GMV), GAAP revenue of more than $400 million.
- StockX GMV 2019: surpassed $1 billion (StockX announcement, Jan 2020; FashionUnited, DBusiness).
- StockX valuation: $3.8 billion announced April 8, 2021 ($195M secondary tender + $60M Series E-1); up 35% from $2.8B (Series E, Dec 2020, led by Tiger Global). (StockX press release; CNBC via NBC; Business of Fashion)
- StockX seller fees (as of Nov 2026, StockX help center "What are StockX's fees for sellers?"): every seller pays a 3% payment processing fee plus a transaction fee set by Seller Level. StockX's pages list Level 1 at 9%, falling to 7% at the top level. Third-party guides note a $5 US minimum seller fee and seller shipping deductions (not confirmed on StockX's page). Some third-party sites cite 9.5%/10% for Level 1 (older or unreliable).
- StockX buyer fees: StockX help center says buyers pay processing fees (plus taxes/duties); fee varies by price/location. 2020 coverage (Sneaker Freaker, Input) reported a 3% buyer processing fee on item price plus shipping (~$13.95 then). Exact current buyer amounts NOT confirmed -> say only "buyers pay processing fees and shipping on top".
- GOAT (goat.com/fees, checked Nov 2026): commission 9.5% plus a seller fee for sellers rated 90+; 15% (rating 70-89), 20% (50-69), 25% (below 50). Canadian sellers 12.4% base. Cash-out fee 2.9% (ACH/PayPal) per GOAT fee page / guides. US flat seller fee amount not confirmed (UK $5 per snippet).
- eBay sneakers (pages.ebay.com/sell-sneakers; checked Nov 2026): 8% final value fee on eligible sneakers sold for $150 or more, with Authenticity Guarantee at no extra fee to the seller (eBay covers authentication). Under $150, standard ~13.6% rate per third-party guides.
- StockX authentication: claims 99.96% accuracy (company claim). Verified Seller program (April 2025) lets vetted sellers ship directly. Nike v. StockX: March 2025 ruling found StockX sold counterfeit Nike pairs; settled Aug 2025 (confidential).
- Market size (careful): Cowen (2019) estimated North American sneaker resale at ~$2 billion and projected ~$6 billion globally by 2025; later Cowen said $30B globally by 2030. Estimates, not used in script.

## Worked example (our math, StockX Level 1 rates as of Nov 2026)
$200 sale: transaction fee 9% x $200 = $18; processing 3% x $200 = $6; total $24 to StockX. Seller gets $200 - $24 = $176 (before any shipping deduction). Buyer pays $200 plus buyer processing fee, shipping and tax on top.

## Surprising fact (our math)
StockX 2020: revenue >$400M / GMV $1.8B = 22.2% -> StockX kept over 22 cents of every dollar traded in 2020 (revenue includes both seller and buyer fees and shipping charges). Seller fees alone are ~12%; the rest comes from buyer-side fees and shipping.
