## Issues and fixes (0-based line indexes)

### 1. The hook's "sold devices below cost" wording is imprecise (lines 0 and 1, cover, post)
Equipment revenue minus cost of equipment is an accounting gross loss across all devices sold, including upgrades, business lines, tablets and hotspots. It is also shaped by how promo credits and contract assets are allocated between equipment and service. It does not mean every device was sold below cost. More precise wording is "lost $3.5B on devices" or "device costs topped device revenue by $3.5B". The spoken hook also drops the word "dollars".
- **Line 0 text:** `In 2025, Verizon lost $3.5 billion on devices.`
- **Line 0 say (12 words):** `In twenty twenty-five, Verizon lost three point five billion dollars on devices.`
- **Line 0 scene.title:** `Lost [on] devices`. Optional; the current title can stay.
- **post.title (41 chars):** `Why Verizon lost $3.5B on devices in 2025`
- **post.tiktok (131 chars):** `Verizon lost $3.5B on devices in 2025. Here's why. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #verizon #phones #financeprostv`
- **post.ig, first line:** `In 2025, Verizon's device costs topped device revenue by $3.5 billion.`
- Line 1 can stay as written; its numbers are correct.
