# Research brief: How Netflix makes money (long-form, posts Nov 3, 2026)

## FY2025 (10-K filed Jan 23, 2026; Q4 letter Jan 20, 2026)
- Revenue $45.2B (+16%); operating income $13.3B; operating margin 29.5% (+3 pts); net income $11.0B; free cash flow ~$9.5B.
- Ad revenue more than 2.5x to over $1.5B.
- Paid memberships: crossed 325M in Q4 2025 (Netflix no longer reports quarterly subscriber counts).
- Content: ~$18B content spend in 2025; plans ~$20B in 2026 (~10% growth in amortization).
- 2026 outlook: revenue $51.0-51.4B (narrowed July 2026), operating margin 31.5%, ad revenue ~ $3B (roughly double).
- Q2 2026 (Jul 16, 2026): revenue $12.6B (+13%), operating margin ~33%.

## US prices (Standard plan, monthly)
- 2019 $12.99 -> 2020 $13.99 -> 2022 $15.49 -> 2025 $17.99 -> Mar 26, 2026 $19.99 (+54% since 2019).
- Mar 2026 plans: Standard with ads $8.99; Standard $19.99; Premium $26.99.

## History
- Founded 1997 (DVD by mail); streaming 2007; first big original House of Cards 2013.
- Q1 2022: lost 200,000 subscribers, first loss in over 10 years -> ad tier (Nov 2022) and paid sharing crackdown (US May 2023).
- Live: WWE Raw from Jan 2025, 10 years, ~$5B; NFL Christmas Day games.
- Warner Bros. deal: agreed Dec 4, 2025 to buy WBD studios + HBO Max; amended to all-cash Jan 2026; WBD terminated Feb 27, 2026 for Paramount Skydance's higher bid; Netflix received a $2.8B termination fee.

## Worked examples
- Operating margin: of every $10 of 2025 revenue, about $2.95 was operating profit.
- Rough revenue per member: $45.2B / 325M = ~$139/yr (~$11.59/mo) (rough: uses year-end members).
- Content per member: $18B / 325M = ~$55/yr; content = ~40% of revenue.
- Standard plan for a year in 2026: $19.99 x 12 = $239.88.
- Revenue 2019 -> 2025: x2.24.
