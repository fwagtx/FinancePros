# Research brief: What happens if you get audited by the IRS? (slot 2026-12-02-hot)

Format: Money Myth / Scam angle. Myth: an audit means agents at your door. Fact: about 9 in 10 individual audits closed in FY2025 were done by mail.
No timely news peg needed (evergreen). Latest data: 2025 IRS Data Book (Publication 55-B, Rev. 4-2026), covering FY2025 (Oct 1, 2024 - Sep 30, 2025).

## Key facts (with sources)

1. Audits by mail (2025 IRS Data Book, Table 3-2, FY2025):
   - All returns: 497,621 examinations closed; 94,562 field, 403,059 correspondence; $26.8 billion recommended additional tax.
   - Individual income tax returns: 441,578 closed; 47,811 field; 393,767 correspondence.
   - Math: 393,767 / 441,578 = 0.8917 = 89.2% by mail (correspondence). About 9 in 10.
   - Footnote: "Field examinations are generally performed in person ... However, some field examinations may ultimately be conducted through correspondence."
   - IRS: "Most IRS examinations are conducted through the mail (correspondence) or face-to-face (field)." (irs.gov Compliance presence)

2. Audit rate (exam coverage), individual returns, 2025 Data Book Table 3-1 (published "percentage covered" = closed + in-process exams / returns filed for that tax year):
   | Tax year | % covered (published) | exams (closed+open) | returns filed | computed % |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 2015 | 0.6 | 877,153 | 150,675,111 | 0.582 |
   | 2016 | 0.5 | 801,260 | 150,447,029 | 0.533 |
   | 2017 | 0.5 | 734,419 | 153,062,634 | 0.480 |
   | 2018 | 0.4 | 556,408 | 153,927,628 | 0.361 |
   | 2019 | 0.3 | 511,048 | 157,951,815 | 0.324 |
   | 2020 | 0.3 | 495,114 | 164,511,483 | 0.301 |
   | 2021 | 0.3 | 510,375 | 161,208,833 | 0.317 |
   | 2022 | 0.3 (still open) | 472,613 | 161,666,477 | 0.292 |
   | 2023 | 0.1 (still open) | 159,266 | 160,934,651 | 0.099 |
   - TY2021 is "the most recent year outside the statute of limitations period" (Data Book highlights). TY2022-2023 will rise as exams open, so the chart stops at TY2021.
   - Cumulative TY2015-2023: 0.36% (computed; also reported by Brotman Law and Galek Tax Law, Sept 2026).
   - Chart series (published, one decimal): [[2015,0.6],[2016,0.5],[2017,0.5],[2018,0.4],[2019,0.3],[2020,0.3],[2021,0.3]]. Roughly halved.
   - Context (older methodology/editions): Tax Policy Center: 0.9% of 2011 returns vs 0.3% of 2018 returns; GAO-22-104960: average audit rate fell from 0.9% (TY2010) to 0.25% (TY2019).

3. By income, TY2021 (Table 3-1 / Data Book highlights): $10M+ TPI 6.6%; $5M-$10M 3.9%; $1M-$5M 0.9%; $1-$25,000 0.5%; all individuals 0.3%. Returns claiming EITC are audited more than average.

4. How the IRS contacts you:
   - IRS audits page: "Should your account be selected for audit, we will notify you by mail. We won't initiate an audit by telephone." Audits are "by mail or through an in-person interview."
   - IRS Tax Tip 2024-63 (July 29, 2024): "The IRS will mail a letter or notice before calling or emailing." The IRS does not "Initiate contact with taxpayers by email, text message or through social media." The IRS "doesn't leave pre-recorded, urgent or threatening messages"; it and its collection agencies "will never ask a taxpayer to pay using any form of pre-paid card, store or online gift card."

5. Deadlines:
   - CP2000 (mismatch notice): IRS video script: "THIS DOESN'T MEAN YOU'RE BEING AUDITED"; "SEND YOUR REPLY ... WITHIN 30 DAYS OF THE DATE OF THE LETTER." No reply -> CP3219A statutory notice of deficiency.
   - Audits by mail: IRS can "ordinarily grant you a one-time automatic 30-day extension."
   - Notice of deficiency: 90 days to petition U.S. Tax Court (150 days if outside the US). (irs.gov "Time IRS can assess tax"; IRS audits page)
   - No response: "we will complete our audit and send you an audit report with our proposed changes."

6. Look-back:
   - "The IRS can usually assess tax, by law, within 3 years after your return was due ... or ... after we received your return, whichever is later." (irs.gov Time IRS can assess tax)
   - 6 years when more than 25% of gross income was omitted (IRC 6501(e)); IRS page phrases it as an exception extending "from 3 to 6 years." Fraud or no return: no limit.
   - IRS audits page: "We usually don't go back more than the last six years." "most audits will be of returns filed within the last two years."

7. Outcomes and rights (IRS audits page): ends as "No change", "Agreed" or "Disagreed". If you disagree: conference with an IRS manager, mediation (ADR), or appeal to the Independent Office of Appeals. Rights (Publication 1) include representation and "A right to appeal disagreements, both within the IRS and before the courts."
   - FY2025: 12,192 closed exams (2.5%) were unagreed (Table 3-2 highlights).

8. Staffing (2025 Data Book Table 6-3): IRS employees in pay status: 99,628 on Sep 30, 2024 -> 80,967 on Sep 30, 2025 (-18.7%). Revenue agents: 10,736 -> 8,609. (Not used in script, word budget.)

## Surprising fact
About 89% of individual audits closed in FY2025 were handled by mail, and the overall rate for 2021 returns was about 0.3% (roughly 3 in 1,000).
