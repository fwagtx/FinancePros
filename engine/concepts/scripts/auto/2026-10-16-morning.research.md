## Facts (Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, released Thu Oct 15, 2026)
- 30-year fixed average: 7.40%, unchanged from Oct 8 (7.40%).
- 15-year fixed: 6.71%, down from 6.73%.
- Year ago (Oct 2025): 30-year averaged 6.27%.
- The flat reading ends seven straight weekly increases.
- 7.40% (first hit Oct 8) is the highest since November 2023 (Nov 16, 2023: 7.44%).
- 2026 low: 5.98% in late February 2026; rates have climbed since.
- Sept 24, 2026: 7.03%, first time above 7% since January 2025.
- Khater (Freddie Mac chief economist): 30-year rate "remained unchanged this week."
- Freddie Mac's number is an average of application-based rates for conventional conforming purchase loans; individual offers differ (credit, down payment, lender).
- Next release: Thursday Oct 22, 2026.

## Context
- Mortgage rates tend to track the 10-year Treasury yield, not the Fed's rate directly. Hot September CPI/PPI reports this week kept rate worries alive (not the main topic).
- Meaning for a normal person: a pause is not a drop; the payment on the same loan is still far above February's. Buyers commonly compare quotes from several lenders because the survey is only an average.
