## What it means for a normal person
- A trade deficit is the gap between what US buyers (households and businesses) purchase from abroad and what the US sells abroad. It is not a debt that gets repaid.
- August's jump was largely business purchases: capital goods (incl. chips), crude oil and gold.
- Imports are subtracted in GDP accounting, so a big import month can weigh on the reported GDP growth number.
- Tariffs are paid by US importers on these goods.
