## Issues

1. **Stale and wrong: headline stat (L26-33; also post L184 and L186). Must fix.**
   - Gallup's April 2026 survey found 58% of US adults own stock, down from 62% in 2025.
   - So the current figure for "own no stock" is **42%**, not 38%. Citing 2025 data a year later, when 2026 data exists, is stale.
   - Fix L26: text "Yet in 2026, 42% of Americans owned no stock at all."
   - Fix L27: say "Yet in twenty twenty-six, forty-two percent of Americans owned no stock at all."
   - Fix the scene: value "42%", sub "Gallup, 2026".
   - Apply the same change to the IG caption (L184) and the YouTube description (L186).

2. **Stale and misleading: trend line and chart (L39-40, L44-87). Must fix.**
   - "Ownership has climbed ... to sixty-two percent" is no longer true. Ownership fell to 58% in 2026, the first drop since 2016.
   - Fix the chart: add the point [2026, 58] and set end to "58%". Consider changing color to "down".
   - Fix L39: text "Ownership rose from 52% in 2016 to 62% in 2025, then dipped to 58% in 2026."
   - Fix L40: say "Ownership climbed from fifty-two percent in twenty sixteen to sixty-two percent in twenty twenty-five, then slipped to fifty-eight this year, and the myth still sticks."
   - If you prefer a shorter line, drop the "rose" framing.

3. **Hook strength (L13-14). Minor.**
   - The hook is true and quick: about 10 words, under 3 seconds.
   - It is a familiar "myth" framing, so it does not create much need for the answer.
   - Stronger option: "One dollar can buy you a piece of a stock. Here's how." Or lead with the surprise: "Four in ten Americans own zero stock, but you can start with one dollar."
   - "Try $1" also reads as a mild call to action. It is acceptable because this is an education video.

4. **Mild advice tone (L158-159). Minor.**
   - "The habit matters more than the amount" is an opinion or recommendation.
   - Softer option: "Five dollars a week adds up to two hundred sixty dollars a year."
   - Style only; this does not cause a FAIL.

5. **Time words.** "In 2025" (L26-27, L39-40) will read as old news on October 03, 2026. The fixes in issues 1 and 2 resolve this.

6. **Narration read-aloud.** Fine overall. On L109, "per S and P five hundred stock" is slightly clunky. Alternative: "and at Schwab, five dollars for any S and P five hundred stock."

## Summary
Every number except one checks out, and the math is correct. The headline stat and the trend chart rely on 2025 Gallup data, but Gallup's April 2026 survey shows 58% ownership (42% own no stock), down from 62%. As written, the video states a stale figure and a "climbing" trend that has since reversed.

VERDICT: FAIL
