# Research brief: How the euro was born, and why it's so hard to run (long-form, posts Nov 5, 2026)

## History
- Maastricht Treaty signed Feb 7, 1992; convergence rules: budget deficit <= 3% of GDP, government debt <= 60% of GDP (plus inflation and interest-rate tests). UK and Denmark negotiated opt-outs.
- ECB set up June 1, 1998 in Frankfurt.
- Euro launched Jan 1, 1999 in 11 countries as an electronic/accounting currency; notes and coins Jan 1, 2002 in 12 countries (Greece joined 2001).
- Bulgaria became the 21st member on Jan 1, 2026 (1 EUR = 1.95583 lev); more than 357 million people share the euro (European Commission).
- EUR/USD: first reference rate 1.1789 (Jan 4, 1999); low ~0.83 (Oct 2000); record high ~1.60 (July 2008); fell below $1 in July 2022 for the first time in about 20 years.

## Crisis
- Oct 2009: new Greek government revealed a far bigger deficit than reported. First bailout May 2010 (EU/IMF, EUR 110 billion); three programs 2010-2015; Greece exited its last program in Aug 2018.
- Greek 10-year bond yield above 40% in March 2012.
- Mario Draghi, London, July 26, 2012: "Within our mandate, the ECB is ready to do whatever it takes to preserve the euro. And believe me, it will be enough."
- Greece debt/GDP (Eurostat): 2007 ~104.6% | 2009 ~128.5% | 2011 ~175% | 2015 ~180% | 2020 ~210% | 2024 ~154% | 2025 ~146%.

## Running it now
- ECB deposit rate: negative from June 2014 (-0.10%) to July 2022; peak 4.00% (Sept 2023); cut to 2.00% by June 2025; raised to 2.50% effective Sept 16, 2026 (decision Sept 10, 2026).
- Euro area inflation: peak 10.6% (Oct 2022); 3.2% in Aug 2026 (energy +14.3%) (Eurostat, Sept 17, 2026).
- Euro area government debt 87.8% of GDP (end-2025, Eurostat Apr 2026).

## Your wallet
- EUR/USD annual averages (ECB): 1999 1.0658 | 2000 0.9236 | 2001 0.8956 | 2002 0.9456 | 2003 1.1312 | 2004 1.2439 | 2006 1.2556 | 2008 1.4708 | 2010 1.3257 | 2012 1.2848 | 2014 1.3285 | 2015 1.1095 | 2018 1.1810 | 2020 1.1422 | 2022 1.0530 | 2024 1.0824 | 2025 1.1300. About 1.13 at end Sept 2026.
- Worked example: a EUR 200 hotel night: at 0.9236 (2000 avg) = $184.72; at 1.4708 (2008) = $294.16; at 1.13 (2025) = $226.
- US goods imports from the EU 2025: $632.9 billion (USTR/Census).
- Euro share of global FX reserves 20.03% vs dollar 57.13% (IMF COFER, Q1 2026).
