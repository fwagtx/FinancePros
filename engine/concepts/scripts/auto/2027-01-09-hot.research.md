# 2027-01-09-hot research brief: Face-off, refurbished vs brand-new electronics

Researched Tuesday, November 10, 2026. Posts Saturday, January 9, 2027 (right after holiday gift season). Evergreen: no news hook; every figure dated.

## Key facts (with dates and sources)

### Apple Certified Refurbished (apple.com, seen Nov 10, 2026)
- Apple's "Why Refurbished" page: "Refurbished iOS devices will come with new battery and outer shell." (exact quote) [S1]
- Same page: "our standard one-year limited warranty with every Apple Certified Refurbished product"; AppleCare can be added. New Apple hardware: "Most Apple hardware comes with a one-year limited warranty." So refurb warranty = the standard 1-year warranty new hardware gets. [S1]
- Page meta description: "Save up to 15% and get a one-year warranty with the Apple Refurbished purchasing program." [S1]
- Other refurbished parts: "genuine Apple replacement parts (as needed)", "thoroughly cleaned and inspected". [S1]
- Caveat: the new battery + outer shell wording is for iOS devices (iPhone). For Macs, third-party sites disagree; do NOT claim new battery for Macs. Whether Apple counts iPads as "iOS devices" is not stated; keep the battery/shell claim to iPhone.

### Worked example (real listing, apple.com/shop/refurbished/ipad, seen Nov 10, 2026) [S2]
- Refurbished iPad mini: "Now $509.00 Was $599.00 Save $90.00" (Apple's own listing; "Was" is Apple's original new price for that configuration).
- Math: $599 - $509 = $90. $90 / $599 = 0.1503 = 15.0% (about 15%). Matches Apple's "up to 15%".
- Second listing same page: iPad mini Now $599 Was $699, save $100 -> 100/699 = 14.3%.
- Uncertain: the exact storage configuration of the $509 unit was not visible in the scrape (likely 256GB Wi-Fi, A17 Pro). Prices on the refurb store change often; quote "as listed Nov 2026".

### Amazon Renewed (Amazon help/policy pages and third-party guides) [S3][S4][S5]
- Standard Amazon Renewed: at least a 90-day Amazon Renewed Guarantee (refund or replacement).
- Renewed Premium: guarantee extended to one year (365 days) in the US; Premium battery must exceed 90% of new capacity.
- Amazon Renewed quality policy: when a battery is present, it must exceed 80% of the capacity of a new equivalent.
- Caveat: coverage can vary by listing/region (UK/EU standard guarantee is 1 year per refurb.me). Amazon's own Premium page not directly fetched; the 1-year figure is consistent across Android Authority, refurb.me, Seller Labs.

### Best Buy refurbished grades [S6][S7]
- Grades: Excellent (little to no visible wear), Good (light signs of wear), Fair (visible wear/scratches). All refurbished items inspected to be fully functional (Best Buy Canada).
- Best Buy US product page: "All refurbished products include a warranty and warranty length varies by condition."
- Exact US warranty length per grade: not confirmed; do not state a number.

### Definitions (retailer guides; no official FTC definition found) [S8][S9]
- Open-box: returned after being unboxed, never or barely used; sold as is or after inspection.
- Refurbished: inspected, repaired/parts replaced as needed, tested, usually sold with a warranty.
- Used / pre-owned: sold without restoration, often no warranty.
- Labels are not standardized; "like new" and "renewed" vary by seller.

### E-waste (UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024, UNITAR/ITU, published March 2024) [S10][S11]
- 62 million tonnes (62 billion kg) of e-waste generated in 2022, up 82% from 2010 (34 Mt).
- 22.3% documented as formally collected and recycled in 2022 (13.8 Mt).
- 7.8 kg per person in 2022.
- Projection: 82 Mt by 2030; documented recycling rate projected to fall to 20% by 2030.
- E-waste rising about five times faster than documented recycling.

## Surprising fact
An Apple Certified Refurbished iPhone comes with a new battery and new outer shell, and the same standard one-year limited warranty as new hardware, for up to 15% off.

## The catch
- Warranty: Apple 1 year; Amazon Renewed 90 days standard (1 year Premium); Best Buy varies by condition.
- Seller quality: "refurbished" is not a standardized label; who did the work matters.
- Battery health: Amazon minimum >80% (standard) or >90% (Premium); Apple iPhones get a new battery.
