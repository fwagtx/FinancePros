# Fact-check: 2026-12-16-hot (credit freeze myth)

Checked: 2026-10-17. Posts: Wed 2026-12-16. Source read: 2026-12-16-hot.raw.json only.

## Verified (no change needed)

- **Freeze does not affect score (L1, L2, L3, L6):** matches FTC/CFPB consumer guidance. A freeze limits access to the report and does not change the data that goes into a score.
- **Free since 2018 and the timing rules (L4, post):** the Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act (P.L. 115-174, sec. 301) added FCRA 605A(i). It took effect Sept 21, 2018. The text says a freeze is removed "free of charge... not later than (i) ... by toll-free telephone or secure electronic means, 1 hour after receiving the request ... (ii) ... by mail, 3 business days." Temporary lifts follow the same timing under (i)(3)(E). The script is correct.
- **What a freeze doesn't block (L5):** FCRA 605A(i)(4) exempts (A) existing creditors and their collectors reviewing an account, (H) "underwriting of insurance" and (I) "employment, tenant, or background screening purposes." So "Existing cards / Job checks / Rental checks / Insurance" is accurate. "Blocks: New accounts / New lenders" is accurate.
- **Fraud alert (L6):** the description "makes lenders verify it's you" is accurate: users must take reasonable steps to verify identity. The script gives no durations, so nothing can be wrong there. For reference, the initial alert lasts 1 year (raised from 90 days by the 2018 law), the extended alert 7 years and the active-duty alert 1 year.
- **Lock (L6):** "a bureau's own product, sometimes paid" is accurate. Lock products are bureau-offered, not set by statute. Equifax and TransUnion offer free locks, while Experian CreditLock comes with a paid plan.
- **Chart, 2020-2024 (L7):** FTC Consumer Sentinel identity theft counts are about 1.39M (2020), 1.43M (2021), 1.11M (2022), 1.04M (2023) and 1.14M (2024, 1,135,291). The script's one-decimal values match.
- **No instructions:** nothing tells viewers to freeze, lift or buy anything. The CTA is "Follow for more" only. There is no advice, price prediction or politics.
- **On-screen text vs spoken:** they match on every line. Limits are respected: board lines are at most 20, list and split items at most 16, timeline "when" values are 4 characters, the stat value is 8 and the sub is 12. There are no keep:true or compare scenes.
