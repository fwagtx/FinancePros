### IEA Oil Market Report, November 2026 (published Fri Nov 13, 2026)
- Global oil supply is topping demand for the first time since the Iran war began; market returning to surplus in Q4 2026, larger surplus in 2027 (IEA; Quartz; The National; Rigzone; BNN Bloomberg; ThePrint; Arab News).
- Observed global inventories rose 52 million barrels in October (1.7 mb/d), the first increase since June (IEA report summary; Quartz/ThePrint).
- Global supply rose 2.3 mb/d to 105.4 mb/d in October, driven by Gulf exports (ThePrint).
- Gulf exports (ex-Oman) 18.4 mb/d in October, still 26% below pre-war levels (IEA via Quartz/ThePrint).
- Since the war began, global inventories fell 462 million barrels in total (IEA via ThePrint).
- 2026 demand forecast to fall 2.7 mb/d to 102.3 mb/d (IEA; MLQ; The National).
- 2027 surplus about 3.8 mb/d (Bloomberg) (not used).
- Risks the IEA flags: continued attacks on shipping/tankers; diesel supply tight.
- Brent about $95.71 after the report (Bloomberg/BNN; not used).
- Next IEA Oil Market Report: Dec 11, 2026.

## What it means for a normal person
- Fill-ups are cheaper than a month ago (about $7 less on 15 gallons, our math) but still pricier than a year ago (82 cents a gallon more).
- Pump prices follow crude oil with a lag; more oil than the world uses means stockpiles rebuild, which is what took pressure off crude. Winter blend also helps every fall.
- Gas is a big part of CPI; it drove much of the inflation jump this year.
- Not a prediction: the IEA itself flags tanker attacks as a risk.
