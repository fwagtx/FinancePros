# 2026-10-30-hot research brief: Scam alert, "send crypto, get double back" livestreams
Researched October 1, 2026. Posts October 30, 2026. Scope: crypto "giveaway"/"doubling" scams run on deepfake or hijacked YouTube livestreams impersonating celebrities/CEOs. NOT pig-butchering, fake exchange support (Oct 28 short), or AI voice clones (covered elsewhere).

## Timely / anchor case: fake Nvidia GTC livestream (October 28, 2025)
- During Nvidia's GTC Washington, D.C. keynote on October 28, 2025, a YouTube channel branded "NVIDIA Live" streamed an AI-generated deepfake of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. (Tom's Hardware, PC Gamer, Engadget, TechSpot, CRN via Yahoo; OECD.AI incident log dated 2025-10-29.)
- The deepfake Huang promoted a fake "crypto mass adoption event" and urged viewers to scan an on-screen QR code; the QR code led to a site claiming to double any cryptocurrency sent to it (TechSpot/Yahoo/CRN coverage; Tom's Hardware: "encouraged viewers to send in supported cryptocurrencies through a QR code").
- Viewers: at one point about 95,000 people watched the fake stream vs about 12,000 on the real keynote (CRN reporting, carried by Yahoo: "Jensen Huang Deepfake Draws Nearly 8x the Views as the Real Nvidia GTC Stream"; Engadget "More people watched a fake NVIDIA livestream than the real thing"). Other outlets (Tom's Hardware, PC Gamer) described it as ~100,000 viewers and ~5x the real event at a different moment. Use "at one point" and attribute to CRN.
- The fake ranked above Nvidia's real feed in YouTube search for a time; YouTube removed it (Tom's Hardware; TechSpot). Hosting channel not named in script (not needed).
- Math: 95,000 / 12,000 = 7.92, so "nearly 8x".

## Hijacked channels (company research, Bitdefender's own statement)
- Bitdefender Labs (blog, July 2024): dozens of hijacked YouTube channels, since July 16, 2024, served looped deepfake livestreams of Elon Musk pushing "crypto-doubling" giveaways via QR codes; some channels were renamed "Tesla"; one hijacked channel had 1.26 million subscribers; channels were taken over via phishing emails with info-stealer malware that stole session cookies. (Bitdefender HotForSecurity blog; CSO Online; Cybernews; The Record.) The news hook Bitdefender described is a political event; not used in script to stay neutral.

## FTC data (exact years)
- FTC Data Spotlight, May 17, 2021 ("Cryptocurrency buzz drives record investment scam losses"): from October 2020 to March 2021 (six months), people reported sending more than $2 million in cryptocurrency to Elon Musk impersonators; giveaway scams falsely claim the celebrity will "multiply" crypto sent. Same period: nearly 7,000 people reported losses to bogus crypto investments, more than $80 million total. (ftc.gov; CNBC May 17, 2021.)
- FTC consumer alert (May 2021, "Spotting cryptocurrency investment scams"): scammers pretend to be celebrities "doing giveaways with claims of multiplying any cryptocurrency you send" (search snippet; not shown as a full quote on screen).
- FTC 2025 Consumer Sentinel (June 2026 release): total reported fraud losses $15.9B in 2025 vs $12.5B in 2024; cryptocurrency was among the highest aggregate-loss payment methods. FTC 2024: crypto payment losses $1.42B (context only).

## FBI IC3 data (exact years)
- Cryptocurrency-related losses reported to IC3: 2023 more than $5.6B (IC3 2023 Cryptocurrency Fraud Report, Sept 9, 2024; up 45% from 2022); 2024 $9.3B, 149,686 complaints (IC3 2024 report, up 66%); 2025 more than $11B ($11.366B, reported as $11.4B by The Block), 181,565 complaints, up 22% (IC3 2025 report, April 2026; FBI/ASIS/Fox Business).
- IC3 does not publish a separate "giveaway/doubling" category; the crypto total covers all crypto-related fraud. Label it as "crypto-related losses reported to the FBI", not as livestream losses.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
- Example: someone sends $1,000 in crypto to a "doubling" address, expecting $1,000 x 2 = $2,000 back. Return received: $0. Net loss: $1,000 - $0 = $1,000, i.e. 100% of what was sent. The "doubling" is the theft mechanism: the only money that moves is the victim's.

## Surprising fact
- The fake Nvidia stream outdrew the real keynote nearly 8 to 1 at one point (about 95,000 vs about 12,000) and ranked above the official feed in YouTube search.

## Exact quotes (word-for-word)
- FTC, consumer.ftc.gov "What To Know About Cryptocurrency and Scams": "Only scammers will guarantee profits or big returns." (verified via search snippet; page blocked to direct fetch)

## Older related case (context, not in script)
- July 15, 2020 Twitter hack: hijacked high-profile accounts promised to double bitcoin sent. Joseph James O'Connor pleaded guilty (May 2023) and was sentenced to five years in prison on June 23, 2023 (TechCrunch, Bloomberg). Convicted, so nameable, but tweet-based rather than livestream.
