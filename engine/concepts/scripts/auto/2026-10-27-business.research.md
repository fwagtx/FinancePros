# Research brief: How Halloween became a multibillion-dollar business (Business, posts Oct 27, 2026)

- NRF 2026 Halloween survey (Prosper Insights; 7,889 consumers, Sept 1-9, 2026):
  - Total spending expected $13.5B (record), up from $13.1B in 2025.
  - $115.14 per person (vs $114.45 in 2025); 74% plan to celebrate.
  - Costumes $4.4B (adults $2B, children $1.5B, pets $0.92B); decorations $4.3B; candy $4.1B; greeting cards $0.8B.
- NRF totals by year: 2020 $8.05B; 2021 $10.1B; 2022 $10.6B; 2023 $12.2B; 2024 $11.6B; 2025 $13.1B; 2026 $13.5B (forecast).
- Spirit Halloween: roughly 1,500+ temporary pop-up stores across North America for the 2026 season (NBC affiliates; Bisnow), often in vacant retail spaces.
- Worked example: decorations $4.3B vs costumes $4.4B, nearly even. Pets $0.92B is about 1 in every 15 Halloween dollars (0.92 / 13.5 = 6.8%).
