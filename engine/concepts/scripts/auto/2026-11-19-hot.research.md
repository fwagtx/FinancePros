# 2026-11-19-hot research: The true cost of a new car over 5 years

Format: Hot Take / "Why is X so expensive" (sticker price vs. what owning actually costs). Evergreen; every number dated.
Neutral and descriptive. No advice, no price predictions.

## Core facts (dated)
- AAA "Your Driving Costs" 2026 (released Sept 15, 2026): average cost to own and operate a NEW vehicle = **$12,863 a year**
  = $1,071.92 a month = $35.24 a day = about 86 cents a mile (based on 15,000 miles a year).
  Model: 5 years of ownership, 75,000 miles. Sales-weighted average MSRP of the vehicles in the study: **$39,376**.
- AAA 2026 annual components (average of all vehicles):
  - Depreciation: **$4,422** a year (largest single cost)
  - Fuel: **$2,595** a year (17.3 cents a mile x 15,000 miles); regular gas averaged $4.152/gal in the study, up 31.8% from 2025
  - Insurance: **$2,098** a year
  - Maintenance, repairs, tires: 11.7 cents a mile (about $1,755-$1,762 a year; exact dollar line not confirmed, not used on screen)
  - Finance charges: **$1,184** a year (5-year loan, 15% down, national average rate)
  - License, registration, taxes: **$802** a year
  - Check: 4,422 + 2,595 + 2,098 + 1,184 + 802 = 11,101; 12,863 - 11,101 = 1,762 for maintenance/repair/tires (consistent with 11.7 cents/mile, rounded).
- AAA 2025 study (Sept 2025), for comparison: $11,577 a year ($964.78/month); depreciation $4,334; finance $1,131.
- Kelley Blue Book (Cox Automotive), released Sept 10, 2026: average new-vehicle transaction price **$50,089 in August 2026**, up 0.5% from July and 1.9% from August 2025; first month of 2026 above $50,000. Incentives 6.5% of ATP.
- Edmunds (released Oct 1, 2026), Q3 2026 new-vehicle financing: average APR **7.0%** (same as Q2 2026 and Q3 2025); average monthly payment **$787** (record; $777 in Q2 2026, $756 in Q3 2025); average amount financed **$44,664** (record); average total interest over life of loan **$9,938** (record; $9,811 Q2 2026, $9,442 Q3 2025).
- Depreciation, independent check: iSeeCars study (950,000+ five-year-old cars sold March 2025-February 2026): average 5-year depreciation **41.8%** (EVs 57.2%, trucks 34.2%, hybrids 35.4%). AAA's own depreciation (below) is higher because it measures a 5-year/75,000-mile new-car trade-in value.

## Worked example (exact math) — AAA's average new vehicle (illustrative example, not a specific car)
1. 5-year total cost: $12,863 x 5 = **$64,315**.
2. Versus the study's average sticker: $64,315 / $39,376 = 1.63x the MSRP; $64,315 - $39,376 = $24,939 more than the sticker.
3. Depreciation over 5 years: $4,422 x 5 = **$22,110** = 22,110 / 39,376 = **56.2%** of the average MSRP.
4. Finance charges over 5 years (AAA assumption: 5-yr loan, 15% down): $1,184 x 5 = $5,920.
5. Per month: $12,863 / 12 = $1,071.92. Per mile: $12,863 / 15,000 = $0.8575 = about 86 cents.

## The surprising fact
Over five years, AAA's average new vehicle costs about $64,315 to own and run, about 1.6 times its $39,376 sticker price, and the biggest cost (depreciation, $22,110) never shows up on a bill.
