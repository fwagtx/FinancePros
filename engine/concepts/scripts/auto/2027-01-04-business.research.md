## Key numbers (Cantaloupe 2026 Micropayment Trends Report, data = calendar 2025, Cantaloupe network)
- Average vending transaction in 2025: $2.01 (up from $1.99 in 2024).
- Average cashless vending ticket 2025: $2.45 vs cash $1.57 (+59%, per report; our check: 2.45/1.57 = 1.56, i.e. about 56-59%; report states +59%).
  Our math check: 2.45/1.57 = 1.5605 -> +56%. The report prints "+59%". USE WITH CARE: on screen we show both tickets, and the hook uses the report's stated 59%.
- Cashless share of vending sales: 78% in 2025, up from 73% in 2024 (cash 22% in 2025, 27% in 2024).
- Contactless = 85% of cashless vending sales in 2025 (78% in 2024); mobile ~38% of cashless by end of 2025.
- Food and beverage vending sales on Cantaloupe network: $3.3 billion in 2025, +5% YoY.
- Micro markets: $1.13B sales 2025 (+13%), 7,451 locations, avg ticket $3.02. Smart Stores avg ticket $4.49 (572 locations).
- Prior report (2025 report, 2024 data): cashless vending ticket $2.24 vs cash $1.78; 2023 report (2022 data): $2.11 vs $1.36. Cashless gap is persistent.

## Industry size
- NAMA Foundation census "2024-25 State of Convenience Services" (with Technomic; released Mar 2026): convenience services revenue
  estimated $31.1B in 2025, up from $26.6B in 2023 (2025 = preliminary estimate). Vending remains the largest business line by revenue and number of businesses.
- NAMA 2020 census: industry fell to about $15.6B in 2020.
- Automatic Merchandiser 2024 report: vending + micro market sales $33.85B (different scope).

## Costs: what is and is not confirmed
- Location commissions: no current official benchmark found. Trade/vendor sources give wide ranges (about 0-25% of gross sales;
  many offices pay none, high-traffic retail/transit higher). Old NAMA Operating Ratio data: typical operator paid 4.5% of sales
  in commissions in 1999 and 6.3% in 2004 (Vending Market Watch). => Script uses commission only as a clearly labelled EXAMPLE (10%).
- Cost of goods: trade guides put snack/vending COGS around 40-55% of sales (vendor blogs, not official). => Script uses $1.00 per snack as an EXAMPLE.
- Card processing: analyst estimate of Cantaloupe take rate ~5.2% (Substack, not company-disclosed). => Script uses 5% as an EXAMPLE.
- Old NAMA data: industry pre-tax profit ~1.5% of sales (2005); not used (too old).

## Worked example (our math, example inputs, real $2.01 average ticket)
Sale: $2.01 (Cantaloupe avg vending ticket, 2025)
- Snack cost, for example $1.00 -> $1.01 left
- Location commission, for example 10% of $2.01 = $0.201 -> about 20 cents
- Card fee, for example 5% of $2.01 = $0.1005 -> about 10 cents
- Left: 2.01 - 1.00 - 0.20 - 0.10 = $0.71, before trucks, drivers, fuel, the machine and spoilage.

## Surprising fact
Card/phone buyers at vending machines spend far more per sale than cash buyers: $2.45 vs $1.57 in 2025 (report: +59%),
and cash fell to just 22% of vending sales in 2025.
