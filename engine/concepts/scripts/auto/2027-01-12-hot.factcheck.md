## Guidance claims
- FTC: a demand for payment by wire transfer, gift card or cryptocurrency is a scam sign. Confirmed on consumer.ftc.gov ("Avoid scams when you travel"; rental and travel alerts). Credit card as the most protective payment is in FTC guidance and BBB guidance.
- BBB: fake agencies offer steep discounts, take payment upfront and vanish. BBB says to use a credit card and to verify with the hotel or airline directly. Confirmed (bbb.org/consumer/travel; BBB-cited fake-booking cases). One caveat: in some BBB cases the reservation was real at first and then cancelled. The advice still stands.
- Fair Credit Billing Act: goods or services not delivered as agreed count as a billing error, and you must dispute in writing within 60 days after the first bill containing the error was sent. It covers credit cards only, not debit (FTC archived page, Georgia consumer office, Cornell LII). The script now says "in writing" and "first bill showing it".
