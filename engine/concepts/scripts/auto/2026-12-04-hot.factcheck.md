## Issues and fixes

1. **Line 1: friend stat used in a family video (scope).** The text says "friend lenders", which is accurate. But "It's common:" presents a friend statistic as evidence about family loans. Fix: "It happens a lot: in a July 2025 LendingTree survey, 32% of people who lent to a friend weren't repaid the last time." This keeps the scope explicit. Alternatively, drop "It's common" and bridge with "Friends and family alike:", only if a family-inclusive statistic is separately verified. As written it is honest enough and not a FAIL.

2. **Line 0: hook.** It is true, specific, about 10 words (under 3 s) and paid off by lines 2-3, so it passes. It implies that non-payment by itself makes the loan a gift, when the test is your intent at the time you lent; line 2 corrects this. It is also a bit low on stakes. A stronger option: "Your unpaid family loan may be a gift to the IRS, not a write-off."

3. **Line 3: $19,000 scope.** The limit covers all gifts to that person in the year, not only the forgiven loan, and the return is a filing requirement rather than tax owed. Fix: "In 2026, gifts over $19,000 to one person need a gift tax return, usually with no tax due." The minimum fix is "over $19,000 in gifts to one person".

4. **Line 5: awkward narration and an unstated simplification.** "$3,000 a year counts" reads poorly out loud. Fix the narration to: "Only three thousand a year offsets income, so about six hundred sixty saved, then four hundred forty." The example assumes federal tax only and that the whole $3,000 is taxed at 22%. Optionally add "federal" to the sub-label. The numbers themselves are correct.

5. **Line 6: ambiguous wording.** "Loans over $10,000 below the Applicable Federal Rate" can be heard as "$10,000 below the rate". Fix: "Interest-free family loans over $10,000 can trigger imputed interest at the IRS's AFR." The board can stay. This simplification of the de minimis rule is acceptable given "can".

6. **Line 7: overstatement.** "Paperwork decides it" overstates the rule, because intent and the facts and circumstances decide; documents are evidence. Fix: "Paperwork helps prove it: a signed note, a payment schedule, and collection attempts." This also keeps the line descriptive rather than an instruction.

7. **Post copy.**
   - The title "Your unpaid family loan might be a gift to the IRS" reads as if you are gifting the IRS. Fix: "The IRS might treat your unpaid family loan as a gift".
   - In the IG and YouTube copy, "offsetting up to $3,000 of income a year" is imprecise: the loss offsets capital gains first, then up to $3,000 of other income. Fix: "offsets capital gains, then up to $3,000 of other income a year".
   - The IG example should carry its assumptions: "Hypothetical: $5,000 bad debt, 22% bracket, no other gains: $660 saved, then $440."

8. **Advice wording.** None found. "Not financial advice" is present in the post copy. Lines are hedged ("may", "can").
