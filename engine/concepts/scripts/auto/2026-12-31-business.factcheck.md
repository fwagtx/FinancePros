## Issues (0-based line index)

1. **L0 hook overstates the stat (FAIL).** "Private clubs make just 13.5% from golf" is not what the data shows. The 13.5% is golf operations and pro shop revenue across CMAA respondent clubs, and that group includes city and yacht clubs with no golf at all. At golf clubs, much of the 49.5% in dues is money members pay to play golf. The hook is specific and under 3 seconds, but it is not true as stated.
   - text: `Golf fees bring private clubs just 13.5% of revenue.`
   - say: `Golf fees bring private clubs just thirteen point five percent of revenue.` (12 words)
   - scene: title `Barely [golf] money` → `Golf fees? [Tiny]`. Label `GOLF` → `GOLF FEES`.
2. **L1 population and year wording.** "2022 data" should say "2022 report", and the line should say the sample is broad.
   - text: `In the club managers' association's 2022 report on hundreds of clubs, member dues brought in 49.5%.`
   - say: `In the club managers' association's twenty twenty-two report on hundreds of clubs, member dues brought in forty-nine point five percent.` (20 words)
   - List title `Club revenue, 2022` → `CMAA 2022 report` on L1-L3.
3. **L3 (no change needed).** "Golf and the pro shop? Just 13.5%" matches the category. Optionally change it to `Golf fees and the pro shop?` to match the new hook. The word count does not change.
4. **L4 stale "record" (FAIL).** 2025 beat 2024 (NGF: +1.2%, a record for the 4th time in 5 years). On Dec 31, 2026, presenting 545M as the record is out of date.
   - text: `Public courses run on volume: Americans played a record 545 million rounds in 2024, and even more in 2025 (National Golf Foundation).`
   - say: `Public courses run on volume: Americans played a record five hundred forty-five million rounds in twenty twenty-four, and even more in twenty twenty-five.` (23 words)
   - Chart: the 2021 (529) and 2022 (510) points are writer-derived. They match NGF's published percentage changes (+0.3% and -3.7%), but I found no published exact totals. Keep them and add the footnote `2021-22 derived from NGF % changes`, or show them as `~529` and `~510`. Optional: add `[2025, 551]` marked as an estimate (545 x 1.012).
5. **L5 preliminary revenue, and on-screen differs from spoken.** $41.4M was the preliminary FY25 figure. Later city documents say about $42.3M. The on-screen text shows "427,508" while the voice says "about 428,000".
   - text: `Take San Diego's city courses: over $41 million in fiscal 2025, from about 428,000 rounds.`
   - say: `Take San Diego's city courses: over forty-one million dollars in fiscal twenty twenty-five, from about four hundred twenty-eight thousand rounds.` (20 words)
   - stat: value `$41M+`, sub `~428,000 rounds`, label unchanged (`San Diego FY2025`, 16 chars).
6. **L6 on-screen differs from spoken, and the final revenue changes the result.** The screen shows $96.84 while the voice says "about ninety-seven". With final revenue the figure is about $98.94.
   - text: `Our math: $41.4M-$42.3M / 427,508 = $97 to $99 a round, counting greens fees, carts, the range, the shop and food.`
   - say: `Our math: ninety-seven to ninety-nine dollars a round, counting greens fees, carts, the range, the shop and food.` (18 words)
   - board lines: `$41-42M / 427,508` (17 chars), `= $97-$99 a round` (17 chars)
7. **L7 (word trim only).** say: `Private clubs sell membership first, and public courses sell tee times by the thousand.` (14 words). Text: drop "So" to match.
8. **Cover (FAIL).** "Not From Golf" overclaims, because dues are largely paid for golf access. Use cover title `Dues, Not Green Fees` (20 chars) or `Dues Pay the Bills`. Label `GOLF FEES`, value `13.5%`.
9. **POST fields.** These must match the new framing and figures.
   - title: `Golf fees bring private clubs just 13.5%. So what pays?`
   - ig line 1: `Golf operations and the pro shop bring private clubs just 13.5% of operating revenue.` Change "In 2022 club data" to "In the CMAA's 2022 Finance & Operations Report (about 440 clubs)".
   - ig/yt: change "a record 545 million rounds in 2024" to "a record 545 million rounds in 2024, and a new record in 2025 (National Golf Foundation)". Change "$41.4M ... about $96.84 a round" to "over $41 million (preliminary; later city figures ~$42.3M) from 427,508 rounds. Our math: about $97-$99 a round, all-in."
   - tiktok: `Golf fees and the pro shop bring private clubs just 13.5% (CMAA 2022 report). Dues: 49.5%. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #golf #business #financeprostv`
10. **Stale data (minor).** The CMAA figures come from a report published in early 2023, nearly four years before the posting date. Newer CMAA reports exist, but I found no public revenue split in them. Keep "2022 report" spoken and on screen so the dating is clear, as fixed above.
11. **No issues found** with advice, price predictions, political opinion, "fiscal 2025" time words, or awkward narration. "Thirteen point five percent" repeated three times is acceptable for emphasis.
