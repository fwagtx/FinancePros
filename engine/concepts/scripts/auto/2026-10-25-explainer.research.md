# Research brief: The 401(k) match is free acorns (Explainer, Acorn Economics, posts Oct 25, 2026)

- Vanguard, How America Saves: the most common single match formula is $0.50 per $1 on the first 6% of pay, an effective 3% match. Many participants do not contribute enough to get the full match (Vanguard research; TheStreet on HAS 2026).
- IRS (Nov 2025 release): the 2026 401(k) employee contribution limit is $24,500 (up from $23,500); catch-up at 50+ is $8,000; ages 60-63 get $11,250. Employer matching contributions do not count toward the $24,500 employee deferral limit; they fall under the separate overall annual additions limit.
- Vesting: employer contributions can follow a vesting schedule (IRS: up to 3-year cliff or 6-year graded for matching contributions); employee deferrals are always 100% vested.
- Worked example: $50,000 salary, 50% match up to 6%.
  - Save 6% = $3,000; match = $1,500.
  - Save 3% = $1,500; match = $750, which leaves $750 a year unclaimed.
- Chartable: employee limit 2024 $23,000, 2025 $23,500, 2026 $24,500 (IRS).
- Surprising fact: the match is an instant 50% on each dollar up to the cap, before any investment return.
