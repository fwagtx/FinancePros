## Issues

1. **FAIL: the core framing is not supported (hook, cover, post title, tiktok).** The script says "Trash's real money? The dump." Line 2 then shows collection at $17.6B against $5.3B for landfills, so the video contradicts its own hook. WM does not report revenue or margin by line of business in a way that shows landfills as the profit center; its reportable segments are geographic. The "30%+ landfill margin" figures found online come from blogs, not filings. Also, $1.57B of the $5.3B is WM's own trucks paying WM's own landfills, so third-party landfill revenue is only $3.78B. The claim that can be supported is the toll-booth or moat point: every truck has to pay to dump, WM owns the dumps, and new landfills take years to permit. Reframe the hook, cover and post to that point (fixes below).
2. **Hook line 1: $5.3B "brought in" is gross.** About 29% of it is intercompany. The hook drops the "gross" caveat that line 2 adds. The fix to issue 1 removes this.
3. **Hook is weak and awkward out loud.** "Trash's real money" is a sibilant mouthful. The hook is also undercut 5 seconds later, so it creates no real need for the answer.
4. **Line 7 spoken says "69.4 percent of its waste".** WM's metric is measured by disposal cost, not share of waste. The screen says so, but the narration does not. The compare also sets two different measures (cost vs volume) side by side. The subs disclose this, but the spoken line should too.
5. **Line 2 "trucks billed" is loose.** Collection revenue is the right term. Change the item label to "Pickup".
6. **Line 4: EREF's $63.48 is an unweighted average of facilities.** This is fine as an average, but re-check before Dec 1 in case EREF publishes a newer annual figure (its report usually comes out in the fall). Minor.
7. **Post tiktok hashtags #moneytok #personalfinance are off-topic.** Minor; the fix is included below.
8. Line 9 is fine as a paraphrase of RSG's 10-K. It is fine as industry framing because it describes a regulatory fact, not one company's numbers.
