# Research brief: El Salvador's Bitcoin experiment (World Money, posts Jan 21, 2027)

Researched Nov 22, 2026. Evergreen: every number carries a date. Anything about current holdings is "as of <date>".

## Timely angle
No single breaking event for the post date. Strongest recent anchors: IMF Executive Board concluded the 2nd and 3rd EFF reviews on Oct 1, 2026 (about $138M released, waivers granted for missed criteria); IMF staff said in Sept 2026 that bitcoin added since June 2025 came from documented private donations, not public funds. Also: ~5 years since bitcoin became legal tender (Sept 7, 2021) and ~2 years since the Jan 2025 reform.

## The surprising fact (hook)
- In a UCA/IUDOP survey (Dec 5-17, 2024, 1,266 interviews, published Jan 2025), 91.8% of Salvadorans said they did not use bitcoin at all in 2024 (other outlets: 91.9%). In 2023 the same survey found only 12% had used it (88% had not). Sources: elsalvador.com / Diario El Mundo / Infobae (EFE) Jan 2025 and Jan 2024.

## Key facts with dates
Background
- El Salvador has used the US dollar as its official currency since Jan 1, 2001 (Ley de Integracion Monetaria; colon fixed at 8.75 per dollar). So it has no independent monetary policy. Sources: IMF blog 2011; Bloomberg Linea Jan 2023; Tico Times Jan 2021.
- Remittances (money sent home, mostly by workers abroad) as % of GDP, World Bank (personal remittances received): 2019 21.0%, 2021 26.2%, 2023 24.7%, 2025 27.5%. (2024 about 24%.) Values can be revised slightly between vintages (e.g., 2020 shows 23.8% or 24.1%).
- Remittances in dollars (BCR): $8,479M in 2024 (record at the time); $9,987.9M in 2025, +17.7% vs 2024 (record). Sources: Diario El Mundo / elsalvador.com citing BCR, Jan 2025 and Jan 2026.

Bitcoin Law
- Passed by the Legislative Assembly the night of June 8-9, 2021, 62 of 84 votes. First country to make bitcoin legal tender. Sources: Al Jazeera June 9, 2021; NBC; EY.
- Took effect Sept 7, 2021 (90 days after publication). Original law required businesses to accept bitcoin (when able). Sources: AP/PBS; The Block Sept 7, 2021.
- Chivo: government wallet launched Sept 7, 2021; each adult who signed up got $30 in bitcoin. Launch day had server problems. Sources: Bloomberg June 25, 2021; Al Jazeera June 25, 2021; The Block Sept 7, 2021.

Did people use it
- NBER working paper 29968 (Alvarez, Argente, Van Patten, 2022; published in Science 2023): nationally representative face-to-face survey of 1,800 households (early 2022). Less than half of those who downloaded Chivo kept using it after spending the $30 bonus = 20% of all respondents. Adoption fell over time; use skewed to young, banked, educated men; privacy/trust concerns key barriers. Sources: NBER w29968; NBER Digest July 2022; Yale Insights.
- Remittances via crypto wallets (BCR): $126.7M in 2022 = about 1.6% of all remittances; $82.9M in 2023, about 1% (down 34.5%). Sources: CriptoNoticias / Diario El Mundo / elsalvador.com citing BCR.

Government buying
- Nov 17, 2022: Bukele posted "We are buying one #Bitcoin every day starting tomorrow." (during the FTX collapse; BTC about $16,700). Sources: Kitco Nov 17, 2022; Benzinga; CryptoSlate.
- Nov 17, 2025: Bitcoin Office disclosed 1,090 BTC added (~$100M), holdings 7,474 BTC. Sources: The Block Nov 17, 2025; Bloomberg Nov 18, 2025.
- Holdings: 7,825.37 BTC as of Nov 4, 2026 per Bitcoin Office dashboard (as reported by coinpaprika/cryptoticker). Other dated figures: 7,643 BTC (May 2026, EFE/El Mundo), 7,748 BTC (Aug 20, 2026, BitcoinTreasuries). UNCERTAIN: secondary sources only; use "about 7,800 coins, as of early November 2026".
- BTC price Nov 4, 2026, 7 a.m. ET: about $78,520 (Fortune price page, Nov 4, 2026).
- July 2025 IMF staff report: increases in the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve Fund reflected consolidation of coins across government wallets; finance officials said public-sector holdings unchanged since February 2025 (The Block, July 19, 2025; CryptoSlate).
- Sept 2026: IMF staff said no public funds used to accumulate bitcoin since June 2025; additions came from documented private donations (The Block, CoinDesk, Sept 4, 2026).

Volcano / Bitcoin bonds
- Announced Nov 2021: $1 billion, 10-year, 6.5% tokenized bond, half to buy bitcoin, half for "Bitcoin City" infrastructure and geothermal mining (Bloomberg Nov 21, 2021; Fortune). Delayed repeatedly in 2022; legal framework (digital assets law) Jan 2023; approved by regulator Dec 2023 (Fortune Dec 12, 2023). Later issuance claims are single-source (Tico Times Oct 2025): NOT used in script beyond "years of delays".

IMF deal
- Staff-level agreement Dec 18, 2024 (IMF PR 24/485). Board approved 40-month EFF Feb 26, 2025, about US$1.4 billion (SDR 1,033.92M, 360% of quota), immediate ~$113M; expected to catalyze over $3.5B from other lenders (IMF PR 25/043).
- Conditions: private-sector bitcoin acceptance voluntary; taxes paid only in US dollars; government to wind down Chivo; limits on public bitcoin accumulation.
- Jan 29, 2025: Assembly reformed the Bitcoin Law (55-2 vote, Decree 199, in force ~90 days after Jan 30 publication). Acceptance became voluntary; bitcoin no longer usable for taxes. Outlets differ on whether it is still called "legal tender" (officials said yes; Reason/IBTimes said effectively no). Script says: acceptance became voluntary and taxes are paid in dollars only. Sources: Reuters via Yahoo; Reason Feb 3, 2025; Benzinga; CryptoSlate.
- First review concluded June 2025 (PR 25/223). Dec 22, 2025 staff statement: Chivo sale talks well advanced (PR 25/440). Sept 2026 staff: majority ownership and control of Chivo transferred to a private operator.
- Oct 1, 2026: Board concluded 2nd and 3rd reviews; ~US$138M (SDR 101.96M) released; waivers for missed performance criteria including bitcoin-related (IMF PR 26/316; Cointelegraph).

## Extra facts used in script
- NBER w29968: among Chivo users, about 10% report spending less cash and 11% report using debit/credit cards less since downloading.
- UCA IUDOP (Jan 2025): those who used bitcoin in 2024 averaged about 14 uses a year (director Laura Andrade).
- Average remittance topped $340 a month in 2025 (Diario El Mundo citing BCR).
- "Chivo" is Salvadoran slang for "cool" (widely reported at launch, 2021). Chivo held balances in bitcoin or dollars.
- Government often announced "buy the dip" purchases in 2021-2022 (e.g., 420 BTC, Bloomberg Oct 27, 2021).
- Nov 17, 2025 addition of 1,090 BTC lifted holdings to 7,474 BTC (The Block, Nov 17, 2025).
- Kitco Nov 17, 2022: one bitcoin about $16,672 at the time of the one-a-day pledge. Our math: $16,672 x 365 = $6,085,280, about $6.1M a year at that price.

## Worked examples (our math)
1. Value of holdings: 7,825 BTC x $78,520 (Nov 4, 2026 price) = $614,419,000, about $614 million. Holdings and price both "as of Nov 4, 2026".
2. IMF loan per person: $1.4 billion / 6.4 million people (World Bank 2023 population) = $218.75, about $219 per person.
