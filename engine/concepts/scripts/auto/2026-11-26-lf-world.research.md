## Mandates (who does what)
- Fed: Federal Reserve Act Sec. 2A: "maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates" (the "dual mandate"); 2% inflation goal. Founded 1913 (after banking panics incl. 1907).
- ECB: established June 1, 1998; euro launched Jan 1, 1999. Primary objective "price stability" (Maastricht Treaty); defined as 2% inflation over the medium term (symmetric since 2021).
- Bank of England: founded 1694 (to lend to the government). 2% CPI target set by the government in the Chancellor's annual remit; operational independence since 1997 (BoE, House of Commons Library).
- Bank of Japan: founded 1882. 2% price stability target adopted January 2013.
- PBOC: founded 1948. PBOC Law Art. 3: "The objective of monetary policy is to maintain the stability of the value of the currency and thereby promote economic growth." Art. 2: formulates and implements monetary policy under the leadership of the State Council (China's cabinet).
- Oldest: Sweden's Riksbank, 1668 (Riksbank.se).

## Japan: the surprise
- Negative rate (-0.1%) introduced 2016; ended March 19, 2024: first hike in 17 years; Japan was the last country with negative rates (AP via Washington Times, Al Jazeera, Mar 19, 2024).
- Path: Mar 2024 0-0.1%; Jul 31, 2024 0.25%; Jan 2025 0.5%; Dec 19, 2025 0.75% (CNBC); Jun 16, 2026 1.00% (CNBC); Sep 18, 2026 1.25%. = six hikes since March 2024.
- BoJ owned a record ~53% (53.34%) of JGBs by March 2023 (nippon.com); share dipped below 50% in March 2026 for first time in 3.5 years (Nikkei Asia).
- Aug 5, 2024: Nikkei -12.4% (-4,451.28 pts), worst day since 1987 Black Monday; yen surged; concern that yen-funded ("carry trade") positions were being liquidated, plus weak US July jobs report. BoJ had hiked July 31, 2024. Dow fell ~1,000 points (1,033) same day. (CNBC, AP/WTTW, Aug 5, 2024)
- USD/JPY near 157 late Sept 2026 (Trading Economics/CNBC; from Oct 1 brief).

- Freddie Mac PMMS: 30-year fixed 7.28% on Oct 1, 2026, highest since Nov 2023 (7.29%); up from 7.03% a week earlier. One year earlier (Oct 2, 2025): 6.34%.
- Savings: top high-yield savings APYs up to 4.50% in late Sept 2026 (Motley Fool Money, Sept 30, 2026; Go2bank 4.50% on balances up to $5,000; NerdWallet up to 4.21%; Bankrate up to 4.25% Oct 2026).
- Dollar: dollar index +1.7% in Sept 2026, best month since June (Reuters/Bloomberg Sept 29-30, 2026). Dollar on one side of 89% of FX trades (BIS Triennial Survey, April 2025).
- Bonds: when yields rise, prices of existing bonds fall.

## WORKED EXAMPLE (hypothetical $400,000, 30-year fixed, principal+interest only)
Payment = L x i / (1 - (1+i)^-360), i = annual rate / 12
- 6.34% (Oct 2, 2025): i = 0.0052833 -> $2,486.33/month
- 7.28% (Oct 1, 2026): i = 0.0060667 -> $2,736.85/month
- Difference: $250.52/month (~$251), $3,006/year (~$3,000), $90,187 over 360 payments (~$90,000).
Savings example: $10,000 x 4.50% APY = $450 in a year.
