# Research brief: Deductible vs premium vs out-of-pocket max (posts Oct 16, 2026)

## Definitions (HealthCare.gov glossary)
- Premium: the amount you pay for your health insurance every month (you pay it whether or not you use care).
- Deductible: what you pay for covered services each year before your plan starts to pay (except free preventive services).
- Coinsurance: your percentage share of a covered service after the deductible (e.g. 20%).
- Out-of-pocket maximum/limit: the most you have to pay for covered services in a plan year. After you spend this on deductibles,
  copayments and coinsurance for in-network care, the plan pays 100% of covered benefits.
- SURPRISING FACT: the out-of-pocket limit does NOT include monthly premiums, out-of-network care, or amounts above the allowed amount.

## Legal cap (ACA maximum annual limitation on cost sharing, HHS/CMS; applies to non-grandfathered marketplace and employer plans, in-network essential health benefits)
- 2026: $10,600 self-only / $21,200 family (revised upward in 2025 from $10,150 / $20,300).
- 2027: $12,000 self-only / $24,000 family (HHS Notice of Benefit and Payment Parameters for 2027; CMS guidance Jan 29, 2026) ~13.2% jump.
- HSA-qualified high-deductible plans have a lower IRS limit: $8,500 self-only in 2026.
- Plans may set a LOWER max than the legal cap.

## Context (KFF 2025 Employer Health Benefits Survey, released Oct 22, 2025)
- Average annual premium, single coverage: $9,325 (worker pays avg $1,440, about 16%).
- Average deductible, single coverage (workers with a general deductible): $1,886; up 17% over 5 years, 43% over 10 years.
- Family premium: $26,993.

## Worked example (hypothetical plan)
Plan: $2,000 deductible, 20% coinsurance, $6,000 out-of-pocket max. $30,000 covered in-network hospital bill.
- You pay the deductible: $2,000. Remaining: $30,000 - $2,000 = $28,000.
- Coinsurance: 20% x $28,000 = $5,600. Total without cap: $2,000 + $5,600 = $7,600.
- Cap: $6,000. You pay $6,000; plan pays $24,000. Premiums are paid on top.
- True worst case for a year = 12 months of premiums + out-of-pocket max. E.g. $120/month -> $1,440 + $6,000 = $7,440.
