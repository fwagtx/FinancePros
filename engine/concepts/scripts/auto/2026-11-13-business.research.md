# Research brief: How buy now, pay later companies make money (2026-11-13-business)

Researched Oct 1, 2026. Posts Nov 13, 2026. Note: sec.gov / company IR pages were blocked for direct fetch in this session; figures below were confirmed through search results quoting the filings and cross-checked by arithmetic (noted).

## Timely angle
- Affirm filed its fiscal 2026 10-K (fiscal year ended June 30, 2026) in late August 2026; Q4 FY2026 results released Aug 27, 2026. Freshest full-year numbers for any BNPL lender.
- Klarna listed on the NYSE (ticker KLAR) in September 2025; its first annual report as a public company (Form 20-F, FY2025) was filed in early 2026.

## Affirm, fiscal 2026 (year ended June 30, 2026), Form 10-K
- Total revenue, net: $4,261,082 thousand ($4.26B), up ~32% from FY2025.
- Interest income: $2,047,485 thousand ($2.05B) = 48.05% of revenue. Up $439.3M (27%) vs FY2025 (10-K MD&A). Average loans held for investment $8.2B (+27%).
- Merchant network revenue: $1,149,932 thousand ($1.15B) = 26.99% of revenue. Up $267.3M (30%) vs FY2025 (10-K MD&A) -> FY2025 ~ $882.6M (consistent).
- Card network revenue: $293,990 thousand ($294M).
- Gain on sales of loans: $596,553 thousand ($597M).
- Servicing income: remainder, ~ $173M (derived: total minus the four lines above; not independently confirmed).
- "Other" (card network + gain on sales + servicing) = $1,063.7M ($1.06B) = 24.96% of revenue.
- GMV FY2026: $50.2B (FY2025: $36.7B). Revenue / GMV = 4,261.1 / 50,200 = 8.49%.
- Active consumers 27.8M (+21%); transactions per active consumer 7.0.
- Net income FY2026 $1.93B, includes a ~$1.5B tax benefit (do not use as an operating-profit claim).
- Product mix (10-K, FY2026 share of GMV): Pay-in-X 16%, 0% APR monthly installments 14%, interest-bearing monthly installments 70%.
- 10-K: merchant fees vary by arrangement and loan terms; "larger merchant fees are generally earned on 0% APR financing products" (company description, paraphrase).
- Consumer APRs: 0% to 36%; terms roughly 3 to 48 months; Affirm says it charges no late fees (Affirm disclosures as reported by US News, LendingTree, NerdWallet).

## Klarna, full year 2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025), Form 20-F / FY2025 results release
- Total revenue: $3,509M, up 25%.
- Transaction and service revenue: $2,500M = 71.2% of revenue (merchant fees, interchange, etc.; merchant revenue was ~76% of transaction and service revenue in H1 2025 per F-1/A).
- Interest income: $937M = 26.7%.
- Gain on sale of consumer receivables: $73M = 2.1%.
- Check: 2,500 + 937 + 73 = 3,510 (rounding to 3,509). One search summary gave interest income as $1,039M; the line-item breakdown that sums to total supports $937M. Treat $937M as the figure, moderate confidence.
- GMV 2025 $127.9B (+22%); 118M active consumers; Q4 2025 first $1B+ revenue quarter.

## Revenue mix compare (the surprise)
- Affirm FY2026: interest 48%, merchant fees 27%, other 25%.
- Klarna 2025: transaction & service (mostly merchant) 71%, interest 27%.
- Surprise: the "interest-free" brand people know (Affirm) earned nearly half its FY2026 revenue from interest; 70% of its FY2026 GMV was interest-bearing loans.

## Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL)
- $1,000 purchase, 12 monthly payments, 15% APR (inside Affirm's 0-36% range), simple amortizing loan.
- Monthly rate r = 0.15/12 = 0.0125. Payment = 1000 x 0.0125 / (1 - 1.0125^-12) = $90.26.
- Total paid = $90.26 x 12 = $1,083.12. Interest = $1,083.12 - $1,000 = $83.12 (unrounded: $83.10).
