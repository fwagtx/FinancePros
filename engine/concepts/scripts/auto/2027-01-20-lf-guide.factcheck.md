# Fact-check: 2027-01-20-lf-guide (identity theft guide, posts Jan 20, 2027)

Checked Nov 21, 2026, by independent web search. The writer's brief was not used as a source.

## Verified claims
- FTC identity theft report counts: 2020 1,387,615 (1.39M); 2021 1,434,695 (1.43M); 2022 1,107,053 (1.11M); 2023 about 1,036,961 (1.04M); 2024 1,135,291 per LendingTree (Experian gives 1,135,265), so 1.14M; 2025 1,358,253 (1.36M) per Experian, citing FTC data. The 2025 count is confirmed, so the hook now uses 2025.
- Government benefits identity theft reports: 12,900 in 2019 and 394,280 in 2020 (FTC blog, Feb 2021).
- Top type in 2024: credit card fraud, about 449,000 reports (449,032 to 449,094 depending on the source).
- Reported fraud losses: 2022 about $8.8B (2023's figure was 14% higher); 2023 more than $10B; 2024 $12.5B; 2025 $15.9B, from about 3 million fraud reports. The 2025 total was in the FTC's testimony to the Joint Economic Committee in March 2026.
- Free freezes since Sept 21, 2018 (Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act). Placing a freeze online or by phone takes 1 business day, lifting it takes 1 hour, and mail requests take 3 business days. Children under 16 can be frozen for free. An initial fraud alert lasts 1 year (it was 90 days before Sept 2018) and an extended alert lasts 7 years. Contacting one bureau notifies the other two.
- Free weekly credit reports became permanent in Oct 2023. AnnualCreditReport.com is the only authorized site, and the FTC says that site and the bureaus won't email you asking for your SSN.
- IRS IP PIN: a 6-digit number, new each calendar year, and the IRS Online Account Profile page is the fastest way to get one. The online tool is generally down from mid-November to mid-January (Nov 16, 2025 to January 2026 last cycle). The IRS hasn't announced exact dates for the 2026-27 outage, so the "generally... each year" wording plus "check that it's back before you file" is correct for Jan 20. Form 14039 is the Identity Theft Affidavit.
- Javelin's 2026 Identity Fraud Study (Apr 21, 2026) puts identity fraud losses at $27.3B in 2025, about flat from $27.2B in 2024.

  - $15.9B / 3M = $5,300
