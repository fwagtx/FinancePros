# 2026-12-08-business research brief: How gift cards make money (breakage, float, unspent cards)

Evergreen; posts Tue Dec 8, 2026 (holiday gift-card season). No news hook needed; every figure carries its period.

## Key facts
1. Breakage = the share of stored-value (gift) card balances a company expects will never be redeemed. Starbucks FY2025 10-K: "based on historical redemption rates, a portion of stored value cards is not expected to be redeemed and will be recognized as breakage over time in proportion to stored value card redemptions." Booked inside store revenue.
2. Starbucks breakage revenue (10-K, fiscal year ended Sept 28, 2025):
   - Company-operated stores: $200.4M (FY2025), $187.6M (FY2024), $196.1M (FY2023)
   - Licensed stores: $22.0M (FY2025), $20.0M (FY2024), $18.9M (FY2023)
   - Total (our sum, not stated in filing): FY2025 $222.4M; FY2024 $207.6M; FY2023 $215.0M
3. Float: Starbucks stored value cards + loyalty (Stars) deferred revenue balance = $1,751.7M at Sept 28, 2025 (up from $1,718.7M at Sept 29, 2024). FY2025 activity: $15,245.8M deferred (card loads, reloads, Stars earned); $15,199.5M recognized from redemptions and breakage. (Balance combines cards AND Stars; label as "cards + Stars".) Customers pay first; Starbucks holds the cash until redemption.
   - Caveat: Starbucks FY2026 10-K (FY ends ~Sept 27, 2026) is likely filed mid-Nov 2026, before posting. Always say "fiscal 2025" so figures stay true.
4. Bankrate survey (Sept 2024, latest found; no 2025 edition found): 43% of US adults have at least one unused gift card, voucher or store credit; about $27B total; average among holders $244.
5. CARD Act of 2009 gift card rules (Regulation E, 12 CFR 1005.20; apply to cards sold on/after Aug 22, 2010): funds can't expire until at least 5 years after issue or last load; dormancy/inactivity/service fees only after at least 1 year of inactivity, max one per month, with clear disclosure. Excludes paper certificates, loyalty/promo cards, non-gift reloadable cards. States can be stricter (e.g., California bans most expiration dates); unclaimed-property (escheat) rules vary by state.

## Worked example
Starbucks FY2025 breakage per day: $222.4M / 365 = $609,300/day, roughly $600K a day.
(Using rounded $222M: 222/365 = $608K. Spoken as "roughly six hundred thousand dollars a day".)

## Surprising fact
Starbucks holds about $1.75B of customers' prepaid money (cards + Stars) at fiscal 2025 year-end, and books roughly $222M a year from balances it expects will never be spent.
