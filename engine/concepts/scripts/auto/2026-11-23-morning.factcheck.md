# Fact-check: 2026-11-23-morning (Kospi plunge / stabilization fund)
Checked Mon Nov 23, 2026, about 7:30 AM CT. Posts at 10:00 AM CT. Korea's Monday session had already closed.

## Verified claims
- Kospi closed Mon Nov 23 at 4,708.54, down 161.42 points (-3.31%). The intraday low was 4,586.80. CONFIRMED by Korea JoongAng Daily/Yonhap. (One JoongAng article says "3.06 percent" in its body text, but its own Yonhap market wrap and the Yonhap photo caption both say 3.31%. We use 3.31%.)
- Record close of 9,114.55 on Mon June 22, 2026. CONFIRMED by Herald Corp (mbiz.heraldcorp.com). The intraday record of 9,385.59 was not independently confirmed, and the script does not use it.
- Our math: 4,708.54 / 9,114.55 - 1 = -48.3%. CORRECT. JoongAng also says the index is "down 48 percent from its June peak."
- Samsung Electronics + SK hynix share of the index is about 47% (mid-October report) and 48.95% on Aug 7. The peak was 59.69% on June 25. No November figure was found. "Roughly half" became "nearly half" (FIXED).
- Finance Minister Lee Hyoung-il (JoongAng spelling) said at a Sejong press conference on Nov 23 that the government is preparing to activate the fund depending on market conditions, and may review its size. CONFIRMED.
- The fund is 10.76 trillion won ($8 billion), was established in 2020 and has never been deployed. A stabilization fund last bought stocks in 2008. CONFIRMED by JoongAng. Dollar check: 10.76T / 1,345.9 won per $ (Nov 23, 3:30 pm close, per Yonhap) = $7.99B, so "about $8 billion" is OK. Note: the separate 100 trillion won "market stabilization program" activated in March 2026 is a broader program (bonds and other markets). It is a different program from this fund, so "never used" stands for the 10.76T fund.
- Fund as a share of the market: Kospi market cap is 3.86 quadrillion won (KRX, via JoongAng). 10.76 / 3,860 = 0.28%, so "under 1%" is CORRECT. The ~0.23% quote in the brief is from an older industry estimate and is not used.
- The AI selloff hit both chipmakers on Monday: Samsung fell about 3% to 177,500 won and SK hynix fell 2.52%. CONFIRMED.

## Issues found and fixes applied
1. Line 0 hook / post: "Korea's stock market has lost almost half its value" overstates it. The 48% is an index-level drop, and no source gives a market-cap drop of that size. -> "Korea's main stock index has lost almost half since June." (10 words, true, specific, under 3 seconds.)
2. Hook title "Korea's [Crash]" / cover "Korea's Crash": the Nov 23 reporting uses "plunge/rout/slump", not "crash". -> "Kospi [Plunge]" / cover "Kospi Plunge". Header -> "Korea's Kospi:\nAlmost Half Gone".
3. Line 3 / board / split: "roughly half" goes beyond the latest data (~47%). -> "nearly half" in the spoken line, the board ("= nearly half") and the split item "Nearly half".
4. Line 4: The news scene source "Korea Times, Nov 23, 2026" could not be verified (no Korea Times Nov 23 article found). -> "Korea JoongAng Daily, Nov 23, 2026". Line also attributed: "Now Korea's finance minister says the government is ready to use a rescue fund."
5. Line 5: "It holds 10.76 trillion won" -> "It's 10.76 trillion won" (it is a pooled commitment, not a balance being held).
6. Line 6: "It buys stocks when everyone's selling" stated an unused fund as an active practice; the fund would buy index-linked products. -> "It's built to buy when everyone's selling." Board line -> "Buys in selloffs".
7. Line 2: the text said "3.3% Monday" while the narration says "on Monday". Text aligned to "3.3% on Monday".
8. Captions: post title, IG and YT updated to "main stock index", "nearly half" and the minister's name and title. Sources changed to Korea JoongAng Daily / Yonhap. "Under 1%" added to the IG caption.
