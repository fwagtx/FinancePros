## Facts (story: Fed minutes)
- Source: FOMC Minutes, October 27-28, 2026, released Wednesday Nov 18, 2026, 2:00 PM ET (federalreserve.gov).
- Exact text: "almost all participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks for inflation and the labor market, further action to raise the target range for the federal funds rate ... would likely be appropriate, in order to ensure a timely return of inflation to the 2 percent objective."
