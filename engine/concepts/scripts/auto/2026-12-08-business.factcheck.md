# Fact-check: 2026-12-08-business ("How Gift Cards Make Money")
Checked Fri Oct 9 2026 for a Tue Dec 8 2026 post. Line indexes are 0-based. Every figure was checked against web search results that quote the Starbucks FY2025 10-K, Bankrate and Regulation E. The math was recomputed in Python.

## Verified claims
- **Breakage, FY2025 (lines 0, 1, 8, cover, posts):** The Starbucks FY2025 10-K (fiscal year ended Sep 28, 2025) reports breakage of $200.4M in company-operated store revenue and $22.0M in licensed store revenue. Prior years were $187.6M/$20.0M (FY2024) and $196.1M/$18.9M (FY2023). The filing gives no total, but both lines are breakage revenue for the same year, so adding them to $222.4M (about $222M) is fair. The IG post shows the breakdown, which is good. The 10-K describes breakage as an estimate from historical redemption rates, booked in proportion to redemptions. Line 1 says "balances it expects will never be redeemed", which matches that.
- **Per day (line 2):** $222.4M / 365 = $609.3K. Using $222M gives $608.2K. "Roughly $600K" / "~$600K" is fine, and the on-screen value matches the spoken one.
- **$1.75B (lines 4, 8, posts):** This is deferred revenue for stored value cards plus the loyalty program. It was $1,751.7M at Sep 28, 2025, up from $1,718.7M at Sep 29, 2024. During FY2025, $15,245.8M was deferred and $15,199.5M was recognized. The figure covers cards AND Stars, and the script labels it that way, which is good. (I confirmed this through an aggregator that reproduces the 10-K revenue note, because sec.gov could not be fetched directly.)
- **Bankrate (lines 5, 6):** The survey was released Sep 2024 (YouGov, Aug 19-21, 2024, n=2,373). It found 43% of US adults have at least one unused gift card, voucher or store credit, about $27B in total. The average per holder was $244 in 2024, $187 in 2023, $175 in 2022 and $116 in 2021. All four chart points are correct (2024 is up 30.5% from 2023). The median is $100, and the script correctly says "average". No 2025 Bankrate edition was found, so "in 2024" stays accurate.
- **CARD Act / Reg E (line 7):** Under 12 CFR 1005.20, effective Aug 22, 2010, funds cannot expire within 5 years of issuance or the last load. Dormancy, inactivity or service fees are allowed only after at least 12 months of no use, and no more than one per month. States may be stricter. "Since 2010 … at least five years … a year of no use. States vary." is accurate.

## Issues (none fatal)
1. **Line 4 (accuracy/wording):** "held $1.75 billion" suggests the whole amount is customer cash. Part of it is deferred revenue tied to Stars that customers earned, not money they loaded. It is a liability, so "owed" is the accurate word. The word count does not change.
   - text: `In September 2025 it owed $1.75 billion in card and Stars balances.`
   - say: `In September twenty twenty-five, it owed one point seven five billion in card and Stars balances.`
2. **Line 8 (apples to oranges):** The compare puts a year-end balance (a stock) next to a full-year revenue figure (a flow), and "cash held" overstates the Stars part. Keep the values but change the subs:
   - left.sub: `year-end balance` (16 chars)
   - right.sub: `FY2025 revenue` (14 chars)
   - The spoken line ("cash up front, and revenue from what's never spent") is acceptable.
3. **Line 1 board (minor precision):** "balances never redeemed" states the estimate as a fact, while the spoken line says "expects". Replace line 2 of the board with `est. unredeemed balances` (24 chars).
4. **Line 0 hook (minor, optional):** "from card money nobody spent" turns an estimate into a fact. Line 1 corrects this right away, so I accept it. The hook is true, specific and fast. No rewrite of 12 words or fewer reads better; "expects" versions run to 13 spoken words, so keep it as is.
5. **Post `ig` (accuracy):** "held $1.75B in loaded card and Stars balances" is wrong because Stars are not loaded. Replace with: `It also owed customers $1.75B in card and Stars balances at Sept 28, 2025: customers pay first, coffee comes later.`
   - Also: `Federal CARD Act rules: gift card funds last at least 5 years from purchase or last load; inactivity fees only after 1 year. States vary.`
6. **Post `yt_desc`:** Change "held $1.75 billion in card and Stars balances" to `owed $1.75 billion in card and Stars balances`.
   - The rest of the post text is accurate: the title, tiktok, "recognized about $222 million in breakage", and the 43%/$244 figures.
