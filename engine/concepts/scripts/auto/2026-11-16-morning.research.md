### Japan (Cabinet Office, Q3 = July-September 2026 preliminary GDP, released Mon Nov 16, 8:50 AM JST)
- Real GDP: -0.1% annualized (about -0.02% q/q, "flat" on a rounded basis). First contraction in four quarters (first in a year). Reuters poll median expected +0.3% annualized. Prior quarter revised to +1.7% annualized. (Reuters via Daily Sabah; Euronews; investingLive; Seoul Economic Daily)
- Private consumption (more than half of the economy): -0.3% q/q, second straight quarterly decline (forecast -0.2%). Causes cited: persistent price pressures (inflation squeezing households) and poor weather, heavy rain and typhoons.
- Capital spending +1.6% (AI, labor-saving tech). Exports -0.2%, imports +2.5%; net exports cut 0.6 pt from growth.
- Nominal GDP +0.4% q/q (+1.6% annualized).
- Next: second preliminary estimate Dec 8; BOJ still expected by economists to consider a December hike (not used - no predictions).

## What it means for a normal person
- Retail sales / consumer spending is the engine of an economy. When households feel squeezed (Japan: high prices) or poorer (China: falling home prices), they cut back and growth stalls. In the US, consumer spending is about two-thirds of the economy, which is why US retail sales and jobs reports move markets. Education only.
