## Issues
1. **Stale-news risk, line 6 and post/yt_desc ("record 33.4% in Q2 2026").** Q3 2026 results come out **Oct 22, 2026**, before the Dec 4 posting date, so Q2 will no longer be the latest quarter. Q3 guidance is a 32.9% gross margin, so 33.4% will probably still be the record. The dated wording ("In Q2 2026...") stays true either way. **Fix:** after Oct 22, check the Q3 deck. If Q3 gross margin is above 33.4%, change line 6 and the post to the Q3 figure. Otherwise keep it as is. If no one can re-check, change "a record" to "its highest on record as of mid-2026".
2. **Fairness, line 2 scene right column "No label cut".** Audiobooks pay publishers and podcasts pay creators and hosts (deals, Partner Program). They have content costs, just not per-stream label royalties. "No label cut" next to "About 2/3 cut" suggests they cost nothing. **Fix:** right items `["Own deals", "Not per-stream"]`. Optionally change the left item "Label royalties" to "Label + publisher" too, because the two-thirds includes publishers.
3. **Line 4 scene label "Video podcast viewers".** This reads as a current audience. The 500M is a cumulative count of users who have ever streamed one. **Fix:** label "Ever watched video pod" or sub "users ever, May 2026".
4. **Line 3 wording "15 hours of audiobooks".** The allowance is listening time. **Fix (text+say):** "...includes 15 hours of audiobook listening."
5. **Hook (line 0) judgment.** It is true, specific and matches Spotify's wording. At 11 spoken words it runs about 3 s, which is borderline. It also gives no reason yet to care about books and video, and the question only arrives in line 1. It is acceptable, not weak. **Stronger option:** "Spotify hands two-thirds of its music money to rights holders." (spoken around 2.5 s). Or fold the tension into line 0: "Two-thirds of Spotify's music money goes to rights holders. That's why it wants you on podcasts."
6. **Minor, header "Why Spotify wants your ears off music".** This slightly overstates the strategy: Spotify wants more listening beyond music, not less music. The post title "more than music" is more accurate. Optional fix: "Why Spotify wants more than your music".
