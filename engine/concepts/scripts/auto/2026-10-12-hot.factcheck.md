## Issues
1. **Stale news (line 7, and 8/post).** Sept 28 is 14 days before the Oct 12 posting date, and "7-week low" will be old news by then (gold was about $4,166-4,213 on Sept 30). Fix: refresh the price and headline just before posting, or reword to "In late September, gold hit a seven-week low..." and keep the dated price in the post text. The numbers are explicitly dated, so they are not wrong.
2. **Headline paraphrased under a Reuters credit (line 7 scene).** The actual Reuters headline is "Gold hits seven-week low as oil surge fuels rate hike bets". Fix: use the exact headline, or change the source label so it does not look like a quote.
3. **Awkward narration (line 4 say).** "that eight hundred fifty needed to hit about twenty-three hundred six" is clunky, and "about" sits next to an exact figure. Fix: "that eight hundred fifty dollars needed to become about twenty-three hundred dollars by two thousand eight." The screen can keep $2,306.
4. **Chart x-axis gaps (line 3).** The points skip 1991-94, 1996-98 and 2000. If the renderer spaces the points evenly, the time scale is distorted. Fix: add the missing years (1991 362, 1992 344, 1993 360, 1994 384, 1996 388, 1997 331, 1998 294, 2000 279) or confirm the axis is proportional. "Slid" also hides the 1987 rebound to $447. This is minor.
5. **Hook (line 1).** It is true, specific and under 3 seconds. It could be a little stronger: "Gold buyers in 1980 waited twenty-eight years just to break even." This is optional.
6. **Line 8 intraday snapshot.** $4,156 was the morning price; by the afternoon it was ~$4,137. The word "about" covers this, but "Sept 28, morning" in the sub would be more precise. Minor.

No advice or price predictions, political opinion, allegations, generalization from one company, or instruction-style takeaways. Line 4 is labeled as an example, with a stated CPI-U assumption. The cover and title are paid off.
