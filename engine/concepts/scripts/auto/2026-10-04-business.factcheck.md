# Fact-check: 2026-10-04-business ("How Adobe Makes Money")

Checked September 30, 2026, for posting October 04, 2026. Line numbers are file lines in `2026-10-04-business.raw.json`, with the script line (L1 to L9) in brackets.

## Verified claims (no change needed)
- **96% of revenue from subscriptions (FY2025):** Correct. Subscription revenue was $22,904M out of $23,769M total, which is 96.4% (Adobe FY2025 10-K and Q4 FY2025 release, Dec 10, 2025).
- **May 6, 2013: Creative Suite ends and Adobe goes Creative Cloud-only:** Correct. Adobe announced it at MAX. TechCrunch, May 6, 2013: "Adobe Goes All In With Subscription-Based Creative Cloud, Will Stop Selling Regular Licenses." Forbes (May 9, 2013) reported the "outcry," so "people were furious" is supported.
- **$2,599 and $49.99 a month:** Correct. The CS6 Master Collection list price was $2,599. The Creative Cloud complete plan was $49.99 a month with an annual commitment.
- **Math:** 2,599 / 49.99 = 51.99, so "about 52 months" is right. 49.99 x 12 = $599.88, also right.
- **Chart points (subscription revenue, $B):** All six match the 10-K figures. FY20 11.626, FY21 14.573, FY22 16.388, FY23 18.284, FY24 20.521, FY25 22.904.
- **RPO of $22.52B as of Nov. 28, 2025:** Correct. It was up 13% from $19.96B (Q4 FY2025 release and 10-K).
- **Gross margin:** Correct. Gross profit was $21,218M on revenue of $23,769M, with cost of revenue of $2,551M. That is 89.3%, so "about 89%" is right.
- **Post text and yt_desc** ($23.77B revenue, 96%, $22.52B, 89%): All correct.
- **No price predictions and no political opinion.** No time words will be wrong on October 04, 2026, because every figure carries a date or fiscal year.

## Issues

1. **Line 15 [L1], hook scene: the label is wrong (misleading on-screen value).** The box reads "Photoshop 96%", which suggests Photoshop is 96% of revenue. The 96% is the share of all revenue that comes from subscriptions, across Creative Cloud, Document Cloud, Experience Cloud and others.
   - **Fix:** `"label": "Revenue from subscriptions, FY2025"`, keeping `"value": "96%"` (source: FY2025 10-K, $22,904M / $23,769M).

2. **Line 14 [L1], opening strength: acceptable but could be stronger.** The line is true, specific and about 2.5 seconds long. However, "basically rent" is abstract.
   - **Optional stronger version:** "You can't buy Photoshop anymore. You rent it, and that rent is 96% of Adobe's money." This also sets up the cover ("Adobe Rents You Photoshop").

3. **Line 20 [L2] and line 26 [L3]: the cover isn't fully paid off.** The cover and the hook scene say "Photoshop," but the narration never says it.
   - **Fix:** In line 20, say "...Adobe stopped selling Photoshop and the rest of Creative Suite as software you own, and people were furious."

4. **Line 26 [L3]: awkward narration and a slightly loose claim.**
   - "twenty-five ninety-nine" sounds like $25.99 when spoken. Fix: "two thousand five hundred ninety-nine dollars" or "about twenty-six hundred dollars."
   - "the full Creative Suite" should be named precisely, because other CS6 bundles were cheaper. Fix: "the top Creative Suite bundle, Master Collection" (CS6 Master Collection list price was $2,599).
   - The spoken "about fifty dollars" versus the on-screen "$49.99" is fine as rounding.
   - Nit: the $49.99 plan had launched in 2012 and required an annual commitment. You could say "about fifty dollars a month on an annual plan."

5. **Lines 55 to 57 [L8]: stale price presented as a present-day lesson, plus mild advice.**
   - "The lesson for you: fifty dollars a month is about six hundred a year, so know what you're renting" uses the 2013 price as if it still applies. As of September 2026, Adobe lists Creative Cloud Pro (the renamed all-apps plan) at **$69.99 a month on the annual plan**, which is **$839.88 a year** (Adobe pricing, confirmed in multiple September 2026 pricing guides). At today's price, the rent passes $2,599 in about **37 months**, not 52.
   - **Fix, option A (keep it historical):** text "$49.99 a month = $599.88 a year (2013 price)"; say "Even at the 2013 price, fifty dollars a month was about six hundred a year."
   - **Fix, option B (make it current):** text "Today: $69.99 a month = $839.88 a year"; say "Today the all-apps plan is seventy dollars a month, about eight hundred forty a year." Update the money scene to $69.99 and $839.88.
   - Rephrase the lesson as an observation rather than an instruction, for example "That's the power of rent."

6. **Line 44 [L6]: RPO is not the latest figure (minor staleness).** $22.52B is correct as dated. However, Adobe's Q3 FY2026 filing (Sept. 10, 2026) shows **RPO of $22.16B as of Aug. 28, 2026**. The spoken line has no date, so a viewer on Oct 4 will hear it as current.
   - **Fix:** Either add "at the end of fiscal 2025" to the spoken line, or update the stat to "$22.16B, as of Aug. 28, 2026 (Adobe Q3 FY2026 10-Q)."

7. **Context (optional, not an error):** On Sept. 3, 2026, Adobe named Anil Chakravarthy as its next CEO, effective Dec. 1, 2026 (CNBC). The script doesn't need it, but a pinned comment could mention it to show the video is timely.

## Summary
- The historical figures, chart data and math are all accurate.
- The failing items are the mislabeled hook value (line 15: "Photoshop 96%") and the stale $50 a month presented as a current lesson (lines 55 to 57).
- The awkward "twenty-five ninety-nine" (line 26) should also be fixed.

Sources: Adobe FY2025 10-K and Q4 FY2025 earnings release (Dec 10, 2025; sec.gov and businesswire.com); Adobe FY2020, FY2021 and FY2023 10-Ks (sec.gov); Adobe Q3 FY2026 results (Sept. 10, 2026; sec.gov 10-Q adbe-20260828, Benzinga and Yahoo Finance transcripts); TechCrunch (May 6, 2013); Forbes (May 9, 2013); ProDesignTools CS6 price sheet; justcreative.com and costbench.com Creative Cloud pricing (September 2026); CNBC (Sept. 3, 2026).

VERDICT: FAIL
