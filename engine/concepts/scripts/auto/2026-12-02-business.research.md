# How They Make Money: Berkshire Hathaway and insurance float (research brief)
Prepared Oct 3, 2026 for posting Dec 2, 2026. Evergreen: every figure carries its year/date. No timely news hook (latest annual figures: FY2025, reported Feb 28, 2026).

## Key facts (with dates)
1. What float is: property/casualty insurers collect premiums upfront and pay claims later (sometimes decades later, e.g. asbestos). The money held in between is "float", which the insurer invests meanwhile. Source: Buffett, 2024 shareholder letter (Feb 2025), as quoted by Berkshire 2024 annual report coverage.
2. Exact quote (Buffett, 2020 letter, Feb 2021): "Berkshire now enjoys $138 billion of insurance 'float' - funds that do not belong to us, but are nevertheless ours to deploy." Script uses the fragment: "funds that do not belong to us, but are nevertheless ours to deploy."
3. 1967: Berkshire bought National Indemnity (and National Fire & Marine) from Jack Ringwalt for $8.6 million. Source: Buffett letters (2020 letter and others); Tontine Coffee-House; enterprising-investor.com.
4. Float at year-end 2025: approximately $176 billion (vs $171B end-2024, $88B end-2015). Source: Berkshire FY2025 10-K / annual report and Greg Abel's 2025 letter (Feb 28, 2026); confirmed by agencychecklists.com, insurancebusinessmag.com, and multiple search summaries. (Mid-2026: about $177.5B per Motley Fool, Aug 2026 - not used.)
5. Underwriting profit (after tax): $7,258M ($7.26B) in 2025; $9,020M ($9.02B) in 2024. Property/casualty combined ratio 87.1% in 2025 (5-yr avg 90.7%). Underwriting profit means premiums exceeded claims plus expenses, so float cost Berkshire less than nothing (negative cost of float); Berkshire reports underwriting profits in each of the three years through 2025. Source: Berkshire FY2025 10-K/annual report; insurancebusinessmag.com; agencychecklists.com.
6. CEO transition: May 3, 2025 Buffett said he would recommend Greg Abel as CEO; May 4, 2025 the board voted unanimously; Abel became President and CEO effective Jan 1, 2026; Buffett remains Chairman. Source: Berkshire news release May 5, 2025 / 8-K; PBS NewsHour; CNBC (Jan 6, 2026). Abel wrote the 2025 annual letter (Feb 28, 2026), his first.

## Surprising fact
Berkshire got paid to hold most of this money: in 2025 its insurance underwriting alone earned $7.26B after tax, so the $176B of float had a negative cost - on top of whatever the float earned invested.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
Assume a $1,000 premium collected today, invested at an assumed 4% a year, with the claim paid in year 5.
- $1,000 x 1.04^5 = $1,000 x 1.2166529 = $1,216.65
- If claims and costs take exactly $1,000 (break-even underwriting), the insurer still keeps $216.65 of investment gain.
- If underwriting is profitable (claims + costs < $1,000), it keeps more than that.

## Caveats / weakly sourced
- berkshirehathaway.com and sec.gov are blocked; all figures come from search-engine extracts of the letters/10-K, each confirmed by at least two queries/outlets. 1980 value ($237M) only from one query and not used on screen.
