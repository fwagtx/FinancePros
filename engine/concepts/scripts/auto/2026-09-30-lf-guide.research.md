# Research brief: How to build credit from zero, step by step (2026-09-30)

## News angle (this month)
- FICO Fall '26 Credit Insights report (Aug 25, 2026): average U.S. FICO Score holds at 714, unchanged since Oct 2025, down 1 point from a year ago; record was 718 (April 2023). (FICO / Business Wire, Aug 25, 2026)

## Surprising fact
- CFPB (June 2025 technical correction): in 2020, 7 million adults (2.7%) had NO credit record ("credit invisible"), and 25.3 million (9.8%) had a record but were UNSCORED. The 2015 "26 million invisible" estimate was corrected roughly in half. 87.5% (225.3M) had a score in 2020. (CFPB, June 2025)

## Rules
- FICO minimum scoring criteria: at least one account open 6+ months AND at least one account reported in the last 6 months (can be different accounts). (myFICO FAQ)
- VantageScore can score with about 1 month of history and an account reported in the last 2 years.
- FICO weights: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10%. (myFICO)
- Utilization = balance / limit; lower is better; common rule of thumb under 30%.
- Late payments usually reported once 30 days past due; can stay up to 7 years (FCRA).
- Hard inquiry: stays 2 years, FICO counts it ~12 months. Checking your own credit = soft inquiry, no effect.
- Free weekly credit reports from Equifax, Experian, TransUnion at AnnualCreditReport.com, made permanent in 2023 (FTC, Oct 2023).
- CARD Act: under 21 needs independent income or a cosigner to get a card; statements must be delivered at least 21 days before the due date; due date same day each month.

## The tools
1. Secured credit card: refundable deposit usually = credit limit; deposits often start around $200 (some issuers accept $49 or $99 for a $200 line). (issuer pages / NerdWallet 2026)
2. Credit-builder loan: lender puts $300-$1,000 in a locked savings account/CD; you pay it off over 6-24 months with interest/fees; then you get the money; on-time payments reported to bureaus; usually no credit check. (CFPB)
3. Authorized user on someone else's card: their history can show on your report if the issuer reports AUs; their late payments can hurt too. (CFPB)
4. Student card / co-signer (CARD Act under 21).
5. Rent / utility reporting services (VantageScore and some newer models count them if reported).

## Worked examples (hypothetical)
- Utilization: $200 limit secured card. $40 balance -> 40/200 = 20%. $180 balance -> 90%.
- Interest: $500 balance carried at 22% APR -> 500 x 0.22 / 12 = about $9.17 in one month. Paying the statement balance in full inside the grace period -> $0 interest.
- Fed G.19: accounts assessed interest paid about 22.15% on average in Q2 2026 (Fed G.19 via The Money Overview).

## Timeline
Month 0 open account(s) -> month 1 first report / possible VantageScore -> month 6 first FICO score -> month 12 one year of on-time history.
