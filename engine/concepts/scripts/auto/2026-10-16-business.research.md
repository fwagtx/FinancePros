# Research brief: How Amazon really makes money: the cloud vs the store (posts Oct 16, 2026)

## FY2025 (Amazon Form 10-K for fiscal year ended Dec 31, 2025; Q4 2025 release, Feb 5, 2026)
- Total net sales: $716.9B (up 12% from $638.0B in 2024).
- Operating income: $80.0B (2024: $68.6B). 2025 includes $2.5B FTC settlement and $2.7B severance charges.
- Segments, 2025:
  - North America: sales $426.3B, operating income $29.6B (2024: $25.0B)
  - International: sales $161.9B, operating income $4.75B
  - AWS: sales $128.7B, operating income $45.6B (2024: $39.8B)
- Check: 29.6 + 4.75 + 45.6 = 79.95 ≈ $80.0B.
- SURPRISING FACT: AWS was 128.7 / 716.9 = 18% of sales but 45.6 / 80.0 = 57% of operating income in 2025.
- North America + International segments ("the store side": online stores, third-party seller services, ads, Prime subscriptions, physical stores) = $588.2B sales, $34.35B operating income.

## Worked example: operating profit per $100 of sales, 2025
- AWS: 45.6 / 128.7 = 0.354 -> about $35 per $100.
- Store side: 34.35 / 588.2 = 0.058 -> about $6 per $100.
- Ratio: about 6x.
