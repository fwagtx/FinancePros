## Facts
### University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers, final October 2026 (released Fri Oct 23, 2026, 10 AM ET)
- Index of Consumer Sentiment: 48.0 final (preliminary Oct 9: 46.3; September 2026 final: 48.1; October 2025: 53.6). Sources: sca.isr.umich.edu; Kitco 2026-10-23; Reuters via Kitco/Korea Times.
- Reuters: second-lowest reading on record, after May 2026's 44.8 (record low). Headline: "US consumer sentiment near record low ahead of midterm elections."
- Current Economic Conditions: 46.7 (Sept 50.9). Consumer Expectations: 48.8 (Sept 46.3).
- Year-ahead inflation expectations: 4.7% (Sept 4.6%; February 2026: 3.4%). Long-run (5-10 yr): 3.5% (Sept 3.4%).

### American Express Q3 2026 (July-Sept), released Fri Oct 23, 2026 ~7 AM ET (company release / 8-K Ex. 99.1)
- Billed business (card member spending): $454.0B, up 8% from $421.0B in Q3 2025 (release; Yahoo/Zacks key metrics).
- Revenue net of interest expense: $20.1B, +9% (record). Analyst est. ~$20.12B (MarketBeat), small miss.
- Adjusted EPS $4.68 (+13% from $4.14); reported EPS $5.42 incl. $982M pretax gain on sale of Global Business Travel Group stake. Net income $3.7B vs $2.9B.

- Reuters headline: "American Express raises 2026 profit forecast as affluent customers keep spending"; spending driven by affluent customers on travel, dining and shopping.

- Weak/unconfirmed: layoffs/$336M charge (BigGo, Benzinga mention) - not used.

## Context / what it means
- The sentiment survey asks a broad sample of Americans; Amex's card base skews affluent (Reuters framing). So strong spending at one premium card company doesn't mean the typical household feels fine - prices/purchasing power drive the gloom (Hsu).
- 4.7% expected inflation means people expect a basket costing $100 to cost about $104.70 in a year - higher than 3.4% in February.
- What to watch: next UMich preliminary reading (November, mid-month), and other card issuers' spending data.
