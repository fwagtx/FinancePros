# 2026-10-21-explainer research brief: What the CPI measures and how inflation is calculated

Written October 1, 2026; posts October 21, 2026. The September 2026 CPI is released October 14, 2026 (after writing), so every figure below is labeled with its month. Do NOT call August 2026 "the latest" in the script.

## Timely angle
- CPI-U rose 3.4% over the 12 months ending August 2026 (not seasonally adjusted), same as July 2026; +0.4% in August (seasonally adjusted). Core (less food and energy) +2.4% over 12 months. Shelter +3.0% over 12 months, +0.3% in August. Released Sept 11, 2026. (BLS CPI news release USDL-26-1496; TD Economics; movewithmomentum.com; cpichart.com)
- Hook angle (the surprising fact): about a quarter of the CPI is "owners' equivalent rent", a rent homeowners never actually pay.

## What the CPI is and how it is built (BLS, CPI Overview / FAQ / Handbook of Methods)
- Measures the average change over time in prices paid by urban consumers for a market basket of goods and services.
- About 80,000 goods and services priced each month, in 75 urban areas, from about 6,000 housing units and about 23,000 retail and service establishments.
- Items classified into more than 200 categories in 8 major groups (food and beverages, housing, apparel, transportation, medical care, recreation, education and communication, other goods and services).
- Prices collected by personal visit, phone, websites/apps. Fuel and a few items priced monthly everywhere; most items monthly in NY, LA, Chicago and every other month elsewhere.
- Weights ("relative importance") = each item's share of consumer spending as of December of the latest year. Bigger spending share = bigger weight.

## Housing weights (BLS relative importance, December 2025)
- Shelter: about a third of the index (search results quote 35.625 and 35.514 for Dec 2025 vintages; Texas A&M PERC blog June 2026 "One-Third of CPI"). Script uses "about a third" only.
- Owners' equivalent rent of residences (OER): relative importance 26.204 (about 26%, about a quarter of the CPI). Wolf Street: OER weighs 26% of CPI, 33% of core CPI. Script uses "about a quarter" / "1/4".
- OER weight comes from the Consumer Expenditure Survey question to homeowners, exact wording: "If someone were to rent your home today, how much do you think it would rent for monthly, unfurnished and without utilities?" (BLS factsheet "Measuring Price Change in the CPI: Rent and Rental Equivalence"; BLS rent/OER FAQ)
- Common misconception (BLS): the PRICE changes for OER do not come from homeowner guesses; they come from actual rents of comparable rental units in the CPI Housing Survey. The question sets only the weight.

## Worked example (exact math)
- CPI-U all items, not seasonally adjusted (1982-84=100): August 2025 = 323.976; August 2026 = 334.980. (BLS CPI release Aug 2026; nysut.org CPI August 2026; search summaries)
- Inflation rate = (new index - old index) / old index = (334.980 - 323.976) / 323.976 = 11.004 / 323.976 = 0.03397 = 3.4%.
- Hypothetical example: a basket that cost $100 in August 2025 cost $100 x 334.980 / 323.976 = $103.3965, about $103.40, in August 2026 (if it moved exactly with the CPI).

## Surprising fact
About a quarter of the whole CPI is owners' equivalent rent: an estimate of rent for homes whose owners pay no rent at all.
