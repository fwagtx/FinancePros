# Research brief: Why is IVF so expensive? (posts Oct 16, 2026)

## Cost
- Executive Order 14216, "Expanding Access to In Vitro Fertilization" (Feb 18, 2025; Federal Register Feb 24, 2025): IVF cost per cycle "can range from $12,000 to $25,000."
- Fertility drugs (injectable gonadotropins) add roughly $2,000-$7,000 per cycle (GoodRx; Carrot/clinic price guides). Spoken only as "thousands of dollars".
- Many patients need more than one cycle.

## What a cycle is (ASRM/SART patient info)
- Hormone injections stimulate the ovaries -> egg retrieval under sedation -> eggs fertilized in the lab -> embryos cultured for days -> embryo transfer. It needs specialist physicians, an embryology lab and anesthesia.

## Insurance
- State fertility mandates do not apply to self-funded employer plans (ERISA).
- KFF 2025 Employer Health Benefits Survey: 67% of covered workers are in self-funded plans.

## Success rate (surprising fact)
- SART 2023 national summary: for patients under 35 using their own eggs, 41.0% of intended egg retrievals resulted in a live birth (2022: 41.3%).

## Chartable series: IVF cycles reported by SART member clinics
2021 368,502 | 2022 389,993 | 2023 432,641 | 2024 449,772
- 2024: 100,158 babies were born through IVF, the first year over 100,000 (ASRM/SART press release). In 2023, 95,860 IVF babies were 2.6% of US births.

## Worked example
- Two cycles at $20,000 each: 2 x $20,000 = $40,000 (within the $12K-$25K per-cycle range).
