# Research brief: How McDonald's app and loyalty program became a money machine
Prepared Nov 8, 2026 for posting Jan 7, 2027 (evergreen; every number dated). Show: How They Make Money.

## Timely angle
- McDonald's Q3 2026 results (released Nov 4, 2026): systemwide sales to loyalty members across 70 loyalty markets were $41 billion for the trailing twelve months (to Sept 30, 2026), and more than $11 billion for the quarter. Same quarter: US comparable sales -0.2%; global systemwide sales +4% to over $38 billion. (McDonald's Q3 2026 release; NRN; WOWT/AP, Nov 4, 2026)
- Definition (McDonald's): "Systemwide sales to loyalty members represents an aggregation of the prior four quarters of sales to loyalty members active in the last 90 days of the respective quarter." Includes franchised restaurants (this is sales at restaurants, not McDonald's revenue).

## Key facts
- Launch: MyMcDonald's Rewards announced June 22, 2021; nationwide in the US July 8, 2021. Earn rate at launch: 100 points per $1 spent on qualifying purchases. (McDonald's corporate / PR Newswire; QSR Magazine)
- 90-day active loyalty users: over 175 million at end of 2024 (Q4 2024 release); nearly 210 million at end of 2025, +19% (Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 11, 2026); nearly 220 million at end of Q2 2026, +13% (Q2 2026 release, Aug 4, 2026). Q3 2026 user count not confirmed.
- Targets (Investor Update, Dec 6, 2023): 250 million 90-day active users and $45 billion annual systemwide sales to loyalty members by 2027 (from ~150M users and over $20B at the time). (McDonald's corporate; PYMNTS)
- Frequency (McDonald's own claim, US): a customer visited 10.5 times in the 12 months before joining the loyalty program and 26 times in the 12 months after (CFO Ian Borden, Q4 2025 earnings call, Feb 2026; Kempczinski cited the same figures, per Customer Experience Dive). Caveat: company figure, before/after for joiners; outside analysts question attribution. Present as "McDonald's says".
- Data/personalization: executives say loyalty data drives personalized offers (Marketing Week; Restaurant Dive). Q3 2026 call: offers such as free fries with a $1 purchase helped loyalty gains; loyalty members ~30% of customer base (NRN, Nov 2026) - single source, not used on screen.

## Points economics (US)
- Earn: 100 points per $1 spent (pre-tax) on qualifying purchases (McDonald's launch release 2021; Milesopedia; The Points Guy).
- US devaluation announced April 10, 2026, effective May 4, 2026 (Doctor of Credit; Tasting Table; Food Republic): 3,000-point tier -> 3,500 (includes medium fries); 4,500 -> 5,000; 6,000 -> 7,000 (Big Mac, Quarter Pounder with Cheese, Happy Meal). Points expire after six months (from Jan 1, 2026 per Loyaltypass; Points Guy says six months).
- Medium fries: 3,500 points since May 4, 2026 (3,000 before). (BiteStaff tier list; Doctor of Credit; Tasting Table)

## Worked example (our math)
- 3,500 points / 100 points per $1 = $35 of spending to earn one free medium fries.
- Before May 4, 2026: 3,000 / 100 = $30. Difference: $5 more spending (+16.7%).
- Big Mac: 7,000 / 100 = $70 of spending (was $60).

## Surprising fact
- McDonald's says US customers went from 10.5 visits a year before joining to 26 visits the year after - about 2.5x.
- Loyalty sales roughly doubled from about $20B (2023) to $41B (TTM Sept 2026).
