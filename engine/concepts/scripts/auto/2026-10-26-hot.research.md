# Research brief: Scam alert: AI voice clones of family members (Hot Take, posts Oct 26, 2026)

- McAfee, "Beware the Artificial Impostor" (May 2023; 7,054 adults in 7 countries):
  - 3 seconds of audio can produce an 85% voice match.
  - 1 in 4 adults had experienced an AI voice scam or knew someone who had.
  - 77% of victims lost money.
- FTC (2025 data, released 2026): imposter scams led all fraud reports, with $3.5B in reported losses and 1M+ reports.
- FTC (Aug 2025): reports of impersonation scammers taking tens or hundreds of thousands from older adults rose more than four-fold.
- FBI PSA I-120324-PSA (Dec 3, 2024): criminals use generative AI, including voice cloning, for fraud. The FBI advises creating a secret word or phrase with family to verify identity, and limiting online content of your voice and image (e.g. private accounts).
- FBI warning on AI voice-cloning distress scams (KPRC, June 2, 2026).
- Playbook: urgency (accident, arrest, bail), secrecy, and payment by wire, gift card, crypto or courier.
- Defense: hang up and call back on a known number; family code word.
