# Fact-check: 2026-12-29-business (Why consoles are sold at a loss)

Checked Oct 30, 2026 for posting on Dec 29, 2026 (evergreen). Each claim was verified on its own through web search excerpts. www.sec.gov cannot be reached from this session, so Sony filing values come from search excerpts of the filings and from secondary coverage. The math was recomputed in Python.

## Verified OK
- Lori Wright (Xbox VP of business development) testified at the Epic v. Apple trial in May 2021. Asked about Xbox margins, she said "We sell the consoles at a loss." Asked "Does Microsoft ever earn a profit on the sale of an Xbox console?" she answered "No." Sources: GeekWire, VGC, NME, TechSpot, Axios. Lines[1] quote, attribution and "under oath" are correct. Note that Apple later challenged her credibility, saying she produced no documents.
- In Aug 2021 (the Q1 FY2021 call, reported by Bloomberg), Sony CFO Hiroki Totoki said the $499 disc PS5 was no longer sold at a loss. The Digital Edition still was. Sources: GamingBolt, TechRadar, TechSpot, SegmentNext.
- Sony network services sales come from the 20-F "sales to external customers by product category" table, in fiscal years ending March 31:
  - FY to Mar 2022: 409,355
  - FY to Mar 2023: 464,676 (FY2023 20-F)
  - FY to Mar 2024: 545,537
  - FY to Mar 2025: 669,873 (FY2025 20-F)
  - FY to Mar 2026: 763.1B (secondary source, which shows it against a prior year of 669.9B)
  - The chart points [2022..2026] = 409.4 / 464.7 / 545.5 / 669.9 / 763.1 are correct, and each value belongs to the fiscal year ending in March of the year it is plotted against.
- Hardware & Others was ¥1,391,622M in the FY to Mar 2026, down ¥191,578M from ¥1,583,200M (secondary source plus the FY2025 20-F). That is -12.1%, so "about 12%" is right.
- In the FY to Mar 2025, the three categories add up exactly to G&NS external sales of 4,543,571, so the category table is the right source.
- A UK class action (Alex Neill v Sony, CAT, certified in [2023] CAT 73) alleges that Sony charges a 30% commission on PlayStation Store sales. The search found no merits judgment. 30% x $70 = $21 is correct.
- In the Feb 2026 Q3 Q&A, Furukawa said Nintendo has traditionally tried to avoid selling individual units at a loss. He also said Nintendo would not be "overly influenced" by short-term memory costs. The paraphrase "it says it tries to avoid selling hardware at a loss" is accurate.
- No advice, no political opinion, and no time words that will be wrong on the posting date. Compare values are 7 characters or fewer. There are no keep:true flags.

## Issues

1. **lines[0] (hook), text/say. Present tense on 2021 testimony.** "Microsoft testified it doesn't profit when it sells an Xbox" reads as a current fact. The testimony is from May 2021, and Xbox hardware prices have changed since then (several price rises in 2025). Fix it with past perfect:
   - text: `Microsoft testified it had never made a profit selling an Xbox.`
   - say: `Microsoft testified it had never made a profit selling an Xbox.`

2. **post.ig (first line) and post.tiktok. Same present-tense problem.**
   - ig line 1: `Microsoft testified it had never made a profit selling an Xbox.`
   - tiktok: `Microsoft testified it had never made a profit selling an Xbox. So where's the money? Not financial advice. #moneytok #xbox #playstation #financeprostv`
   - #personalfinance was also dropped because it is off-topic.

3. **cover.value "30%" next to label "XBOX" and title "Sold at a Loss". This is a mismatch.** The cover reads as an Xbox 30% figure, such as a 30% loss. In the video, 30% is only the alleged Sony store cut. Replace it so the cover matches the hook:
   - cover.value: `LOSS`
   - Alternative: keep "30%" but set cover.label to `PS STORE`. That fits less well with the title.

4. **lines[2], text/say. "until August 2021" suggests losses ended exactly then.** Sony said in Aug 2021 that the disc model was no longer sold at a loss. The digital model still was.
   - text: `Sony lost money on early PS5s too. By August 2021, it said the disc model no longer did.`
   - say: `Sony lost money on early P S fives too. By August twenty twenty-one, it said the disc model no longer did.`

5. **lines[5]. The digital figure is unverified and the hardware label is too narrow.**

Only the ¥4,685.7B segment total (which includes intersegment sales) could be confirmed. The fix below swaps in network services, which is verified: 763.1/669.9 = +13.9%.
   - (b) Sony's category is "Hardware and Others", not hardware alone.
   - (c) "That last year" sounds awkward read aloud.
   - text: `In the year to March 2026, hardware and other sales fell about 12%, while network services rose about 14%.`
   - say: `In the year to March twenty twenty-six, hardware and other sales fell about twelve percent, while network services rose about fourteen percent.`

6. **post.ig and post.yt_desc. "hardware sales fell about 12%" has the same category issue.** Replace it with `hardware and other sales fell about 12%` in both fields.

7. **lines[7]. "Profit per box" overstates what Nintendo said, and "the exception" overgeneralizes.** Nintendo said it tries to avoid per-unit losses, not that every unit makes a profit. It also said Switch 2 earns less per unit than Switch 1. The left panel's "Lose on box" for PS5 also contradicts lines[2].
