## Context / what it means
- Online holiday spending has set a record every year; this year's early pace is faster than Adobe expected.
- Part of the growth is everyday necessities, not only gifts; NRF estimated higher prices account for about half its expected 4.1% Nov-Dec sales growth (not used in script).
- For a normal person: more of the spending is on credit-like pay-later plans; those payments come due after the holidays (January).
