# Research brief: How car dealerships make money, it's not the car (posts Nov 9, 2026)

## AutoNation FY2025 Form 10-K (filed Feb 2026)
- Total revenue $27,631.4M (+3.2%).
- Share of revenue vs share of gross profit (2025):
  - New vehicles: 49% of revenue, 13% of gross profit (new vehicle gross profit -14% y/y)
  - Used vehicles: 28% of revenue, 9% of gross profit
  - Parts and service: 17% of revenue, 48% of gross profit (record after-sales gross profit ~$2.4B, +7%)
  - Finance and insurance: 5% of revenue, 30% of gross profit (+8%)
- New vehicle gross profit per unit: $1,794 (2019); $2,804 (Q3 2024); $2,281 (Q3 2025).

## Industry
- Fixed operations (service and parts) ~half of dealership gross profit (Presidio-NCM; public dealer groups 48%-59% in Q4 2025).
- NADA: franchised dealers' service and parts sales exceeded $164B in 2025.

## Worked example
Per $100 of AutoNation revenue: $49 from new cars, $17 from parts and service, $5 from F&I.
Per $100 of AutoNation gross profit: $13 from new cars, $48 from parts and service, $30 from F&I.
