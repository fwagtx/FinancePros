# Fact-check: 2027-01-04 Explainer (non-competes), posts Mon Jan 4, 2027, noon CT
Checked Nov 5, 2026. Each claim was checked independently by web search.

## Verified claims
- GAO-23-103785 (May 2023): the GAO reviewed two nationally representative studies, which estimated that 18% of workers were subject to non-competes. The 18% comes from those studies, not from GAO's own survey. 18% is closer to "nearly 1 in 5" than to "about 1 in 5", which is 20%. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-103785
- Ryan LLC v. FTC (N.D. Tex.) set the rule aside nationwide on Aug 20, 2024. On Sept 5, 2025 the FTC voted 3-1 to dismiss its appeals (5th and 11th Cir.), and the Fifth Circuit dismissed Ryan on Sept 8, 2025. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/federal-trade-commission-files-accede-vacatur-non-compete-clause-rule
- LATER DEVELOPMENT: a final rule published Feb 12, 2026 (91 FR 6507, FR Doc 2026-02866) removed the Non-Compete Clause Rule from the CFR. The FTC still brings case-by-case Section 5 actions, for example a June 2026 Rollins/Orkin order. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-12/pdf/2026-02866.pdf
- State bans: California, Minnesota (agreements from July 1, 2023), North Dakota and Oklahoma are correct. The list leaves out WYOMING. SF 107 was signed Mar 19, 2025 and took effect July 1, 2025. It voids most non-competes, except for executive and management personnel and their professional staff, trade secrets, and sale of a business. It covers agreements from July 1, 2025. https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/wyoming-enacts-law-to-restrict-the-use-of-noncompete-agreements/
- Illinois, 820 ILCS 90/10(a): a non-compete is not allowed unless actual or expected annualized earnings EXCEED $75,000. The threshold becomes $80,000 from Jan 1, 2027, then $85,000 in 2032 and $90,000 in 2037. This applies to agreements entered on or after Jan 1, 2022. "Earn $78,000 → a new non-compete is void" is correct for agreements entered on or after Jan 1, 2027. The math (80,000 - 78,000 = 2,000) is correct. The HB 3213 proposals were not found enacted, and they would not change the 2027 step. https://www.ilga.gov/documents/Legislation/ILCS/Documents/082000900K10.htm
- Washington ESHB 1155 was signed Mar 23, 2026 and takes effect June 30, 2027. It voids nearly all non-competes, including existing ones. It will still be in the future on the posting date, so "bans most on June 30, 2027" is correct. https://www.duanemorris.com/alerts/washington_states_near_total_noncompete_ban_what_employers_need_to_know_0426.html
- "Can't stop you quitting; can limit rival work for a set time, in a set area": this is accurate as a general description. It is not true everywhere, because in ban states the agreement can't limit anything, so it needs "where allowed".
- No political opinion and no price predictions. The cover "Can It Stop You?" is paid off in line 2. On-screen and spoken values match on every line. Spoken words total 139, within 115-140. post.title is 56 characters and post.tiktok is 148.
