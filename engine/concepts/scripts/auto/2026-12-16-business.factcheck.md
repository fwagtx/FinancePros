## Issues and fixes

1. **Stale and wrong time framing on the posting date (lines 1, 2; cover; post).** Trepp publishes each month's data early in the next month, so by Dec 16 the October and November 2026 office rates will be out and September's 12.16% will be two reports old. The present-tense "is late" then describes old data. Green Street's November index will also be out by then.
   - Preferred fix: before posting, update lines 1, 2 and 3, the cover and the post to Trepp's November 2026 office rate. Re-check "1 in 8" against the new number, and refresh Green Street to its latest monthly reading.
   - Fallback if the numbers aren't refreshed, using past tense and dated wording:
     - Line 1 text: "In September, 1 in 8 office loans sold to bond investors was late." / say: "In September, one in eight office loans sold to bond investors was late."
     - Line 2 sub: keep "Trepp, Sep 2026" (15 chars).

2. **Cover and title generalize to all office loans (misleading).** 12.16% is office CMBS only, not all office loans. Bank loans make up most of the market and default far less.
   - cover.title: `1 in 8 Office CMBS Loans Late` (29)
   - post.title: `1 in 8 office CMBS loans was late. How landlords cope` (53, under 60)
   - tiktok: `1 in 8 office CMBS loans was late (Trepp, Sep 2026). How landlords cope. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #realestate #financeprostv` (148, under 150)

3. **Line 7 board shows the wrong unit.** "Real rent: $45/yr" reads as $45 a year. It should be per foot per year.
   - lines[2]: `Real: $45/ft/yr` (15, under 20)

4. **Line 3 chart skips 2022.** The points jump from 2021 to 2023 with even spacing, which hides the Dec 2022 low of 1.58% and makes the rise look like one straight climb.
   - points: `[[2021, 2.53], [2022, 1.58], [2023, 5.82], [2024, 11.01], [2025, 11.31]]` (title, end and narration unchanged).
   - Line 3 say: add "percent" so it reads aloud correctly: "...and eleven point three one percent four years later."

5. **RentCafe wording (post.ig, yt_desc).** "90,300 planned office-to-apartment units in 2026" is wrong. RentCafe counts the pipeline at the start of 2026.
   - ig: "Landlords cope with free rent, build-out cash and conversions: RentCafe counts 90,300 office-to-apartment units in the pipeline at the start of 2026, up 28% in a year."
   - yt_desc: "...RentCafe counts 90,300 office-to-apartment units in the 2026 pipeline."

6. **Weak hook length (line 1, minor).** At about 12 words, "Roughly one in eight office loans sold to bond investors is late" runs slightly over 3 seconds.
   - Stronger and shorter: text "1 in 8 bond-backed office loans is late." / say "One in eight bond-backed office loans is late." Use "was late" if issue 1 isn't refreshed.

7. **Line 4 framing (minor).** "Why? Empty desks" is fine, but vacancy is actually easing slightly (down 25 bps YoY). The current wording is accurate, so this is optional. Possible sub alternative: `Cushman, Q3 2026` (16) is already fine.

8. **Line 8 (minor).** The B/C "Vacancy rising" claim varies by market. Optional: change the title to `Two office markets` (already) and add nothing else, since space is tight. Acceptable as is.
