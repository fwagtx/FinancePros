# Research brief: How billboards make money (posts Sun Jan 10, 2027)

Company: Lamar Advertising (largest US billboard operator by display count among public REITs). Latest annual report: Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025, filed Feb 2026). Latest quarter: Q3 2026 (released Nov 5, 2026). Evergreen angle, no news hook needed.

## Key facts (with period and source)
- Billboard displays: "As of December 31, 2025, we owned and operated approximately 159,300 billboard advertising displays in 45 states and Canada." (Lamar FY2025 10-K, direct quote) [S1]
- Digital: "approximately 5,500 digital billboard advertising displays in 43 states and Canada." "These 5,500 digital billboards generated approximately 33% of billboard advertising net revenues." (FY2025 10-K, direct quote) [S1]. Earnings-call summaries give 5,553 digital units at year-end 2025 [S3].
  - Share of faces (our math): 5,500 / 159,300 = 3.45%, i.e. roughly 1 in 29-30.
- Revenue FY2025: total net revenues $2,266.2M (2024: $2,207.1M, +2.7%). Billboard $2,013.85M; logo $89.2M; transit $163.2M (10-K revenue disaggregation via edgar.tools mirror; MD&A: "Net revenues increased $59.1 million or 2.7% to $2.27 billion") [S1][S2][S4]
- Adjusted EBITDA FY2025: about $1.06B (+2.4%); net income $593.1M (includes $68.6M gain on sale of Vistar Media stake) (Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 20, 2026) [S2][S4]
  - Adjusted EBITDA margin (our math): 1.06 / 2.266 = about 47%.
- Q3 2026 (latest quarter): net revenue $624.5M vs $585.5M in Q3 2025, +6.6%; drivers cited: political, national, digital (release Nov 5, 2026) [S5][S6]
- Structure economics: "We own the physical structures on which the advertising copy is displayed. We build the structures on locations we either own or lease." Leases cover "sites upon which advertising structures are built, including our billboard land leases." (FY2025 10-K) [S1]. Third-party summary: ~11,200 owned sites vs ~71,500 leased sites in 2025 (NOT verified in filing; do not use on screen).
- REIT: Lamar elected to treat displays as real property for tax; IRS private letter ruling said rent from ad space on displays qualifies as "rents from real property"; as a REIT it must distribute at least 90% of REIT taxable income; REIT status from tax year beginning Jan 1, 2014 (Lamar 2014 8-K/10-Q filings) [S7]
- Digital rotation: Lamar product page: ads rotate "every 6-to-8 seconds"; Lamar CEO (via Billboard Insider): industry standard is 6-8 slots, Lamar uses both depending on market [S8][S9]
- Regulation/scarcity: Highway Beautification Act of 1965 controls billboards along Interstate and Federal-Aid Primary highways (size, spacing, lighting; generally restricted to commercial/industrial areas within 660 ft); states that fail "effective control" lose 10% of federal-aid highway funds (FHWA history, Wikipedia, GAO) [S10][S11]. 10-K: local zoning sign controls; states have bought and removed billboards with federal funds.
- Industry: US out-of-home (OOH) ad revenue was a record $9.46B in 2025, +3.6% (OAAA, March 2026); digital OOH +10.5%, 36.3% of total [S12][S13]

## Worked example (OUR MATH, FY2025 10-K inputs, approximate counts)
- Digital revenue: 33% x $2,013.85M = $664.6M; / 5,500 digital faces = about $120,800 per digital face per year.
- Printed revenue: 67% x $2,013.85M = $1,349.3M; / (159,300 - 5,500 = 153,800) printed faces = about $8,770 per printed face per year.
- Ratio: about 14x. Caveats: averages; digital boards sit in busier spots and are often larger; "displays" counts include posters; 33% is "approximately".

## Surprising fact
About 3.5% of Lamar's billboards (the digital ones) earned about a third of its billboard revenue in 2025.
