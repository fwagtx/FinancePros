# Research brief: How shipping companies make money on containers (Maersk). Posts Nov 3, 2026

Company: A.P. Moller - Maersk (annual reports / FY results releases).

## Key numbers
- FY2025 (released Feb 5, 2026): revenue $54.0B, EBITDA $9.5B, EBIT $3.5B. Ocean revenue $35.0B (-6.5%), average freight rates -23%, Ocean EBIT $1.4B (-71%). Terminals and logistics grew.
- Ocean share of 2025 revenue: 35.0/54.0 = 65%.
- EBIT history: 2022 $30.9B (record), 2023 $3.93B, 2024 $6.5B, 2025 $3.5B.
- Revenue history ($B): 2019 38.89 | 2020 39.74 | 2021 61.79 | 2022 81.53 | 2023 51.07 | 2024 55.48 | 2025 54.0 (chartable).

## Freight rates (Drewry World Container Index, per 40ft container)
- 2019 average about $1,420; peak $10,377 in September 2021.

## Worked example
- Operating profit per $1 of revenue: 2022 30.9/81.53 = $0.38; 2025 3.5/54.0 = $0.065.
- Profit drop 2022 -> 2023: 30.9 -> 3.93 = -87%.
- Why: freight rates swing; ship, fuel, crew, port and charter costs don't fall as fast (high fixed costs, operating leverage).
