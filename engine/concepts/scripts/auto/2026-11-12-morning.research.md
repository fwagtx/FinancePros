## What it means for a normal person
- A stronger dollar buys more foreign currency: trips abroad (Japan especially) cost fewer dollars; imported goods can get somewhat cheaper over time.
- It hurts US companies that sell overseas (their foreign sales are worth fewer dollars) and makes the US pricier for foreign visitors.
- The driver is the same thing that pushes loan rates: expected Fed hikes.
- Exchange rates move daily; Japan has signaled it could step in again near 160.
