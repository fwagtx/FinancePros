# Fact-check: 2026-11-12-business (How hospitals profit from your doctor)
Checked Oct 1, 2026 for a Nov 12, 2026 post. All math recomputed in python.

## Verified claims
- **Kaufman Hall Q2 2026, $314,231.** Confirmed. Kaufman Hall/Vizient Physician Flash Report Q2 2026 gives a median **investment/subsidy per physician FTE** of $314,231, up from $313,330 in Q2 2025. The sample is 200,000+ employed physicians and APPs. Kaufman Hall defines the metric as net patient service revenue minus total expenses, divided by physician FTEs. It measures the operating shortfall of the employed medical group, and the report presents it as an annualized figure. "Loses" is a fair plain-English word for an operating subsidy. Two caveats: (a) the figure leaves out downstream hospital revenue, which is the point of the video, and (b) it is a median from Kaufman Hall's benchmark of hospital and health-system medical groups. It is not a figure for "hospitals" in general. Sources: Becker's ASC, "The $314K physician investment"; kaufmanhall.com Physician Flash Report.
- **PAI-Avalere series.** Confirmed: Jan 2019 62.2%, Jan 2022 73.9%, Jan 2024 77.6%, Jan 1 2026 82.0%. In Jan 2026 the split was 59.7% hospitals and 22.3% other corporate entities. The base is "hospitals, health systems or corporate entities", which includes insurers, PE and others, so "hospitals or corporations" is accurate. Rounding to 62 and 74 on the chart is fine. Sources: PAI 2022 release, PCC, Advisory Board 2024, Becker's, Medical Economics.
- **CMS $119 vs $341.** Confirmed. The example is from CMS's CY2026 OPPS rule (final Nov 2025): HCPCS 96413, chemo IV infusion up to 1 hour, pays $119 under the PFS and $341 under the OPPS, which CMS calls 186% more. The $119 matches the CY2025 PFS rate of $119.36. Math: 341 - 119 = 222, and 222/119 = 186.6%. These are **administration** payments only. The drug is paid separately, so "same drug" on the board is not part of CMS's example. Source: CHA CY2026 OPPS summary.
- **CY2027 OPPS proposed rule.** Confirmed. CMS released it July 2, 2026 (Federal Register July 7) and took comments to Aug 31. It proposes paying the PFS-equivalent rate (about 40% of OPPS) for imaging **without contrast** at excepted (pre-Nov 2, 2015) off-campus PBDs. Rural SCHs are exempt, and CMS estimates $260M in savings. **No final rule as of Oct 1, 2026.** Recent final rules came out Nov 1, 2024 and Nov 21, 2025, so the CY2027 final rule could be out before Nov 12. Sources: AHA (July 2, 2026), Federal Register 2026-13656, Advis, America's Essential Hospitals.
- **Merritt Hawkins $2.4M.** Confirmed. The 2019 Physician Inpatient/Outpatient Revenue Survey (released Feb 2019, 2018 data) found an average of $2,378,727 a year in net inpatient plus outpatient revenue for affiliated hospitals, based on CFO estimates across 18 specialties. It counts all admissions, tests, treatments and procedures doctors perform or order, not only "referrals". Sources: Healthcare Dive, Fierce Healthcare, PR Newswire.
