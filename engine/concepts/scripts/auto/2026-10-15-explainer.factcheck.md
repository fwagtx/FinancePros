# Fact-check: 2026-10-15-explainer (posts Oct 15, 2026; checked Oct 1, 2026)

## Verified claims
- **58% in four years (cover, line 1, post).** CPI motor vehicle insurance annual average went from 565.3 (2021) to 893.5 (2025), +58.06% (python). The yearly changes compound to +58.2%. Source: Triple-I Facts + Statistics: Auto insurance (BLS CPI).
- **$1.12 per $1 of premium, 2022 (line 2).** AM Best put the US personal auto combined ratio at 112.2 for 2022, the worst in at least 30 years. "Claims and costs" correctly describes a combined ratio. Sources: AM Best via Insurance Journal and Actuarial Review.
- **Chart (line 3): 2021 3.8, 2022 7.9, 2023 17.4, 2024 17.8, 2025 6.0.** All match the Triple-I and BLS annual average changes.
- **$4,818 average repair, 2025 (line 4).** CCC Crash Course 2026 gives this as the preliminary average total cost of repair for repairable claims (+1.7% vs 2024).
- **About $486 recalibration (line 5).** CCC reports $485.56, so "about $486" is fine.
- **More totaled cars (line 6).** CCC reports a record 23.1% total-loss rate in 2025.
- **-5.1% insurance vs +5.2% repairs, 12 months to Aug 2026 (line 7, post).** Matches the BLS CPI August 2026 release: motor vehicle insurance -5.1% y/y, motor vehicle maintenance and repair +5.2% y/y.

## Issues
1. **Line 7, post (ig, yt_desc): stale data on the posting date.** BLS releases September 2026 CPI on **Oct 14, 2026**, the day before the video posts. "12 months to August 2026" will still be true, but it will be one release old. Fix: on Oct 14, pull the September figures for motor vehicle insurance and for maintenance and repair. Update text, say, the compare scene title/values and all post copy to "12 months to September 2026". If they can't be updated, keep the explicit "to August 2026" dating everywhere. Never say "latest" or "right now".
2. **Line 5: "late 2025" looks wrong.** CCC presents $485.56 as the 2025 figure in Crash Course 2026, in a 2017-to-2025 comparison. It is not described as a late-2025 or Q4 reading. Fix: "averaged about $486 in 2025" (text, say, board line 3 "(CCC, 2025)").
3. **Header vs content.** The header "Why insurance prices keep climbing" conflicts with line 7: car insurance prices are now falling year over year. Fix: "Why car insurance got so expensive" or "Why car insurance soared".
4. **Line 6: home insurance appears out of nowhere.** "And for homes, storms and rebuilding costs" brings in home insurance with no data, in a car insurance video. The list item "Storms and rebuilding" is also unsupported for auto. Fix: drop the home clause, or make it auto-specific (e.g. "more severe weather claims" plus "pricier parts and labor"). Otherwise cite a home-insurance stat.
5. **Line 4: label precision.** "Average car repair" is really the CCC average cost of a **repairable collision claim**, and the figure is preliminary. Fix: on-screen "Average collision repair claim, 2025"; say "the average collision repair hit...". The sub "repairable claims, CCC" is OK.
6. **Line 7 say: awkward ending.** "...while repairs rose five point two." is missing "percent", which sounds clipped. Fix: "...while repair prices rose five point two percent."
7. **Line 3: spoken rounding vs on-screen (minor).** On screen: 7.9 / 17.4 / 17.8. Spoken: "about eight percent" / "over seventeen percent in both". The rounding is consistent and acceptable. For a strict match, say the exact figures.
8. **Cover/title (minor).** "Car Insurance Up 58%" without a date can read as current, while prices are down 5.1% y/y. The cover label "2021-25" and the twist in line 7 pay it off, so this is acceptable. "2021-25" should stay visible.
9. **Line 8 (minor).** "Cool off once profits return" is a reasonable generalization of the underwriting cycle. It is not tied to one company's numbers. OK.

## Other checks
- **Opening:** "Car insurance prices rose fifty-eight percent in four years." It is true, specific and about 2.5 s. OK. It could be stronger with the twist teased: "Car insurance jumped 58% in four years, and now it's falling."
- **Rules:** no advice or price predictions, no political opinion, no quotes or allegations, no instruction-style takeaways. "Not financial advice" is present in the post copy.
- **Math:** 893.5/565.3 = 1.581; compounded yearly changes = 1.582. OK.
