## Verified claims
- **L1 $19,000 annual exclusion, 2026, per giver per recipient.** Confirmed. The IRS gift tax FAQ table lists 2026 as $19,000 per donee, which is $38,000 from two spouses. Morgan Lewis and other summaries of the IRS 2026 inflation adjustments say it is unchanged from 2025. Below $19,000 per recipient, no Form 709 is needed unless the parents elect gift splitting or the gift is a future interest.
- **L3 $15M lifetime exemption, per person, 2026.** Confirmed. It rose from $13.99M in 2025 under the July 2025 law (P.L. 119-21), per Morgan Lewis and the IRS release. "Filing isn't paying" is correct: the taxable excess reduces the exemption, and tax is due only after cumulative taxable gifts pass the exemption.
- **L2 math.** 50,000 - 2 x 19,000 = 12,000, which is 6,000 per parent if each gives $25,000. Recomputed in python. Correct.
- **L0 hook.** It is true and fair. The sentence addresses the giver ("Gift your kid..."), and Form 709 is filed by the giver. $50K from two parents exceeds $38K, so at least one Form 709 is required. The hook is specific, says no tax is due, and is paid off by L2-L3.
- **L4-L5 lender.** Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-4.3-04 says the gift letter must be signed by the donor, state the actual or maximum dollar amount, and say "no repayment is expected". It also asks for donor details and relationship. The lender must verify the transfer of funds. The Fannie Mae large-deposit sourcing rule (B3-4.2-02) supports "big unexplained deposits get flagged". Correct.
- **L6 below-AFR loans.** IRC 7872 treats the forgone interest on a below-market gift loan as a gift from lender to borrower. "Can count" leaves room for the exceptions: the $10,000 aggregate de minimis rule (it does not cover loans used for income-producing assets) and the $100,000 net-investment-income cap. Fair. Calling AFR the "IRS minimum rate" is a common simplification and is acceptable.
- **L7 NAR.** NewsNation/KXAN coverage of the NAR 2025 Profile says "more than 1 in 5" first-time buyers got help from relatives or friends through a gift or loan. Walnut Invest gives 22%. The NAR 2025 Profile covers July 2024 to June 2025 transactions. Supported, with caveats in Issue 2.

3. **L2 hidden assumption (WARN, clarity).** "Two parents giving $50,000 cover $38,000" only works without extra filing if each parent gives their own share ($25,000 each). Each parent then files their own Form 709 for a $6,000 excess. If one parent writes the whole $50,000 check, the $38,000 coverage needs gift splitting, which is itself elected on Form 709. Recommended replacement (spoken words stay 20, total 135):
   - L2 text: `So two parents giving $25,000 each cover $38,000; the other $12,000 goes on Form 709.`
   - L2 say: `So two parents giving twenty-five thousand each cover thirty-eight thousand; the other twelve thousand goes on Form seven oh nine.`
   - L2 board lines: `$50,000 ($25K each)` / `- $38,000 (2 x $19,000)` / `= $12,000 on Form 709` (19, 23 and 21 characters)

4. **L4/L5 list title (NIT).** "Transfer proof" sits under the title "Lender gift letter", but transfer proof is a separate lender requirement and is not part of the letter. Optional: change the title on both L4 and L5 to `Lender wants` (12 characters). Keep:true stays valid.

5. **L3 absolute wording (NIT).** "Filing isn't paying" is wrong only for parents who have already used up $15M of exemption on earlier gifts, which is negligible for this audience. The posts already say "usually". No change needed. Optional: "Filing usually isn't paying" (+1 word, 136 total).

6. **Time words.** "In 2026" (L1) and "2026" (L3 stat sub) are explicitly dated and still correct on Dec 8 2026, and gifts made in December count for 2026.
