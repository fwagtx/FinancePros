## Pick: Gas prices at record levels for the season (AAA)
Distinct from the already-covered "oil above $100" story: this is the pump-price/household angle.

### Facts (exact values, dates)
- AAA (release Oct 1, 2026, "National Average Dips Following Record-Setting September"): September 2026 monthly national average was $4.33/gal, 50 cents above the previous September record of $3.83 set in 2023. (LendingTree cites $4.36 for Sept 2026 - different method; use AAA.)
- AAA (release Oct 22, 2026, "Gas Prices Not Budging Much as Crude Oil Remains High"): national average $4.27 (ticker $4.2701), down 6 cents in a week; drop attributed to switch to cheaper winter-blend gasoline; crude "nearing $100 per barrel" amid instability in the Strait of Hormuz. Exact quote: "This month is poised to set a record as the most expensive October ever for gas prices following similar records set in August and September of this year."
- AOL headline (syndicated): "Gas prices on pace for most expensive October ever, AAA says."
- AAA Oct 23 figure: $4.29 (ticker $4.2926), more than $1 higher than the same time last year.
- AAA national average widget: $4.2858 as of 10/26/26 (Monday) -> about $4.29. (Weakly sourced: read from widget text in search results; consistent with Oct 23 $4.29.)
