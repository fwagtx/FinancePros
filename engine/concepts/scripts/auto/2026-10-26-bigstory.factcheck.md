## Issues

### 1. REQUIRED: the hook and cover frame $80B as a plain loss (lines 0 and 4, cover, post)
"Sixteen billion dollars in sales, over eighty billion in losses, one company" and the cover "Made $16B, Lost $80B" are numerically true.

The problem is the framing. About 90% of that loss ($72.4B) is a non-cash fair-value charge. Viewers will hear "lost $80B" and assume operating or cash losses. The script does not explain the non-cash nature until line 4, roughly 15 seconds in, after many viewers have already scrolled away. The cover and TikTok captions never explain it at all.

Because this story is about Anthropic, the company that makes the AI checking it, the framing has to be neutral both ways. It should be neither sensational nor promotional. The fix keeps the striking contrast and adds the qualifier up front.

There is also a rounding issue: "Lost $80B" rounds $80.7B *down*. Use "$80.7B", "over $80B" or "$80B+".

**Fixes (exact text):**
- **Line 0** text: `$16 billion in sales, over $80 billion lost, mostly on paper.`
- **Line 0** say: `Sixteen billion in sales, over eighty billion lost, mostly on paper.` This is 11 words, within the 12-word hook limit.
- **Line 0** scene.title: `$16B sales, [paper] loss` (22 characters without the brackets, the same convention as the current title). Keep value `$80B+` and label `ANT`.
