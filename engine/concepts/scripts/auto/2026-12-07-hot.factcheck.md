# Fact-check: 2026-12-07-hot ("Do Taxes Fund the Fed?")

Checked Oct 8, 2026 for posting Mon Dec 7, 2026. Line indexes are 0-based. federalreserve.gov and FRED blocked direct fetch here; figures verified from search excerpts of Fed releases and FRED, plus Bloomberg Law, Reuters/Kitco, Wolf Street, First Trust and ABA Banking Journal.

## Verified claims
- **L0 hook, "Your taxes don't pay for the Federal Reserve."** True. The System is self-financed. The Board's own costs are paid by assessments on the Reserve Banks under FRA sec. 10, which "shall not be construed to be Government funds or appropriated moneys", and the Board sits outside the federal budget (Budget Appendix; SF Fed Dr. Econ). The hook is 8 spoken words, specific and true. Fine.
- **L1, "Congress gives it no budget."** True: no congressional appropriations.
- **L2, interest on securities plus fees for services.** True. Income is mostly interest on Treasuries and MBS (2025: $106.6B + $48.0B). Priced services bring in roughly $0.5B a year. Calling these "bonds" is fair.
- **L4, 2025 earned about $155B and paid out about $167B.** Confirmed from the 2025 audited statements, released Mar 25, 2026 (Bloomberg Law, Wolf Street): interest income $155.3B, interest expense $167.4B. Of that, $147.7B was interest on reserve balances paid to banks (88%) and $19.7B went on overnight reverse repos. Net interest = -$12.1B. "Mostly to banks" is fair. Spoken and on-screen values match.
- **L5, deferred asset, paid from future profits, "Taxpayers didn't cover that."** Accurate. This is the Fed's own description: the deferred asset is the net earnings the Reserve Banks must realize before remittances resume.
- **L6, about $233B as of Sep 30, 2026 (H.4.1).** Consistent with the record. FRED RESPPLLOPNWW: -$233.5B on Aug 26, 2026; -$236.7B on Jun 17; Q2-end $235.5B (First Trust, Sep 3, 2026). I could not fetch the Oct 1 release directly. The brief's per-bank data nets to -$233,140M (gross deferred asset of the nine banks in deficit is $233,368M), so "about $233B" holds either way. The figure is explicitly dated, so it stays true on Dec 7. By then the live number will probably be a little lower, about $230B, which is acceptable. Sub "Sep 30, 2026 H.4.1" is 18 characters, OK.
- **L7, "most of the Fed sends the Treasury nothing."** Fair. Only 3 of 12 Reserve Banks have cleared their deferred asset and are remitting again (First Trust, Jun 2026; FRED district series show Atlanta, Dallas and St. Louis positive). "Missing checks, not a tax bill" is accurate and neutral: forgone remittances reduce Treasury revenue, but no one is billed.
- Math: 243.5 - 233.1 = 10.4B decline since end-2025, consistent with the Fed returning to small profits. No worked-example errors.
- Limits: the compare values ($155B, $167B) are 5 characters; split items are 12-14; titles are 12 or fewer; board lines are 23 or fewer. Total spoken words: 135. No keep:true flags.

## Issues and fixes

1. **L3 (risky number): "$107.4 billion in 2021" is the Fed's preliminary, unaudited figure (Jan 14, 2022).** The audited statements (Mar 21, 2022) report 2021 remittances of **$109.0B**, with earnings of $107.9B. The figure is defensible as the Board's announced transfer, but it conflicts with the audited number. Safer fix that stays true under either figure:
   - text: `After costs, it sends the leftover profit to the Treasury: over $100 billion in 2021.`
   - say: `After costs, it sends the leftover profit to the Treasury: more than one hundred billion dollars in twenty twenty-one.`
   - scene value: `$100B+` (6 chars), sub: `2021, Fed`
   - (Alternatively use audited `$109B`: say "about one hundred nine billion dollars".)
   - Posts: in ig, replace `$107.4 billion in 2021` with `over $100 billion in 2021`; in yt_desc, replace `sent $107.4 billion in profit to the Treasury in 2021` with `sent over $100 billion in profit to the Treasury in 2021`; in the ig Sources line, keep "2021 results".

2. **L4 (timeline wording): "Then rates jumped. In 2025..."** reads as if rates jumped in 2025, when the Fed was actually cutting. Rates jumped in 2022. Fix:
   - text: `Then rates jumped in 2022. In 2025 it earned about $155 billion in interest, but paid out about $167 billion.`
   - say: `Then rates jumped in twenty twenty-two. In twenty twenty-five it earned about one hundred fifty-five billion in interest, but paid out about one hundred sixty-seven billion.`
   - Word count with fixes 1 and 2: 135 - 1 + 3 = **137** (within 115-138).

3. **yt_desc (stale tense): "Since late 2022 its interest costs have topped its income"** is no longer true. The Fed has had positive net income since about Q4 2025 (First Trust: three straight positive quarters through Q2 2026), and the deferred asset is shrinking. Replace that sentence with:
   `From late 2022 its interest costs topped its income for years, and it booked those losses as a deferred asset, about $233 billion as of Sept 30, 2026 (Fed H.4.1), being repaid from future profits.`

4. Minor (no change required): post title "Not quite." hedges, since the video's answer is "no". It is acceptable because the video ends on the indirect cost (missing checks). The TikTok post and tags are fine. There is no advice or price prediction, and the "Not financial advice" disclaimers are present. Neutral: no position on Fed independence or interest on reserves.
