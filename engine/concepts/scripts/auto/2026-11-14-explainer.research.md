# Research brief: Why gold goes up when people get scared
Slot: 2026-11-14-explainer (posts Sat Nov 14, 2026). Researched Oct 2, 2026. Evergreen: every number carries its date/period. No price predictions, no buy/sell/hold.

## Timely angle (dated, not "this week")
- 2025 was gold's strongest year since 1979. Gold set 53 new all-time highs in 2025 (World Gold Council, Gold Demand Trends Full Year 2025, published Jan 29-30, 2026; reported by Upstox/Xinhua).
- 2025 gain: about 65% on the year (BullionVault, Dec 31, 2025); "biggest gain since 1979" (RTE Dec 29, 2025; CNN Dec 24, 2025; NBC News). The 1979 gain was 133.4% (BullionVault).
- 2025 annual average price: $3,431/oz, up 44% vs 2024 (WGC FY2025). (BullionVault/LBMA survey cites $3,435; we use WGC's $3,431.) Q4 2025 average $4,135/oz, +55% y/y (WGC).
- Prices in 2026 moved a lot (record above $5,500 earlier in 2026 per InvestingNews; about $4,346 on Sep 16, 2026 per Fortune). NOT used in script (volatile, would be stale; avoids anything resembling a prediction).

## Core mechanism (definitions)
1. Safe haven: gold is a physical asset no government or company issues, so it carries no default/credit risk; in crises people move money into assets seen as stores of value (WGC; SSGA "US Real Rates Still Matter for Gold", 2025).
2. Opportunity cost / real interest rates: gold pays no interest, coupon or dividend. Holding it means giving up what a safe interest-bearing asset would pay. Real rate = nominal rate minus inflation. When real rates fall, gold's zero yield looks less costly (WGC; SSGA 2025). Scary periods often bring rate cuts: the Fed cut three times in 2025 to 3.50%-3.75% (NPR Jan 4, 2026; Business Standard Jan 1, 2026).
3. Dollar: gold is priced in US dollars; a weaker dollar means gold costs more dollars and is cheaper for buyers using other currencies. WGC lists a weaker dollar as reducing gold's opportunity cost. US Dollar Index fell about 9.4% in 2025 (Barchart 9.37%; others 9.6%), its worst year since 2017 (Bloomberg Dec 31, 2025; NPR Jan 4, 2026; Business Standard Jan 1, 2026).
4. Central banks: bought 863 tonnes of gold in 2025 (WGC FY2025), down from the record 1,136 t in 2022 but far above the 2010-2021 average of 473 t a year. Top 2025 buyers: Poland 102 t, Kazakhstan 57 t, Brazil 43 t (WGC via Mining.com/FastBull).
- Total gold demand 2025: record 5,002 t, worth about $555B; ETF holdings +801 t; investment demand +84% (WGC FY2025).

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
- $10,000 in a savings account paying 4% a year earns 10,000 x 0.04 = $400 a year. The same $10,000 in gold earns $0 interest. That $400 is the opportunity cost.
- If the rate drops to 2%: 10,000 x 0.02 = $200. The cost of holding gold instead halves.
- Real rate example: 4% nominal - 3% inflation = 1% real.

## Surprising fact
- Gold pays nothing (zero interest, zero dividends), yet in 2025 its average price rose 44% and it set 53 record highs; central banks themselves bought 863 tonnes.
