# Research brief: How Marriott makes money without owning hotels
Slot: 2027-01-20-business (How They Make Money). Researched Nov 21, 2026. Evergreen: no timely news hook used; all numbers dated.
Fiscal year used: FY2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025), from Marriott's Form 10-K filed Feb 10, 2026 and its Q4/FY2025 earnings release of Feb 10, 2026.
Note: sec.gov and marriott.gcs-web.com could not be fetched directly from this sandbox (proxy refused); figures were taken from search-result excerpts of those official documents and cross-checked against outlets (Nasdaq/PR Newswire copy of the release, Hotel Business, HOTELS magazine, CoStar).

## Key facts (FY2025 unless stated)
- System size at Dec 31, 2025: 9,805 properties, 1,779,936 rooms (10-K). Year-end 2024: 9,361 properties, 1,706,331 rooms. Release: "more than 9,800 properties". Net rooms growth ~4.3% in 2025 (~73,600 net rooms).
- Rooms by type (10-K property table, Dec 31, 2025):
  - Franchised / licensed / other: 7,644 properties, 1,183,513 rooms (our math: ~66.5% of rooms)
  - Managed: 1,966 properties, 565,764 rooms (~31.8% of rooms)
  - Owned / leased: 51 properties, 14,406 rooms (~0.8% of rooms; ~0.5% of properties). 14 hotels (5,539 rooms) in US & Canada, 37 (8,867 rooms) international.
  - Category rows sum to ~9,661 properties / 1,763,683 rooms; the gap to the 9,805 total (~144 properties) is likely other categories (e.g. residences/timeshare lines) not captured in the snippet. UNCERTAIN: exact category definitions. Script uses only the 51 owned/leased, 14,406 owned/leased rooms, 9,805 total, and 1.18M franchised rooms.
- Gross fee revenues 2025: $5,438 million (+5%).
  - Franchise fees: $3,325M (2024: $3,113M). Includes co-branded credit card fees.
  - Base management fees: $1,322M.
  - Incentive management fees: $791M (+3%).
  - Co-branded credit card fees: $716M in 2025 (up over 8%); recognized within franchise fees. Residential branding fees $72M (-10%).
  - Net fee revenues: $5,303M (2024: $5,067M).
- Total revenues 2025 (as reported): $26,186M (2024: $25,100M).
- Cost reimbursement revenue 2025: $19,204M (2024: $18,482M), about 73% of total revenue. Reimbursed expenses: $19,503M, so cost reimbursements, net = -$299M. Meaning: hotel owners repay Marriott for costs it incurs on their behalf (mostly hotel-level staff/payroll at managed hotels, plus system-wide programs like Marriott Bonvoy loyalty, reservations, marketing). Revenue and matching expense; essentially pass-through with no profit by design.
- Adjusted total revenues excluding cost reimbursement revenue: $6,997M in 2025 (2024: $6,618M).

## Fee definitions (10-K)
- Franchise: initial application fee + continuing royalties that "typically range from four to seven percent of room revenues" for all brands, plus up to 4% of food and beverage revenues for certain full-service brands; agreements generally 10 to 25 years. Also fees for other IP uses, primarily co-branded credit cards, plus timeshare and residential branding.
- Base management fees: typically a percentage of property-level revenue (owner filings, e.g. Host Hotels FY2023 10-K, cite generally 2-3% of gross revenues for Marriott-managed hotels).
- Incentive management fees: typically a percentage of a hotel profitability measure.

## Worked example (our math, illustration)
A hypothetical franchised hotel with $10,000,000 in room revenue in a year, paying a 5% royalty (inside Marriott's typical 4%-7% range):
$10,000,000 x 0.05 = $500,000 a year to Marriott, before other program fees. The owner keeps the building, the debt and the staff costs.

## Surprise fact
Marriott owned or leased just 51 of its 9,805 properties at the end of 2025 (about half a percent), yet reported $26.19B of revenue, of which $19.2B was cost reimbursements that mostly pass straight through.

## Uncertain

- Base management fee rate (2-3%) comes from owner filings, not Marriott's own 10-K; not used in the script.
