# Research: How charity auctions and galas raise money (case: the Met Gala)

## Timely angle
- The 2026 Met Gala (May 4, 2026) raised a record $42 million for the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Costume Institute, up from the prior record of $31 million in 2025. (WWD, May 2026; Fortune, May 5, 2026; IBTimes)
- 2026 tickets: $100,000 each (up from $75,000 in 2025); a table of 10 starts at $350,000. (CBS News, "How much does a Met Gala ticket cost in 2026?"; Outlook Business)
- Lead sponsors 2026: Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sanchez Bezos (reported ~$10M; reported figure, not used in script). Event drew boycott calls/protests (political/controversy angle: NOT used).

## The surprising fact
- The Costume Institute is the only curatorial department at the Met required to raise its own operating funds; the gala is its main source of annual funding for exhibitions, publications, acquisitions and capital improvements. (Britannica "Met Gala"; AP via ABC News 2024; Forbes 2017)

## Tax rules (IRS Publication 1771, Rev. 3-2016)
- A donor may deduct only the amount paid in excess of the fair market value of goods or services received (applies to auction purchases and gala tickets).
- Charities must give a written disclosure statement for any quid pro quo payment over $75 (IRC 6115), stating the deduction is limited to the excess and giving a good-faith estimate of the value received.
- IRS example: $100 paid for a concert ticket worth $40 -> deduction may not exceed $60; disclosure required because payment > $75.

## How galas raise money (general model)
- Revenue from ticket and table sales, corporate/individual sponsors, and auctions (live/silent) of donated items; donated items cost the charity nothing, so the winning bid is nearly all revenue. (nonprofitaccountingbasics.org "Dos and Don'ts for Charity Auctions"; IRS Pub 1771)

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
- Assumptions: a donated weekend trip with fair market value $500 sells at a charity auction for $2,000.
- Winner's deductible amount = $2,000 - $500 = $1,500.
- Charity revenue: $2,000 (item was donated; ignore event costs).
- Payment > $75 -> charity must give written disclosure.
