# Fact-check: 2026-12-22-business (How Publix is owned by its employees)

Checked: Oct 23, 2026. Posts Tue Dec 22, 2026, 2 PM CT. Source read: 2026-12-22-business.raw.json only. The line numbers below are **0-based indexes into `lines`**.

## Verified
- **Who can buy:** The FY2025 10-K says stock "is made available for sale by the Company only to its current employees and members of its Board of Directors through the ESPP and Directors Plan and to participants of the 401(k) Plan". Employees also receive stock through the ESOP. The stock is not traded on an established securities market.
- **Price setting:** The 10-K says the board sets the price "since there is no trading market", and that "an independent valuation is obtained". The price changes quarterly: Mar 1, May 1, Aug 1 and Nov 1.
- **March 1 prices, split-adjusted (5-for-1 split, effective after the close on Apr 14, 2022):**
  - 2020: $48.90 / 5 = **$9.78**
  - 2021: $60.20 / 5 = **$12.04** ($60.20 confirmed; it rose to $61.30 on May 1, 2021)
  - 2022: $68.80 / 5 = **$13.76**
  - 2023: **$14.55**, 2024: **$15.20**, 2025: **$19.20**, 2026: **$19.65**
  - All seven chart points are correct. Note that Mar 1, 2026 was a *decrease* from $20.40. The chart shows only March points and is labeled that way, so it is not misleading.
- **ESOP share value:** $13.9B fair value of shares held by the ESOP as of Dec 27, 2025 (FY2025 10-K; $12.9B a year earlier). Correct.
- **Eligibility:** The Publix PROFIT Plan page says an associate becomes a participant after one year of employment with 1,000+ work credit hours. Each year's contribution also requires 1,000+ hours in that anniversary year, and non-vested associates must be employed on Dec 31. Contributions are made in stock at no cost to associates, but they are **discretionary and set by the board each year**.
- **Sell-back:** The 10-K says the company "currently repurchases common stock subject to certain terms and conditions", and distributed ESOP shares carry a put option ($815M of such shares at FY2025 year-end). "Generally sell back to Publix" is fair.
- **Worked example:** 100 × $13.76 = $1,376 and 100 × $19.65 = $1,965 (+42.8%). The math is correct.
- **Background, not in the script:** FY2025 sales of $62.7B and roughly 260,000 associates appear only in secondary sources. Neither number is used in the script, so this is not an issue.
