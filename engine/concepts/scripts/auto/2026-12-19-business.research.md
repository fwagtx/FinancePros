# Research brief: How Modelo became America's best-selling beer for Constellation Brands
Show: How They Make Money | posts Saturday, December 19, 2026 | written October 20, 2026

## The surprising fact
Constellation Brands sells Modelo in the US, but it does not own the Modelo company. Grupo Modelo belongs to Anheuser-Busch InBev (Bud Light's parent). Constellation holds perpetual US rights to the Mexican brands (Modelo, Corona, Pacifico, Victoria) because the US Justice Department made that a condition of AB InBev buying Grupo Modelo in 2013.

## The 2013 deal (Constellation 8-K, Feb 14 2013; Brewbound; Shanken News Daily)
- AB InBev agreed in 2012 to buy the rest of Grupo Modelo; the DOJ sued (Jan 2013) on antitrust grounds.
- Revised deal (Feb 2013): Constellation pays $4.75 billion in total = $1.85B for AB InBev/Modelo's remaining 50% of Crown Imports + $2.9B for the Piedras Negras brewery (Coahuila, Mexico) and perpetual US brand rights (subject to post-closing adjustment).
- $2.9B priced on assumed 2012 EBITDA of $310M (~9x).
- DOJ settlement April 2013 ("substantially in line" with the Feb proposal); court approval followed; deal closed June 2013.
- Piedras Negras then met ~60% of Crown's demand; Constellation planned expansion to supply 100% of US needs.

## Modelo becomes #1 (2023)
- After the April 2023 Bud Light boycott: Circana/IRI four weeks ending May 28, 2023: Modelo Especial store sales ~$333M (+15.6%) vs Bud Light ~$297M (-22.8%) (CNN/NBC reporting).
- Bump Williams Consulting / NielsenIQ: Modelo passed Bud Light in US retail dollar sales for the month ending June 3, 2023 (8.4% vs 7.3% share). Fox Business: Modelo surpassed Bud Light on a dollar-sales basis in June 2023 and held the lead since.
- Constellation beer chief: planned for Modelo to become No. 1 "over the next couple of years; it just happened quicker than we anticipated."

## Does it still hold the title? (careful: two metrics)
- Volume: Michelob Ultra (AB InBev) became the No. 1 US beer by volume per Circana, 52 weeks ending Sept 14, 2025 (Fox Business, VinePair, CNN).
- Dollars: VinePair/CNN (Sept 2025): Modelo still No. 1 in off-premise dollar sales; Modelo costs ~$7 more per case than Ultra (Circana).
- Constellation Q2 FY2027 results (quarter ended Aug 31, 2026; released Oct 6, 2026): Modelo Especial "maintained its position as the No. 1 U.S. beer brand by dollar sales" (company statement, reported by Pulse 2.0, Beancount, Panabee). Phrase as "No. 1 by dollar sales, per Constellation's October 2026 report"; do NOT call it No. 1 by volume.

## Constellation beer segment (latest fiscal year: FY2026, ended Feb 28, 2026; 8-K earnings release Apr 8, 2026 + FY2026 annual report/10-K)
- Beer net sales: $8,315.2M, down 3% (FY2025: $8,539.8M; FY2024: $8,162.6M).
- Shipments: 415.4M cases, -3.8%. Depletions: -2.1% (Modelo Especial ~-3%, Corona Extra ~-7%, Modelo Chelada ~-1%; Pacifico and Victoria grew).
- Beer operating margin: 38.0%, down 170 bp (FY2025: 39.7%), "driven by a decline in shipment volumes, as well as higher COGS due to aluminum tariffs and unfavorable fixed cost absorption, partially offset by favorable pricing."
- Q4 FY2026 beer margin 33.2% (vs 36.6%); depletions +0.6%.
- Tariff: management expected ~ $70M aluminum-tariff impact on beer in FY2026 (company commentary, via coverage).
- FY2027 guidance (April 2026): beer net sales -1% to +1%, beer operating margin 37%-38%.
- Q2 FY2027 (Aug 31, 2026 quarter): beer net sales $2,473.6M (+5%), shipments +5.5%, depletions -0.6%, Modelo Especial depletions ~-2%, beer margin 39.0% (vs 40.6%).
- Hispanic consumer softness: WEAKLY SOURCED as a company figure. Brewbound reports Constellation weathering slowing beer sales and changed spending among Hispanic consumers tied to immigration enforcement; management has referenced Hispanic consumer caution on calls. Not used in script.

## Worked example (exact math)
FY2026 beer operating margin 38.0%:
- $8,315.2M x 0.380 = ~$3,159.8M (~$3.16B) beer operating income (approximation from reported margin).
- Per $10 of beer net sales: $10 x 0.38 = $3.80 operating profit.
- Growth: $8.32B / $2.84B = ~2.9x since FY2014.
