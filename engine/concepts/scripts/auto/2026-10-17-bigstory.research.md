- Crude is the biggest input in gasoline and diesel; pump prices tend to follow crude with a lag of weeks (mechanism, not a forecast).
- Diesel moves trucking and shipping costs; jet fuel moves airfares; heating oil moves winter bills.
- Energy already drove much of September's inflation (covered Oct 14); higher oil keeps pressure on the Fed (Fed meets Oct 27-28).
