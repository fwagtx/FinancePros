## Issues

1. **STALENESS (main issue).** The College Board's next edition comes out every fall. The 2024 edition came out in October 2024 and the 2025 edition on November 6, 2025. The 2026 edition, with 2026-27 prices, will almost certainly be out before December 12, 2026. Several present-tense lines would then state outdated numbers as if they were current:
   - Line 0: "Private colleges list $45,000" / "pays" (present tense, no year).
   - Line 1: "aid cuts that" (the year is given, but the verb is present tense).
   - Line 3: "averages $13,900 that year".
   - post.title, tiktok, ig line 1 and yt_desc all use present tense with no year.
   The fix is to put all of them in the past tense and pin them to 2025-26 (see fixes below).
2. **Hook accuracy and labels.** "The average freshman" means first-time, full-time students at private nonprofits, so it is OK as long as "average" stays. "Private colleges list $45,000" implies every college lists $45,000. It is an average, so say "averaged".
3. **Net tuition vs total cost.** $16,910 is net **tuition and fees**, not the full cost. The total budget is $65,470 before aid. The compare label "Net price" and the cover title "Real Price" suggest the total cost, so relabel them as net tuition.
4. **Average vs median mix (line 5).** The script applies an average spending rate to a median endowment. Label the rate "average". The math is fine.
5. **Line 6 (minor, no change needed).** The script leaves out that schools with fewer than 3,000 tuition-paying students are now exempt. The line is accurate as written: "up to 8%" and "schools with the most endowment per student" are both correct. "From 2026 tax years" is correct.
6. **Hook strength.** The hook is true, specific and makes the viewer want the answer. It is about 3 seconds long. OK.
7. **Neutrality.** No advice, no price predictions and no political opinion. The tax law is described factually. OK.
8. **On-screen vs spoken.** All numbers match. OK.
9. **keep:true.** None used. OK.
10. **Renderer limits.** All pass, both before and after the fixes:
    - Compare values are 7 characters.
    - Stat label is 14 and sub is 18.
    - Compare subs are 15 and 16.
    - Headline is 32.
    - Board lines are 25 or less.
    - Chart end is 5.
    - Prop label is 4.
    - post.title is 57 and tiktok is 140.
    - Spoken total was 135 and is 137 after the fixes.
