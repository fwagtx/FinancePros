## Context / meaning for a normal person
- CPI is the score for whether pay keeps up with prices: if your raise is below the CPI rate, your real pay shrank.
- CPI feeds into Fed decisions (next meeting Dec 9), which flow to card APRs, loans, savings rates.
- Wholesale prices (PPI) are what businesses pay; they can show cost pressure before it reaches stores (general relationship, not a forecast).
