# 2026-12-03-hot research brief: Money myth: "stablecoins can never lose their peg"
Researched Sunday, October 4, 2026. Posts Thursday, December 3, 2026. Evergreen; every figure dated.

## Core facts
1. USDC depeg, March 2023
   - Fri night Mar 10, 2023: Circle disclosed $3.3 billion of USDC reserves were held at Silicon Valley Bank (closed by California regulators Mar 10). About 8% of roughly $40 billion in reserves. (CNBC Mar 11, 2023; Decrypt; Axios)
   - USDC fell to about 87 cents on Saturday Mar 11, 2023 (CNBC: "below 87 cents"; CoinDesk data: low $0.8726; Decrypt headline "falls to 87 cents").
   - Recovered to about 99 cents on Monday Mar 13, 2023 after regulators said SVB deposits would be available (CNBC Mar 13, 2023; CoinDesk Mar 13, 2023 "regains dollar peg").
2. TerraUSD (UST), May 2022
   - Algorithmic stablecoin: peg relied on an arbitrage mechanism with sister token LUNA, not 1:1 cash reserves.
   - Market cap above $18.5 billion before the crash (Reuters/Thomson Reuters Practical Law summary); first broke peg ~May 9, 2022; CNBC May 11, 2022: "below 30 cents"; traded at about 11 cents on May 16, 2022 (Reuters via Practical Law). Wikipedia: "plunged to 10 cents"; Terra/LUNA collapse wiped out ~$45B in a week.
   - Never recovered.
3. Not FDIC insured
   - FDIC fact sheet (Jul 28, 2022): FDIC deposit insurance does not cover crypto assets and does not protect against failure of non-bank companies (exchanges, custodians, wallet providers). FTC consumer alert Oct 2023 same. Commentary on 2026 FDIC GENIUS proposal: no pass-through deposit insurance to stablecoin holders.
   - Standard FDIC coverage on bank deposits: $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category.
4. GENIUS Act (Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act)
   - Signed July 18, 2025.
   - Payment stablecoin issuers must hold reserves at least 1:1, in assets such as US dollars/coins, insured deposits, Treasury bills/notes/bonds with remaining maturity of 93 days or less, certain repos and government money market funds; monthly public reserve disclosure. (House Financial Services section-by-section; Paul Hastings; Georgetown ILJ)
   - Effective date: the earlier of 18 months after enactment (January 18, 2027) or 120 days after primary regulators issue final implementing regulations. (Astraea law; Morgan Lewis Apr 2026)
   - Implementation status as of early Oct 2026: OCC proposed rules (Feb 2026), FDIC and Treasury proposals (2026), Federal Reserve proposed reserve/application rules (late Sept 2026, ABA Banking Journal). Treasury published an interim final rule on Sept 30, 2026 on the certification review/state-regime procedures (reported by Yahoo Finance; verify). Agencies missed the July 18, 2026 one-year rulemaking deadline. Safe statement for the video: "takes effect by January 18, 2027 at the latest" (true because no full final rules issued by Sept 20, 2026).
5. Market size (DefiLlama)
   - Total USD-pegged stablecoins (DefiLlama stablecoincharts/all API, peggedUSD): about $312.0 billion on Oct 4, 2026. DefiLlama homepage showed total stablecoin market cap $306.9 billion on Oct 4, 2026 (slightly different aggregation). Press: $302.8B-$314B in Sept 2026; record above $321B earlier in 2026. Safe spoken: "over three hundred billion dollars as of October 2026".

## Worked example
$1,000 of USDC at the Mar 11, 2023 low (~$0.87): 1,000 x 0.87 = $870 (with $0.8726 tick: $872.60). Paper drop about $130.
$1,000 of UST at ~$0.11 on May 16, 2022: 1,000 x 0.11 = $110, a $890 loss.

## Surprising fact
Even the fully reserved, heavily audited USDC lost 13 cents on the dollar in a weekend, because 8% of its cash sat in one failed bank.
