## Core facts (The Coca-Cola Company, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended Dec 31, 2025, filed Feb 20, 2026; Q4/FY2025 earnings release Feb 10, 2026)
- Two lines of business: **concentrate operations** (sells beverage concentrates, syrups and certain finished beverages to authorized bottling operations, which combine concentrate with water and sweeteners, then bottle and sell) and **finished product operations** (company-owned/consolidated bottling and distribution).
- 10-K: finished product operations generate **higher net operating revenues but lower gross profit margins** than concentrate operations.
- FY2025 net operating revenues: **$47,941 million** (FY2024: $47,061 million), up 2%.
- FY2025 concentrate operations revenues: **$28,462 million** = **59%** of net operating revenues; finished product operations **41%** (same 59/41 in FY2024).
- FY2025 unit case volume: concentrate operations **85%**, finished product operations **15%** (unchanged vs 2024). System sold 33.8 billion unit cases in 2025.
- FY2025 operating margin (GAAP): **28.7%** vs 21.2% in FY2024; comparable (non-GAAP) 31.2% vs 30.0%.
- FY2025 gross margin: **61.6%** vs 61.1% in FY2024; increase partly due to prior-year refranchising of bottling operations in the Philippines, Bangladesh and certain territories in India.
- Five bottling partners combined = 44% of worldwide unit case volume in 2025. Products sold in 200+ countries and territories; 2.2 billion servings per day.

## Comparison: a bottler (Coca-Cola Consolidated, Inc., largest Coca-Cola bottler in the US; FY2025 results, Feb 18, 2026)
- Net sales $7.23 billion (+4.8%); income from operations $950.7 million.
- Operating margin ~ 950.7 / 7,230 = 13.1% (about 13%; computed from rounded net sales, show as "~13%").

## Worked example (exact math)
- Concentrate share of FY2025 revenue: $28,462M / $47,941M = 0.5937 = 59.4% (10-K rounds to 59%).
- Finished product revenue FY2025: $47,941M - $28,462M = $19,479M.
- Implied FY2025 operating income: $47,941M x 28.7% = about $13,759M (~$13.8B).
- Profit per dollar of sales, FY2025: Coca-Cola ~28.7 cents vs Coca-Cola Consolidated ~13.1 cents ($950.7M / $7.23B).

## The surprising fact
- 85% of Coca-Cola's 2025 unit case volume came through concentrate operations: independent/partner bottlers, not Coca-Cola, make, bottle and deliver most of the drinks. Coca-Cola mainly sells the syrup and the brand.
