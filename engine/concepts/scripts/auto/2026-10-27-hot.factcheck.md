## Issues and fixes

1. **Hook and cover have no end month, and will be stale (L1, cover, post.title, ig, tiktok). Must fix.** "Up 55% since January 2020" implies "through today". By Oct 27, BLS will have released September CPI (mid-October), so the figure must be labeled as August 2026.
   - Fix text: "Car repair prices are up 55% from January 2020 to August 2026."
   - Fix say: "Car repair costs fifty-five percent more than in January twenty twenty, as of August."
   - Change cover and hook scene `label` from "Jan 2020" to "Jan 2020 to Aug 2026" (20 chars).
   - Apply the same "to Aug 2026" wording to post.title, ig line 1 and tiktok. yt_desc is already correct.
   - Hook strength: true and specific, but the spoken line runs about 4 seconds. A shorter option: "Car repairs cost 55% more than in 2020," with the month range shown on screen.
2. **L4: unverified attribution. Must fix.** Replace "CCC says post-repair scans and calibrations are now routine" with "CCC says calibrations showed up on 28% of repair estimates in 2025, up from 22%." Optionally change the list item to "Calibrations on 28% of repairs" (30 chars, so shorten to "Calibrations: 28% of jobs").
3. **L5: the wording overstates the claim.** "Each collision fix took 26.7 labor hours" reads as every repair. Use "The average collision repair took 26.7 labor hours in 2025, up from 23.0 in 2012." Labor hours actually fell 0.8 hours in 2025, from a 2023 peak of 27.6. Saying "near a record" or "down from a 2023 peak" would avoid implying they are still rising.
4. **L2 say is awkward:** "car insurance fell five point one" drops "percent". Fix: "...fell five point one percent."
5. **L6 say:** "a hundred twenty to a hundred fifty-nine an hour" should be "...a hundred fifty-nine dollars an hour". The spoken line also drops "in fall twenty twenty-four", which is on screen. The pay-versus-billed-rate "yet" contrast is fair, but shop rates cover overhead and not just wages, so consider "...bill customers..." and avoid implying markup.
6. **L7 say:** the hypothetical framing is dropped. Fix: "If a one hundred dollar repair in January twenty twenty rose with the index, it would cost about one hundred fifty-five dollars by August twenty twenty-six."
7. **Index provenance (internal):** confirm that both 300.112 and 465.136 come from NSA series CUUR0000SETD. The result does not change at rounding.

No advice, price predictions, political opinion or instruction-style takeaways. The cover is paid off. The CCC figures are attributed to CCC and not generalized beyond collision repair.

VERDICT: FAIL
