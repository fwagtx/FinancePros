## Social Security (SSA, announced October 14, 2026)
- 2027 COLA: 3.6%, based on CPI-W Q3 2025 to Q3 2026; applies to December 2026 benefits payable in January 2027. (SSA COLA fact sheet 2027; SSA news release; Al Jazeera Oct 14, 2026; Kiplinger; ASPPA "biggest in 5 years")
- Prior COLA (paid starting January 2026): 2.8%. Biggest since the 8.7% COLA paid in 2023.
- Average retired worker: about $75 more per month (outlets differ: $2,095 -> $2,170 per NewsNation/Newsweek; $2,088 -> $2,163 per ASPPA). Not used on screen because of discrepancy.
- 2027 taxable maximum (OASDI wage base): $192,300, up from $184,500 in 2026 (+$7,800). (SSA 2027 fact sheet)
- COLA history by year first paid: 2020 1.6, 2021 1.3, 2022 5.9, 2023 8.7, 2024 3.2, 2025 2.5, 2026 2.8, 2027 3.6 (SSA colaseries; CPA Practice Advisor).

## Worked example (our math)
- Hypothetical $2,000 monthly Social Security check x 3.6% = $72.00 more per month -> $2,072 a month; x 12 = $864 more a year.
- Washington full-time minimum-wage worker: ($17.73 - $17.13) = $0.60/hr x 2,080 hrs = $1,248 more per year (gross). (backup example)

## Minimum wages effective January 1, 2027
- Washington: $17.73/hr, up from $17.13 (+3.5%); L&I news release 26-17, Sept 30, 2026; Washington State Standard / OPB call it the nation-high statewide rate.
- California: $17.40/hr, up from $16.90; DIR news release 2026-66, Aug 13, 2026 (Dept. of Finance certified July 31, 2026).
- Michigan: $15.00/hr, up from $13.73; set in statute MCL 408.934; Michigan LEO wage & hour page.
- State count: trackers disagree (13-14 states with increases fixed by law or announced; some pending). EPI's 2027 list not found. -> Script avoids an exact count ("several states").
- Federal minimum wage: $7.25/hr, unchanged since July 24, 2009 (US DOL; NerdWallet). Longest stretch without a raise since the FLSA (1938).

## Surprising fact
- The federal minimum wage has been $7.25 since 2009, while Washington's will be $17.73 in 2027, more than double.
