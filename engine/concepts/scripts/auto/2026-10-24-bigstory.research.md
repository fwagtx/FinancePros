PICK: #1 United Airlines fuel costs and fares. Distinct from the Oct 17 "oil above $100" Big Story: this is about one airline's results and who pays (fares).

## Facts (United Airlines Holdings Q3 2026 = July-Sept 2026; results Tuesday Oct 20, 2026)
- Jet fuel averaged $3.90 per gallon in Q3 2026, up 60.3% from $2.43 a year earlier (United earnings release via SEC 8-K exhibit; CNBC; Startup Fortune).
- Total fuel expense $4.84 billion, up close to 62% (CNBC; Startup Fortune 61.6%).
- CEO Scott Kirby: United is passing along about 72% of the oil price surge to consumers, and he wants that closer to 100% (CNBC). Reuters (via KFGO): United "recovered only 72% of its additional fuel costs through revenue gains in the third quarter."
- In July, United expected to recover about 80%-90% of the increase in Q3 and 100% by Q4 (Reuters/KFGO).
- Revenue rose 17.4% to $17.9 billion (CNBC; beancount "record $17.9B revenue"; Live and Let's Fly "record revenue").
- Net income $1.03B ($3.14/share), up 8.5%; adjusted EPS $2.54 vs $2.78 a year earlier (CNBC).
- Cut full-year 2026 profit outlook: July guide $9-$11/share adjusted; new outlook implies at most $7.22 at the high end (CNBC).
- United expects Q4 2026 fuel to average $4.80 per gallon (CNBC; Reuters/KFGO). CFO expects a similar recovery proportion in Q4.
- Passengers up more than 2% y/y (one summary; not used).
- Fuel bill as share of revenue: 4.84 / 17.9 = about 27% (our math; not used on screen).

## Chartable data
Per-gallon fuel cost: Q3 2025 $2.43 -> Q3 2026 $3.90 (company). Q4 2026 $4.80 is company expectation, not data. Use compare, not chart.

## Meaning for a normal person
- Airline fuel costs flow into ticket prices; United says it has passed through about 72% so far and wants more. CNBC (Oct 23): airfares are up as airlines trim capacity.

## What to watch
- Q4 fuel prices; other airlines' reports (American Airlines etc.).
