## Facts (Tesla Q3 2026 = July-September 2026, reported Wednesday Oct 21, 2026)
- Total revenue: $28.05 billion, roughly flat year over year (Electrek; DriveTeslaCanada; minichart). CNBC consensus $27.9B; Electrek consensus ~$28.06B.
- GAAP net income: $787 million, down 43% from $1.37 billion a year earlier (Q3 2025) (DriveTeslaCanada; Motorbiscuit; technology.org; minichart; KuCoin headline "Profit Falls 43%"). 787/1373 = -42.7%.
- GAAP EPS $0.22 vs $0.39 a year ago; non-GAAP (adjusted) EPS $0.48 vs $0.50 a year ago; beat consensus ($0.43 per CNBC, $0.45 per Electrek).
- R&D: $2.76 billion, up 69% from $1.63 billion (minichart, technology.org, Electrek-based summaries).
- Operating expenses $4.78B, up 39% from $3.43B. Tesla attributed higher costs to AI projects, stock-based compensation including Elon Musk's 2025 award, and higher administrative costs.
- Operating income $718 million, down 56% from $1.62 billion; operating margin 2.6% vs 5.8%.
- Gross margin 19.6% vs 18.0% a year ago (Electrek "margins rebound").
- Segments (CNBC): automotive revenue $20.56B, -3%; services and other $4.61B, +33%; energy $2.9B, -16%.
- Free cash flow $1.5B, down 62%; capex $4.7B.
- Deliveries Q3 2026: 486,532, down 2.1% y/y (reported Oct 2).
- Net income included a $288M unrealized bitcoin gain partly offset by a $142M unrealized loss on Tesla's SpaceX stake.
- Cybercab robotaxi rides began in Austin on Sept 3; exec Lars Moravy on the call: "We're trying to get Cybercabs to customers by the end of this year, 2026."
- After-hours stock move: sources conflict (CNBC: down >3%; others: up) -> do not use.
