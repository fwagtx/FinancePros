**PICK: #1, diesel prices and the weekend Russia moves.** Angle: diesel (not gasoline) at about $6.28, 71% above a year ago. The deal, the export-ban easing and the surprise ceasefire all target that price, and experts doubt it will move much.

## Facts (with dates and sources)

- **Sun Oct 11, 2026**: Trump posted on Truth Social that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an "energy ceasefire," effective immediately. He gave no details. (CNN, Oct 11; AFP/France 24, Oct 11; CBS News; Ukrainska Pravda; Kyiv Post)
  - Zelensky told Axios the announcement was "news for me." He said Kyiv was awaiting details and would accept an energy ceasefire if Russia did. (France 24/AFP, CNN)
  - Russia did not immediately confirm. CNN asked the White House and Kremlin for details. (CNN, France 24 headline: "Zelensky surprised, Moscow mum")
  - Context: Trump had blamed Ukraine's strikes on Russian refineries for soaring diesel prices. (CNN/CBS via search)
- **Fri Oct 9, 2026**: After a call with Putin, Trump said Russia will supply more than 300,000 tons of diesel immediately, 500,000 tons in November and 1 million tons "immediately thereafter," plus another 3 million tons later. Outlets differ slightly on the later installments. (CNBC Oct 9, France 24 Oct 9, Fortune Oct 10, Bloomberg Oct 9)
  - Treasury temporarily waived sanctions on Russian diesel under a general license through April 2027. (CNBC; The National says until April 2027; NBC says through April 7)
- **Sat Oct 10, 2026**: Russia partially lifted its diesel export ban, allowing about 500,000 tons to be sold abroad, effective immediately, per TASS. (Kyiv Independent) Deputy PM Alexander Novak told TASS that Russia would begin lifting the ban immediately, and that the move would not affect Russian consumers. (NBC) Other bans remain: gasoline exports through January and jet fuel through November. (Kyiv Independent)
- **Prices (AAA)**: The national average diesel price was about $6.277/gal ($6.28) on Oct 9-10, down from a record of $6.52 on Sept 22 (Fortune gives $6.528), and **71% above year-ago levels**. (Fortune Oct 10 citing AAA; CNBC Oct 9 gives $6.28 and the $6.52 record)
- **Prices (EIA weekly, US on-highway diesel, $/gal)**: The record is **$6.529 for the week of Sept 21, 2026**, which beat the prior nominal weekly high of $5.810 from June 2022. The latest reading is **$6.199 for the week of Oct 5, 2026**, up $2.488 from a year earlier (about +67%). The next release is Oct 14. (EIA Gasoline and Diesel Fuel Update; Oilprice; IndexBox)
- **Expert view**: "It's kind of shuffling deck chairs on the Titanic," said Michael Lynch of the Energy Policy Research Foundation (Fortune, Oct 10). He argued that Russian diesel would just leave its existing customers looking elsewhere, "and that will keep the price basically where it is now." Clayton Seigle (CSIS) said the price effect would probably be small.
- **Background**: the eight-month US war with Iran is straining diesel (Fortune). Refining capacity has been damaged by wars in Eastern Europe and the Middle East (CBC/AP).

## What it means for a normal person

Diesel moves trucks, trains and farm equipment, so its price feeds into shipping and food costs, not only into what truckers pay at the pump. The weekend moves are aimed at supply, but experts quoted by Fortune expect little price relief, because Russian barrels would shift away from other buyers rather than add much new supply.

## What to watch next

- Kremlin confirmation of the energy ceasefire, or the lack of one.
- The next EIA diesel price release on Oct 14.
- When the first Russian cargoes arrive.
