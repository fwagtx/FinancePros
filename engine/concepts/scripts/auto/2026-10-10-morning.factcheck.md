# Fact-check: 2026-10-10-morning (Moderna back in the Nasdaq-100)

## Verified claims
- **Rejoined the Nasdaq-100 on Oct 9, replacing WBD (lines 1, 5).** Confirmed. On Oct 1, 2026, Nasdaq said MRNA would replace Warner Bros. Discovery before the market opened on Fri Oct 9, 2026 (Nasdaq IR, StreetInsider/GlobeNewswire, Newsquawk). WBD left because Paramount Skydance's purchase of WBD closed on Oct 6, 2026. WBD stopped trading on Nasdaq, and the combined company now trades as Skydance (SKYD) on the NYSE (Paramount Skydance 8-K, Shacknews, Outlook India). Saying it left "after merging with Paramount" is acceptable shorthand.
- **Oct 9 close of $225.00, up 14.21% (lines 1, 0 cover).** Confirmed by StockAnalysis and the Benzinga quote: $225.00, +$28.00 (+14.21%) from $197.00. Check: 197 × 1.1421 = 224.99. Intraday reports ranged from +5% to +11%, which is consistent with a close-weighted surge.
- **Ended 2025 near $30, up more than 600% in 2026 (line 2).** Supported. The 2025 year-end close is about $29.49 (Digrin), the Dec 30 close was $30.40 (Motley Fool AU), and the Dec 18 price was $31.75. Measured to $225, the gain is +663% from $29.49, +640% from $30.40 and +609% from $31.75. "More than 600%" holds under every plausible base, and press coverage says the stock "more than sextupled."
- **Removed in the Dec 2024 annual reconstitution (lines 3, 6).** Confirmed. Nasdaq announced it on Dec 13, 2024, effective before the open on Dec 23, 2024. MRNA, ILMN and SMCI left, and PLTR, MSTR and AXON joined.
- **August late-stage trial win (line 4).** Confirmed. On about Aug 19, 2026, Merck and Moderna said the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial of intismeran autogene plus Keytruda met its primary endpoint, recurrence-free survival, at an interim analysis. It also met a key secondary endpoint, distant metastasis-free survival (CancerNetwork, CURE, American Pharmaceutical Review). "Hit its main goal in a late-stage trial" is accurate. "The stock took off" is fair: shares rose about 177% around the readout, then fell 18% the next day, and the stock is still far higher.
- **Index-rule explanation (lines 6, 7).** Accurate. The Nasdaq-100 holds the 100 largest non-financial companies listed on Nasdaq, by market cap. It is rebuilt each December, and a member that is removed between rebuilds (here, because of a merger) gets a replacement. The line says "Nasdaq-100 funds," which is correct because broader funds such as Nasdaq Composite or total-market funds held MRNA all along. There is no buy/sell advice and no price prediction. The trade-offs are framed neutrally.

Index funds normally buy at the close before the effective date (Oct 8), not on Oct 9. The day's coverage gives mixed drivers: a biotech rally (24/7 Wall St: "Novavax Soars 16%, Moderna Surges 11%... as Biotech Rallies") and anticipation of the ESMO melanoma data (Presidential Symposium, Oct 24). Separate the two facts.
