## Issues
1. **Lines 1–2 (and the post's `ig`, `tiktok` and `yt_desc` fields): the attribution says "the SBA".** The source is the SBA Office of Inspector General, the agency's watchdog. Fix: say "The SBA's watchdog says: suspect fraud." In line 2, write "The SBA inspector general's warning: …".
2. **Line 2: the quote is not word-for-word.** The line says "The SBA's wording" / "That's the SBA's own wording", but the text that follows is a paraphrase. The source says "promising to get approval of an SBA loan but requires any payment up front … suspect fraud." Fix option 1: quote it exactly. On screen: "If someone promises to get approval of an SBA loan but requires any payment up front, 'suspect fraud.'" Fix option 2: drop "wording" and say "The SBA inspector general's warning: someone promises SBA loan approval but wants payment up front? Suspect fraud."
3. **Line 7 (minor): the narration leaves out that the judgment is partly suspended.** A viewer may take $48.3M as the amount actually collected. Fix: say "a forty-eight point three million dollar judgment, mostly suspended" if the order supports "mostly". If not, say "partly suspended", and add "(partly suspended)" to the news card. The post already says this.
4. **Line 8 (minor): the on-screen text puts 'business loans' in quotes.** The FTC alleged Seek advertised business loans and lines of credit, so the quote marks read as editorializing. Fix: on screen, "The FTC alleged it pitched business funding but opened personal credit cards, charging 10% of the limits."
5. **Timeliness (minor):** The Seek Capital news is about 11 months old on the posting date. It carries an explicit date ("November 2025"), so nothing will read wrong. The script contains no relative time words ("this week", "recently").
6. **Hook:** It is strong, specific and short (under 3 seconds). Only the attribution fix in issue 1 is needed.

VERDICT: FAIL
