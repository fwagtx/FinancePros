# Research brief: How SoFi went from student loans to being a bank
Show: How They Make Money. Posts Friday, January 1, 2027 (evergreen; every number dated). Researched November 2, 2026.

## Timely angle
SoFi reported Q3 2026 results on October 27, 2026 (8-K earnings release). By posting date this is "the latest quarter"; always say "Q3 2026" / "as of September 30, 2026", never "just reported".

## Origin (student loans)
- Founded August/fall 2011 by four Stanford Graduate School of Business students (Mike Cagney, Dan Macklin, James Finnigan, Ian Brady). (Wikipedia; SoFi "Our Story")
- First program: Stanford-only pilot, about 40 alumni lent about $2 million to about 100 students (~$20,000 each). Alumni-funded, school-specific student loan funds. (Wikipedia; Seeking Alpha)
- 2012: launched student loan refinancing; SoFi says it was the first company to refinance both federal and private student loans. (sofi.com/our-story; InvestmentNews) -- not used in script.
- Later expanded to personal loans, mortgages, and moved away from the alumni-funded model. (Wikipedia)

## Becoming a bank
- March 8-9, 2021: agreed to buy Golden Pacific Bancorp (Sacramento-area community bank: branches in Sacramento, Yuba City, Live Oak; about $150 million in assets) for $2.55/share in cash, about $22.3 million total. (SoFi press release March 2021; Janney deal announcement; PYMNTS)
- January 18, 2022: Federal Reserve approved bank holding company application; OCC gave conditional approval for the bank merger. (SoFi FY2022 10-K; SoFi/BusinessWire release Jan 18, 2022)
- February 2, 2022: acquisition closed; cash price $22.3 million from cash on hand; Golden Pacific Bank began operating as SoFi Bank, National Association. OCC condition: at least $750 million initial paid-in capital. (SoFi FY2022 10-K)

## Why a charter matters (funding)
- Q3 2026: "The average rate paid on deposits in the third quarter was 145 basis points lower than that paid on warehouse facilities, which translates to approximately $745.0 million of annualized interest expense savings." (SoFi Q3 2026 earnings release, Oct 27, 2026, 8-K)
- Q3 2026 net interest margin 6.14%. (same)
- Q4 2022: 88% of $7.3B deposits from direct-deposit members; "we are benefiting from a lower cost of funding for our loans." (SoFi Q4 2022 release)
- Commentary (secondary): SoFi exited Q4 2025 with no balance drawn on warehouse facilities.

## Worked example (our math)
Deposit rate 1.45 percentage points (145 bps) below warehouse funding in Q3 2026.
$10,000 lent x 1.45% = $145 a year less interest cost on that loan's funding.
Scale check: SoFi's own figure is ~$745.0M annualized savings.
Also: NII share of GAAP net revenue in Q3 2026 = 929.5 / 1,385.1 = 67.1% (our math; not used on screen).
Deposit growth 2022->2025: 37.5 / 7.3 = 5.1x (our math).

## Surprising fact
SoFi became a nationally chartered bank by buying a three-branch, ~$150M-asset California community bank for about $22.3 million (closed Feb 2, 2022). Instead of years on a new charter application, it bought one.

## Uncertain / caveats
- "Bought a bank for $22.3M": price for Golden Pacific Bancorp (holding company); SoFi also had to put in $750M+ of capital.
- 2022 year-end total deposits: company says $7.3B; 10-K table shows interest-bearing $7.27B.
- Fee-based revenue is a non-GAAP measure.
- 2011 pilot amounts ($2M, 40 alumni, ~100 students) from secondary sources.
