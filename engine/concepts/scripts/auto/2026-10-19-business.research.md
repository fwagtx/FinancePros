# 2026-10-19-business research brief: How eye doctors make money on the exam and the glasses

Researched September 30 / October 1, 2026. Posting date: Monday, October 19, 2026. No hard news angle in the week; evergreen "How They Make Money" angle built on National Vision's FY2025 Form 10-K.
Note: sec.gov, company IR sites and most news sites are blocked for direct fetch in this environment; numbers below were confirmed through search results quoting the filings/releases (cross-checked across searches where noted).

## Core company: National Vision Holdings (owns America's Best and Eyeglass World)
- Fiscal 2025 = 53 weeks ended January 3, 2026 (Form 10-K filed 2026; SEC accession 0001628280-26-014379).
- FY2025 net revenue: $1,987.5 million, up 9.0% (Q4/FY2025 earnings release, March 4, 2026; confirmed in several search results). Growth driven by comparable store sales, new stores and the 53rd week.
- FY2025 revenue disaggregation (10-K, in thousands; returned identically by two separate searches):
  - Eyeglasses and sunglasses: $1,269,126 (FY2024: $1,145,725)
  - Contact lenses: $324,196 (FY2024: $307,314)
  - Eye exams: $254,699 (FY2024: $233,621)
- FY2024 total net revenue: $1,823,320 thousand (FY2024 10-K).
- America's Best signature offer: two pairs of glasses plus a free eye exam (price varies by lens type and state; not used on screen because exact current price is inconsistently reported).
- Optometrist structure (FY2024 10-K, as of December 28, 2024): 2,393 optometrists in its network; 636 directly employed by National Vision; 1,478 employed by professional corporations or similar entities it contracts with; 279 sublease space in stores. The 10-K flags the risk that state "corporate practice of optometry/medicine" laws could force it to restructure these arrangements. (Latest optometrist count found; FY2025 10-K figure not confirmed.)

## Worked example (exact math, FY2025 10-K)
- Glasses per exam dollar: $1,269.1M / $254.7M = 4.98, about 5 to 1.
- Eye exams' share of revenue: $254.7M / $1,987.5M = 12.8%.
- Eyeglasses + contacts share: ($1,269.1M + $324.2M) / $1,987.5M = 80.2%.

## The surprising fact
The exam is the small line: at National Vision, eye exams were 12.8% of FY2025 revenue, while eyeglasses and sunglasses were about five times larger. And most of its optometrists (1,478 of 2,393 as of Dec 2024) were employed by separate professional corporations, not the company itself.

## Rule that shapes the business: FTC Eyeglass Rule
- Long-standing requirement: eye doctors must give patients a free copy of their prescription immediately after a refractive eye exam, before offering to sell eyewear. Updated rule announced by the FTC in June 2024 (published in the Federal Register July 26, 2024): prescribers with a financial interest in selling eyewear must ask patients to sign a confirmation they received the prescription and keep it 3 years. (FTC press release June 2024; FTC business blog; Federal Register 89 FR, July 26, 2024.)

## Medicare context
- Original Medicare does not cover routine eye exams (refractions) for glasses/contacts, or eyeglasses, except one pair after cataract surgery with an intraocular lens (medicare.gov "Eye exams (routine)").
