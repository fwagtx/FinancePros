## Issues

1. **Line 0 (hook): the timing is misleading on Jan 2, 2027.** "Checks got a 3.6% raise this January" is past tense, but most beneficiaries will not have a January payment by Jan 2. Their January payments arrive Wed Jan 13, 20 and 27. Only SSI recipients, and people paid on the 3rd, got the new amount early. Jan 3 is a Sunday, so their payment came Thu Dec 31, 2026. "This January" also adds nothing when the video posts in January. The hook is specific, but it could hit harder.
   - text: `Your Social Security check grows 3.6% this month.`
   - say: `Your Social Security check grows three point six percent this month.` (11 words)
   - The scene value +3.6% stays.

2. **Line 1: wording.** "Last time" is vague, and "October fourteenth, twenty twenty-six" is long to say. By posting day it is simply "last October."
   - text: `SSA announced it last October, up from 2.8% for 2026.`
   - say: `The Social Security Administration announced it last October, up from two point eight percent for twenty twenty-six.`

3. **Line 2: awkward spoken figure.** "becomes two thousand seventy-two" drops the unit. The math is correct. One optional caveat: the $2,072 is the gross benefit, before any Medicare Part B deduction.
   - text: unchanged
   - say: `Our math: a two thousand dollar monthly check becomes two thousand seventy-two dollars.`

4. **Line 4: ambiguous spoken amount.** "seventeen seventy-three" can sound like a year.
   - say: `Minimum wages rose too. Washington's is seventeen seventy-three an hour in twenty twenty-seven, the nation's highest state rate.`

5. **Line 5: wrong tense on the posting date.** "Goes to" describes a future change, but on Jan 2 it has already happened.
   - text: `California rose to $17.40, and Michigan to $15.00.`
   - say: `California rose to seventeen forty, and Michigan to fifteen dollars.`

6. **Line 7: "resets" is loose.** The IRS adjusts these figures for inflation each year. 401(k) limits move in $500 steps and some years do not change. Also see the note above: the 2027 IRS figures were not yet published on Nov 3.
   - text: `Tax brackets and 401(k) limits are tied to inflation too, so the IRS adjusts them yearly.`
   - say: `Tax brackets and four oh one K limits are tied to inflation too, so the I R S adjusts them each year.`
   - The board lines stay.

7. **Line 8: time word.** "In January" is acceptable, but "this month" reads better on Jan 2. This is generic budgeting, not financial advice.
   - text: `So this month, check your paycheck and your budget against the new numbers.`
   - say: `So this month, check your paycheck and your budget against the new numbers.`

8. **Post (ig/yt), minor.** "Social Security checks got a 3.6% raise starting January 2027" is acceptable. "benefits rose 3.6% for 2027" is the most precise wording. No change required.
