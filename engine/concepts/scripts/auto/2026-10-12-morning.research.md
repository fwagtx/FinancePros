## Facts (with dates)

- **Sat Oct 10, 2026**: Missile strike on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport. Saudi authorities: 12 killed, 300+ wounded (309 per civil aviation). An injured Indian national later died, raising the toll to 13 (Indian Embassy, per reports). Houthis (Yemen) claimed responsibility. Described as the deadliest attack on the kingdom in more than a decade. (CNN Oct 10: "kills at least 12, hundreds wounded"; Al Jazeera Oct 10: "12 killed, 300 wounded"; Express Tribune)
- **Sun Oct 11, 2026**: Another attack on the airport; Saudi authority reported 1 killed, 11 wounded (single report). Airport closed until at least late Sunday; 600+ flights canceled (Al Jazeera "what to know", Oct 11). Commerzbank: fourth attack on Riyadh's main airport in a single week (FXStreet, Oct 12).
- **Mon Oct 12, 2026 overnight oil**: Brent rose as high as **$105.63** early (Bloomberg Markets Wrap via Moneyweb; Yahoo Finance/Tickmill also cite $105.63). Another report cites a high of $105.48 (+76 cents, +0.73%; Nation Thailand). Then Brent fell: **-0.8% to $103.91** (Bloomberg wrap via Moneyweb), **-1.2% to $103.43** (Yahoo/Tickmill), "below $103" (Gulf News). So: spiked above $105, then slipped under $104.
- Why the limited move: the strikes hit an airport, not oil infrastructure; no Saudi exports were reported lost; Gulf exports held steady (Gulf News; FXStreet/Commerzbank: risk premium elevated but limited). Also traders weighed the US claim of a Russia-Ukraine energy ceasefire (covered Oct 11 - mention not needed).
- Asia: MSCI Asia-Pacific -0.2%; South Korea -2.9% after long weekend (Bloomberg wrap). US stock futures slightly higher (Yahoo: Dow and S&P futures +0.1%; Tickmill S&P +0.3%).

- Oil feeds into gas and diesel, airfares, and shipping costs. Brent averaged about $114 in September vs about $71 in February (EIA).
