## Issues found and fixed
1. **Line 4, compare `winner`.** The narration is about the 57¢ that's left over (right box), but `winner` was "left" (Bank $1.18). Changed it to `"winner": "right"`.
2. **Line 4, wording.** "Stripe's own costs" left out Stripe's profit, which is the point of the video. Changed to "network fees, fraud and Stripe's own cut." (text and say).
3. **Line 5, misleading framing.** "Debit's cheaper" suggests merchants pay less on debit, but Stripe charges the same 2.9% + 30¢. The lower interchange means Stripe keeps more. Changed to "On debit, Stripe keeps more: big-bank debit interchange averaged 22¢ to 24¢ in 2023, per the Fed." (text and say). Updated the IG and YT captions to match.
4. **Line 3, precision and text/say mismatch.** The Nilson 2.35% figure covers Visa and Mastercard credit cards only, not all credit cards. Also, the spoken "about" was missing from the on-screen text. Changed to "Visa and Mastercard credit swipe fees averaged 2.35% in 2024, per the Nilson Report." (text and say now match).
5. **Line 8, weak CTA.** "That's the money." reads awkwardly. Changed to "That's how Stripe makes money. Follow for more."
6. **Line 7, length.** Cut "alone" and "Slivers become real money." to keep 140 spoken words (the cap). Text and say still match.
