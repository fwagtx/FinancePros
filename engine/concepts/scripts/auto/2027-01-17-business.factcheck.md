## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 0, weak or unclear hook.** "Your bill doesn't." is elliptical: it reads as "your bill doesn't earn $31 an hour" and never says what the bill is. The hook value on screen ($30.67) also differed from the spoken "thirty-one".
   - Fix: text "Plumbers make about $31 an hour. Some bill $200." / say "Plumbers make about thirty-one dollars an hour. Some bill two hundred." (11 words, both facts true: BLS median and the top of Angi's range). Hook scene value changed from $30.67 to $31.
2. **Line 1, awkward narration.** "thirty dollars sixty-seven an hour".
   - Fix: say "Government data puts the May 2025 median at thirty dollars, sixty-seven cents an hour for plumbers, and thirty dollars, thirty-eight cents for electricians." This matches the $30.67 and $30.38 on screen.
3. **Line 4, spoken vs screen mismatch.** The spoken line said "turns thirty-one dollars into about forty-four" while the screen shows $30.67 → $43.81, and the phrase "Add benefits, thirty percent of..." was clumsy.
   - Fix: say "Benefits were thirty percent of private employer pay costs in June 2026, so our math turns that wage into about forty-four dollars." "That wage" points to the $30.67 on screen, and "about forty-four" is $43.81 rounded.
4. **Line 5, misleading source.** "as one industry survey found" applies to plumbers a figure that came only from HVAC companies.
   - Fix: text "Techs can't bill every hour. At 65% billable (an HVAC study), that's $67.40 per billed hour (our math)." / say "...If sixty-five percent are billable, as one study of HVAC techs found, that's about sixty-seven dollars each." "About sixty-seven" matches $67.40.
5. **Post title and TikTok, title not paid off.** "a $100+ bill" is never said or shown in the video.
   - Fix: title "Plumbers make $30.67 an hour. Why bill up to $200?" TikTok now says "Bills run $45-$200/hr."
6. **Captions.** The IG and YouTube captions were updated to the new hook and the HVAC note. The source is now credited as "Field Promax via ACHR News".
7. **Small trims to fit 140 words.** Line 3: "the gas" changed to "gas". Line 5: "either" dropped. Line 7: "instead" dropped.
