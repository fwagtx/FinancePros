# Research brief: How sports betting apps make money: the vig (Business, posts Oct 24, 2026)

- AGA, Commercial Gaming Revenue Tracker, full year 2025 (Feb 2026): sports betting revenue $16.96B (+22.8%) on $166.94B handle (+11.0%). Hold = 16.96 / 166.94 = 10.2%. Taxes paid to states: $3.71B (+32.4%).
- DraftKings 10-K FY2025: total revenue $6,054.5M (+27%); sportsbook handle $53.6B; sportsbook revenue $3.83B; Sportsbook Net Revenue Margin 7.1% (+1.1 pts, from better hold and lower promotional reinvestment).
- The vig at -110: risk $110 to win $100. Two opposite $110 bets = $220 taken in, $210 paid to the winner, $10 kept. 10 / 220 = 4.55%.
- Parlays: multi-leg bets carry a much bigger built-in edge (around 20%+ on many-leg tickets vs about 4.5% on a single -110 leg). National hold has risen as the parlay share grew (Washington Post 2025; Legal Sports Report).
- Chartable: hold rising from about 7.5% in 2021 to 10.2% in 2025 (industry analyses).
- Surprising fact: about 10 cents of every dollar bet in 2025 stayed with the sportsbooks.
