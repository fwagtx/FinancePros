# Research: How subscriptions quietly drain your money (2026-11-21-hot, Money Myth)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- C+R Research survey (May 2022, ~1,000 US adults): people estimated subscription spending at $86/month; when walked through every category, itemized spending averaged $219/month (about 2.5x). Gap $133/month. (C+R Research; cited by CBS New York, Yahoo Finance, The American Prospect.)
- Netflix US Standard plan price history: $8.99 (2014), $9.99 (Oct 2015), $10.99 (Oct 2017), $12.99 (Jan 2019), $13.99 (Oct 2020), $15.49 (Jan 2022), $17.99 (Jan 2025), $19.99 (Mar 26, 2026). March 26, 2026 hike hit all plans: ads $7.99->$8.99, Standard $17.99->$19.99, Premium $24.99->$26.99. (CNBC and TechCrunch, Mar 26, 2026; Android Authority price timeline.)
- Amazon/FTC: Sept 25, 2025, Amazon agreed to pay $2.5B ($1B civil penalty + $1.5B refunds to ~35M customers) to settle FTC claims it enrolled people in Prime without consent and made cancelling hard; Amazon did not admit wrongdoing. (CNBC, CNN, Axios, Sept 25, 2025.)
- FTC "click-to-cancel" rule (negative option rule amendments, 2024) vacated by the 8th Circuit on July 8, 2025. FTC began new rulemaking: ANPRM announced March 2026, comments due April 13, 2026. ROSCA still enforced. (Latham, Steptoe, Gibson Dunn, Jones Day May 2026.)

## Chartable series
Netflix Standard (US, monthly): 2014 8.99; 2015 9.99; 2017 10.99; 2019 12.99; 2020 13.99; 2022 15.49; 2025 17.99; 2026 19.99.

## Worked example
Gap: $219 - $86 = $133/month; x12 = $1,596/year.
Actual: $219 x 12 = $2,628/year.
Netflix: 19.99 / 8.99 = 2.2236 -> +122% since 2014. Year of Standard at $19.99: $239.88.

## Surprising fact
People's guesses were about 60% low: actual spend was 2.5x the guess.
