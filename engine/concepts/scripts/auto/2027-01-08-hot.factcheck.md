- **Line 3: drought liquidation in 2022 (NPR, Sept 2022).** Confirmed. NPR, Sept 1, 2022, "Droughts are hitting cattle ranchers hard - and that could make beef more expensive" (Scott Horsley). It reports the most beef cows slaughtered in July 2022 of any month since 1986. The headline "Drought hits ranches" is a fair paraphrase.
- **Line 4: heifer kept in 2025, beef "until about 2029".** UF/IFAS Oct 2025 Florida Cattle Market Update says a heifer retained in fall 2025 and bred in 2026 calves in 2027, and that calf enters beef production in 2028-2029. Status note: imports partly resumed at Douglas, AZ on Aug 24, 2026, followed by a phased reopening of other ports (AZFamily/KOLD, DTN, Aug 24-25, 2026). The line is past tense ("In 2025 ... paused"), so it stays true on Jan 8, 2027. The wording is neutral. No change.
