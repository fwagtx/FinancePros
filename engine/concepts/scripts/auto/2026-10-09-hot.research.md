# Research brief: Why is furniture so expensive? (2026-10-09-hot)

Angle (surprising fact): Over the long run furniture got RELATIVELY cheaper. The BLS CPI "Furniture and bedding" index is only about 9.3% higher in 2026 than in 1993, while overall consumer prices rose about 130% in the same stretch. What people feel is the recent spike (2021-2022) plus tariffs on upholstered furniture since October 2025.

## Key facts (with dates)
- Furniture and bedding CPI (BLS, via officialdata.org / in2013dollars.com, which use raw BLS CPI data): prices 9.30% higher in 2026 vs 1993; average 0.27%/yr. $20 of 1993 furniture and bedding costs $21.86 in 2026.
- Overall CPI-U (in2013dollars.com, BLS data): 1993 CPI 144.500, 2026 CPI 333.020; cumulative +130.46%, avg 2.56%/yr. $20 in 1993 = $46.09 in 2026.
- 2022: furniture and bedding prices 12.19% higher than 2021 (annual average), vs overall inflation of 8.00% in 2022 (officialdata.org, BLS data). August 2022 12-month furniture increase 12.8% vs 8.3% overall.
- 2021: +8.14%; 2019: +2.34%; 2010: -4.05%; 2013: -1.24% (in2013dollars 2010-2026 table).
- Tariffs: Presidential proclamation of September 29, 2025 (Section 232) imposed 25% tariffs on upholstered furniture (upholstered wooden seating such as sofas and chairs), 25% on kitchen cabinets and vanities, 10% on softwood timber/lumber, effective 12:01 am ET October 14, 2025 (ABC News, RVIA, STR Trade Report, NAHB).
- Planned increases on Jan 1, 2026 (upholstered to 30%, cabinets/vanities to 50%) were delayed by a December 31, 2025 proclamation to January 1, 2027 (CNN Jan 1 2026, Thompson Hine, Mohawk Global, Facilities Dive). 25% remains in effect until then.
- UK, EU and Japan have trade-deal caps on these Section 232 tariffs (10%, 15%, 15%); so the 25% applies to most other countries (Vietnam has no finalized cap per searches).

## Worked examples
1. Long-run: $1,000 of furniture in 1993 x 1.093 = about $1,093 in 2026. $1,000 of general goods x (333.020 / 144.500 = 2.3046) = about $2,305.
2. Tariff (hypothetical example): a sofa with a $500 import (customs) value x 25% = $125 tariff, before the retailer sets its shelf price.

## Surprising fact
Furniture prices fell for about two decades (the $20 basket cost $21.76 in 2000 but only $18.41 in 2020) and are only about 9% above 1993 levels, while prices overall more than doubled.
