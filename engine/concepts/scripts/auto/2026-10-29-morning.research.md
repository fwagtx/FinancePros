## The facts
### Alphabet Q3 2026 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026; released after the close Wed Oct 28, 2026; SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1)
- Consolidated revenue: $127.6B, up 25% year over year (13th straight quarter of double-digit growth, per release).
- Google Cloud revenue: $30.7B, up 103% year over year (Q3 2025: $15.16B). Release credits Google Cloud Platform across enterprise AI infrastructure and enterprise AI solutions, plus core GCP; TPU system sales "a meaningful contributor."
- Cloud operating income: $11.1B vs $3.6B a year earlier (secondary report; not used on screen).
- Net income: $26.2B, EPS $2.12. Down about 25% from Q3 2025 ($34.98B), which reports (Constellation Research, InvestingLive/KuCoin summaries) attribute mainly to ~$8.7B of losses on equity investments.
- Google Search & other grew 12% (slowed from 17% prior quarter); search and ad sales missed forecasts per InvestingLive. Not used.
- Capex ~$51.4B in the quarter (not used - capex angle already covered).
- Stock reaction: not confirmed in search results; not used.

### Microsoft fiscal Q1 2027 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026; reported Oct 28, 2026)
- Revenue $92.3B, up 19%. GAAP net income $36.6B, up 32%; GAAP EPS $4.92.
- Azure and other cloud services revenue growth: 48% (vs 40% a year earlier). Microsoft Cloud revenue $63.9B, up 30%.
- Q2 guide: Azure growth 49-50% constant currency. CNBC: shares up about 4% after the report.

## Context
- "Cloud" = companies renting computing power (servers, chips, storage) in data centers instead of owning them; AI model training/running is driving demand. Google also sells its own TPU AI chips/systems.
- Growth has accelerated every quarter for a year: 34% -> 48% -> 63% -> 82% -> 103%.
- Big Tech spent heavily on AI; this quarter shows the revenue side at Google Cloud. But Alphabet's overall profit fell, driven by investment losses, so a fast-growing unit does not mean every number rose.
