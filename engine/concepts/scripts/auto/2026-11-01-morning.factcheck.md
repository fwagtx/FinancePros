# Fact-check: 2026-11-01 morning short (Binance $6.3B hack)

Checked on Nov 1, 2026 with web search. WebFetch could not reach cnbc.com or wikipedia.org because those hosts are blocked in this session, so I checked those claims through search snippets and other outlets: Reuters via Global Banking & Finance, France 24, Cointelegraph, crypto.news, FBI.gov, ABC, NBC, NPR and Bloomberg.

## Verified claims (OK)
- Hack on Thu Oct 29, 2026. About $6.3B taken ($6.36B by Binance's count), most of it moved within about 20 minutes. Widely reported as the largest crypto theft on record. Sources: CNBC Oct 29/30, Bloomberg, PBS, NPR.
- About $3B of the loot frozen. Source: CNBC Oct 30.
- FBI blamed North Korea ("TraderTraitor") on Sat Oct 31. The FBI said more than $6B. The same group did the roughly $1.5B Bybit hack in Feb 2025, which was the previous record. Sources: fbi.gov alert, ABC, NBC, The Next Web.
- Chainalysis yearly totals: 2022 $3.8B (Feb 2023 report, called a record), 2023 $1.7B, 2024 $2.2B, 2025 $3.4B. One article repeating later Chainalysis data lists 2022 as $3.7B, so writing "about" is the right hedge. Note that the 2025 figure only runs to early December 2025, so "all of 2025" is a slight stretch but acceptable.
- Hook math: $6.3B is more than the $3.4B taken in all of 2025, so the hook is true.
- Crypto held on an exchange is not FDIC insured. FDIC insurance is $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category.
- No political opinion, no price predictions, no advice. The header and cover ("Biggest Crypto Hack Ever: $6.3 Billion") are delivered by lines 1 and 2.

## Issues

1. **Line 4 timeline, and post ig and yt_desc: withdrawals did NOT come back Saturday / Oct 31 (factual error).** Reuters (via Global Banking & Finance), France 24, Cointelegraph and crypto.news all report that phase one of withdrawals opened on **Sunday Nov 1**. Bitcoin came first, just after 0600 GMT, then ETH, BNB and some USDT/USDC. No date has been given for phase two. A CNBC Oct 31 headline uses the word "resumes", but the Nov 1 wire reports date the actual restart to Sunday.
   - Fix line 4 scene events: `{"when":"Thu","what":"$6.3B drained"},{"when":"Fri","what":"~$3B frozen"},{"when":"Sun","what":"Withdrawals reopen"}`
   - Fix post ig: "Binance resumed withdrawals Oct 31 and says it replaced about 90% with its own funds." → "Binance began reopening withdrawals Nov 1 and says it replaced about 95% with its own funds."
   - Fix yt_desc: "Binance resumed withdrawals Oct 31 and says it replaced about 90%" → "Binance began reopening withdrawals Nov 1 and says it replaced about 95%"

2. **Line 4: "locked out for two days" is wrong.** Withdrawals were suspended from Thu Oct 29 until Sun Nov 1, which is about three days. Phase one also covers only some coins, so some users are still locked out.
   - text: "Customers were locked out for three days. About $3 billion of the loot is frozen."
   - say: "Customers were locked out for three days. About three billion of the loot is frozen."

3. **Line 5, and post: "about 90%" is already out of date on the posting date.** The 90% figure was Saturday's. On Sun Nov 1 Binance said it had covered about 95% (roughly $6B of $6.34B) with its own money, and that the 10 largest affected tokens are fully backed. These are Binance's own claims and have not been independently verified.
   - text: "Binance says it replaced about 95% of stolen assets with its own money."
   - say: "Binance says it replaced about ninety-five percent of stolen assets with its own money."
   - scene value: "~95%"

4. **Line 3: awkward and inaccurate wording, "blamed North Korea, the group behind…".** North Korea is a country, not a group.
   - text: "Saturday, the FBI blamed North Korean hackers, the same group behind last year's $1.5 billion Bybit hack."
   - say: "Saturday, the FBI blamed North Korean hackers, the same group behind last year's one point five billion dollar Bybit hack."

5. **Line 2 (minor): on-screen text and narration don't match.** The narration says "about" and drops "billion" after "three point eight"; the screen shows neither "about".
   - text: "Chainalysis counted about $3.4 billion stolen in all of 2025. Record-year 2022: about $3.8 billion."
   - say: "Chainalysis counted about three point four billion stolen in all of twenty twenty-five. Record-year twenty twenty-two was about three point eight billion."

6. **Line 7 (minor precision): the $250K limit is per depositor, per bank, not unlimited per person.**
   - text: "Bank deposits are insured up to $250,000 per bank by law. Exchanges pay back only if they can."
   - say: "Bank deposits are insured up to two hundred fifty thousand dollars per bank by law. Exchanges pay back only if they can."

7. **Line 0 hook: passes.** It is true, specific and about 11 words, so roughly 3 seconds. It could hit harder and land faster: "One hack just stole more crypto than all of last year." (same text and say). Optional.

8. **Time words:** "On Thursday" and "Saturday" are correct for a Sunday post (Oct 29 and Oct 31). "Last year's Bybit hack" (Feb 2025) is correct in 2026. The only time-word errors are the ones in issues 1 and 2.

9. **Post title** "One hack stole $6.3B, more than all crypto theft in 2025" is fine. The tiktok text is fine. The sources line is fine.

VERDICT: FAIL
