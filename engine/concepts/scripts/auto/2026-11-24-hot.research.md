# Research: Scam alert: fake Black Friday and Cyber Monday shopping sites (2026-11-24-hot)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- BBB 2025 Scam Tracker Risk Report: online purchase scams were 28.4% of reports; 89.3% of people targeted reported losing money; median loss $68.
- FTC Consumer Sentinel: reported fraud losses $15.9B in 2025 (FTC testimony Mar 25, 2026; ~3M fraud reports); $12.5B 2024; ~$10.0B 2023; $8.8B 2022; $5.8B 2021; $3.3B 2020 (annual Data Books; later revisions possible). Scams starting on social media: $2.1B in 2025.
- Fair Credit Billing Act: credit card billing-error disputes must be sent within 60 days after the statement with the error; liability for unauthorized credit card use capped at $50. Gift cards, wire transfers, crypto are hard to reverse (FTC).
- FTC warning signs for fake shopping sites: unusually low prices, payment by gift card/wire/crypto, little contact info, new or look-alike web addresses; report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
- Black Friday 2026 = Nov 27; Cyber Monday = Nov 30.

## Chartable series
FTC reported fraud losses ($B): 2020 3.3; 2021 5.8; 2022 8.8; 2023 10.0; 2024 12.5; 2025 15.9.
