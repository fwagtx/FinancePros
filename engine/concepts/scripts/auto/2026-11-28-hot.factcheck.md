## Issues and fixes

### 1. Hook is too long (line 1, minor but required)
"Americans gave four billion dollars on Giving Tuesday twenty twenty-five. Scammers noticed." The sentence is 12 words, and "twenty twenty-five" adds four syllables, so it runs about 3.5 to 4 seconds. Moving the year to the on-screen label tightens it to 9 words, about 2.5 seconds.
- text: `Giving Tuesday pulled in $4 billion. Scammers noticed.`
- say: `Giving Tuesday pulled in four billion dollars. Scammers noticed.`
- scene: unchanged. The `"label": "Dec 2025"` field carries the year.

### 2. Spoken value differs from on-screen value (line 3, FAIL-level)
The text says "$1.97 billion" but the narration says "about two billion". The narration also says "about" twice.
- text: `US giving that day roughly doubled, from about $2 billion in 2019 to $4.0 billion in 2025.`
- say: `US giving that day roughly doubled, from about two billion dollars in twenty nineteen to four billion in twenty twenty-five.`
- scene: unchanged. The chart points are all verified.

### 3. The FTC alert's date is probably wrong (line 2 scene, post ig, yt_desc)
Two independent search summaries give Dec 1, 2025 as the publication date, while only the URL slug suggests November. A date that is true either way avoids the conflict.
- line 2 scene.source: `FTC consumer alert, 2025`
- post.ig Sources: replace `FTC consumer alert, Nov. 2025` with `FTC consumer alert, 2025`
- post.yt_desc: replace `The FTC (Nov. 2025)` with `The FTC (2025)`

### 4. The FTC headline is presented as a quote but drops a word (line 2 scene)
The scene reads "FTC: Not everyone asking for money is a charity". The FTC's actual wording is "...is a real charity."
- scene.headline: `FTC: Not everyone asking for money is a real charity`
- text and say are unchanged, because the paraphrase there is accurate and keeps "real".

### 5. The $50 compare is muddled, and the caption and narration do not match (line 6)
Fake fundraisers also run on GoFundMe, so a "GoFundMe vs fake page" comparison suggests that a GoFundMe page means a real one. The 2.9% + $0.30 rate also applies only to personal fundraisers. The caption shows "$1.75", which is never spoken, and the narration drops the word "fee", so "plus thirty cents leaves..." reads awkwardly.
- text: `Example, $50 gift to a real GoFundMe: after its 2.9% + $0.30 fee, $48.25 reaches the organizer. A fake page passes on $0.`
- say: `Say you give fifty dollars to a real GoFundMe. After its two point nine percent plus thirty cent fee, forty-eight twenty-five reaches the organizer. A fake page passes on zero.`
- scene.left: `{"label": "Real page", "value": "$48.25", "tone": "up", "sub": "GoFundMe 2.9%+30¢ fee"}`
- scene.right: unchanged (`Fake page`, `$0`, `100% lost`)
- post.ig: change `a $50 gift on GoFundMe loses` to `a $50 gift to a real GoFundMe personal fundraiser loses`

### 6. Stale-news note (line 2, minor, no change needed)
"FTC warns" cites an alert from the 2025 season, which will be about a year old when the video posts. Because the scene source shows the year, this is acceptable. The FTC also posted a charity alert in Aug 2026 ("When donating, support those in need, not a scammer"), so the warning is still current.

### 7. Minor notes (no change needed)
- Line 8: Standard 8 is one of 20 BBB WGA standards, and "yardstick" frames it as a benchmark rather than advice.
- Cover "Scammers Love Giving Tuesday": the hook and the list of tactics pay it off.
