# Fact-check: 2027-01-14-hot (landlord keeps your security deposit)
Checked Nov 15, 2026 for posting Thu Jan 14, 2027 6 PM CT. Independent WebSearch verification (statute sites partly blocked; secondary sources and court opinions used where noted).

## Verified claims
- CA Civ. Code 1950.5: 21 days to return deposit / itemize; bad-faith retention = statutory damages up to 2x the deposit plus actual damages; no deductions for ordinary wear and tear. AB 414 (eff. Jan 1, 2026) changed refund method (electronic refunds) only; 21 days and deductions unchanged. Still accurate for Jan 2027.
- AB 2801 (Ch. 280, Stats. 2024): move-out photos (before/after repairs or cleaning that will be deducted) from April 1, 2025; move-in photos for tenancies beginning on/after July 1, 2025. Note: photo duty is tied to deductions for repairs/cleaning (fixed, see line 4).
- CA small claims: $12,500 for individuals (SB 71, eff. 2024; CCP 116.221). AB 1827 (would raise to $15,000) was held under submission in Senate Appropriations on 08/13/2026 and was not enacted, so $12,500 stays current for Jan 2027.
- NY GOL 7-108(1-a)(e): itemized statement within 14 days of vacating, or landlord forfeits any right to retain any portion (HSTPA 2019; one-month cap since June 14, 2019). Confirmed by court decisions.
- TX Prop. Code 92.103 (30 days after surrender), 92.107 (no duty to refund/itemize until written forwarding address), 92.109(a) bad faith = $100 + 3x amount wrongfully withheld + reasonable attorney's fees. Justice court limit $20,000 (Gov't Code 27.031, eff. Sept 1, 2020); no 2025-26 change found.
- MA G.L. c.186 s.15B: 30 days; under 15B(7) treble damages + 5% interest + court costs + attorney's fees (confirmed via case law quoting the subsection).
- Math: $100 + 3 x $1,000 = $3,100. Correct; on-screen values match spoken.
