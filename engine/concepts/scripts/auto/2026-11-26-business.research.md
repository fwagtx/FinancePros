# Research: How Uber makes money on every ride (2026-11-26-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- Uber FY2025 (results Feb 4, 2026): Gross Bookings $193B (+19%); revenue $52.0B (+18%); trips 13.567B (+20%); free cash flow ~$10B; 202M MAPCs in Q4 2025.
- Mobility segment FY2025 (summed from quarterly releases; verify): gross bookings ~$97.5B (Q1 21.18, Q2 23.76, Q3 25.11, Q4 27.44); revenue ~$29.67B (2024: $25.09B); Mobility adjusted EBITDA ~$7.90B (+22%, from $6.50B).
- Q2 2026: Mobility gross bookings $28.99B (+20% cc); total GB $58.0B; revenue $14.2B (+12%).
- Take rate debate: Uber said its take of rider payments was 21% worldwide in Q3 2025 after insurance and third-party costs ("Understanding Uber's Share of Driver Earnings," Uber Under the Hood). A 2026 report by Columbia Business School's Len Sherman with GigU argued Uber's US take rate climbed above 50%, citing upfront fare pricing (launched 2022) that decoupled rider prices from driver pay (Fast Company/Yahoo Finance, 2026).
- Mechanism: riders pay upfront fare; drivers paid by separate upfront offer; Uber keeps difference minus insurance, taxes, fees.

## Worked example (hypothetical $25 ride)
At 21%: Uber keeps $5.25, $19.75 to driver and costs. At 50%: $12.50.
