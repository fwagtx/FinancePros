# Fact-check: 2027-01-27-lf-guide (How to check and fix your credit report)

Posting date: January 27, 2027. Checked November 28, 2026. Firecrawl had no credits left, and ftc.gov, files.consumerfinance.gov, consumerfinancemonitor.com and the UNT web archive could not be fetched from this session. Every claim was checked with web search results that quote the primary sources, plus reputable secondary coverage.

## Verified claims (no change needed)
- FTC accuracy study: 1,001 participants and 2,968 reports, with study associates helping. 1 in 5 had an error that a bureau corrected after a dispute. 5% had errors that could mean less favorable terms on things like auto loans and insurance. "Slightly more than one in 10" saw a score change. About 1 in 20 had a maximum score change of more than 25 points. Report to Congress dated Dec 2012, press release Feb 11, 2013.
- CFPB complaints received (Consumer Response Annual Reports): 2020 about 542,300 (0.54M); 2021 about 994,000 (0.99M); 2022 about 1,287,300 (1.29M); 2023 about 1,657,600 (1.66M); 2024 about 3,187,900 (3.19M); 2025 about 6,635,400 (6.64M). All chart points match, the 2025 total is more than double 2024's, and the "2x" note is correct.
- 2025: more than 5.8M credit or consumer reporting complaints, about 88% of the total (PYMNTS, Orrick). 5.8/6.64 is 87%+, and "more than 5.8M" supports "about 88%".
- Free weekly reports at AnnualCreditReport.com started in 2020 during the pandemic and became permanent in 2023 (FTC consumer alert, Oct 13, 2023). AnnualCreditReport.com is the only site authorized by federal law. The phone number 1-877-322-8228 is the long-standing official AnnualCreditReport.com request line. The search results did not repeat it, but the source is well established.
- FCRA, 15 U.S.C. 1681i: notice to the furnisher within 5 business days; 30 days to investigate; 15 more days (45 total) if the consumer sends relevant information during the window or disputes after a free annual report (1681j); results within 5 business days of completion, plus a revised report if the file changed; inaccurate or unverifiable information must be corrected or deleted. Under 1681i(b) the bureau "may limit such statements to not more than one hundred words" if it helps the consumer write a clear summary. The quote is accurate.
- How long negative items stay: most negative items 7 years; Chapter 7 bankruptcy 10 years; hard inquiries 2 years (bureau practice).
- Credit freezes have been free nationwide since Sept 21, 2018. An initial fraud alert is free and lasts 1 year (since 2018), and the bureau you contact notifies the other two. An extended alert lasts 7 years and needs an identity theft report (an IdentityTheft.gov report qualifies).
- FICO weights: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30% (myFICO).
- Credit repair: under CROA it is illegal to charge before the promised services are fully performed (FTC).
- Utilization example: $600 + $400 = $1,000, and $1,000 / $5,000 = 20%. $600 + $1,400 = $2,000, and $2,000 / $5,000 = 40%. "Doubled" is correct. On-screen values match the spoken values.

## Issues found and fixes applied (0-based line index)
1. **Line 0, weak hook.** The old line was 11 words (about 3.7s at 180 wpm). It also stated a finding without making the viewer need an answer. Fix: TEXT "1 in 5 had credit report errors. Is yours?" / SAY "One in five had credit report errors. Is yours?" This is 9 words (about 3s). It is true per the FTC headline, and line 2 gives the source.
2. **Line 1, overpromise.** "Fixing it takes one free website and one letter" contradicts the script's own advice to dispute with both the bureau and the furnisher. Fix: "Checking takes one free website, and fixing an error often starts with one letter."
3. **Line 2, date precision.** The report is dated Dec 2012 and was released Feb 2013. Fix: "published in 2013" changed to "released in 2013".
4. **Line 39, overstatement and mismatch.** The FTC reported a maximum score change of more than 25 points, not a "jump". The TEXT said "That's real money" while the SAY said "That can be". Fix: both now say "saw their score move by more than 25 points. That can mean real money on a loan rate." The stat label is now "Big score change".
5. **Line 45, unverifiable wording, mismatch, neutrality.** "Some came from third parties filing in bulk" is not the CFPB's wording, and the SAY added "consumer groups disputed that", which the TEXT did not have. Fix: both now read "In June 2026, the CFPB said third parties and high-volume filing may make the data less reliable. Consumer groups disagreed." This follows the CFPB June 24, 2026 announcement as reported by Consumer Finance Monitor, Mondaq and Banking Dive ("third parties, automated tools, and high-volume dispute strategies"), and NCLC's objections. It is neutral and attributed. "June 2026" is still correct on the posting date.
6. **Line 57, overclaim.** "It stops flowing back into all three files" is not guaranteed. Fix: "the lender has to correct it with every bureau it reports to." This is the furnisher duty under FCRA 1681s-2.
7. **Line 68, on-screen text differs from narration.** The TEXT said "has to flag" while the SAY said "generally has to". Fix: the TEXT now says "generally".
8. **Line 72, unsupported add-on.** The SAY added "and keeps the bureau honest", which the TEXT did not have. Removed.
9. **Line 81, overclaim.** "So nobody can open credit in your name" is too absolute. Fix: "which makes new credit in your name much harder to open" in both TEXT and SAY.
10. **Line 90, mismatch.** The TEXT said "why it failed" while the SAY said "why it got stuck". Fix: both now say "got stuck".
11. **Line 97, overpromise.** "Fixing it took one letter" was inconsistent with disputing with both the bureau and the furnisher. Fix: "Fixing it started with one letter."
12. **Line 98, mismatch and unsupported claim.** The TEXT said "Ten minutes a year" while the SAY said "Ten minutes". The claim was also arbitrary, since reports are available weekly. Fix: "A little time to proofread it is a pretty good deal."
13. **POST yt_desc.** Added NCLC comments (2026) to the sources, because line 45 now mentions consumer groups.
