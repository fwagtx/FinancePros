# Research: Scam alert: power of attorney abuse (2026-11-25-hot)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- FinCEN Financial Trend Analysis (April 2024): BSA reports filed June 15, 2022 - June 15, 2023: 155,415 filings, about $27 billion in elder financial exploitation-related suspicious activity. Elder theft (by a trusted person): average $98,863, median $23,762 per report. Elder scams (strangers): average $129,483, median $33,499. Adult children were the most frequent elder-theft perpetrators. Banks filed 72%; scams 80% of activity.
- Power of attorney: legal document letting an agent act for the principal; agents are fiduciaries. Uniform Power of Attorney Act (adopted in many states): agent must act in good faith, within scope, loyally for the principal's benefit, avoid conflicts; liability for misconduct.
- CFPB/FinCEN red flags: sudden large withdrawals or transfers, new names added to accounts, unpaid bills despite funds, agent refusing to share information/isolating the person.
- DOJ National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311). Adult Protective Services in every state.
