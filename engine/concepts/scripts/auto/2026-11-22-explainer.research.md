# Research: Why car dealers ask what monthly payment you want (2026-11-22-explainer)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- Edmunds Q3 2026 (release Oct 1, 2026): avg new-vehicle amount financed $44,664; APR 7.0%; term 70.5 months; monthly payment $787 (records). 84+ month loans a record 25.5% of financed new-vehicle purchases (23.9% Q2 2026). $1,000+ payments a record 21.2% (20.3% Q2 2026, 19.1% Q3 2025). Avg total interest record $9,938.
- Edmunds Q1 2026: 84+ month loans 22.9% (record then); avg payment $773; $1,000+ 20.0%.
- Edmunds Q1 history of 84-month share: 13.4% (Q1 2019), 15.8% (Q1 2024), 19.8% (Q1 2025).
- FTC: "payment packing" = dealers quote a higher monthly payment than needed and pack add-ons (service contracts, GAP, etc.) into it; small add-on costs barely move the monthly payment (FTC CARS rule NPRM; Consumer Federation of America).
- FTC CARS Rule (Dec 2023) would have required total-cost disclosure when quoting monthly payments; vacated by the 5th Circuit Jan 27, 2025; FTC withdrew it effective Feb 12, 2026.
- Truth in Lending Act / Regulation Z: closed-end credit disclosures include the "Total of Payments", amount financed, finance charge and APR.

## Chartable series (Edmunds, share of new-vehicle loans 84+ months, Q1 each year)
2019 13.4; 2024 15.8; 2025 19.8; 2026 22.9. (Q3 2026: 25.5%.)

## Worked example (hypothetical $35,000 financed at 7% APR, standard amortization)
- 60 months: $693.04/mo; total paid $41,582.52; interest $6,582.52
- 72 months: $596.72/mo; interest $7,963.50
- 84 months: $528.24/mo; total paid $44,372.48; interest $9,372.48
- 60 -> 84: payment drops $164.80; interest rises $2,789.96 (~$2,790)
- $1,500 add-on at 7% over 84 months adds $22.64/mo.

## Surprising fact
Stretching from 5 to 7 years cuts the payment by about $165 but adds about $2,790 of interest on the same car.
