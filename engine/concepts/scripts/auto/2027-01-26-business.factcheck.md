## Issues found and fixed (applied to the raw JSON)

   - text: "Adidas ended the deal in October 2022, after Ye's antisemitic remarks."
   - say: "Adidas ended the deal in October twenty twenty-two, after Ye's antisemitic remarks."
2. **Line 3, unsound inference.** The claim "Yeezy margin neared 42%, our math" divided the €500M operating-profit hit that Adidas warned of by the €1.2B revenue. That number is the profit contribution of the remaining inventory, not Yeezy's margin, so I removed it. I replaced it with the sourced comparison: the €500M warning against FY2023 operating profit of €268M.
   - text: "Adidas warned scrapping unsold Yeezys would cut €500M of operating profit. Its 2023 total: €268M."
   - say: "Adidas warned scrapping unsold Yeezys would cut five hundred million euros of operating profit. Its twenty twenty-three total: two hundred sixty-eight million."
   - scene: compare "Yeezy risk", with "Write-off hit" €500M (sub "Adidas warning") against "2023 profit" €268M (sub "operating profit").
3. **Line 4, text and voice mismatch.** The caption said "the next year" but the voice said "next year". Both now match, and the line was reworded to follow the new line 3.
