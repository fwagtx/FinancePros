## Verified (no change needed)
- **NIQ $16.54 (2019) to $21.90:** Fox Business, NIQ data on U.S. disposable diaper sales. The article says "Now, the average price is $21.90." Breitbart, Aug 17, 2023, dates the Fox story to Aug 2023. Math: 21.90/16.54 = 1.324, so +32% is correct. **Caveat:** $21.90 is a mid-2023 "now" figure, not a full-year 2023 average. See the fix on line 1.
- **Circana +35%, 2019-2023:** Bloomberg, Feb 8, 2024. The Bloomberg Linea version repeats it. This independently backs "about a third higher by 2023", so the hook's "2019 to 2023" framing is defensible.
- **PPI WPU091501234:** "Disposable diapers and similar disposable products, purchased sanitary paper stock and wadding". FRED snapshot: Feb 2026 = 129.038, which matches the brief's Oct 2025-Feb 2026 plateau. I could not open the 2017-2021 values directly; the brief's 111.2 (flat Aug 2018-Jun 2021) is plausible. Jan 2026/Jan 2021 = 129.038/111.2 = 1.160, so +16% is correct. Starting the chart in 2017 is fine: 2017-2021 runs 100.9 to 100.0, which really is flat "for years".
- **SAP gel:** acrylic acid comes from two-stage gas-phase oxidation of propylene, which is crude oil/naphtha-derived (USPTO patents; Sanyo Chemical 2018 notice per brief). "An oil and gas product" is OK.
- **P&G, AP, Oct 19, 2021:** "had raised prices on product lines including baby, family, home and fabric care" to offset higher commodity and freight costs. Line 5 and the "AP, Oct 2021" source are correct.
- **NDBN Diaper Check 2023:** 47% ("1 in 2") of U.S. families with young children report diaper need, meaning they can't afford enough diapers. YouGov survey, Apr 21-May 18, 2023, released Jun 15, 2023. Line 6 is correct.
- **NDBN Fast Facts (Jun 2023):** "$80 to $100 or more per month per baby". $80 x 12 = $960, labeled as our math. Line 8 is OK.
- **SNAP:** the USDA FNS retailer notice "Only Accept SNAP Benefits for Allowable Items" lists "Baby bottles/diapers/wipes" as not allowed. The page was updated Nov 24, 2025. Line 7 is correct, and the 2026 state SNAP restriction waivers don't change it.
- **Line 9 "Compare price per diaper, not per box":** a general consumer shopping tip, not investment advice and not a price prediction. OK as written.
- **Time words:** none are relative ("this year", "now", "recently"). All are absolute years, which is correct for Jan 6, 2027.
- **Limits:** stat label "Diaper need, 2023" (17) and sub "NDBN Diaper Check" (17) are OK. Board lines are 16/17 and 16/11, OK. News headline "P&G raises prices" (17) is OK. post.title is 43 characters, OK. TikTok is 136 characters, OK. List items are OK.

  - The screen lacks "about" while the narration has it.
