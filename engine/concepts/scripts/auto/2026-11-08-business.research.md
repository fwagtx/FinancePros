# Research brief: How free apps make money (posts Nov 8, 2026)

## Ads: Meta (FY2025 10-K / Q4 release, Jan 2026)
- Total revenue $200.97B (+22%); advertising revenue $196.175B (+22%) = 97.6% of revenue.
- Ad impressions +12%, average price per ad +9% (2025).
- Daily active people (Family of Apps) 3.58B average in Dec 2025.
- Ad revenue per daily active person (rough): 196.175 / 3.58 = ~$54.80 a year.
- Meta advertising revenue by year ($B): 2019 69.66 | 2020 84.17 | 2021 114.93 | 2022 113.64 | 2023 131.95 | 2024 160.63 | 2025 196.18

## In-app purchases and subscriptions (freemium)
- Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026: consumers spent $167B on in-app purchases in 2025 (+10%); non-game apps ~$85B (+21%), surpassing games for the first time; AI apps >$5B.
- App store commissions: Apple and Google generally take 15%-30% of digital purchases (15% for small developers and many subscriptions after year one).
