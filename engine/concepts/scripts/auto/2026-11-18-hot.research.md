# 2026-11-18-hot research: How much $1,000 in the S&P 500 grew (Jan 1, 1980 to Dec 31, 2025)

Format: Hot Take / Face-off: price-only vs dividends reinvested (and nominal vs inflation-adjusted).
Strictly historical. Fixed period: start = close of Dec 31, 1979 (i.e., invested at start of 1980), end = close of Dec 31, 2025. No predictions, no advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## Worked example (exact math)
1. Dividends reinvested (nominal): $1,000 x (1.3250 x 0.9508 x ... x 1.2502 x 1.1788) = **$202,134** at Dec 31, 2025.
   Annualized: (202,134 / 1,000)^(1/46) - 1 = **12.2% a year** (12.23%).
2. Price only (no dividends): $1,000 x 6,845.50 / 107.94 = $1,000 x 63.42 = **$63,419** (9.44% a year).
   Dividends reinvested ended about 3.2x larger (202,134 / 63,419 = 3.19).
3. Inflation: CPI ratio 324.054 / 76.7 = 4.2250 (Dec 1979 -> Dec 2025). $1,000 of Dec 1979 money = $4,225 in Dec 2025 money.
   Total return, inflation-adjusted: 202,134 / 4.2250 = **$47,843 in Dec 1979 dollars** (about 47.8x the buying power; 8.77% a year real).
   Price-only, inflation-adjusted: 63,419 / 4.2250 = $15,011 in 1979 dollars.

## Drawdowns (calendar-year total returns, S&P DJI)
- 2008: -37.00% -> the example pile fell from $30,628 to $19,296 (about -$11,332).
- 2022: -18.11% -> fell from $132,624 to $108,605 (about -$24,019).
- 2000-2002: three straight down years (-9.10, -11.89, -22.10); pile $26,849 (end 1999) -> $16,752 (end 2002), about -38%.

## Surprising fact
Reinvested dividends more than tripled the ending value: $202,134 vs $63,419 price-only (Jan 1980 to Dec 2025). Most index headlines quote price only.
