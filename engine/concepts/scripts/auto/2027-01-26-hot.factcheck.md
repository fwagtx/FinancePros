## Issues found and fixes applied

1. **Line 0, hook prop (renderer).** The prop was a card with label "850". Fix: prop changed to `"paper"` with label "850". The cover prop also changed to `"paper"` to match.
2. **Line 0, slow hook.** The original was 12 words, about 4 seconds, so it missed the 3-second target. Fix: text "You don't need an 850 for top rates." / say "You don't need an eight fifty for top rates." (9 words, under 3 seconds). It is true and specific. To make it stronger, a later version could open on the surprise number: "Only 1 in 58 people has an 850, and they don't get better rates."
3. **Line 1, "people" was inaccurate.** Experian's figure counts consumers with a credit file, not all people. Fix: text "Good, because only 1.7% of consumers had one in June 2026." / say "Good, because only one point seven percent of consumers had one in June twenty twenty-six."
4. **Line 2, trimmed for the word budget (no fact change).** Text "Mortgages top out earlier: Fannie Mae's best score tier starts at 780." / say "... starts at seven eighty."
5. **Lines 3-4, LLPA described as a fee.** An LLPA is a loan-level price adjustment that Fannie Mae charges the lender. It is usually passed to the borrower through the rate or points, not paid as a closing fee. Fixes:
   - Line 3: text "With 20% down, a 780 and an 850 get the same price adjustment: 0.375%." / say "With twenty percent down, a seven eighty and an eight fifty get the same price adjustment, zero point three seven five percent." Compare title changed to "Same LLPA".
   - Line 4: text "Our math: on a $400,000 loan, $1,500 either way, often built into your rate." / say "Our math: on a four hundred thousand dollar loan, fifteen hundred dollars either way, often built into your rate."
6. **Line 6, scoring model not stated.** Experian's 781+ tier is on VantageScore 4.0, not FICO, while the 850 stat is FICO. Fix: text "Car loans: Experian's top VantageScore tier starts at 781, averaging 4.4% on new cars in Q2 2026." / say "Car loans: Experian's top VantageScore tier starts at seven eighty-one, averaging four point four percent on new cars in the second quarter of twenty twenty-six."
7. **Line 7, on-screen item not spoken (renderer).** The list item "781+ for car loans" was not spoken on this line. Fix: text "The real finish line: about 780 for mortgages, 781 for car loans, though lenders vary. 850 is bragging rights." / say "The real finish line: about seven eighty for mortgages, seven eighty-one for car loans, though lenders vary. Eight fifty is bragging rights."
8. **Post captions (ig, yt_desc), "fee" wording and model basis.** Fixes:
