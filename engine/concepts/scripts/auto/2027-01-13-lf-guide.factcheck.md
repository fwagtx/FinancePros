# Fact-check: 2027-01-13-lf-guide (The Guide, "How to get out of student loan debt")
Checked Nov 14, 2026 for posting Wed Jan 13, 2027, 4 PM CT. I verified every claim myself with web search. fsapartners.ed.gov and studentaid.gov block direct fetch, so I confirmed official pages through search snippets. I re-ran all the math with a Python simulation.

## Verified (no change needed)
- **Hook / lines 0-1:** Over 1 in 10 is correct. In NY Fed Q3 2026 (released Nov 10, 2026), 10.9% of student loan balances were 90+ days delinquent or in default, up from 10.6% in Q2. This is the stock measure. The 7.72% figure is the flow into delinquency, a different measure, and the script does not use it. The figure is about dollars, not people, and the script says dollars.
- **Lines 2-3, chart:** NY Fed student loan balance, Q3 of each year: 2019 $1.50T, 2020 $1.55T, 2021 $1.58T, 2022 $1.57T, 2023 $1.60T, 2024 $1.61T, 2025 $1.65T, 2026 $1.656T (up $5B in the quarter). These match the NY Fed press releases. The 2026 point (1.66) and the note ($1.656T) are consistent.
- **Line 14:** FSA Data Center (GENERAL-26-57, Sept 22, 2026, data through June 30, 2026) shows 42.3M recipients and more than $1.7T. GENERAL-26-38 (June 2026) shows 42.6M and $1.7T. "About 42 million" is OK. FSA counts recipients at the loan level, which is close enough to "people".
- **Line 18:** 6.52% fixed for undergrad Direct Loans first disbursed July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 (8.07% grad, 9.07% PLUS). Confirmed.
- **Lines 21-23, Tiered Standard:** The terms are under $25K: 10 yrs; $25K-<50K: 15; $50K-<100K: 20; $100K+: 25. New loans made on or after July 1, 2026 get only Tiered Standard or RAP. Confirmed.
- **Lines 24-32, RAP:** All confirmed by the CRS IF13075 statute summary and other explainers:
  - 1-10% of AGI, rising 1 point per $10K, 10% above $100K
  - $10 minimum (AGI of $10K or less pays $10)
  - $50/month off per dependent, with a $10 floor
  - unpaid interest waived on on-time payments
  - principal match up to $50
  - forgiveness after 360 payments
- **Lines 26-28, RAP example (recomputed):** $45,000 falls in the $40,001-$50,000 bracket, so 4%. 4% x $45,000 = $1,800 a year, which is $150 a month. With one dependent, $150 - $50 = $100. Correct.
- **Lines 33-34:** Existing borrowers keep IBR and the old standard plans. PAYE and ICR close, and borrowers must move to IBR or RAP by July 1, 2028 (some sources say June 30, 2028). OK.
- **Lines 35-38, SAVE:**
  - ED announced the Missouri settlement on Dec 9, 2025.
  - About 7M borrowers were in SAVE, with interest accruing in forbearance since Aug 1, 2025.
  - Servicers sent 90-day notices in waves from July 1, 2026, with later batches as late as October. A final 30-day notice follows, then placement in Standard or Tiered Standard.
  - The script's "check your notice for your exact date" wording is safe for Jan 2027, when some deadlines may still be open.
- **Lines 39-40, Parent PLUS:** Parent PLUS loans are not eligible for RAP. The new caps are $20K per year and $65K total per student, from July 1, 2026. Confirmed.
- **Lines 43-46, PSLF:**
  - Rules confirmed: 120 payments that need not be consecutive, 30+ hours a week, government or 501(c)(3) employer, Direct Loans on a qualifying plan (RAP, IBR, or 10-yr standard).
  - CRS LSB11472 (updated Aug 31, 2026): through April 2026, about 1.254M borrowers had $93.4B forgiven under PSLF and related programs. Confirmed.
- **Line 47:** The Oct 31, 2025 final rule ("substantial illegal purpose") was vacated June 30, 2026 by D. Mass. (Judge Joun) and D.D.C. (Judge Ali). Confirmed.
- **Lines 49-50, taxes:** IDR forgiveness has been federally taxable since Jan 1, 2026, because the ARPA exclusion expired. PSLF remains tax-free. Confirmed.
- **Lines 52-63, avalanche vs snowball:** I re-simulated monthly at 10-year minimums.
  - Minimums are $43.41, $102.33 and $96.43, a total of $242.17. Adding $200 gives $442.17.
  - Minimums only: $9,059.94 interest over 120 months.
  - Avalanche: $3,327.42 interest, 53 months, first loan paid off in month 27.
  - Snowball: $3,839.60-3,839.67, 54 months, first loan paid off in month 18.
  - Difference: about $512. The writer's figures differ from mine by cents because of rounding conventions. Every on-screen and spoken value is correct.
- **Lines 66-68, consolidation:** (4,000 x 5.5% + 9,000 x 6.53%) / 13,000 = 6.2131%, which rounds up to the nearest 1/8 as 6.25%. Correct. ED's servicing presentation confirms that consolidating on or after July 1, 2026 limits the borrower to RAP or Tiered Standard.
- **Lines 69-72, refinancing:** The federal benefits lost are correctly listed. Student loans carry no prepayment penalty: federal loans by statute, private loans under HEOA 2008.
- **Lines 74-82, default:**
  - Default comes after 270 days.
  - Administrative wage garnishment can take up to 15% of disposable pay, with 30 days' notice. The government can also seize tax refunds.
  - Rehabilitation takes 9 payments within 10 months, and it removes the default record but not earlier late payments.
  - A second rehabilitation is allowed from July 1, 2027. Line 81's "right now" is still true on Jan 13, 2027.
  - Consolidation out of default is the other route.
  - All confirmed.
- **Lines 83-85, scams:** The script matches FTC guidance:
  - charging fees before help is illegal
  - never share your FSA ID
  - no one can promise fast forgiveness
  - help is free at studentaid.gov or from your servicer
  - report scams at ReportFraud.ftc.gov
- **Neutrality:** The script names no politicians or parties and uses "a 2025 law". It does not recommend a specific plan, and line 62 stays neutral. Good.
