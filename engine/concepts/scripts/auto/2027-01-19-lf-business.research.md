# Research brief: How LinkedIn makes money from your job hunt
Slot: 2027-01-19-lf-business (posts Tue Jan 19, 2027, 4 PM Central). Researched Nov 20, 2026. Evergreen: every number dated.
Microsoft's fiscal year (FY) ends June 30 (FY2026 = July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026).

## News angle (evergreen-safe)
- Starting with fiscal 2027 (first reported in October 2026), Microsoft no longer reports LinkedIn revenue as its own line. Disclosed in a Form 8-K on Sept 2, 2026: LinkedIn Marketing Solutions and Premium Subscriptions move into "Search and advertising"; Talent Solutions and Sales Solutions move into "Industry solutions." (Secondary: Windows Report, People Matters, ppc.land, Sept-Oct 2026. Treat the mapping as reported by outlets.) So FY2026 ($19.8B) is the last full year with a stand-alone LinkedIn revenue figure.

## Members
- "over 1.3 billion members and five consecutive years of double-digit member growth" (Microsoft FY2026 Annual Report shareholder letter, Oct 2026).
- Passed 1 billion members (Microsoft FY2024 Annual Report letter).

## Acquisition
- Announced June 13, 2016: $196 per share in cash, about $26.2 billion including net cash; 49.5% premium. Microsoft's largest deal at the time. Closed Dec 8, 2016. (LinkedIn 8-K / DEFA14A June 2016 on SEC; MobileSyrup Dec 8, 2016.)
- LinkedIn founded 2002 (Reid Hoffman and co-founders), launched 2003, IPO 2011 (widely reported background).

## Business lines (Microsoft 10-K FY2026 description)
- Talent Solutions: hiring tools sold to employers and recruiters (Recruiter, job postings incl. promoted jobs) plus Learning.
- Premium Subscriptions: paid tiers for members (Premium Career, Premium Business).
- Marketing Solutions: ads/sponsored content; 10-K ties it to member engagement.
- Sales Solutions: Sales Navigator, for salespeople.
- 10-K: revenue "driven by demand from enterprises and professionals for subscription offerings"; "Growth will depend on our ability to increase LinkedIn member engagement."
- Microsoft does not break out revenue by line. Last public split: LinkedIn's own 2015 results (calendar 2015, total $2,991M): Talent Solutions $1,877M, Marketing Solutions $581M, Premium Subscriptions $532M (LinkedIn Q4/FY2015 release, Feb 4, 2016; SEC). Our math: about 63% / 19% / 18%.
- Premium: Nadella, Microsoft FY2025 Q2 call (Jan 29, 2025): Premium subscriptions passed a record $2 billion in revenue over the prior 12 months (TechCrunch Jan 29, 2025). Our math: vs FY2025's $17.8B, about 11% (rough; periods differ).

## Premium Career price (UNCERTAIN: LinkedIn site not reachable; two outlets agree)
- US: $39.99/month billed monthly; $239.88/year billed annually (about $19.99/month). (Lilach Bullock, Oct 2026; Technobezz, Nov 2026.) Prices can vary by device/tax/promo.
- Our math: $39.99 x 12 = $479.88 a year monthly; annual plan $239.88, saves $240.00.
- Features: InMail messages, who viewed your profile, applicant insights, LinkedIn Learning, AI tools.

## The hiring market
- Talent Solutions was held back by a weaker hiring market in FY2025 and into FY2026 (Microsoft earnings commentary via StaffingIndustry Apr/Jul 2025; Social Media Today Oct 2025). Growth in FY2025-26 led by Marketing Solutions.
- Applications: LinkedIn data, about 11,000 job applications per minute, up 45% in a year (NYT, June 2025; Semafor June 24, 2025). Earlier: 9,000/minute (TechCrunch Jan 15, 2025).

## Worked examples (our math)
1. Revenue per member, FY2026: $19,817M / 1.3B members = about $15.24 per member per year.
2. Per day, FY2026: $19,817M / 365 = about $54.3M per day.
3. Payback: LinkedIn revenue FY2017-FY2026 sum = $115.3B (latest-filed figures) = about 4.4x the $26.2B price. (Revenue, not profit.)
4. Growth FY2018 to FY2026: 19,817 / 5,259 = about 3.8x.
5. Premium Career year: monthly $479.88 vs annual $239.88.

## Surprising fact
The biggest payer is the employer, not the job seeker: in 2015, Talent Solutions (sold to recruiters) was about 63% of revenue; Premium (sold to members) about 18%. The job seeker is mostly the inventory recruiters and advertisers pay to reach.
