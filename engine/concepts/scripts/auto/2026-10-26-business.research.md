# Research brief: How Disney makes money: theme parks vs movies (Business, posts Oct 26, 2026)

- Disney fiscal 2025 (year ended Sept 27, 2025; Q4 FY25 release Nov 13, 2025; 10-K):
  - Total revenue $94.425B (+3%).
  - Entertainment (movies, TV, streaming): revenue $42.466B, segment operating income $4.7B (+19%). Margin 4.7 / 42.5 = 11.1%.
  - Experiences (parks, cruises, resorts, consumer products): revenue $36.156B, record operating income $10.0B (+$723M). Margin 10.0 / 36.2 = 27.7%.
  - Sports (ESPN): revenue $17.672B.
- Experiences OI is 10.0 / 4.7 = 2.1x Entertainment OI.
- Disney said in Sept 2023 that it planned to roughly double Experiences capital spending, to about $60B over about 10 years (Disney investor materials / CNBC Sept 2023).
- Flywheel: films and characters feed rides, merchandise and cruises.
- Worked example: per $100 of revenue, Experiences keeps about $28 of operating income; Entertainment keeps about $11.
- FY2026 results are due mid-November 2026, after posting; FY2025 is the latest full year.

SOURCES:
- The Walt Disney Company Q4 and full-year FY2025 earnings (Nov 13, 2025); Form 10-K FY2025
- CNBC (Sept 19, 2023) on the $60B parks investment plan
