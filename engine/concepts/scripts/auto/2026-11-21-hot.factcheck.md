## Issues and fixes

### 1. Cover title claims the viewer guessed (the video doesn't pay it off). Moderate
The cover says "You Guessed $86. It's $219", but the people who guessed were 2022 survey respondents, not the viewer. "It's" also presents a 2022 average as a current fact.
- cover.title: `They Guessed $86. It Was $219`
(cover.value stays `$219`)

### 2. Post title is in the present tense for 2022 data. Minor
- post.title: `People guessed $86 a month on subscriptions. It was $219`

### 3. Weak opening: the spoken hook never says "subscriptions" and runs a little long. Moderate
The current `say` ("People guessed eighty-six dollars a month. The real number was two nineteen.") never names the topic. A viewer who only hears it doesn't know what the $86 was for. "Two nineteen" can also sound like a time of day. The hook is about 13 words, which is roughly 4 seconds. The fact itself is true, specific and a strong hook. It just needs to be tighter and to name the topic.
- L1 text: `People guessed $86 a month on subscriptions. It was $219.`
- L1 say: `People guessed eighty-six bucks a month on subscriptions. It was two nineteen.`
  - A shorter option, about 2.5 seconds: `Subscriptions: you'd guess eighty-six a month. People really paid two nineteen.`
  - Do not use the shorter option if the rule against putting the viewer in the guess applies. In that case keep the first `say` above.
- L1 scene: no change. Keep `title: "Real bill: [$219]"`, `label: "2022 survey"`, `value: "$219"`, `mood: "shock"`.

### 4. L6 reads awkwardly out loud. Minor
"They slip by three ways" is missing a word.
- L6 text: `They slip by in three ways: free trials that turn paid, yearly renewals, and price hikes on autopay.`
- L6 say: `They slip by in three ways: free trials that turn paid, yearly renewals, and price hikes on autopay.`
- L6 scene: no change.

### 5. L7 allegation framing could be tighter (optional). Minor
"Settle FTC claims" is already acceptable. Adding "without admitting wrongdoing" is optional; Amazon did not admit wrongdoing.
- L7 text: no change needed.
- L7 say: no change needed.

### 6. Pre-post recheck (optional)
Shortly before November 21, confirm that Netflix US Standard is still $19.99. As of September 2026 it was still $19.99 and no new US increase had been reported.
