# Research brief: How VPN companies make money (posts Wed Dec 23, 2026, 2 PM CT; written Oct 24, 2026)

Evergreen: no news hook used. All figures carry their year/source.

## 1. Subscription economics (worked example, labeled EXAMPLE)
- NordVPN Basic list prices as reported in 2026 (prices are promotional and change often):
  - 1-month plan: $12.99 per month (Tom's Guide NordVPN price page 2026; VPNPro 2026; VPNAlert 2026; security.org). Note: grey.co and one security.org FAQ list $14.99 -> minor conflict.
  - 2-year plan: $3.39/month, billed $81.36 up front pre-tax, first purchase, 30-day money-back guarantee (Tom's Guide January 2026 deal page; security.org 2026). Other promos seen: $2.91, $3.25, $3.49/month.
  - Renewal: plans renew at a higher price (security.org; grey.co: "plans renew at a higher annual rate").
- EXAMPLE math: $12.99 x 24 = $311.76 paying monthly for 24 months. 2-year deal = $81.36 up front ($3.39 x 24 = $81.36). Difference $230.40; 81.36/311.76 = 26.1% -> about 74% less.
- Why companies push long plans (business logic, attributed generally): cash collected on day one; customer locked for 24 months, so no monthly chance to cancel (lower churn); higher renewal price later.
- Kape Technologies FY2022 results (RNS, 21 Mar 2023): recurring revenue = 86.8% of total revenue; ~7.4 million paying subscribers; revenue $623.5M (up 170.3% from $230.7M in 2021); adjusted EBITDA (pro forma) $176.0M; profit after tax $70.9M.

## 3. Consolidation
- Kape Technologies owns ExpressVPN (deal agreed Sept 2021, $936M), CyberGhost, Private Internet Access, ZenMate; Kape still appears as the parent as of early 2026 (no sale/spin-off found). (TechRadar, Tom's Guide, Business Wire 2021; Wikipedia Kape Technologies)
- Nord Security (NordVPN) and Surfshark finalized a merger announced Feb 2, 2022; brands keep operating separately/autonomously. (Nord Security blog; PR Newswire; TechAdvisor)

## 4. Affiliate marketing (the surprise)
- Kape bought Webselenese, owner of VPN review sites vpnMentor and Wizcase, in March 2021 (Kape FY2021 results RNS: $155.1M consideration; TechRadar "$150m"; Cloudwards $149.1M -> figures conflict, so script omits the price). Critics (Restore Privacy, NYT-linked report) noted the sites rank Kape-owned VPNs highly; Kape said the sites keep editorial independence.
- NordVPN affiliate program as reported by affiliate directories (Webgains, Affilimate, UpPromote, Lasso): 100% of first payment on 1-month plans, 40% on new 1-year/2-year plans, 30% on renewals. WEAKLY SOURCED (third-party directories, not NordVPN's own page; terms can change). EXAMPLE: 40% x $81.36 = $32.54.
- FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255, revised June 2023): material connections such as commissions must be clearly and conspicuously disclosed; advertisers can be liable for endorsers' deceptive claims. (Manatt, Mondaq summaries of FTC update)

## 5. Free VPNs and data
- Onavo Protect (Meta-owned free VPN app): Federal Court of Australia in 2023 ordered Facebook Israel and Onavo Inc to pay A$10M each (A$20M total) for misleading users; app promoted as keeping data protected while personal activity data was shared (aggregated/anonymised) with Meta for commercial benefit; installed 270,000+ times in Australia Feb 2016-Oct 2017. Meta noted the ACCC acknowledged listings were not deliberately misleading and disclosures were in its terms. (ACCC; B&T; iTWire; TechCrunch)
- CSIRO/UNSW/ICSI 2016 study of 283 Android VPN apps: 75% used third-party tracking libraries, 38%+ had some malware presence, 18% did not encrypt traffic (CSIRO paper; CIO; Technology Decisions).

## 6. Market size (estimates only, weak)
- Fortune Business Insights (via secondary page): global VPN market $69.82B in 2025 (includes enterprise). TheBestVPN: $77.8B in 2025. Estimates vary widely; not used in script.

## Surprising fact
The company that owns ExpressVPN, CyberGhost and Private Internet Access also owns VPN review sites (vpnMentor, Wizcase).
