# 2026-11-12-lf-world research: Emerging markets: India, Brazil, Mexico explained

## Term and index
- "Emerging markets" coined in 1981 by Antoine van Agtmael of the World Bank's IFC (World Bank timeline), to replace "Third World" when pitching a fund.
- MSCI Emerging Markets Index covers 24 countries (MSCI factsheet). Exact country weights vary by month; India, Brazil and Mexico are all members.

## IMF World Economic Outlook, April 2026
- 2026 real GDP growth forecasts: India 6.5%, Brazil 1.9%, Mexico 1.6%.
- Nominal GDP 2026 (USD): US $32.38T, China $20.85T, Germany $5.45T, Japan $4.38T, UK $4.26T, India $4.15T (6th), Brazil $2.64T.
- GDP per capita 2026 (nominal USD): India $2,813; Brazil $12,313; Mexico $15,779. Mexico / India = 5.6x.

## Population (UN World Population Prospects 2024)
- 2025: India 1.4639 billion (largest in world); Brazil 212.8 million; Mexico 132.0 million. India median age 29.2.

## India
- Remittances: largest recipient in the world; $135.4B in FY2024-25 (Government of India / RBI, Economic Survey).
- Generic drugs: Indian companies supplied 47% of US generic prescriptions in 2022 (IQVIA, cited by CT Mirror Oct 2025).
- RBI repo rate: 5.25% (held at August 2026 meeting).
- Rupee per US dollar, annual average (Federal Reserve G.5A via FRED AEXINUS): 2021 73.94; 2022 78.58; 2023 82.57; 2024 83.66; 2025 87.15.
  Worked: $100 bought 7,394 rupees in 2021 vs 8,715 in 2025 (+1,321, +17.9%).
- US tariffs on Indian goods changed several times in 2025-2026 (50% in Aug 2025; deal cut to 18% Feb 2026; later changes). Not used in script beyond "tariffs have swung".

## Brazil
- Selic: cut to 13.75% on Sept 16, 2026 from 14.00% (fifth straight quarter-point cut, so from 15.00%) (Bloomberg / Rio Times).
- Soybeans: ~80% of Brazil's soybean exports went to China in Jan-Oct 2025 (USDA AMS / Purdue). China is Brazil's largest buyer.
- World's largest coffee producer and exporter (USDA FAS Coffee Annual 2026).

## Mexico
- Top US goods trading partner in 2025: $872.8B total goods trade (Census via CRS IF11175). US goods deficit with Mexico $171.5B in 2025 vs $1.7B surplus in 1993.
- Avocados: 83% of US fresh avocado imports in 2025 (USDA data).
- Remittances: $61.8B in 2025, down 4.6%, biggest fall since 2009 (Banxico).
- Banxico rate: 6.50%, held Sept 24, 2026 (third straight hold).

## How it reaches an American wallet
- Prices of avocados, coffee, generic drugs; jobs/trade links; many US retirement accounts hold EM funds (MSCI EM index); a stronger dollar makes imports cheaper and EM debts in dollars more expensive.
