# Research brief: How Disney makes money (lf-business, posts Tue Dec 22, 2026)

Prepared Friday, October 23, 2026. Disney's fiscal year 2026 (FY2026) ended in early October 2026 (a 53-week year); FY2026 full-year results are expected around mid-November 2026 and were NOT available when this was written. So: full-year figures = fiscal 2025 (year ended September 27, 2025, Form 10-K / Q4 FY2025 release of Nov 13, 2025). Latest quarter = Q3 fiscal 2026 (quarter ended June 27, 2026, released August 5, 2026). Always label the period; never say "latest" or "this year".

## Timely angle
- Disney+ and Hulu raised prices effective September 23, 2026: ad-free Disney+ (Premium) went from $18.99 to $21.49 a month; ad-supported Disney+ from $11.99 to $12.49 (Hollywood Reporter / AppleInsider / Yahoo, Sept 2026).
- Josh D'Amaro, previously chairman of Disney Experiences (the parks division), was named CEO on Feb 3, 2026, to succeed Bob Iger effective March 18, 2026 (Bloomberg, ABC, Yahoo Finance, Feb 3, 2026). A parks chief running the whole company fits the surprise.
- The video posts during the December holiday peak, when Magic Kingdom one-day tickets hit their 2026 top price ($209 on select late-Nov/Dec dates).

## The surprise (hook)
FY2025 segment operating income (10-K):
- Experiences (parks, cruises, consumer products): $9,995M (record ~$10.0B, +$723M vs FY2024 $9,272M)
- Entertainment (TV networks, streaming, movies): $4,674M (+19% from $3,923M)
- Sports (ESPN): $2,882M (+20% from ~$2.4B)
- Total segment operating income: $17,551M (~$17.6B, +12% from $15.6B)
=> Experiences ($10.0B) earned more than Entertainment + Sports combined ($4.674B + $2.882B = $7.556B).
=> Experiences = 9,995 / 17,551 = 56.9% (about 57%) of segment operating income, but 36,156 / 94,400 = 38.3% (about 38%) of total revenue.

## FY2025 segment revenue (10-K, year ended Sept 27, 2025)
- Total revenue: $94.4B (+3% from $91.4B)
- Entertainment: $42,466M ($42.5B), +3%
- Sports: $17,672M ($17.7B), flat
- Experiences: $36,156M ($36.2B), up from $34.2B
(Segment revenues sum above $94.4B because of intersegment eliminations.)
Operating margins (segment OI / segment revenue, my math):
- Experiences 9,995 / 36,156 = 27.6% (about 28 cents per dollar)
- Sports 2,882 / 17,672 = 16.3%
- Entertainment 4,674 / 42,466 = 11.0% (about 11 cents per dollar)

## Entertainment sub-lines, FY2025 (10-K)
- Linear Networks (ABC, Disney Channel, FX, etc.): revenue $9,364M (down 12% from $10,692M); OI $2,955M (down 14% from $3,452M)
- Direct-to-Consumer (Disney+, Hulu): revenue $24,614M (+8% from $22,776M); OI $1,327M vs $143M in FY2024
- Content Sales/Licensing and Other (box office, licensing, home entertainment): OI $392M (up from $328M)
- Check: 2,955 + 1,327 + 392 = 4,674 = Entertainment OI.

## Q3 fiscal 2026 (quarter ended June 27, 2026; released Aug 5, 2026; SEC 8-K, shareholder letter)
- Revenue $25.2B (+7%); total segment OI $5.6B (+21% from $4.6B); adjusted EPS $2.06 vs $1.61
- Experiences: revenue ~$9.97B (+10%), OI $3.02B (+20%) (a tariff refund contributed; per Yahoo/Seasonal Entertainment Source ~4 points)
- Entertainment: revenue $11,345M (+6%), OI $1.68B (+64%)
- Streaming ("Entertainment SVOD") OI $712M vs $329M a year earlier, operating margin about 13% (Disney release; NewscastStudio, Variety)
- Sports OI $858M, down 17% (Yahoo Finance summary)
- Disney expects double-digit SVOD margin for FY2026 (company outlook, excluding 53rd week). Do not use as a prediction.

## Parks ticket prices (Magic Kingdom, adult one-day)
- Oct 1971: $3.50 admission, most rides cost extra (ticket books) (Spectrum News 13 2014; disneyworldorlandoflorida.com; Cheapism)
- Late 1982: $15 one-day ticket after ride coupons ended; Dec 1987: $28 (Cheapism / Park Lore list)
- June 2012: at least $89 for one day at any Disney World park (Spectrum News 13, 2014)
- 2016: date-based pricing begins
- 2026: Magic Kingdom one-day adult ranges $139 to $209 depending on date; child (3-9) ticket is $5 less than the adult price for the same date; $209 on select late-November/December 2026 dates (TouringPlans "Every Regular Disney World Ticket Price in 2026"; WDWMagic April 22, 2025 for Jan-Oct range $139-$199; Theme Park Shark March 2026). Prices before tax.
Note: sources conflict on some 1980s-2000s years, so no evenly spaced chart; use a 4-point timeline (1971, 1987, 2012, 2026) instead.

## WORKED EXAMPLE (labeled example; 2026 prices before tax)
Family of four: 2 adults + 2 kids aged 3-9, one day at Magic Kingdom.
- Peak date ($209 adult, $204 child): 2 x $209 = $418; 2 x $204 = $408; total = $826
- Lowest date ($139 adult, $134 child): 2 x $139 = $278; 2 x $134 = $268; total = $546
- Difference: $826 - $546 = $280 for the same park, same family, different date.
- Excludes tax, parking, food, hotel, Lightning Lane.
Streaming math: ad-free Disney+ at $21.49/month x 12 = $257.88 a year (price effective Sept 23, 2026).

## Cruises and investment
- Sept 2023: Disney said it planned to spend about $60B on parks and cruises over ~10 years, nearly double the prior decade (AP via KSAT/WITN, Sept 19, 2023; Disney filing). About 70% to parks (Iger, earnings call).
- Disney Adventure (Disney Cruise Line's 8th and largest ship, about 6,700 passengers, based in Singapore) began its maiden voyage March 10, 2026 after shipbuilding delays (Disney christening release; CNBC Mar 4, 2026; Blooloop). Disney said the fleet has 8 ships with 5 more planned by 2031 (as of March 2026).

## ESPN / Sports
- FY2025 Sports revenue $17.7B, OI $2.9B.
- ESPN's standalone streaming service ("ESPN" DTC / "ESPN Unlimited") launched Aug 21, 2025 at $29.99/month (Hartford Business / ESPN, Aug 2025). Existing ESPN+ users moved to a lower tier.
- Aug 2025: ESPN and NFL agreed (subject to approvals) that the NFL would take a 10% stake in ESPN in exchange for NFL Network and other media assets (Fox Business, ESPN). Closing status not confirmed -> do not state as completed.
- Sports OI fell 17% in Q3 FY2026 to $858M (rights costs pressure).

## The flywheel
- In 1957 Walt Disney drew a chart with theatrical films at the center, connected by arrows to TV, music, publishing, merchandise and Disneyland (FlowingData 2015; Economist 2016; Daring Fireball 2023). Paraphrase only; no direct quote.
- Modern version: a film creates characters -> streaming library -> park rides/meet-and-greets -> toys/merch -> cruises; each part advertises the others.

## Risks
- Linear TV shrinking: Linear Networks revenue -12% and OI -14% in FY2025.
- Sports rights get more expensive; Sports OI -17% in Q3 FY2026.
- Parks depend on consumer spending and travel; competition (Comcast's Universal opened Epic Universe in Orlando in May 2025 - well known, general knowledge).
- Heavy capital spending (~$60B plan) must pay off.
- Streaming: subscribers no longer disclosed; growth now comes partly from price increases.

## For a normal person (education only)
- Ticket prices vary by date (date-based pricing since 2016): $280 difference in the example.
- Streaming price increases (Sept 2026) are now a profit lever.
- Disney is a component of the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average, so broad index-fund owners hold a small slice of Disney (general knowledge). No advice.
