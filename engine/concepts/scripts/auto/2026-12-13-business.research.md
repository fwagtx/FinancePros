## Core facts (dated)
1. FY2025 total net revenue: $12.5 billion (about $12,457M = FY2024 $11,851M + $606M), up 5.1% y/y. US net revenue $11.0B. Source: Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 19, 2026; edgar.tools shows $12.46B.
2. FY2025 gross profit: about $3.8 billion = 30.2% of net revenue (FY2024 also ~30.2%). Source: Q4/FY2025 release / trade coverage.
3. FY2025 net loss: $313 million (diluted EPS -$2.44); Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) $743M. Source: Q4/FY2025 release.
4. Inventories on balance sheet: $71 million at Dec 31, 2025 (vs $76M at Dec 31, 2024). Source: Q4/FY2025 release balance sheet (via search), StockAnalysis matches. One analyst blog ("eightx" teardown) says $76M for FY2025 - that appears to be the 2024 figure; two sources say $71M for 2025.
   - Math: $71M / $12,457M = 0.57% of annual net revenue (under 1%).
5. Accounts payable ~$1,202M at Dec 31, 2025 (aggregators only; not used on screen).
6. Catalog: FY2025 10-K overview: "over 40 million products from approximately 20 thousand suppliers" (FY2024 10-K: over 30 million products, over 20 thousand suppliers). Source: FY2025 10-K via search.
7. Drop-ship model (10-K language, consistent FY2016-FY2025): orders are sent through integration with suppliers' back-end systems directly to a supplier's warehouse; Wayfair arranges shipment from the supplier's loading dock to the customer's home. Drop-ship is described as the primary fulfillment method; increasingly fulfilled through CastleGate and the Wayfair Delivery Network (WDN).
8. Revenue recognition (FY2025 10-K): Wayfair records product sales GROSS (full price as revenue) when it concludes it controls the product before transfer: it is responsible for fulfilling the promise to the customer, accepts responsibility for acceptability of goods, bears inventory risk from shipment through delivery, sets prices and chooses suppliers. Revenue recognized on delivery.
   - So: Wayfair rarely holds stock long-term, but it does briefly bear the risk in transit; do NOT say it "never owns" anything.
9. CastleGate: Wayfair's logistics network where suppliers position their inventory in Wayfair-run fulfillment centers closer to shoppers (Wayfair seller site sell.wayfair.com; Retail Dive 2024 quoting Wayfair exec: "suppliers also leverage the CastleGate fulfillment network to position inventory"). Seller site: "CastleGate operates 15 fulfillment centers across three countries"; supports two-day delivery to 97% of Wayfair customers (company marketing claim, undated). Suppliers own the stock and pay storage fees (supplier interview, In Practise - secondary). CastleGate Forwarding handles inbound ocean freight from Asia (FY2025 10-K). Subsidiaries CastleGate Logistics Inc. (US) and CastleGate Logistics Canada Inc. (10-K Exhibit 21.1).
   - Wayfair exec (Retail Dive): CastleGate has "materially higher exact on-time reliability rates than drop-ship".
10. Q2 2026 (quarter ended Jun 30, 2026; release Aug 4, 2026): net revenue $3.5B (+7.5%); gross profit $1,054M = 30.0% of net revenue; active customers 21.7M (+3.3%); orders delivered 10.6M; average order value $332 (vs $328); net loss $1M; Adj. EBITDA $242M; free cash flow $301M.
11. Q4 2025: active customers 21.3M; repeat customers placed 79.1% of orders.
12. Wayfair Ads / supplier advertising: 10-K mentions suppliers can buy sponsored placements, but NO dollar figure for ad revenue is disclosed. Not used in script.

## Worked example (illustration using averages, Q2 2026)
- Average order value Q2 2026: $332. Gross margin Q2 2026: 30.0%.
- $332 x 0.300 = $99.60 gross profit kept by Wayfair (illustrative: applies the average margin to the average order).
- $332 - $99.60 = $232.40 = cost of goods sold (mainly what Wayfair pays the supplier for the product plus shipping/fulfillment costs, which Wayfair includes in cost of goods sold).
- Gross profit then must cover advertising, technology, staff, overhead; FY2025 ended with a $313M net loss.

## Surprising fact
A company with ~$12.5B in 2025 sales had only $71M of inventory at year-end (under 1% of sales): ~20,000 suppliers hold most of the stock and ship it, or park it in Wayfair's CastleGate warehouses.
