# Research brief: How Chase makes money from Sapphire Reserve's fee and travel portal
Slot: 2026-12-09-business (How They Make Money). Researched Saturday, October 10, 2026. Posts Wednesday, December 9, 2026.
Most recent JPMorgan annual filings: FY2025 Form 10-K (filed Feb 2026) and the 2025 Annual Report. Q3 2026 earnings had not been released as of the research date, so full-year FY2025 is the latest annual period.

Note on access: sec.gov and jpmorganchase.com could not be fetched directly from this environment (proxy blocked). The figures below are taken from search results that quote those official documents directly, cross-checked where possible.

## Angle and surprising fact
- Hook fact: Chase (the bank) booked $13 billion of travel through Chase Travel in 2025, and JPMorgan calls Chase Travel the #3 consumer leisure travel provider in the US. Most people think of it as a points portal, not one of the country's largest travel sellers.
- Surprise: even though Sapphire Reserve's fee jumped to $795 in 2025, JPMorgan's total card income FELL 14% in FY2025 ($5.497B -> $4.720B). The 10-K says net interchange fell because higher rewards costs and partner payments more than offset higher card sales volume, partly offset by higher annual fees. The perks are the cost; the fee, portal and interest help cover it.
- Avoids recent videos: not about swipe-fee totals (Nov 28) or airline co-brand deals (Dec 1).

## Facts (with dates and sources)
### Sapphire Reserve 2025 refresh
- Annual fee raised from $550 to $795. Applies to new applicants from June 23, 2025; existing cardholders at the first renewal on or after October 26, 2025. (NerdWallet; Frequent Miler; One Mile at a Time; Bankrate; Doctor of Credit)
- Authorized user fee went from $75 to $195 (NerdWallet).
- Earning (since the refresh; June 23, 2025 for new cardholders, Oct 26, 2025 for existing): 8x points on all Chase Travel purchases (replacing 5x flights / 10x hotels and car rentals via Chase Travel); 4x on flights and hotels booked direct (replacing 3x on all travel); 3x dining; 1x other. (AwardWallet, Frequent Miler, NerdWallet, Motley Fool)
- Points Boost: base 1 cent per point; up to 2 cents per point on select ("top booked") flights and hotels booked through Chase Travel. Old fixed 1.5 cents via portal grandfathered for pre-Oct 26, 2025 points until Oct 26, 2027. (AwardWallet, NerdWallet)
- New credits include $500/yr for The Edit hotel collection (two $250 credits), booked through Chase Travel (multiple outlets). Not used in script.

### Chase Travel (the portal)
- "Chase Travel is the #3 consumer leisure travel provider in the U.S., with $13 billion in booked volume in 2025, tripling since 2021 and up 12% year-over-year." JPMorgan Chase 2025 Annual Report (Form ARS, FY2025). This is JPMorgan's own ranking claim (attribute: "per JPMorgan").
- Cross-check: Travel Weekly Power List 2026 lists Chase Travel Group 2025 sales of $12.6B (different definition, sales vs booked volume).
- 2025 Investor Day (May 19, 2025): 4.2 million customers booked travel in 2024; #3 US consumer leisure travel provider.
- Chase built the portal in-house via acquisitions of cxLoyalty (deal 2020, closed Jan 2021) and Frosch (2022), moving off Expedia. As a travel agency, it earns booking commissions (trade press; Chase executive comments from 2021 suggest ~10% industry commissions, single source, NOT used in script).

### JPMorgan card income (the chartable series), FY = calendar year
"Card income" (consolidated income statement) = interchange and merchant processing income, minus reward costs and partner payments, plus other card income (annual fees etc.), firmwide.
| Year | Card income ($M) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 4,420 | JPMorgan 10-K (FY2024 three-year table, via search excerpt) |
| 2023 | 4,784 | JPMorgan 10-K (FY2024 three-year table, via search excerpt) |
| 2024 | 5,497 | JPMorgan 4Q25 Earnings Release Financial Supplement |
| 2025 | 4,720 | JPMorgan 4Q25 Earnings Release Financial Supplement (-14% y/y) |
Chart in $B: [2022, 4.42], [2023, 4.78], [2024, 5.50], [2025, 4.72].
- Math: (5,497 - 4,720) / 5,497 = 777 / 5,497 = 14.1% decline -> "14%".
- FY2025 10-K explanation (MD&A): card income decreased driven by lower income in CCB, reflecting lower net interchange income, as well as higher amortization of new account origination costs, partially offset by higher annual fees. "Net interchange income decreased as the impact of increased debit and credit card sales volume was more than offset by higher rewards costs and partner payments."
- Accounting (JPMorgan 10-K): partner loyalty program rewards are reported as a reduction of card income; rewards cost is netted against interchange.
- Unconfirmed / not used: a blog (lji.io) says FY2025 interchange & merchant processing income was $36.2B and reward costs/partner payments $29.7B (about 82%). Single secondary source; not used.

### Other JPMorgan card figures (FY2025 / 4Q25)
- Debit and credit card sales volume up 7% y/y in 4Q25; 10.4 million new card accounts in FY2025 (4Q25 earnings call).
- 4Q25: NII ex-Markets increase "primarily driven by higher firm-wide deposit balances and revolving balances in card" (4Q25 release / coverage). Supports: interest on card balances is a revenue source.
- Card Services net charge-off rate 3.14% in 4Q25; FY2026 guidance ~3.4% (FY2025 10-K).
- Card Services & Auto net revenue $7.3B in 4Q25, up 5% (includes auto; not used).

## Worked example (exact math)
Example: a $1,000 hotel booking by a Sapphire Reserve cardholder (rates since the 2025 refresh).
- Through Chase Travel: 1,000 x 8 = 8,000 points. Booked direct with the hotel: 1,000 x 4 = 4,000 points. Portal earns 2x as many points.
- The difference is Chase's lever: it pays more points to keep the booking inside its own travel agency, where it (per trade press) earns the booking commission. Script uses the per-dollar rates (8x vs 4x) rather than the commission figure.

## Script facts used
1. $13B Chase Travel booked volume, 2025; up 12%; #3 US consumer leisure travel provider per JPMorgan (2025 Annual Report).
2. Reserve fee $550 -> $795 (2025 refresh; June 23, 2025 for new applicants).
3. 8x Chase Travel vs 4x direct flights/hotels; Points Boost up to 2 cents/point on select Chase Travel bookings.
4. JPMorgan card income FY2022-FY2025 chart; -14% in 2025 to $4.72B.
5. 10-K reason: rewards costs and partner payments outran sales growth; higher annual fees partly offset.
6. Interest on revolving card balances helped NII (4Q25).
