## Facts (picked story)
- CNBC (Oct 15, 2026): 20 of the most valuable global AI and semiconductor stocks shed more than $1 trillion in market value over four trading sessions, Monday Oct 12 through Thursday Oct 15, 2026. Cited causes: interest-rate-hike fears, AI financing worries, competition.
  - Seoul Economic Daily (Oct 16) and SBS (Korea) repeat: "AI, chip stocks lose $1 trillion in market value in four days."
- Nvidia: share price fell 5.3% over Oct 12-15, wiping out about $291 billion in market value (CNBC figures as relayed by search summaries; single-outlet number).
- Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron together accounted for about 40% of the total loss (CNBC via search summary). SK hynix lost ~$243.8B, Samsung ~$237.3B.
- iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) fell eight straight sessions through the Oct 15 close ($514.71), down about 12.6% over the streak; Seeking Alpha calls it the longest streak since Sept 2015 (Yahoo says since Oct 2008 - conflicting, so the "longest since" is NOT used). Early Oct 16 reports said it was heading for a ninth decline (not final at writing time; not used).
- Causes reported (CNBC, Forbes Oct 15, CNN Oct 15, Yahoo): hotter-than-expected inflation data revived Fed rate-hike fears, which would make debt-funded AI buildouts more expensive; investors questioning whether data center/chip spending pays off fast enough; worries about CoreWeave's debt load; memory chip oversupply/competition concerns.
- Bonds: investors moved into Treasuries. 10-year Treasury yield closed about 5.061% on Thu Oct 15, down 12.8 basis points (Investing.com); Trading Economics 5.08%; CNBC intraday 5.089%, "down more than 10 basis points."
- Context: in late July 2026, CNBC reported 20 of the world's most valuable chip stocks lost $1.3 trillion in a multi-day selloff (CNBC, July 29, 2026).
- Counterpoint: TSMC posted a sixth straight quarter of record profit (CNBC Daily Open, Oct 16, 2026).
- Friday Oct 16 midday: Nasdaq Composite down about 1.4% (~26,343) at 12:06 pm ET; Micron -3.9%, AMD -3.3% in morning trading (intraday, not final; not used as final numbers).

## What it means for a normal person
- Nvidia, Micron, Broadcom, AMD etc. are large members of the S&P 500, so S&P 500 index funds and many 401(k) target-date funds hold them; big moves in these stocks show up in those balances.
- Mechanism: AI buildout is heavily financed with debt; higher rates raise the cost; that's why inflation data moved chip stocks.

## What to watch next
- Friday Oct 16 close (whether SOXX streak hits nine), Fed rate expectations, upcoming big-tech earnings and their AI spending plans.
