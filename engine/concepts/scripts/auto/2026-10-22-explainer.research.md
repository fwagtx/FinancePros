# Research brief: What a stock split is (and why it doesn't make you richer)
Slot: 2026-10-22-explainer (posts Thursday, October 22, 2026). Researched September 30, 2026.

## Definition (official)
- SEC (Fast Answers, "Stock Split"): a stock split is an increase in the number of a corporation's shares without a change in shareholders' equity. Companies often split shares to make them more affordable to investors. The share price decreases, but a shareholder's total market value stays the same. SEC example: 100 shares at $100, 2-for-1 split -> 200 shares at $50. A split does not dilute existing owners. Dividends per share fall proportionately.

- Announced February 18, 2026 (Booking Holdings 8-K / Q4 2025 earnings release).

- Shares outstanding after the split: 774,878,436 as of April 20, 2026 (Booking Q1 2026 10-Q cover) = about 775 million.

## Worked example (hypothetical, round numbers)
- Assume 1 share at $4,000 (round illustration, close to Booking's pre-split level).
- 25-for-1 split: 1 x 25 = 25 shares; $4,000 / 25 = $160 per share.
- Value check: 25 x $160 = $4,000. Before = after.

## Chartable real data series: one pre-2000 Nvidia share, cumulative share count after each split
Nvidia splits: 2-for-1 (June 2000), 2-for-1 (Sept 2001), 2-for-1 (April 2006), 3-for-2 (Sept 2007), 4-for-1 (July 2021), 10-for-1 (June 2024; record June 6, distributed after close June 7, split-adjusted trading June 10, 2024 per Nvidia 8-K May 22, 2024).
| Year | Shares from 1 original share |
|---|---|
| 1999 | 1 |
| 2000 | 2 |
| 2001 | 4 |
| 2006 | 8 |
| 2007 | 12 |
| 2021 | 48 |
| 2024 | 480 |
Math: 2 x 2 x 2 x 1.5 x 4 x 10 = 480. (Motley Fool, May 3, 2026: "Nvidia Has Split Its Stock 480-for-1 Since 2000"; Investing.com split history; stocksplithistory.com.)
Price context: the 2024 split took Nvidia from over $1,200 a share to around $120 (capital.com).

## Surprising fact
- One Nvidia share held before 2000 is now 480 shares, but the splits alone added zero value; each split cut the price per share by the same factor. Any gain came from the business (and market price) growing, not from the splits.

## Extra (not used in script)
- Morningstar/FactSet: 54 splits among current S&P 500 members over the prior decade (article ~2024); one year later, 34 were up and 20 were down. Mixed results. Article date not pinned, so not used.
