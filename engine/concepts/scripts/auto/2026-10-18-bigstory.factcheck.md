## Issues and fixes

1. **Chart x-axis labels (L2), minor.** The x values are bare day numbers 13 to 16. If the renderer prints these as axis labels, viewers see "13, 14, 15, 16" with no month. Also, the 22% point sits at "13" but was really the reading just before the 8:30 a.m. release on Oct 14. Yahoo describes it as "22% a day earlier", so 13 is defensible. Fix: if the template supports string or date labels, use "10/13", "10/14", "10/15", "10/16". If it does not, change the chart title to make the month explicit:
   - `"title": "Odds of Oct Fed hike"` -> `"title": "Oct hike odds, Oct 13-16"`
   The points themselves need no change.

2. **"Jumped to 50%" is the intraday peak (L2), minor, accuracy nuance.** The odds hit about 50% within an hour of CPI and closed the day near 32%. The line is not wrong, but "as high as" is more precise and costs 3 spoken words (129 -> 132, still in range):

   - say: `After a hot inflation report on October fourteenth, the odds jumped from twenty-two to as high as fifty percent, per C M E FedWatch.`
   This is optional; the current text is defensible.

3. **"Just" timing words (hook, cover, post.title).** The odds collapsed Thursday and Friday (Oct 15-16), and the video posts Sunday Oct 18, two days later. "Just" is borderline but acceptable for a 2-day-old move, and the story is still live: the meeting is Oct 27-28 and odds remain near 15%. No change is required. A stricter option:

   - post.title: `Wall Street's bet on an October Fed hike collapsed this week` (59 chars)

4. **Quote source line (L3), optional.** Adding the venue strengthens attribution:
