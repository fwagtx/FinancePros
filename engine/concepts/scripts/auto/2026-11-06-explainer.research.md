# Research brief: Pay transparency laws - why job posts show salary ranges
Slot 2026-11-06-explainer. Researched October 1, 2026. Posts November 6, 2026. All figures dated; no legal advice.

## Key data (Indeed Hiring Lab, share of US Indeed postings with explicit pay info)
Source: Indeed Hiring Lab public dataset, github.com/hiring-lab/pay-transparency (pay-transparency-country.csv, last updated Sep 16, 2026).
Hiring Lab's published figures use the 3-month average column (its US snapshot reported 50.4% for July 2026 = the 3ma value 50.38).
Chartable series, US, August of each year, 3-month average (%):
- Aug 2020: 19.8
- Aug 2021: 25.8
- Aug 2022: 28.0
- Aug 2023: 39.7   (largest one-year rise: +11.7 points, Aug 2022 -> Aug 2023; NYC law took effect Nov 2022, CA and WA Jan 2023 - timing only, not a causal claim)
- Aug 2024: 45.7
- Aug 2025: 48.6
- Aug 2026: 51.1   (latest point; monthly unsmoothed value 51.8)
- July 2026 3ma: 50.4% - Hiring Lab called it the highest reading in a series starting January 2020.
By sector, Aug 2026 (3ma): Childcare 81.2% (highest), Pharmacy 28.4% (lowest), Physicians & Surgeons 32.6%.
Other countries (Hiring Lab, mid-2026): Canada 57% (June 2026); UK 56%, Germany 12% (Hiring Lab UK, May 2026).

## Laws requiring pay in job postings (effective dates, verified by two+ sources)
- Colorado, Equal Pay for Equal Work Act: Jan 1, 2021 (first state to require pay ranges in postings). HR Dive; Trusaic.
- New York City (Int. 134-A): Nov 1, 2022; employers with 4+ employees. Crowell; SGR Law.
- California (SB 1162, 15+ employees) and Washington: Jan 1, 2023. CNBC Dec 2022; Littler.
- (Also: New York State Sept 17, 2023; Hawaii Jan 1, 2024; DC June 30, 2024; Maryland Oct 1, 2024 - not used in script.)
- Illinois (15+ employees) and Minnesota (30+): Jan 1, 2025. Gunderson; Foley.
- New Jersey (10+): June 1, 2025. Vermont (5+): July 1, 2025. Massachusetts (25+): Oct 29, 2025. SHRM; Hunton.
- Cleveland, Ohio: Oct 27, 2025 (15+). Columbus, Ohio: ordinance effective Dec 3, 2025, enforcement from Jan 1, 2027. Ogletree; NatLawReview.
- Virginia: signed Apr 22, 2026, effective July 1, 2026, no size threshold, also bans pay-history questions. Williams Mullen; Morgan Lewis; Governor's post.
- Maine (LD 54): signed Apr 24, 2026, effective July 29, 2026 (10+ employees). Morgan Lewis; Brann & Isaacson.
- Delaware: effective Sept 26, 2027 (26+ employees) - takes effect AFTER the Nov 6, 2026 posting date. Foley (May 2026); Clockspot.
- Nothing new found taking effect between Oct 1 and Nov 6, 2026 in the US. Greece's EU-directive employer obligations apply from Nov 1, 2026 (Mayer Brown, July 2026).

## EU
- EU Pay Transparency Directive (2023/970): member states had to transpose by June 7, 2026. Article 5: employers must give applicants the initial pay or its range, in the vacancy notice or before the interview; bans asking about pay history. EU Council; European Commission explainer (June 5, 2026).
- Only four of 27 met the deadline (Slovakia, Italy, Lithuania, Malta) per Mayer Brown / DCI Consult (July 2026); Commission declined to extend.

## Surprising fact
After Colorado's law took effect in 2021, some national employers (WSJ named Johnson & Johnson, McKesson, Cardinal Health) posted remote jobs saying they could not be done from Colorado. Colorado's labor department (CDLE) said on July 21, 2021 that such remote postings by Colorado employers still had to comply. Sources: Axios (June 19, 2021); HR Dive; CDLE compliance letter; Gibson Dunn.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
A posting lists $60,000 to $75,000. Spread = 75,000 - 60,000 = $15,000. As a share of the bottom: 15,000 / 60,000 = 25%.
