# Research brief: How Keurig makes money on coffee pods, not machines
Made Nov 23, 2026 for posting Fri Jan 22, 2027 (evergreen; every number dated).
Note: sec.gov and the KDP newsroom were not directly fetchable from this session; figures below were confirmed through web search results that quote the filings/releases (listed in SOURCES). Flagged items are marked UNCERTAIN.

## Timely angle (no "this week" news needed)
- Aug 25, 2025: Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP) announced a deal to buy JDE Peet's (EUR 31.85/share, ~EUR 15.7B equity value; law firm Cleary Gottlieb describes it as ~$18B) and to later separate into two U.S.-listed companies: "Beverage Co." and "Global Coffee Co." (Keurig single-serve + JDE Peet's).
- Apr 1, 2026: KDP acquired 96.22% of JDE Peet's shares (joint release). KDP Q3 2026 10-Q: owns 100% of JDE Peet's ordinary shares as of Oct 26, 2026.
- Separation: KDP's 2026 SEC filings say it is expected "in early 2027, subject to market and other conditions" and may not happen on that timeline. NOT complete as of Nov 23, 2026. It could happen before or after Jan 22, 2027, so the script must not say whether the split has happened. Safe wording: "closed its deal for JDE Peet's in April 2026, part of a plan to split coffee into its own company."
- Leadership for Global Coffee Co. changed several times in 2026 (Rafael Oliveira named Apr 2026, then left; a June 23, 2026 release; Russ Torres reportedly named later). UNCERTAIN; do not use names.

## Core numbers (KDP FY2025 Form 10-K, filed Feb 24, 2026; Q4/FY2025 release Feb 24, 2026)
- U.S. Coffee segment net sales, FY2025: $3,990 million (+0.6% vs $3,967M in 2024). Net price +4.8%, volume/mix -4.2%.
- Growth "driven by an increase in sales for K-Cup pods, partially offset by a decline in brewer sales."
- K-Cup pod volume FY2025: -4.8% (price elasticity). Appliance (brewer) volume FY2025: -19.9% (10-K volume table, per search summary; moderately confident).
- Net sales disaggregation FY2025 (10-K): K-Cup pods $3,213M in U.S. Coffee ($564M International; $3,777M total).
- OUR MATH: pods share of U.S. Coffee net sales 2025 = 3,213 / 3,990 = 80.5% ("about 80%"). Rest (brewers/appliances and other) = 3,990 - 3,213 = $777M (19.5%).
- U.S. Coffee FY2025 GAAP operating income $962M (-10.8%); adjusted operating income $1,233M = 30.9% of net sales (adjusted, non-GAAP). OUR MATH check: 1,233/3,990 = 30.9%.
- Q3 2026 (Nov 3, 2026 release): U.S. Coffee net sales about $1.02B, +2.7% (secondary summaries; not used in script).
- Older comparison (FY2023 10-K disaggregation): U.S. Coffee pods $3,207M, appliances $725M of $4,071M.
- Pre-merger proof the model is old: Keurig Green Mountain FY2014 10-K (fiscal year ended Sept 2014): portion packs $3,604.5M, brewers and accessories $822.3M, of $4,707.7M total net sales (our math: pods ~77%).

## Worked example (OUR MATH, illustrative price)
- Ground coffee: BLS average price, coffee 100% ground roast, all sizes, U.S. city average (series APU0000717311): $9.459/lb in February 2026 (FRED/BLS). Screen: $9.46.
- Pods: example retail listing, Walmart, Green Mountain 24-count K-Cup box at $15.57, shown as $1.97 per ounce (listing undated; ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY).
- OUR MATH: $1.97/oz x 16 oz = $31.52 per pound of pod coffee. 31.52 / 9.46 = 3.3x the average price of ground coffee per pound.
- Caveat: brands/blends differ; illustrative, not a price survey.

## Keurig 2.0 "pod lock" episode
- 2014: Keurig 2.0 brewers launched with a system that read pod lids and rejected unlicensed (non-Keurig-licensed) pods; it also did not accept the old My K-Cup reusable filter (Engadget Feb 2015, Quartz, Fortune May 2015, TechCrunch May 2015).
- Fiscal Q2 2015 (quarter ended March 2015; reported May 6, 2015): brewer and accessory net sales fell 23% vs prior year (company release attributes it mainly to a 22% drop in brewer volume and high retail inventory; CEO tied the slump to the slower 2.0 transition).
- CEO Brian Kelley on the call (May 2015): "Quite honestly, we were wrong" (about removing My K-Cup) (The Register, VTDigger; one transcript has "we're wrong"). Keurig brought back a My K-Cup for 2.0. It did not formally drop the lock then (Fox Business). Do not claim when/if it was fully dropped.

## Surprising fact
- About 80% of Keurig's U.S. coffee sales in 2025 came from pods, not brewers (our math from the 10-K), and the segment's adjusted operating margin was 30.9%.
