# Research: Why Domino's makes money selling dough to its franchisees (2026-11-24-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts (Domino's Pizza, Inc. FY2025 10-K and Q4 FY2025 release)
- FY2025 (ended Dec 28, 2025) total revenues $4,940.0M (+5.0%). Supply chain revenues $2,989.5M = 60.5% of revenues. U.S. franchise royalties and fees $677.1M.
- Supply chain revenues: FY2022 $2,754.7M; FY2023 $2,715.0M; FY2024 $2,845.8M; FY2025 $2,989.5M.
- Supply chain centers sell food, equipment and supplies (including dough) to U.S./Canadian stores.
- Profit sharing: Domino's generally offers company-owned stores and participating franchisees that buy all their food from the supply chain centers 50% of the centers' pre-tax profit (10-K).
- Supply chain gross margin: 11.3% in Q3 2025; 11.6% in Q1 2025; 12.2% in Q1 2026.
- U.S. franchisees generally pay a 5.5% royalty on sales plus technology fees, and contribute 6.0% of sales to national advertising (10-K).

## Worked example (hypothetical store, $1,000,000 annual sales)
