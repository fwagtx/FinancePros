# Fact-check: 2026-10-20-business ("How Free Google Search Makes Billions")

Posting date: Oct 20, 2026. Checked Oct 1, 2026.

## Verified claims
- **2025 Search & other = $224.5B.** Alphabet FY2025 10-K: $224,532M (2024: $198,084M). Correct.
- **Per-day / per-second math.** 224.532B / 365 = $615.2M/day; / 86,400 = $7,119.86/s, which rounds to $7,120. Correct.
- **Chart 2021-2025** (148.95, 162.45, 175.03, 198.08, 224.53): matches Alphabet filings ($148,951M / $162,450M / $175,033M / $198,084M / $224,532M). Correct. "$149 billion" for 2021 is correct.
- **Q2 2026: +17% to $63.3B.** Alphabet Q2 2026 release: Search & other grew 17% to $63.3B, up $9.1B year over year. **Q2 2025 $54.2B** matches the 10-Q ($54,190M). Recomputed growth: 63.3/54.19 = +16.8%, which rounds to 17%. Correct.
- **~$20B Apple payment in 2022.** Court documents unsealed in May 2024 in US v. Google (Bloomberg/AP and others) say Google paid Apple $20B in 2022 to be Safari's default search engine. Correct. The "~" and the "US antitrust case" source are appropriate.
- **Sept 2025 remedies ruling.** On Sept 2, 2025, Judge Mehta barred exclusive contracts but did not ban default payments to Apple, Samsung and Mozilla. He also declined a Chrome divestiture. "Barred exclusive deals but let payments continue" is accurate.
- **Ad mechanics.** Advertisers bid in auctions, and Search ads are mostly cost-per-click. "Often pay per click" is suitably hedged.
- **Staleness / Q3 timing.** Alphabet has not yet confirmed its Q3 2026 date. Estimates put it around Oct 27-28, 2026, after market close. Q3 2025 was reported Oct 29. Q2 2026 should therefore still be the latest quarter on Oct 20.
- **On-screen vs spoken:** all values match.
- **Sub-label length (max 28 characters):** all pass. The longest is the label "Google Search & other, 2025" at 27 characters. Subs are "Alphabet 2025 10-K" (18), "Q2 2025" (7), "Q2 2026, +17%" (13) and "US antitrust case" (17).
- The script has no advice, no price predictions and no political opinion. The cover/title is paid off.

## Issues (minor, non-blocking)
1. **Line 1 (hook): scope.** "Free Google Search made $224.5 billion" uses the *Search & other* line, which also includes ads on Gmail, Maps and Play. Line 2 clarifies this, so it is acceptable. For a tighter, time-anchored hook: "Free Google Search brought in $224.5 billion last year." Also note it is revenue, not profit ("brought in" reads more precisely than "made"). The spoken number is about 3 seconds, which is borderline. Optionally say "two hundred twenty-four billion dollars" in the hook.
2. **Line 3 (money scene): label.** The from-label is "Google Search, 2025". Consider "Search & other, 2025" (20 characters) to match line 2 and the chart title.
3. **Line 7 (compare scene): title.** "Google Search, Apr-Jun" is fine; "Search & other, Apr-Jun" (23 characters) would be more consistent.
4. **Line 9 (ruling): context.** The ruling is accurate as worded. Final judgment was entered Dec 5, 2025, and Google, the DOJ and the states have all appealed to the D.C. Circuit (still pending as of Aug-Sep 2026). Optional addition to the post/description: "The ruling is under appeal." This is not needed on screen.
5. **Q3 date.** Before Oct 20, confirm Alphabet's announced Q3 2026 date. If it falls on or before Oct 20 (unlikely), the Q2 line and post text should be updated to Q3.

VERDICT: PASS
