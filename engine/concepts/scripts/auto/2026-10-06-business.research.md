# Research brief: How Visa makes money without lending you a dime
Slot: 2026-10-06-business (posts Tuesday, October 6, 2026). Researched September 30, 2026.

## Timing / news angle
- Visa's fiscal year runs October 1 to September 30. Fiscal 2026 ends September 30, 2026; fiscal Q4/full-year 2026 results had no announced date as of September 30, 2026 (Visa usually reports late October). So the most recent full-year annual report (10-K) is FISCAL 2025 (year ended September 30, 2025, 10-K filed November 2025). Use FY2025 for all annual figures.
- Most recent quarter: fiscal Q3 2026 (three months ended June 30, 2026), reported July 28, 2026: net revenue $11.6 billion (+14%), processed transactions 71.7 billion (+10%), payments volume topped $4 trillion in a quarter for the first time (+10% constant dollar). Not needed in the short, context only.
- No need for a "this week" hook; the evergreen angle is the business model itself.

## Core business model (Visa FY2025 10-K)
- Visa is not a financial institution: it "does not issue cards, extend credit or set rates and fees for account holders." Banks (issuers) issue the cards, extend the credit and set the interest rate; Visa provides the network and branded products. (Visa FY2025 10-K / annual report)
- Interchange: paid by acquirers (merchant's bank) to issuers (cardholder's bank). Visa sets default interchange rates in the US and administers settlement but "generally does not receive any portion of the interchange reimbursement fees." Merchant discount rates are set by acquirers, not Visa. (Visa 10-K, fee discussion; same language in FY2022/FY2024 10-Ks)
- Credit card interest goes to the issuing bank, which also bears the credit risk (Visa does not extend credit, so it has no loan book / loan losses).

## FY2025 revenue (fiscal year ended September 30, 2025; Visa 10-K and Q4 FY2025 earnings release, Oct 28, 2025)
| Line | FY2025 |
|---|---|
| Service revenue (paid by clients based on payments volume) | $17.5 billion (+9%) |
| Data processing revenue (authorization, clearing, settlement per transaction) | $20.0 billion (+13%) |
| International transaction revenue (cross-border, currency conversion) | $14.17 billion (~$14.2B) (+12%) |
| Other revenue (advisory, other services) | $4.053 billion (~$4.1B) |
| Client incentives (paid back to banks/clients, a contra-revenue) | -$15.751 billion (~$15.8B) |
| **Net revenue** | **$40.0 billion (+11%)** |
- Check: 17.5 + 20.0 + 14.17 + 4.05 = 55.72 gross; 55.72 - 15.75 = 39.97, about $40.0 billion. Gross fees before incentives: about $55.7 billion (computed).
- GAAP net income FY2025: $20.1 billion ($10.20 per share), +2%.
- FY2025 payments volume: $14.2 trillion (FY2024: $13.2 trillion); total volume incl. cash: $16.7 trillion.
- FY2025 processed transactions: 257.5 billion (+10%).
- Nearly 5 billion payment credentials, 175+ million merchant locations (FY2025 annual report).

## Worked examples (exact math)
1. Profit per revenue dollar, FY2025: $20.1B net income / $40.0B net revenue = 0.5025, about 50 cents of every revenue dollar.
2. Net revenue per $100 of payments volume, FY2025: $40.0B / $14.2T = 0.002817 = 0.28%, about 28 cents per $100 of payments volume. (Caveat: not all revenue is tied to payments volume; this is an average ratio, not a fee rate.)
3. Incentives: $55.7B gross fees - $15.8B client incentives = about $40.0B net revenue.

## The surprising fact
- Visa lends nothing, carries no credit risk, and does not get your card interest or the interchange fee, yet about half of every revenue dollar (FY2025) became net income: $20.1B on $40.0B.
