## The story
- On or about Oct 29, 2026, crypto exchange Binance was hacked; about $6.3 billion in assets taken (Wikipedia/one outlet: $6.36B), drained from ten blockchains in about 20 minutes. Binance's own hot wallet was affected (co-CEO Richard Teng). Described as the largest crypto theft on record (CNBC, PBS, Bloomberg, NPR).
- Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals on Oct 29 (Bloomberg).
- About $3 billion of affected assets frozen on-chain as of Friday Oct 30, with help from Circle, Tether, Ripple. Frozen is not the same as recovered.
- Oct 31 (Sat): Binance resumed withdrawals in phases (Bitcoin network first, then Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, XRP, Base). Binance says it used company funds (SAFU fund, Futures Insurance Fund, corporate treasury) to buy tokens replacing about 90% of stolen assets (CNBC Oct 31; Korea Times says 95%; use "about 90%" per Binance/CNBC). Binance pledged to "fully cover the financial impact."
- Oct 31 (Sat): FBI said North Korea (group "TraderTraitor") was behind it; same group blamed for the $1.5B Bybit theft in Feb 2025 (RTE, ABC Australia, Yahoo). (President Trump said "We don't know if it's North Korea" -- political, not needed.)
- Analysts (Fundstrat's Sean Farrell): not a solvency event for Binance.

## Context / chartable series (Chainalysis, total crypto stolen per year from hacks)
- 2022: $3.8B (record at the time; Chainalysis, Feb 2023; some summaries say $3.7B)
- 2023: $1.7B (Chainalysis, Jan 2024; later revised ~$1.8B)
- 2024: $2.2B (Chainalysis, Dec 2024)
- 2025: $3.4B (Chainalysis, Dec 2025; Jan through early Dec 2025); North Korea ~$2.02B of it; Bybit alone $1.5B (Feb 2025), previous single-hack record.
- Chart points: [[2022,3.8],[2023,1.7],[2024,2.2],[2025,3.4]] ($B). This ONE hack ($6.3B) is bigger than any full year, and about 4x Bybit (our math: 6.3/1.5 = 4.2).

## What it means for a normal person's money
- Crypto held on an exchange is not FDIC insured. FDIC fact sheet (Jul 28, 2022): deposit insurance covers only US-dollar deposits at insured banks, only if the bank fails; it does not cover crypto assets or non-bank companies.
- Binance users are being made whole because Binance chose to and can pay from its own funds (SAFU etc.), not because of a government guarantee.
- Customers could not withdraw from Oct 29 until phased reopening Oct 31.

## What to watch
- Whether Binance replaces the remaining ~10% (less liquid tokens).
- How much of the ~$3B frozen is actually recovered; FBI expects further laundering.
