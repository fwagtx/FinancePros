## Issues and fixes

1. **The hook (L0) is slightly misleading.** "One cancer pill" suggests a single pill costs about $16K. The figure is a 30-day supply of the drug. The hook is still specific and works, so change one word to keep it true.
   - L0 `text`: "Why does one cancer drug list at nearly $16,000 a month?"
   - L0 `say`: "Why does one cancer drug list at nearly sixteen thousand a month?" (12 words)
   - post.title: "Why one cancer drug lists near $16,000 a month" (46 characters)
   - post.tiktok: change "pill" to "drug". The length stays at 133 characters or fewer.
   - post.ig, first line: change "pill" to "drug".

2. **L3 attributes the independent estimate to drugmakers.** "Drugmakers cite ... $2.6 to $2.8 billion" credits the independent Wouters (JAMA) cancer estimate to industry. The $2.6B (Tufts) and $2.8B (Wouters, cancer only) figures also measure different things.
   - L3 `text`: "Drugmakers cite R&D costs: $2.6B per drug (Tufts); an independent study found $2.8B for cancer drugs."
   - L3 `say`: "Drugmakers cite research costs: about two point six billion per drug, two point eight for cancer." (16 words)
   - The scene labels stay as they are ("Wouters, 2020" / "cancer, median"; "Tufts, 2016" / "all drugs, average"). They already meet the limits.

3. **The L6 RAND figure is missing its qualifier.** 3.22x is the lower bound after US rebates. The gross figure is 4.22x. Without the qualifier the number reads as a raw price ratio.
   - L6 `text`: "Critics cite pricing power: after rebates, US brand-name prices ran at least 3.22x other countries' in 2022 (RAND)."
   - L6 `say`: "Critics cite pricing power: after rebates, US brand-name prices ran at least three point two two times other countries' in twenty twenty-two." (22 words)
   - L6 scene `caption`: "RAND, 2022, after US rebates" (optional)

4. **The script needs to separate "list price" from "what patients pay".** L1, L2 and L7 are all list-price or Medicare-price figures. L8 is the only line about patients, and it does not mark the contrast. Add one clause.
   - L8 `text`: "Patients pay less: in 2027, Part D caps yearly out-of-pocket costs at $2,400."
   - L8 `say`: "Patients pay less: in twenty twenty-seven, Part D caps out-of-pocket costs at twenty-four hundred dollars." (15 words)

5. **The L4 wording overstates the stat.** The BIO figure is the likelihood that a development program reaches approval. It is not a share of "drugs".
   - L4 `text`: "Only 5.3% of cancer drug candidates went from Phase I to approval (2011-2020)."
   - L4 `say`: "Only five point three percent of cancer drug candidates reach approval." (11 words)

6. **The L2 IQVIA basis should say "list prices".** The spoken line can stay as it is.
   - L2 `text`: "Global cancer drug spending hit $252 billion in 2024 at list prices, per IQVIA."
   - Note: the chart points come from successive IQVIA editions. The 2022 figure was later revised to about $198B. This is a minor point and not worth changing.

7. **L7 trim (optional, for pacing):**
   - L7 `say`: "Example: a year of Ibrance is about a hundred eighty-nine thousand at list, ninety-four thousand at Medicare's twenty twenty-seven price." (20 words)
