# Research brief: What happens if you never pay into Social Security? (Hot Take, posts Sun Oct 4, 2026)

Format: Money Myth / "what happens if" explainer. No specific news hook this week; evergreen, every number dated to 2026.

## Core rules (confirmed)
- You earn Social Security "credits" (quarters of coverage) only on earnings where you pay Social Security tax. (SSA, How You Earn Credits 2026, EN-05-10072)
- Retirement benefits require 40 credits (people born 1929 or later). Max 4 credits per year, so about 10 years of work. (SSA, Retirement Benefits 2026 EN-05-10035; SSA FAQ KA-02459; SSA Benefits Planner credits page)
- Credits already earned stay on your record if you stop working. Benefit AMOUNT is based on your highest 35 years of earnings. (SSA EN-05-10035)
- 2026: one credit = $1,890 of covered earnings; four credits (max) = $7,560. 2025 was $1,810. (SSA EN-05-10072; Money.com; Yahoo Finance)
- With 0 credits (or fewer than 40): no retirement benefit on your own record. SSDI also requires work credits (generally 40, 20 in the last 10 years; fewer for young workers). (SSA credits page; disability guides)
- Fallback: SSI (Supplemental Security Income) is funded by general tax revenues, needs-based (low income and resources), no work history required. 2026 max federal SSI: $994/month individual, $1,491 couple. (SSA "How much you could get from SSI" ssa.gov/ssi/amount; Nolo)
- Exception: a spouse can receive up to 50% of the worker's full-retirement-age benefit on the worker's record, even with little/no own earnings. (SSA Retirement Benefits EN-05-10035; SSA blog 2024-07-11)
- Average retired-worker benefit 2026: $2,071/month (up from $2,015 after 2.8% COLA). (SSA 2026 COLA Fact Sheet)

## The surprising fact
- Medicare Part A (hospital insurance) is premium-free only with 40 quarters (on your own or spouse's record). With fewer than 30 quarters, the 2026 Part A premium is $565/month (up $47 from $518 in 2025); 30-39 quarters pay $311/month. (CMS 2026 Medicare Parts A & B premiums announcement, Nov 2025; AHA News 2025-11-17; Center for Medicare Advocacy)

## Worked examples (exact math)
- Earnings for a full year of credits, 2026: $1,890 x 4 = $7,560.
- Part A premium for a year with <30 credits, 2026: $565 x 12 = $6,780.
- Average retiree vs SSI max, 2026: $2,071 - $994 = $1,077 a month gap.
