## Facts (all forecasts are projections, not results)
- NRF/Prosper survey, released Nov 19, 2026: 182.7 million consumers plan to shop Thanksgiving Day through Cyber Monday 2026, down about 2% from 186.9 million last year (NRF's 2025 forecast figure). Our math: 186.9 - 182.7 = 4.2 million fewer. (Chain Store Age; SGB; DC Velocity)
  - Black Friday most popular: 69% (126 million). Cyber Monday 36% (66.1 million).
  - Last year's actual count was 202.9M (vs 186.9M forecast) - so the final tally can differ.
- NRF holiday forecast, Oct 15, 2026: Nov-Dec retail sales up 4.2% to $1.013 trillion (2025: up 3.1% to $972 billion). Excludes autos, gas stations, restaurants. (Retail Dive; AJOT; Retail Insight Network; AP via mynews13)
- Adobe Digital Insights forecast, Sept 28, 2026 (Business Wire / Adobe):
  - US online holiday sales Nov 1-Dec 31: $275.1B, up 6.7%.
  - Cyber Week (Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday): $47.5B, up 7.4%.
  - Cyber Monday (Nov 30): $15.1B, up 6.2% - would be first day ever above $15B online; season's biggest online day.
  - Black Friday (Nov 27): $12.9B, up 9.2%. Thanksgiving: $6.9B.
  - BNPL: $1.09B on Cyber Monday (2nd straight year over $1B); $807M Black Friday; $21.3B Nov-Dec, up 6.6%.
- Dates: Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26, Black Friday Nov 27, Cyber Monday Nov 30, 2026.
