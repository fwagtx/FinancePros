## Facts (with dates)
- Mon Oct 5, 2026: OpenAI announced a new visual ad format: ads with imagery (product inspiration, usage, experiences) shown alongside/after image generation results in ChatGPT. Begins appearing later in October 2026, U.S. only for now, with an initial test group of advertisers. Ads clearly labeled, separate from the generated image, and OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. Also expanding measurement (Pixel, Conversions API, attribution partners, incrementality tests) and brand-suitability work with DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science. (TechCrunch Oct 5, 2026; BleepingComputer; Android Headlines)
- Which plans see ads: only Free and Go. Go costs $8/month in the US. Plus ($20/month), Pro ($200/month), Business, Enterprise, Edu: no ads. (Axios Feb 9, 2026; MacRumors Feb 9, 2026; Tom's Guide; OpenAI "Introducing ChatGPT Go")
- ChatGPT ads (text/sponsored) first began testing in the US on Feb 9, 2026 on Free and Go tiers; advertisers do not get access to chats, chat history, memories or personal details; no ads for users predicted to be under 18; not near sensitive topics like health or politics. (Axios, CBS News, MacRumors, Feb 9-10, 2026)
- Aug 31, 2026, OpenAI blog "A milestone in expanding access to AI": ChatGPT Ads reached $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in less than 200 days; tens of thousands of advertisers; self-serve expanding to India, Europe, Middle East, North Africa. "Advertising does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides." (openai.com; Digiday; PPC Land)
- Run rate = current monthly revenue x 12 (a snapshot, not a full year of booked revenue). Secondary reports put the month at roughly $83M (not used on screen).
- Ads expanded to India in August 2026 (TechCrunch Aug 27, 2026).
- ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion weekly users: OpenAI to developers at DevDay, Sep 29, 2026 ("our collective 1.2B weekly users"); TechCrunch Oct 5, 2026 repeats "ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users"; The Decoder.

## Context
- ChatGPT has a free tier and paid plans; ads are OpenAI's way to fund free and low-cost users. Picture ads during image generation are the first visual format. Competitor Meta's Muse app is free to everyone (TechCrunch).
- OpenAI reportedly targets ~$2.5B ad revenue in 2026 (secondary reports; not used).

## What it means for a normal person
- Free and $8 Go users in the US will start seeing labeled picture ads near images they generate, starting with a test later in October.
- Cheapest ad-free plan is Plus at $20/month = $240/year.
- Ads are targeted using conversation topics (per Feb 2026 policy); users can turn off personalization and hide ads.

## What to watch
- Global rollout of visual ads; whether ads spread to other ChatGPT features; OpenAI ad revenue updates.
