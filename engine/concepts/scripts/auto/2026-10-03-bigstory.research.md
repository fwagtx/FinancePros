## Facts (each with date)
- Freddie Mac PMMS, released Thu Oct 1, 2026: 30-year fixed averaged **7.28%** as of Oct 1, 2026, up from **7.03%** the prior week (Sep 24) and **6.34%** a year earlier (Oct 2, 2025). 15-year averaged 6.60% (vs 6.42% prior week, 5.55% a year ago). PMMS covers conventional conforming purchase loans, 20% down, excellent credit.
- 7.28% is the highest weekly PMMS reading since Nov 22, 2023 (7.29%) -> "highest since November 2023". (FRED MORTGAGE30US)
- Sep 24, 2026 reading (7.03%) was the first above 7% since Jan 16, 2025 (7.04%). 2026 low: 5.98% on Feb 26, 2026.
- US 10-year Treasury yield touched **5.34%** on Thu Oct 1, 2026 (intraday), highest since 2002 (Reuters, Quartz). It retreated to ~5.26% late morning Oct 1.
- Official Treasury par yield (close): 10-yr 5.29% Sep 30; 5.24% Oct 1; **5.28% Fri Oct 2, 2026**. 30-yr 5.63% Oct 2. 10-yr was 4.79% on Sep 1, 2026.
- Reuters (Oct 1): 10-yr posted its biggest quarterly rise in yields this century in the July-September 2026 quarter.
- UK 30-year gilt yield rose above **6%** on Oct 1, 2026, highest since 1998 (Reuters; Investing.com/Seoul Economic Daily: as high as 6.029%, highest since Feb 1998).
- France 10-year highest since 2002, near 5% (Oct 1, Reuters). Japan: yields at multi-decade highs; 10-yr highest since the mid-1990s (Quartz); fifth straight quarter of double-digit gains (Reuters).
- Causes (Reuters Oct 1): surging energy costs fanning inflation; AI and data-center boom increasing competition for capital and raising growth/rate expectations. After a Fed hike in September 2026, traders expect at least three more Fed hikes before mid-2027.
- IIF estimate: advanced economies paid over $3.3 trillion in interest on internationally traded government bonds over the past year.
- Reuters: "Higher rates raise financing costs for companies and mortgage borrowers and force governments to spend more on interest payments."

## Worked example (math, labeled as example)
$400,000 30-year fixed loan, principal and interest only:
- at 6.34% (Oct 2, 2025): $2,486.33/month
- at 7.28% (Oct 1, 2026): $2,736.85/month
- difference: about $251/month (~$3,000/year)
