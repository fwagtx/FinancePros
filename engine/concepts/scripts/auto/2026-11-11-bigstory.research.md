## Facts (Anthropic IPO)
- Tue Nov 10, 2026: Anthropic (maker of the Claude AI assistant) launched its IPO roadshow and set a price range of $70 to $78 per share (Fortune, CNN, Yahoo Finance, Nov 10).
- Selling 825 million shares on the Nasdaq under ticker "ANT"; would raise up to $64.4 billion at the top of the range (Fortune Nov 10; CNN Nov 10 also: $64.4B at top). At midpoint: ~$61B raised, $1.33T fully diluted valuation (CNN, corrected).
- Valuation: up to $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis at the top (Fortune, CNN). Reuters calc ~ $1.02T at top using expected outstanding shares (difference = fully diluted vs outstanding shares). Fully diluted = counts all shares that could exist, incl. employee stock awards/options.
- Below the ~$2 trillion "some had floated weeks ago" (Fortune), amid an AI stock sell-off.
- Timing: CNN: expected "late next week", date could move. Fortune: trading "could start as early as Wednesday or Thursday next week." (CNBC reportedly: price Nov 18, trade Nov 19 - not independently confirmed.) => say "as soon as next week".
- Revenue (CNN, from filing): $17B in most recent quarter, up 1,127% y/y; $33.2B in first nine months of 2026 vs $4.6B in all of 2025.
- Annualized revenue run rate ~ $81B end of October, up from ~ $71B late September (Fortune, amended filing).
- Other than SpaceX, no company has gone public with a target valuation above $1T (CNN).
- Private valuation history: Feb 2026 Series G $30B at $380B; May 28 2026 Series H $65B at $965B post-money (AP via WTOP/KSAT, May 28 2026).
- Retail: Bloomberg (Oct 28) reported Anthropic weighing 15-20% of shares for retail; not finalized. Vanda Research estimate of $250M first-day retail buying (Yahoo, Nov 11) - an estimate only, not used.

## Context: IPO price vs first trade (SpaceX, June 2026)
- SpaceX priced its IPO at $135/share on June 11, 2026, raising ~$75B (largest IPO ever per multiple outlets); opened at $150 on June 12 (about 11% above IPO price) (Anadolu Agency; Forbes Australia; CBS News).
- Yahoo Finance (Nov 11): SpaceX "opened at $150 in June, $15 above its IPO price... By August, it was in a nearly 30% hole. Five months later, SpaceX is still below its first trade."
- Lesson (education, not prediction): most everyday investors who buy on day one pay the first-trade price, not the IPO price; the IPO price goes mainly to investors allocated shares before trading starts.

- Valuation headline depends on share count method ($1.4T fully diluted vs ~ $1.02T outstanding, Reuters).
