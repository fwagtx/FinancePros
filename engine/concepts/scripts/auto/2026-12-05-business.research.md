# Research brief: How affiliate links pay creators a cut of your purchase (slot 2026-12-05-business)
Researched Oct 6, 2026. Show: How They Make Money. Evergreen; post Dec 5, 2026.

## Timely angle
None strong this week. Note: Amazon's old public "fee schedule" URL (affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule) now redirects to a login for a per-account "custom rate card" (seen Oct 2026). The public "Associates Program Standard Commission Income Statement" help page still lists fixed standard rates (fetched Oct 6, 2026). Script says "standard rates" and dates them "as of October 2026".

## How tracking works
- An affiliate ("Special") link carries the creator's tracking tag; the store attributes the purchase to that tag and pays the creator a commission (Amazon Operating Agreement: Special Links "must properly use the special tagged link formats we provide").
- Amazon Associates Program Policies, "Session" definition (word-for-word, fetched Oct 6, 2026): a session begins "when a customer clicks through that Special Link and ending upon the first to occur of the following: (x) 24 hours elapse from that click, (y) the customer places an order for a Product, other than a digital product ... or (z) the customer clicks through a Special Link to an Amazon Site that is not your Special Link". Commission applies if during the session "the customer purchases a Product by adding a Product to his or her shopping cart and completing the order for that Product no later than 89 days after their initial click-through".
  => 24-hour window; items added to cart during that window can count if ordered within 89 days of the click. Another creator's link ends the session.

## Amazon Associates fixed standard commission rates (US), as of Oct 2026
Source: Amazon Associates Central, "Associates Program Standard Commission Income Statement", Table 1 (affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ), fetched Oct 6, 2026:
- 10.00%: Luxury Beauty, Luxury Stores Beauty, Amazon Explore
- 5.00%: Digital Music, Physical Music, Handmade, Digital Videos
- 4.50%: Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive
- 4.00%: Amazon devices (Fire, Kindle, Echo, Ring...), Luxury Stores Fashion, Apparel, Watches, Jewelry, Luggage, Shoes, Handbags & Accessories
- 3.00%: Toys, Furniture, Home, Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Pets, Headphones, Beauty, Musical Instruments, Business & Industrial, Outdoors, Tools, Sports, Baby, Amazon Coins
- 2.50%: PC, PC Components, DVD & Blu-Ray
- 2.00%: Televisions, Digital Video Games
- 1.00%: Amazon Fresh, Physical Video Games & Consoles, Grocery, Health & Personal Care
- 0.00%: Gift Cards, Wireless Service Plans, Alcoholic Beverages, Kindle subscriptions, Vehicles, restaurant food, etc.; Coach 0%
- 4.00%: All Other Categories
- Bounties also exist (e.g., $3.00 Prime Free Trial; Audible free trial $5-$20).
Range in Table 1: 0% to 10%. (Some third-party blogs cite "Amazon Games 20%"; not in the current Table 1, not used.)

## Price to the buyer
- Shoppers generally pay the same price whether or not they use an affiliate link; the commission comes out of the seller's/retailer's side (Shopify affiliate-program guides; multiple affiliate guides). No official Amazon statement found. Script says "usually" — typical, not universal (some programs/coupons differ).

## FTC disclosure
- FTC finalized revisions to the Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255, announced June 29, 2023 (Federal Register Jul 26, 2023, doc 2023-14795). Material connections not reasonably expected by the audience must be disclosed "clearly and conspicuously"; the 2023 revision added a definition of "clear and conspicuous" (Arnold & Porter, Federal Register).
- FTC FAQ "FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking" (modified Jul 15, 2025): "You should disclose your relationship to the retailer clearly and conspicuously on your site, so readers can decide how much weight to give your endorsement. You could say something like, 'I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post.'" ... "The closer the disclosure is to your recommendation, the better."
- Amazon Operating Agreement (last updated Oct 15, 2025) requires the statement: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
- Note: the Guides are guidance; FTC enforces under Section 5 (deceptive practices). Script says the guides "say" creators should clearly disclose (not "law requires").

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
$200 purchase in a 4% category (e.g., "All Other Categories" or Apparel at 4.00%): $200 x 0.04 = $8.00 to the creator. Buyer pays $200 (price usually unchanged); Amazon pays the $8.

## Industry size (not used on screen)
- Forrester (via Statista/press) projected US affiliate marketing spending of $8.2B in 2022, up from $7.4B in 2021. Other figures ($9.56B 2023, $10.72B 2024, $13B 2023) come from aggregator blogs with unclear methodology and conflict. No clean, consistently sourced real series -> chart skipped.

## The surprise
The click window is only 24 hours, and the cut varies by category from 0% (gift cards) to 10% (luxury beauty), while the shopper's price usually doesn't change.
