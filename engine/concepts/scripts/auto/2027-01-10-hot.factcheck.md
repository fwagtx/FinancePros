- 12 CFR 1005.2(m) (eCFR / Cornell): an unauthorized EFT is one "initiated by a person other than the consumer without actual authority". A payment you send yourself is generally not unauthorized.
- Nuance: 12 CFR 1005.11(a) also counts "an incorrect electronic fund transfer" as an error. In its Fall 2021 Supervisory Highlights and its Dec 2021 EFT FAQs, the CFPB treats some misdirected P2P payments as incorrect EFTs that banks must investigate, for example when the recipient-side info was wrong. When a consumer reports an error, the bank has to investigate, even though the result may be "no error". So the script must not suggest Reg E gives a typo no protection of any kind.
