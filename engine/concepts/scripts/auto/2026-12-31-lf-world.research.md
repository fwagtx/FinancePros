## Timely angle (dated, not "news this week")
- September 2026: Korea's monthly exports hit a record $120.9 billion (up 83.5% from a year earlier); semiconductor exports were $60.30 billion, the first time any single item passed $60 billion in a month (previous record $46.7 billion, August 2026). Trade surplus $49.9 billion. (Korea Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources press release, Oct 1, 2026; Korea Times Oct 1, 2026; Seoul Economic Daily Oct 1, 2026.)
- January-September 2026 exports: $814.5 billion, already above the full-year 2025 record. (Same MOTIR release.)
- Full-year 2025 exports: record $709.7 billion (MOTIE, Jan 1, 2026 via WAM/Yonhap); MOTIR's Oct 2026 release cites 2025 as $709.3 billion (revised). Both round to $709 billion -> use "$709B" / "about seven hundred nine billion". First year above $700 billion. 2025 chip exports $173.4 billion (+22.2%); autos ~$72 billion record.
- Oct 1-20, 2026: chips 44.7% of exports (Korea Customs Service via Korea Times, Oct 21, 2026). Use only as dated if at all.

## The surprising facts
1. In 1960, South Korea's GDP per person was about $158 (World Bank, current US$; IndexMundi reproducing WDI: $158.25). In 2024: $36,239 (World Bank via YCharts/Trading Economics: 36,238.6).
2. Japan's own Cabinet Office reported that in 2023 South Korea passed Japan in GDP per person for the first time: Korea $35,563 vs Japan $33,849 (Korea Times, Dec 2024/Feb 2025 coverage of Japan Cabinet Office). Korea's BOK says per-capita GNI also exceeded Japan's in 2024.
3. Korea is described as the first former aid recipient to join the OECD's Development Assistance Committee (accepted Nov 25, 2009; effective Jan 1, 2010) (World Bank blog; Korea Times 2010; KEIA). It received World Bank IDA money from 1962, stopped borrowing from IDA in 1973, became an IDA donor in 1977 (World Bank blog).

## Worked examples (OUR MATH, labeled)
1. Growth multiple: 36,239 / 158 = 229.4 -> about 229 times (current dollars, not inflation-adjusted).
   Inflation adjustment: US CPI-U annual average 1960 = 29.6; 2024 = 313.689 (BLS via Minneapolis Fed / FRED CPIAUCNS). 313.689 / 29.6 = 10.6. 229.4 / 10.6 = 21.6 -> "about 22 times" in real terms (rough: uses US prices).
2. Gap to the US: US GDP per capita 1960 = $3,007 (World Bank; UNSURE exact, one source) and 2024 = $85,810 (World Bank via Trading Economics). 3,007 / 158 = 19.0 -> US was about 19 times richer per person in 1960. 85,810 / 36,239 = 2.37 -> about 2.4 times in 2024.
3. Exports: 1964 first year at $100 million (Nov 30, 1964 -> "Export Day"; Wikipedia "Trade day in South Korea", Korea JoongAng Daily, National Archives of Korea). 2025: $709 billion. 709,000,000,000 / 100,000,000 = 7,090 -> about 7,000 times.
4. Car tariff (simplified illustration, tariff applies to the import/customs value, not sticker price): $30,000 x 15% = $4,500; $30,000 x 25% = $7,500; before April 2025 under KORUS: $0 (Korean cars entered largely duty-free; CRS).
5. Hyundai+Kia share of US new-car sales 2025: 1,836,172 / 16,300,000 = 11.3% -> about 1 in 9 (1/9 = 11.1%).
6. DRAM: TrendForce Q1 2025 shares SK hynix 36.0% + Samsung 33.7% = 69.7% -> about 70% of world DRAM revenue.

## History timeline
- Korean War: June 25, 1950 - armistice July 27, 1953 (EBSCO). Korean govt estimate: nearly 1.5 million civilian casualties in the South, ~$3.1 billion property destruction (kdevelopedia). In the mid-1950s US aid financed roughly half or more of the government budget (Seth: up to nearly 80% of revenues at one point; Breen: half) -> say "about half or more of the budget".
- 1962: First Five-Year Economic Development Plan (1962-66) begins under Park Chung-hee's government; shift toward export promotion by 1964 (countrystudies.us/Library of Congress; Korea Univ.). Exports went from 5% of GDP in 1963 to 28% in 1973 (one source; optional).
- Nov 30, 1964: exports pass $100 million for the first time -> "Export Day".
- 1973: Heavy and chemical industry drive declared (January 1973) — steel, ships, machinery, chemicals (MOFA; Korea Univ.).
- 1975: Hyundai Pony, first Korean-developed car (Mitsubishi engine, Giugiaro design) (Wikipedia; Hyundai).
- 1983: Samsung decides to enter memory chips (Feb 8, 1983, Tokyo); develops a 64K DRAM by Dec 1983, third country after US and Japan (Samsung Semiconductor newsroom; SEAJ).
- 1986: Hyundai Excel launches in the US at $4,995; 168,882 sold in 1986, record first year for an import brand (Hyundai; Wikipedia; multiple).
- 1997 Asian financial crisis: IMF approved SDR 15.5 billion (about $21 billion) stand-by credit on Dec 4, 1997, record at the time (IMF Press Release 97/55). Total international package about $55-58 billion (sources differ: avoid total).
- Jan-Apr 1998: Gold-collecting campaign — about 3.5 million people donated about 227 tons of gold, worth about $2.1-2.2 billion (Wikipedia; Korea Herald; multiple).
- Aug 23, 2001: Korea repaid its IMF drawings (SDR 14.4 billion, about $19.5 billion) in full, nearly three years early (loan due May 2004) (IMF News Brief 01/82, Aug 22, 2001; JoongAng Daily).
- Nov 2009/Jan 2010: joins OECD DAC as donor.
- March 15, 2012: KORUS FTA enters into force (USTR; Venable).

## Tech giant today
- Samsung Electronics 2025: revenue record 333.6 trillion won (+10.9%); operating profit 43.6 trillion won (+33.2%) (Samsung shareholder letter; JoongAng Daily; Kyunghyang, Jan 29, 2026). SK hynix 2025 operating profit 47.2 trillion won, above Samsung's for the first time.
- DRAM: SK hynix 36% + Samsung 33.7% of global DRAM revenue in Q1 2025 (TrendForce) -> ~70%.
- Samsung TVs: No. 1 global TV brand by revenue for 20 straight years (2006-2025); 29.1% share in 2025 (Omdia via Samsung newsroom; company-reported).
- Samsung phones: Q2 2025 US smartphone share 31% vs Apple 49% (Canalys). Global 2025: Apple 20%, Samsung 19% (Counterpoint).
- Cosmetics: Korea's cosmetics exports 2025 record $11.43 billion (+12.3%) (Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, Jan 2026); US became No. 1 market at about $2.2 billion (+~15%), passing China ($2.0 billion). Korea became world No. 2 cosmetics exporter after France ($24.3B), ahead of the US ($10.8B).

## How it reaches an American's wallet
- US goods trade with Korea, 2025 (Census/USTR): US imports from Korea $125.5 billion (-4.5%); US exports to Korea $69.1 billion (+5.0%); goods deficit about $56.5 billion (down 14% from 2024). US services surplus with Korea $13.3 billion (USTR).
- Cars: Hyundai Motor Group (Hyundai, Genesis, Kia) US sales 2025 record 1,836,172 (2024: 1,708,293), +7.5% (Korea Herald; JoongAng Daily, Jan 2026). Kia 852,155 record. US total new-vehicle sales 2025 about 16.3 million (Cox Automotive / KBB).
- Tariffs (neutral, dated): KORUS (in force March 15, 2012) made most trade duty-free; Korean cars entered the US largely duty-free (CRS). April 3, 2025: US 25% Section 232 tariff on imported autos, including Korea's (CRS). July 30, 2025: US-Korea deal set 15%; Korea pledged $350 billion of US investment ($200 billion cash, capped at $20 billion a year, plus $150 billion for shipbuilding) (KEIA; CNBC). Auto rate cut to 15% effective Nov 1, 2025 (GHY; CRS). Jan 26, 2026: President Trump said he would raise it to 25% over legislative delay (CNBC). March 12, 2026: Korea's legislature passed the special act to implement the $350 billion pledge (CNBC). CRS (May 2026): South Korea faces a 15% total tariff on autos. Feb 2026: the general IEEPA-based tariffs stopped being collected after the Supreme Court ruling (CBP stopped Feb 24, 2026); auto Section 232 tariff unaffected. Sept 22, 2026: Korea confirmed its first project under the pact, a gas-fired power plant in Texas (Japan Times; ~$22.3 billion per BigGo, UNSURE).
- Factories: Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America (Ellabell, Georgia) opened March 2025, part of a $12.6 billion investment including battery JVs (Hyundai). Hyundai plans $26 billion of US investment through 2028 (Bloomberg, Aug 26, 2025). Samsung: $37 billion+ total in Taylor, Texas, chip plant (Samsung/Korean press). (Avoid company job-support claims except as "company says".)
