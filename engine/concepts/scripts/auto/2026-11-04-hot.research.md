# Research brief: Why is car insurance so expensive right now (posts Nov 4, 2026)

## CPI motor vehicle insurance (BLS; BTS Transportation CPI), 12-month change, December to December
2022 +14.2% | 2023 +20.3% | 2024 +11.3% | 2025 +2.8%
- August 2026: -5.1% over 12 months (BLS CPI, Aug 2026 release Sept 11, 2026; BTS); index fell 0.8% in August after -0.3% in July.
- Cumulative Dec 2021 -> Dec 2025: 1.142 x 1.203 x 1.113 x 1.028 = 1.572, about +57%.

## Why: insurers were losing money on auto
- Personal auto net combined ratio (claims + expenses per $100 of premium), Triple-I: 2022 112.2 | 2023 104.9 | 2024 95.3 | 2025 91.8. Above 100 = underwriting loss.
- Net written premiums rose 14.4% in 2023 and 12.8% in 2024 (Triple-I).
- Drivers cited: higher repair costs, high-tech parts and sensors, more severe claims, legal system costs (Triple-I).

## Price level
- Bankrate (2026): average full coverage $2,697 a year ($225/mo). Other trackers $2,513-$2,697.

## Worked example
- $1,000 premium in Dec 2021 terms -> about $1,572 by Dec 2025 (x1.572).
- 2022: for every $100 of premium, insurers paid about $112.20 in claims and expenses -> lost about $12.20. 2025: $91.80 -> kept about $8.20 before investment income.
