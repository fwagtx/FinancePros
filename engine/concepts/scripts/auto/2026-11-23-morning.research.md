## Facts (picked story)
- Mon Nov 23, 2026: Kospi closed down 3.31% at 4,708.54 (Korea JoongAng Daily "Kospi slips 3.3% as AI stock sell-off hits Samsung, SK hynix"; Al Jazeera ~3.3%). Intraday it fell as much as ~5.1-5.8%; program trading briefly halted (JoongAng: down 5.11% ~11:33 a.m.). One intraday report: -3.59% at 4,695.08 (not the close).
- Previous session (Fri Nov 20): Kospi -5.21%; fell below 5,000 for the first time since February.
- Samsung Electronics -3.06% to 177,500 won; SK hynix -2.52% to 1.09 million won (JoongAng, Nov 23).
- Samsung + SK hynix = roughly half of the Kospi's market cap (multiple reports; "more than half of the weighting as of June" per CNBC/Reuters coverage).
- Record closing high: 9,114.55 on June 22, 2026 (Reuters / Wikipedia). Record intraday: 9,385.59 on June 19, 2026.
- OUR MATH: 4,708.54 / 9,114.55 - 1 = -48.3% from record close (FourWeekMBA also reports "48% off its high").
- 2026 started at 4,309.63 (Jan 2 close, record at the time, JoongAng); passed 5,000 on Jan 27, 6,000 on Feb 25, 9,000 (close 9,063.84) on June 18 (Korea Herald / JoongAng).
- Cause: global AI stock rout (doubts about AI profitability, financing, valuations); Wall Street's Nov 19-20 selloff spilled into Seoul.
- Policy: Finance Minister Lee Hyoung-il (Nov 23) said the government is ready to activate the 10.76 trillion won (~$8 billion; Korea Times $8.12B) market stabilization fund depending on conditions and could adjust its size (Korea Times, Korea JoongAng Daily, Nov 23, 2026).
- Fund set up in 2020; never deployed (activation considered in 2022, not carried out). Korea has not deployed a stabilization fund since 2008 (Korea Times Nov 11, 2026). Korea set up such funds in 1990, 2003, 2008, 2020, 2022.
- How it works: pooled money from financial institutions that buys stocks to keep forced selling from deepening a rout.
- Size: Kospi market cap 3.86 quadrillion won vs 1.98 quadrillion at end of 2020 (report via search). OUR MATH: 10.76T / 3,860T = ~0.28% (<1%). One official put it at ~0.23% of domestic market cap.
- Other measures: easing daily share-buyback limits; tighter monitoring of illegal short selling.
- Context: Japan closed Monday (Labour Thanksgiving Day). Taiwan Taiex +2.2%, Shanghai +0.2%. US futures rose Sunday night after last week's selloff (Friday closes Dow 48,147.33, S&P 6,910.39, Nasdaq 22,617.73).

## What it means for a normal person
- Concentration: when two companies are about half an index, one sector's bad week drags the whole market. Same AI selloff that hit US tech.
- Government "rescue funds" are small relative to markets (<1% here) and history shows mixed results (1990 bear market continued ~2 years after the fund).
