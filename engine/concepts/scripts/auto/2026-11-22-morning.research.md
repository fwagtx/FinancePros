## Story: Thanksgiving dinner cost rises for first time since 2022
Source: American Farm Bureau Federation (AFBF) 41st annual Thanksgiving dinner survey, released Wednesday Nov 18, 2026; reported by AP (via Gray TV stations, WSOC, Korea Times), Fox TV stations, Feedstuffs.

Facts (all from the 2026 AFBF survey):
- Classic Thanksgiving dinner for 10: $58.63 (about $5.86 per person).
- Up "more than 6%" / "up 6%" from 2025 ($55.18). Our math: 58.63 / 55.18 = +6.25%.
- First overall increase since the 2022 record.
- Still $5.42, or 8.5%, below the record $64.05 in 2022.
- 16-pound turkey: $23.68 average, up from $21.50 in 2025 (about 10%; $1.48 per pound).
- Turkey was the largest driver of the increase. High diesel prices made it more expensive to process and transport staples (milk, rolls, potatoes, cranberries), per Farm Bureau; farm labor shortage also cited.
- About a third of the items declined in price, including the veggie tray, frozen pie crusts, frozen green peas and whipping cream.
- Regions: South $54.33 (cheapest), Midwest $56.89, West $58.74, Northeast $61.57 (most expensive).
- Retailer comparisons (not for script; brand mentions avoided): Aldi $39, Walmart $35 (different menu).
- Separate Wells Fargo tracker: $78.93, down 0.4% (different method; not used).

## Context
- Survey first run in 1986, same items/quantities each year for comparison.
- 2025 survey: down 5%, third straight yearly decline; turkey $21.50 (down 16%+).

## What it means for your money
- Hosting 10 costs about $3.45 more than last year (our math: 58.63 - 55.18). Turkey alone is $2.18 more.
- Where you live matters more than the yearly change: Northeast vs South gap is $7.24 (our math).
