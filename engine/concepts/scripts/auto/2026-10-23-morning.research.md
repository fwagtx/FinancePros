## Facts (Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, released Thursday Oct 22, 2026)
- 30-year fixed averaged 7.32% (week of Oct 22, 2026), down from 7.40% the prior week (Oct 15).
- 15-year fixed averaged 6.68%, down from 6.71%.
- A year earlier: 30-year 6.19%, 15-year 5.44%.
- Reported as the first weekly decline since August (last decline: Aug 20, 6.67% -> 6.65%; Oct 15 was flat at 7.40%).
- Rate first topped 7% on Sept 24, 2026 (7.03%), first time above 7% since early 2025 (NPR / Freddie Mac).
- Survey basis: conventional conforming purchase loans, excellent credit, 20% down. Individual offers vary.
- Next release: Thursday Oct 29, 2026.
- Context: NAR median existing-home price Sept 2026 $417,500 (+1.3% y/y), released Oct 13; Yun: highest September median on record.

## Worked example (my math, standard amortization, principal & interest only, $400,000 30-yr loan)
- 7.40%: $2,769.52/mo
- 7.32%: $2,747.72/mo  -> about $22/mo less than at last week's rate (matches outlet estimate of ~$22)
- 6.19% (year-ago rate): $2,447.28/mo -> 7.32% costs about $300/mo more ($300.44)
- Over a year: ~$3,605 more vs. year-ago rate.

## What it means for a normal person
- Buyers: a small dip; payments still far above a year ago. Refinancing math vs. a 6% loan rarely works at 7.3%.
- Rates on adjustable products / HELOCs follow different benchmarks.
- One week is not a trend; watch the Oct 29 Freddie Mac release.
