# Research brief: How rent-to-own stores make money on a TV (business angle)
Slot: 2027-01-21-business (posts Thu Jan 21, 2027; written Nov 22, 2026). Evergreen; every number dated.
Company: Upbound Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: UPBD), parent of Rent-A-Center (stores) and Acima (virtual / in-partner-store lease-to-own) and Brigit (added 2025).
NOTE: Jan 7, 2027 explainer covered the consumer side ($20/week x 65 weeks, APR, NerdWallet 468%, CA/VT caps). None of that is reused here.
NOTE ON ACCESS: sec.gov, investor.upbound.com, nasdaq.com, barchart, stocktitan could not be fetched from this session; all figures below are from web-search extracts of the SEC-hosted 10-K / 8-K text and company releases, cross-checked where two extracts agreed.

## Key facts (FY2025 = year ended Dec 31, 2025; 10-K filed Feb 2026; results released Feb 19, 2026)
- Total revenues FY2025: $4,695.1M (4,695,061 thousand); FY2024: $4,320.6M (4,320,564 thousand). Up 8.7% (company: "$4.7 billion in consolidated revenue, an 8.7% Y/Y increase"). Source: Q4 2025 earnings release (8-K ex-99), FY2025 annual report.
- Revenue lines FY2025 (thousands): Rentals and fees 3,627,019 (2024: 3,513,658, +3.2%); Merchandise sales 829,268 (2024: 773,744, +7.2%); Subscriptions and fees (Brigit) 206,024 (2024: 0); Other 32,750 (2024: 33,162). Sum = 4,695,061 -> checks out.
  - Our math: rentals and fees share = 3,627.0 / 4,695.1 = 77.25% -> "about 77%".
- Cost of rentals and fees FY2025: $1,441.8M (2024: $1,355.5M), = 39.8% of rentals and fees revenue (2024: 38.6%). (10-K MD&A.) Cost of rentals and fees is mainly depreciation of the rented merchandise.
  - Our math: of every $100 of rental/fee revenue, about $39.80 is cost of rentals -> about $60.20 gross profit (about 60% gross margin on rent).
- Merchandise sales gross margin FY2025: (15.5)% (2024: (14.3)%). Cost of merchandise sold FY2025 $957.6M (+8.2%). (10-K MD&A.) i.e. merchandise sales (incl. early-purchase payouts) were booked BELOW cost. SURPRISE FACT.
  - Our math check: (829.3 - 957.6) / 829.3 = -15.5%. Matches.
- Ownership rate: "Ownership is attained in approximately 38% of lease purchase agreements in the Rent-A-Center segment" (FY2024 10-K, filed Feb 2025). FY2023 10-K: ~36%; FY2022: ~35%. FY2025 figure not retrieved (uncertain if it changed).
- Re-rent loop: 10-K: "On average, a product is leased (turned over) to multiple customers before a customer acquires ownership." Average total product life in RAC segment ~16 months (FY2023 10-K). Merchandise returned in the Acima segment "is often moved to a Rent-A-Center Business store where it is offered for lease."
- Losses (lease charge-offs = net book value of unrecoverable on-rent merchandise with past-due customers, as % of revenue): Q4 2025 Rent-A-Center 4.9% (Q4 2024 5.0%); Acima 10.1% (up 110 bps y/y). Source: Q4 2025 release/slides (Feb 19, 2026), Investing.com slide summary, Yahoo call summary. Full-year 2025 rates not retrieved.
- Acima (lease-to-own offered at checkout in other retailers, virtual and staffed): FY2025 GMV +8.6%, segment revenue +11.1% (annual report); 10-K: Acima segment revenues increased approximately $251.0 million in 2025. Q3 2025 call: Acima activated its 100,000th merchant location.
- Rent-A-Center segment FY2025: revenue -4.2%, same store sales -2.2% (annual report). Footprint: ~2,300 company-branded retail units in US, Mexico, Puerto Rico (2025 releases); FY2024 10-K: 1,728 RAC stores US/PR, 132 Mexico, 448 franchised (as of Dec 31, 2024; via TradingView summary).
- Brigit (acquired Jan 2025): $206M revenue in 2025, 1.6M paying subscribers.

## Worked example (our math)
Every $100 of rent and fees in 2025: cost of rentals 39.8% -> $39.80; left $60.20 (~$60).
Merchandise sales: $829.3M sold, $957.6M cost -> -$128.3M, -15.5%.
