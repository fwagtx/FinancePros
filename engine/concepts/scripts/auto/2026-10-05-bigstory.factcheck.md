# Fact-check: 2026-10-05-bigstory (ChatGPT picture ads)
Checked Mon Oct 5, 2026 ~1 PM CT. Posts today, 4 PM CT. Line numbers are 0-based indexes into `lines`.

## Verified
- L1: Oct 5, 2026 (a Monday). OpenAI said visual ads will appear next to ChatGPT image-generation results, with a US-only test starting later in October with an initial group of advertisers. The ads are labeled and "won't influence the answers" (TechCrunch, BleepingComputer, Android Headlines, PPC Land, all Oct 5). Saying "On Monday" is correct for a same-day post.
- L2: Ads run only on Free and Go. Go costs $8/month in the US and Plus costs $20/month. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu have no ads (Axios and TechCrunch, Feb 9, 2026; the image-ad test also covers Free and Go per reports). The compare values "Ads" (3 chars) and "No ads" (6) are within the 7-character limit.
- L3: 100M weekly users (TechCrunch, Nov 6, 2023), 300M (Dec 2024), 800M+ (Altman at DevDay, Oct 6, 2025), 1.2B (DevDay, Sep 29, 2026). OpenAI's own wording is "reaches 1.2 billion people every week", so "weekly users" is fine.
- L4: Text ads started testing in the US on Feb 9, 2026. OpenAI announced on Aug 31, 2026 that the run rate had passed $1B annualized in under 200 days. Run rate = current monthly revenue x 12, so the caption's definition is correct.
- L5: OpenAI says ads are labeled, ads don't influence answers, and advertisers don't get chat contents (they get aggregate views and clicks). This is correctly framed as "OpenAI says". "Same answers" is a loose but acceptable paraphrase. Nothing is presented as a word-for-word quote.
- L6 math: $20 x 12 = $240. Correct.
- No keep:true lines. Spoken and on-screen numbers match on every line. No political content and no price predictions.

## Issues
1. **Factual omission that makes L6 and the TikTok post misleading (main FAIL reason).** Free users can turn ads off for free by accepting lower daily message limits, and possibly losing some tools such as image generation or deep research. This is in OpenAI's "Ads in ChatGPT" help page, The Decoder, Yahoo Tech and Windows Report. So the TikTok caption "Ad-free starts at $20/month" is false. L6 ("So the cheapest ad-free plan is Plus") is technically true about *paid plans*, but the "So..." framing presents $240 a year as the only way to avoid ads. That reads as a nudge to upgrade, not neutral information.
   - Fix L6 text: "Ad-free options: opt out on Free (fewer messages), or Plus at $20/month = $240 a year."
   - Fix L6 say: "To skip ads, free users can opt out and get fewer messages, or pay for Plus: twenty dollars a month, two hundred forty a year." The money scene can stay $20 to $240. Rename the from-label to "Plus, per month" (already the label) and drop the leading "So" so the line doesn't read as advice.
   - Fix TikTok: "Free ChatGPT is getting picture ads. You can opt out for fewer messages, or pay $20/month for Plus. Not financial advice..."
   - Fix IG: add "Free users can opt out of ads in exchange for lower message limits."
2. **L2 wording (minor).** "Ads show only on Free and Go" is correct. Consider adding "(US test)" to L1/L2 only if space allows, since the picture ads are US-only for now. L1 already says this.
3. **L3 chart x-axis (minor, acceptable).** The points sit at whole years (2023 to 2026), but the real milestones are Nov 2023, Dec 2024, Oct 2025 and Sep 2026. The caption on the same screen gives the exact dates, so this is acceptable. Better: use fractional x values (2023.85, 2024.95, 2025.75, 2026.7) or month labels if the template supports them. The spacing is uneven in reality (13, 10 and 11 months), so the distortion is small. The script also skips the 900M point from Feb 2026, which is fine.
4. **Hook L0 (minor).** It is true, specific and under 3 seconds. It slightly overstates scope: picture ads are a US test with a few advertisers and also hit Go. Stronger and more exact: "ChatGPT will start showing picture ads next to the images it makes." Or keep it and add "in the US" in L1 (already there). Not a FAIL by itself.
5. **L4 nuance (minor).** By August, ads were also running in the UK, Europe and 40+ countries, so the $1B is not from "text ads in the US" alone. The caption doesn't claim US-only, so this is OK.
6. Narration reads naturally. "next to images ChatGPT makes" matches the reports, which describe placement during and alongside image generation.

## Recommended fixes summary
- L6 text and say plus the TikTok caption: mention the free opt-out (fewer messages) and remove "Ad-free starts at $20/month".
- Optional: tighten the hook scope and the chart x values.
