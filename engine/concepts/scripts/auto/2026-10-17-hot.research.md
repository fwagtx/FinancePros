# Research brief: Scam alert: pig-butchering crypto investment scams (posts Oct 17, 2026)

## Scale (FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, IC3)
- 2025 IC3 Annual Report: cryptocurrency investment fraud was the costliest crime type, with $7.2 billion reported lost in 2025. Total reported losses were over $20.8 billion, from more than 1 million complaints.
- Chartable series, crypto investment fraud losses reported to IC3: 2022 $2.57B | 2023 $3.94B (IC3 2023 Crypto Fraud Report) | 2024 $5.8B | 2025 $7.2B.
- All investment fraud: 2021 $1.45B, 2022 $3.31B, 2023 $4.57B, 2024 $6.57B.

## How it works (FBI / IC3 / US Secret Service)
- It starts with unexpected contact: a "wrong number" text, a dating app or social media.
- The scammer builds trust as a friend or romantic partner, often over weeks, and never meets in person or on video.
- They pitch a crypto "opportunity" and send the victim to a fake platform or app that shows fake profits.
- Withdrawals are blocked. The victim is asked for "taxes", "fees" or more deposits to unlock the funds.
- FBI advice: don't send money to people you meet online; don't pay fees to unlock an account; report it at ic3.gov.
- Operation Level Up (FBI) has contacted victims and reduced potential losses by more than $500 million since 2024.

## Worked example (hypothetical)
- Deposit $10,000. The app shows $25,000 (a fake +150%).
- "Pay a 15% tax to withdraw": 15% x $25,000 = $3,750.
- Total lost: $10,000 + $3,750 = $13,750. Nothing comes back.
