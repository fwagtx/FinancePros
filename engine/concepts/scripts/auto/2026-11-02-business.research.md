# Research brief: How dollar stores make money (Dollar General). Posts Nov 2, 2026

Company: Dollar General (NYSE: DG). Fiscal 2025 = year ended Jan 30, 2026 (10-K filed March 2026; Q4 release March 12, 2026).

## FY2025 numbers
- Net sales $42.7B (+5.2%); same-store sales +3.0%.
- Gross profit $13,099.7M -> gross margin 30.66% (of net sales).
- Operating profit about $2.2B (+28.6%) -> about 5.2% of sales.
- Consumables 82.0% of net sales.
- About 21,000 stores; about 75% of the US population lives within 5 miles of one (company); ~80% of stores in towns of 20,000 people or fewer; average ~7,500 sq ft selling space.

## Worked example (FY2025 averages)
- $10 of sales: cost of goods ~ $6.93; gross profit ~ $3.07 (30.66%).
- After rent, wages, and other costs: operating profit ~ $0.52 (2.2/42.7 = 5.2%).
- Model: small, cheap-to-run stores close to rural customers, selling everyday consumables at high frequency.
