# Research brief: Why you can have a great income and a bad credit score (posts Nov 7, 2026)

## Facts
- FICO scores use only information in your credit report; income is not part of a credit report or FICO Score. Lenders may separately consider income and employment (myFICO; CFPB).
- FICO weights (general population): payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of credit history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10% (myFICO; Federal Reserve Education).
- Late payments (30+ days) can stay on credit reports for up to 7 years (Fair Credit Reporting Act; CFPB).
- Credit utilization = revolving balances / credit limits; a major part of "amounts owed".

## Worked example
$5,000 balance / $10,000 limit = 50% utilization; paying down to $1,000 = 10%.
