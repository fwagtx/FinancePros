## Issues (must fix)
1. **Line 0 (hook): inaccurate scope plus weak open.** "Sextortion reports hit 137 a day" is wrong: 137/day is financial sextortion only. All sextortion was more than 80,000 (about 219/day). It also opens on a date ("In twenty twenty-five"), which wastes the first second.
   - text: `Child sextortion-for-cash reports: 137 a day.`
   - say: `Child sextortion-for-cash reports: a hundred thirty-seven a day.` (8 words, under 3 s)
   - scene: keep (title "A money scam, [137] a day", value "137/day").
2. **Line 2: math wording and a spoken/on-screen mismatch.** "nearly 5x more" is a ratio error ("as many", not "more"). On screen says 10,731 but the narration says "about eleven thousand".
   - text: `In 2022 it was about 11,000, so 4.6x as many (our math).`
   - say: `In twenty twenty-two it was about eleven thousand. That's four point six times as many.`
   - Chart unchanged.
3. **POST title, IG line 1, TikTok: same scope error.** Each needs "financial".
   - title: `Financial sextortion hit 137 reports a day in 2025`
   - ig line 1: `Financial sextortion reports hit 137 a day in 2025.`
   - tiktok start: `137 financial sextortion reports a day in 2025. Don't pay, report it.` (rest unchanged)
4. **Line 7 / safety resources: no CyberTipline for minors.** The video sends everyone to ic3.gov only. For minors, NCMEC's CyberTipline is the primary channel (FBI tips.fbi.gov / 1-800-CALL-FBI also apply).
   - text: `Under 18? Use the CyberTipline and Take It Down. Need to talk? Call or text 988.`
   - say: `Under eighteen? Use the CyberTipline and Take It Down. Need to talk? Call or text nine eight eight.`
   - board lines: `["Teen? CyberTipline", "+ Take It Down", "Need help? 988"]`. Mood: `"think"`, not `"happy"`, because "happy" is tonally wrong next to a crisis line.
   - IG: change "Under 18: Take It Down." to `Under 18: CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org) and Take It Down.`
5. **Line 6: add a no-blame line.** NCMEC says "the blackmailer is to blame, not the child." Nothing in the script is victim-blaming, but given the suicide risk this protective line should be explicit.
   - text: `So don't pay, stop replying, save everything, and report it at ic3.gov. It's not your fault.`
   - say: `So don't pay, stop replying, save everything, and report it at I C three dot gov. It's not your fault.`
   - list unchanged (items within 14 chars).
