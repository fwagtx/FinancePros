# Fact-check: 2027-01-16-business (Starbucks cold drinks, posts Sat Jan 16, 2027, 2 PM CT)

Checked Nov 17, 2026. I verified each claim with independent web search. Some primary hosts (sec.gov, fool.com, q4cdn PDFs) were blocked by the proxy and Firecrawl had no credits, so where noted I relied on search-result snippets of those primary documents.

## Claim verification

| # | Claim | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0/1 | Roughly 2/3 of drinks at US company-operated stores are cold (Investor Day, Jan 2026) | **Verified.** The Starbucks blog and MarketBeat both say "roughly two-thirds of the beverages sold in U.S. company-operated coffeehouses are cold". The same source puts international at ~60% cold, so the claim "most Starbucks drinks are cold" holds. |
| 2 | Cold = 75% of US beverage sales, Q3 FY2023 | **Verified.** The Aug 1, 2023 call and the Q3 slide say "Cold business reached 75% of U.S. beverage sales". Q3 FY23 ran Apr 3 to Jul 2, 2023, so calling it "one summer quarter" was imprecise. "Hot 25%" is my own inference (100 minus 75), which is reasonable. |
| 3 | "Hot coffee is now the smaller business" | **Unfair as written.** It rests on one seasonal, warm-weather quarter from 2023, and "now" plus "business" implies a current dollar split that Starbucks does not publish. The Jan 2026 count data does support "hot is now the minority by count". |
| 4 | Niccol quote, Oct 29, 2026 | **Partly verified.** Transcript snippets (Motley Fool, Benzinga) read "...the combination of customers moving to cold, which also results in usually a little bit bigger size, [and then?] the idea of modifying with cold foam, whether it's matcha, cold brew, iced coffee, or iced Americanos, continues to see a really nice impact in the afternoon." I could not confirm the connector "and then", and the on-screen quote stopped mid-sentence at "cold foam". The speaker is Niccol per the Motley Fool snippet. |
| 5 | Add-ons/modifiers over $1B a year (2023) | **Verified but dated.** The Q3 FY23 call said modifiers "contribute to over $1 billion in revenue annually". There is fresher data from Investor Day, Jan 2026: cold foam is "one-third of its billion-dollar customization business". |
| 6 | Beverages 61% / food 19% of FY2025 revenue (10-K) | **Derived, not stated.** The 10-K's stated mix is 73% beverage / 23% food / 4% other, and it covers company-operated store sales only. Applied to total revenue, it works out to about 60.6% / 19.0%, which is consistent with the script. **The FY2026 10-K (fiscal year ended Sep 27, 2026) has been filed** on EDGAR (accession 0000829224-26-000162). I could not read its mix table. A secondary source cites about $22.4B beverage (≈59%) and $7.4B food (≈20%) of $37.9B. A Jan 2027 post citing "fiscal 2025" would therefore be stale. |
| Cover | "Cold Pays More" | **Overclaims.** Starbucks reports no profit or margin by hot vs. cold. The evidence covers sales share, cup size and add-ons only. |

## Issues found and fixes applied

1. **Hook (line 0) was weak, with no open loop.** It was true but only a stat, with nothing that makes the viewer need the answer.
   - Old: "About two out of three Starbucks drinks in the US are cold."
   - New: "Most Starbucks drinks are now cold. Here's why that pays." (10 words, under 3s.) This is true for the US (~2/3) and for international (~60%).
   - Hook tag changed to label COLD / value MOST, so no unspoken number appears on screen.
2. **Line 1.** I folded the exact figure into this line so the hook stays short.
   - Text: "Roughly 2 out of 3 drinks at its US company-run stores are cold, Starbucks said in January 2026." The "~2/3" stat matches what is spoken.
3. **Line 2: "one summer quarter" was imprecise.**
   - Text: "In dollars, cold hit 75% of US beverage sales from April to June 2023. Hot was just 25%."
   - The spoken line now says "twenty-five percent" to match the 25% on screen.
4. **Line 3 overgeneralized from one quarter.**
   - Text: "That's a warm quarter, but by count, hot is now the minority." This is supported by the Jan 2026 count data.
5. **Line 4 quote was not verifiable as exact and did not end naturally.**
   - The quote is now the verified clause: "…customers moving to cold, which also results in usually a little bit bigger size" (14 words). It ends at a natural clause boundary.
   - The narration was tightened to "...cold drinks usually come bigger, and many get cold foam."
6. **Line 5 used dated 2023 data.**
   - Updated to the Jan 2026 Investor Day framing: "Starbucks calls add-ons a $1 billion business; cold foam is about a third of it."
   - Stat changed to $1B / "Cold foam: ~1/3". This removes the "+" that was not spoken.
7. **Line 6 was stale for Jan 2027 and mislabeled.** The FY2026 10-K is out, and "beverages" in the 10-K means drinks sold in company-operated stores.
   - Text: "Drinks sold in its own stores bring in about 60% of all revenue; food, about 20%."
   - Board changed to "Share of revenue / Drinks: ~60% / Food: ~20%".
   - These rounded figures hold for FY2025 (60.6/19.0) and for FY2026 (≈59/≈20 per the secondary source), so the line stays true whichever 10-K is current.
8. **Line 7 overclaimed: "usually means... extras".**
   - Text: "So a cold drink often means a bigger cup, an add-on, and a bigger check."
9. **Cover.** "Cold Pays More" became **"Cold Rules Sales"** (16 chars), with label ICED changed to COLD and value 75% kept. The video pays this off with 75% of beverage sales and 2/3 of drinks.
10. **Post title.** It implied an all-time "more sales than hot coffee". It is now "How cold drinks drive Starbucks sales". I updated the IG, TikTok and YouTube captions to match the new figures and wording, and added "Starbucks does not report profit by hot vs. cold" to the YouTube description.

**Other checks**
- No advice, price predictions or political opinion.
- No math errors. 75 + 25 = 100, and the ~60/~20 figures are consistent with the derivation.
- Time words: "now", "January 2026" and "October 2026" all remain correct on Jan 16, 2027. Starbucks' Q1 FY27 report is expected in late January 2027, after the posting date.
- check.py: OK, 9 lines, 139 spoken words, hook 10 words.

**Residual caveat:** I could not open the exact FY2026 10-K mix table or the official FactSet transcript PDF because the proxy blocked them. The rounded revenue shares and the shortened quote were chosen to stay accurate within what search snippets confirm.
