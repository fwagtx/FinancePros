# Fact-check: 2027-01-03 Hot Take, "Money myth: New Year's money resolutions fail because of willpower"

Checked Nov 4, 2026, for posting Sunday Jan 3, 2027, 6 PM Central. Every claim below was checked against web sources, not the brief. (workplace.vanguard.com and psca.org are blocked in this session, so the Vanguard PDF could not be opened directly. Its figures were confirmed through Vanguard-hosted summaries and trade press: NAPA, InvestmentNews, PlanAdviser, PlanSponsor, 401k Specialist.)

## Verification results

| Claim | Status | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Fidelity: "save more" was the top 2026 money resolution, at 44% | TRUE, but going stale | Fidelity 17th Annual New Year Financial Resolutions Study, Business Wire, Dec 11, 2025: save more 44%, pay down debt 36%, spend less 30%. The 44% is a share of people *considering a resolution*, not of all Americans. Fidelity releases a new study every December, so the 2027 study will probably be out by Jan 3, 2027. |
| Vanguard 2025: 94% participation in auto-enroll plans vs 64% in voluntary plans | TRUE (numbers); WORDING WRONG | How America Saves 2026 (June 16, 2026). | Share of Vanguard plans with auto-enrollment, 2018-2025: 48, 50, 54, 56, 58, 59, 61, 61 | ALL TRUE | 2018: 48% (HAS 2019, InvestmentNews). 2019: 50% (HAS 2020, PlanAdviser / Vanguard chart). 2020: 54%. 2021: 56% (HAS 2022). 2022: 58%. 2023: 59%. 2024: 61% (HAS 2025). 2025: 61% (HAS 2026). ## Issues and exact replacements

**1. Line 0 (hook): adequate but weak.** At 11 words it fits under 3 seconds, but it is a framing claim with no specific fact, so it does not make the viewer need the answer. The cover's 94% is on screen but not spoken until line 2.
Stronger, true, specific (9 words):
- text: `94% vs 64%. The gap isn't willpower.`
- say: `Ninety-four percent versus sixty-four. The gap isn't willpower.`
- The scene stays the same (pot / AUTO / 94%). The title can stay "Not a [willpower] problem."
(If you keep the original hook, total spoken words are 136; with this one, 134.)

**2. Line 1: stale time word.** On Jan 3, 2027, "for 2026" is last year's study, and Fidelity's Dec 2026 study will probably be out. Also, the 44% is of people making resolutions.
- text: `A year ago, Fidelity found saving more was the top money resolution, picked by 44%.`

- sub: `Fidelity, Dec 2025` (keep). Label: `Top resolution: save more` (25 chars; if over the 18 limit, keep `Want to save more`).
- Before posting, check whether Fidelity's 18th study (Dec 2026) is out. If it is, swap in its figure and say "This year, Fidelity found..."

**3. Line 2: "joined" wording is misleading. Also trimmed for word count.**
- text: `In 2025, 94% of workers took part in Vanguard plans that auto-enroll. Opt-in plans? 64%.`

- Compare title (lines 2 and 3): `401(k) participation, 2025` (replaces "sign-ups," which is not what the measure is).

**4. Line 3: "Same kinds of workers" is unsupported.**

**5. Line 4: minor trim for word count (all data verified).**
