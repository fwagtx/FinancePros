## Issues

1. **lines[5] (text + say): inaccurate mechanism. Required fix.**
   Problem: "lean on the same benchmark" refers back to the prime rate named in lines[4]. Savings yields do not follow prime. Banks set them, and they loosely follow the Fed's short-term rate (fed funds). The sentence also says flatly that savings "get no fresh boost". Banks can change savings rates at any time, so the claim should be limited to this decision.
   Suggested replacement (text and say):
   "Savings account yields tend to move with the Fed's rate, so this decision gives them no fresh boost either."

2. **lines[4] (text + say): minor precision fix (optional).**
   Problem: "credit card rates, which follow the prime rate" overgeneralizes, because a small share of cards have fixed APRs.
   Suggested replacement (text and say):
   "For you, a hold means most credit card rates, which follow the prime rate, get no new bump from this meeting."
