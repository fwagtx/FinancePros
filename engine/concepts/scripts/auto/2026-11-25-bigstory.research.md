## Context
- PCE = Personal Consumption Expenditures price index: prices on what households buy (including what's paid on their behalf, like health care). It is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge.
- Inflation broadening plus a strong economy (4.3% GDP) is why markets expect the Fed to RAISE rates in December, not cut.

## What it means for a normal person (education, not advice)
- A Fed hike typically pushes up variable rates: credit card APRs, HELOCs, new auto/personal loan rates. Savings and money-market yields tend to rise too.
- Prices still rising ~3.8% a year means a raise below that is a pay cut in real terms.
