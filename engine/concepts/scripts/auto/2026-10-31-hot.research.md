# Research brief: Face-off, 15-year vs 30-year mortgage (posts Oct 31, 2026)

## Rates (Freddie Mac PMMS, week of Sept 24, 2026)
- 30-year fixed: 7.03% (6.95% prior week; 6.30% a year earlier). First time above 7% since January 2025.
- 15-year fixed: 6.42% (6.26% prior week).
- Earlier September 2026: Sept 3 30y 6.71% / 15y 6.04%; Sept 10 6.76% / 6.09%; Sept 17 6.95% / 6.26%.

## Worked example: $400,000 loan, principal and interest only (standard amortization)
Payment M = P*i / (1 - (1+i)^-n), with i = rate/12.
- 30-year at 7.03%: M = $2,669.27; total paid $960,939; total interest $560,939.
- 15-year at 6.42%: M = $3,466.86; total paid $624,035; total interest $224,035.
- 15-year costs $797.59 more per month, and saves $336,904 in interest. It's paid off 15 years sooner.
- The surprise: the 15-year's payment is only about 30% higher, but its total interest is about 60% lower.
