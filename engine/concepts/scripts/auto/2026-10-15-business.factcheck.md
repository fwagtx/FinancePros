## Issues
1. **L9 scene (must fix, factual):** the split panel says "Charity gets: Price minus value." That's wrong. The charity receives the full price paid. For a donated auction item it keeps the whole $2,000. The price-minus-value gap is the donor's *deductible gift*, which is what the narration correctly says. Fix: change the right column title to "Your donation" or "Tax-deductible gift" and keep the item "Price minus value". You could also change the left title to "You get (fair market value)".
2. **L8 clarity (minor):** "Pay over seventy-five dollars, and the charity has to tell you that in writing" leaves out the condition: the rule applies only when you get something back. "That" also has no clear antecedent. Suggested say/text: "Pay over seventy-five dollars for something you get back, and the charity must tell you in writing what's deductible." Stat label: "Written disclosure required for quid pro quo payments over".
3. **L1 hook (minor, could be stronger):** it's true, specific, and short enough (about 2.5 s), but it has no open loop. It's also five-month-old news (gala was 2026-05-04) by the Oct 15 post date. That's acceptable for an explainer, but the hook needs the curiosity gap more. Suggested: "The Met Gala raised forty-two million dollars in one night. Here's who actually pays." Or: "...and most of it isn't really a donation."
4. **L3 chart (minor):** the x-axis jumps from 2019 to 2021. If points are evenly spaced, add a small "no 2020 gala" note or keep proportional year spacing.
5. **L7 (minor nuance, optional):** a deduction only helps if the buyer itemizes or qualifies for a deduction. It's fine as is because the line says "deductible part".
6. **Time words:** "in 2026" and "2025" are absolute, so nothing will be wrong on the posting date. Post copy "(May 2026)" is fine.

Cover/title payoff: "$42 Million in One Night" is paid off in L1 to L4, and "How charity galas raise millions" in L5 to L9. OK.

VERDICT: FAIL
