# Research: How Spotify pays artists (How They Make Money, posts Oct 14, 2026)

## How the money flows (Spotify Loud & Clear FAQ)
- Spotify does NOT pay a per-stream rate. Royalties are paid by "streamshare": a rights holder's share of total streams in a market/period, applied to the royalty pool. Listeners pay for access, not per stream.
- Spotify pays roughly two-thirds of every dollar generated from music to rights holders (labels, publishers, distributors, collecting societies).
- Spotify does not pay artists or songwriters directly; rights holders pay artists under their own contracts.
- Since April 2024: a track must have at least 1,000 streams in the previous 12 months (plus a minimum number of unique listeners, number not public) to earn recorded-music royalties. Spotify said tracks with 1-1,000 annual streams averaged $0.03/month and were 0.5% of streams. (Spotify for Artists blog "Modernizing Our Royalty System"; Spotify support "Track monetization eligibility"; MBW)

## Payouts (Loud & Clear 2026, published March 2026)
- More than $11 billion paid to the music industry in 2025; up more than 10% year over year; all-time total nearly $70 billion.
- More than 13,800 artists generated at least $100,000 from Spotify alone in 2025 (about 1,400 more than prior year); more than 1,500 generated over $1 million.
- Roughly half of royalties generated by independent artists and labels.

## Chartable series: Spotify annual payouts to music industry (USD, Loud & Clear / Spotify newsroom)
- 2020: $5B (per 2022 Loud & Clear coverage, Consequence/MusicTech)
- 2021: $7B+
- 2022: not confirmed in searches (omitted from chart)
- 2023: $9B (Billboard, Variety, MBW)
- 2024: $10B (Loud & Clear 2025)
- 2025: $11B+ (Loud & Clear 2026)

## Company financials: Spotify Form 20-F, fiscal 2025 (foreign private issuer; files 20-F, not 10-K)
- Revenue: EUR 17,186 million. Gross profit EUR 5,496 million; gross margin 32% (30% in 2024).
- Premium gross margin 34%; ad-supported 18%.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
- Assume a royalty pool of $1,000,000 and an artist whose tracks get 1% of all streams: 0.01 x $1,000,000 = $10,000 paid to the rights holder (label).
- Assume a label contract paying the artist a 20% royalty: 0.20 x $10,000 = $2,000 to the artist.

## Surprising fact
- There is no per-stream rate at all; "per-stream" figures online are after-the-fact averages.
