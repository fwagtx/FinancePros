## The story (facts with dates)
- Oct 8, 2026: The Financial Times reported OpenAI told prospective investors its annualized ("run-rate") revenue was approaching/near $50 billion at the end of September. CNBC confirmed OpenAI told investors it hit roughly $50 billion annualized at the end of September.
- The figure widely circulating before: about $70 billion (FT and most outlets); CNBC had cited $68 billion "widely reported late last month." Gap ~ $20 billion (Yahoo Finance, TechCrunch headlines: "$20 billion lower").
- Why the gap (per FT/CNBC sourcing): accounting. Anthropic counts sales made through cloud partners (AWS, Google Cloud) in full (gross); OpenAI counts only the share it keeps (net). Investors adjusted OpenAI's numbers upward to compare, producing the ~$70B estimate. The earlier $68B "also included gross revenue from partners" (CNBC, person familiar).
- OpenAI also told investors run-rate grew 77% in its third quarter, enterprise run-rate +107% (person familiar, via CNBC/FT relay). OpenAI made no public statement confirming the $50B figure (not found).
- "Annualized revenue" = one period's revenue extrapolated to a full year; not cash received.
