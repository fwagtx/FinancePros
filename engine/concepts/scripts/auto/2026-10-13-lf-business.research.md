# Research brief: How Amazon makes money (it's not the store) — long-form, posts Tue Oct 13, 2026

Latest full fiscal year: FY2025 (Form 10-K, year ended Dec 31, 2025; Q4 2025 release Feb 5, 2026). Latest quarter: Q2 2026 (release July 30, 2026). Q3 2026 results come after the posting date, so FY2025 + Q2 2026 are the latest figures.

## Timely angle
- Q2 2026 (July 30, 2026): net sales $200.6B (+20% from $167.7B); first quarter over $200B... NOTE: Q4 2025 was $213.4B, so do NOT call Q2 2026 a first. Operating income $27.5B.
- AWS Q2 2026: sales $42.2B (+37%, fastest in 18 quarters per coverage), operating income $16.6B. NA op income $9.1B, International $1.7B.
  -> AWS = 16.6 / 27.5 = 60% of Q2 2026 operating income.
- Advertising Q2 2026: $19.8B (+26%). 3P seller services Q2 2026: $46.8B (from $40.3B). Online stores Q2 2026: $70.4B (+15%). Subscription Q2 2026: $13.7B (+12%).
- 2026 capex plan raised to about $220B (from about $200B guided in Feb 2026), memory costs cited (CNBC, July 30, 2026).
- Jassy quote (Q2 2026 call, July 30, 2026), exact: "We now believe we will spend approximately $220 billion in cash CapEx in 2026" ... "But even at that amount, we will still not have enough capacity to meet all the demand we have in 2026, and I believe this dynamic will also be true in 2027 too." (CNBC; Beth Kindig transcript post; Yahoo/Motley Fool)
- Free cash flow, trailing 12 months ended June 30, 2026: -$7.6B (negative); operating cash flow TTM $161.4B (+33%). Driver: $66.1B YoY increase in purchases of property and equipment (Amazon Q2 2026 release via StockTitan 8-K summary).

## FY2025 (10-K)
- Net sales $716.9B (2024: $638.0B, +12%). Operating income $80.0B ($79,975M). 2025 includes $2.5B FTC settlement charge and $2.7B severance.
- Segments: North America sales $426.3B / op income $29.6B; International $161.9B / $4.75B; AWS $128.7B / $45.6B.
- Store side (NA + Intl, includes stores, 3P services, ads, subscriptions): $588.2B sales, $34.35B op income.
- SURPRISE: AWS = 18% of sales (128.7/716.9) but 57% of operating income (45.6/80.0).
- Net sales by product group, FY2025 (sum = $716.9B):
  Online stores $269.3B | Third-party seller services $172.2B | AWS $128.7B | Advertising $68.6B ($68,635M, +22% from $56.2B) | Subscription $49.6B (+12%) | Physical stores $22.6B | Other $5.9B.
- Cash flow 2025: operating cash flow $139.5B (+20%); purchases of property & equipment $131.8B gross; free cash flow $11.2B (2024: $38.2B).
- Third-party sellers = 61% of paid units in Q4 2025 (Amazon quarterly disclosure, via Marketplace Pulse/SmartScout).

## Worked examples
1. Profit per $100 of sales, FY2025: AWS 45.6/128.7 = 0.354 -> $35; store side 34.35/588.2 = 0.058 -> $6. About 6x.
2. Prime math (prices as of Sept 2026): monthly $14.99 x 12 = $179.88; annual $139; difference $40.88 a year.
3. Hypothetical seller example (label as example): $20 item, 15% referral fee (standard rate in most categories, unchanged for 2026 per seller-fee guides) = $3.00 to Amazon, before any shipping/fulfillment fees.
4. 2022: AWS op income $22.8B > total Amazon op income $12.2B, because North America lost $2.8B and International lost $7.7B (FY2022 10-K).

## History
- July 5, 1994 founded by Jeff Bezos in Bellevue, WA; July 1995 launched as online bookstore; May 1997 IPO at $18/share.
- 2005: Prime launched, $79/yr, free two-day shipping.
- March 14, 2006: AWS launches S3 (storage); EC2 later in 2006.
- January 29, 2024: Prime Video began showing ads by default in US; ad-free add-on $2.99/month (Axios, Jan 29, 2024).

## Competition / risks
- Cloud market share Q2 2026 (Synergy Research via The Register, Aug 2026): AWS 28%, Microsoft 20%, Google 15%; market $143B for quarter, +43%.
- FTC antitrust suit (filed Sept 2023): trial scheduled March 29, 2027 (MLex; Judge John Chun). Allegations; Amazon denies wrongdoing.
- FTC Prime case: Sept 25, 2025 settlement $2.5B ($1B civil penalty + $1.5B refunds); Amazon did not admit wrongdoing (NPR, Sept 25, 2025).
- AWS outage Oct 20, 2025 knocked many apps offline for hours (AP/Reuters widely reported).
- Amazon does not report advertising profit separately.
