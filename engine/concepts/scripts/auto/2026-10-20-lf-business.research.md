# Research brief: How Costco became a membership-fee machine (long-form, posts Oct 20, 2026)

## Timely angle
Costco reported fiscal 2026 results on Sep 24, 2026 (52 weeks ended Aug 30, 2026). Analysts (Motley Fool, Sep 23, 2026) note the boost from the Sept 2024 fee hike is fading.

## Fiscal 2026 (Costco Q4/FY2026 earnings release, Sep 24, 2026; 8-K)
- Net sales: $297.2B (+10.1% from $269.9B). Membership fees: $5.907B (FY2025: $5.323B, +11%). Total revenue: $303.2B.
- Operating income: $11.69B (FY2025: $10.38B). Net income: $9.226B ($20.76 a share), vs $8.099B.
- Fees as share of operating income: 5.907 / 11.69 = 50.5%, "about half".
- Operating profit from merchandise: 11.69 - 5.91 = $5.78B, which is 5.78 / 297.2 = 1.9% of net sales, about 2 cents per dollar.
- Paid members: 84.1M (+3.8%). Executive members: 42.3M (+9.4%). Cardholders: 150.4M.
- Renewal rates: 92.3% US and Canada, 89.8% worldwide (Q4 FY2026).
- Warehouses: 939 at the end of FY2026 (914 at the start; 28 openings, 25 net new).
- Q4 included a one-time benefit of $0.15 a share from IEEPA tariff refunds.

## Fees and history
- Fee history (Gold Star / basic): $25 (1983), $45 (2000), $50 (May 2006), $55 (Nov 2011), $60 (June 2017), $65 (Sept 1, 2024).
- Executive: $120 to $130 (Sept 2024). The 2% reward cap went from $1,000 to $1,250.
- Price Club opened in 1976 (Sol Price, San Diego). Costco opened its first warehouse in Seattle in 1983 (Jim Sinegal, Jeffrey Brotman). The two merged in 1993.
- Hot dog and soda: $1.50 since 1985. Rotisserie chicken: $4.99 since 2009 (CNN, NPR, CNBC).
- About 4,000 items vs about 30,000 at a typical supermarket. Kirkland Signature is about a third of sales (CNBC 2025).
- Markup: Costco co-founder Jim Sinegal described capping markups at about 14% (15% for Kirkland), per many interviews and profiles.

## Worked example: when Executive pays off
- Extra cost: $130 - $65 = $65. Reward: 2% of eligible purchases.
- Break-even: $65 / 0.02 = $3,250 a year = $270.83 a month.
- Cap: $1,250 / 0.02 = $62,500 of spending to max out the reward.

## Surprising fact
- About half of Costco's operating income comes from membership fees. The store itself makes about 2 cents per dollar of sales.
