He lists a third factor himself: the Dow vs the overall market. "The Dow's 89% fall took 25 years on price alone, but about 7 counting dividends and deflation" says something Hulbert did not. FIX L95: "On price alone, the Dow took 25 years to get back. Counting dividends and deflation, the average stock took about 7." In the compare scenes, relabel the right card "Avg stock, div+defl".
2. **L36-38: a single-source estimate stated as fact, with no spoken attribution.** Hulbert only appears in the on-screen sub. FIX L38 say: "Count both, and by market analyst Mark Hulbert's math, someone who bought the average stock at the 1929 high was back to even by late 1936." Also, Hulbert ties the ~14% yield to the moment the Dow hit 41.22. FIX L36: "When the Dow bottomed in 1932, dividend yields were close to 14%."
