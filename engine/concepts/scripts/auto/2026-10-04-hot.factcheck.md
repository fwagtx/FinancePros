## Issues

1. **Lines 4 and 6 (time words and tense).** "One credit took $1,890 ... in 2026" and "Medicare hospital coverage cost $565 a month that year" use the past tense, but 2026 is still the current year on October 4, and these amounts are in effect today. "That year" is also vague and sounds as if the figure is out of date. The post text ("took", "maxed out at", "cost ... in 2026") does the same thing.
   - Fix for line 4: "One credit takes $1,890 of taxed pay in 2026, and the bar rises every year."
   - Fix for line 6: text "The surprise: under 30 credits, Medicare Part A costs $565 a month in 2026." and say "...Medicare hospital coverage costs five hundred sixty-five dollars a month in twenty twenty-six."
   - Make the same present-tense change in the ig, tiktok and yt_desc text. None of the numbers are stale. The 2027 credit amount and SSI amount come out with the COLA in mid-October, and the 2027 Part A premium comes out around November, so all of these land after the post date.
2. **Line 1 (hook): true, but weak.** "Never pay into Social Security, and your retirement check is $0" is accurate. It says in about 3 seconds what most viewers already assume, so it creates little need to keep watching. The surprising, specific fact in this video is the Medicare cost. A stronger version: "No Social Security credits? Medicare can charge you $565 a month." (say: "No Social Security credits? Medicare can charge you five hundred sixty-five dollars a month."). You could also keep the current hook and add "...and that's not even the worst part." Not a FAIL item on its own.
3. **Line 8 split (minor accuracy nuance).** The "Married to a worker" column lists only the spouse benefit. Being married to someone with 30 or more quarters also qualifies you for the reduced Part A premium, and 40 or more quarters (on a qualifying spouse's record) makes Part A premium-free. That undercuts the "Paid Medicare Part A" item on the left side. Optional fix: add "Premium-free Part A via spouse" to the right-hand items (CMS/Medicare.gov: premium-free Part A if you or your spouse paid Medicare taxes for 10 years).
4. **Line 5 (minor nuance).** SSI also requires being age 65 or older, blind or disabled, plus income and asset limits ($2,000 in resources for an individual). "For people with very little" is fine for a short. Optionally say "for people 65-plus with very little".
5. **Line 8 (minor nuance).** "Up to half" is the maximum, reached at the spouse's full retirement age. It is correctly hedged with "up to", so no change is needed.
