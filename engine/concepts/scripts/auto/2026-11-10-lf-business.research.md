# Research brief: How Apple makes money (long-form, posts Nov 10, 2026)

DATE NOTE: Apple's fiscal year ends late September. The latest full-year 10-K available on Oct 1, 2026 is fiscal 2025 (year ended Sept 27, 2025; 10-K filed Oct 31, 2025). Apple reports fiscal 2026 Q4/full-year results around Oct 29-30, 2026, BEFORE the Nov 10 post date. So the script labels every full-year figure "fiscal 2025" and never calls it "latest"; it says fiscal 2026 full-year results came out in late October 2026 and are not covered.

## Fiscal 2025 net sales by category (10-K, year ended Sept 27, 2025)
- iPhone $209.586B (+4%) = 50.4% of total
- Services $109.158B (+14%) = 26.2%
- Wearables, Home and Accessories $35.686B (-4%) = 8.6%
- Mac $33.708B (+12%) = 8.1%
- iPad $28.023B (+5%) = 6.7%
- Total $416.161B (+6%); Products total $307.003B
- 10-K: Services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud, digital content, payment and other services. Services growth "primarily from higher advertising, App Store, and cloud services".

## Fiscal 2025 gross margin (10-K)
- Products: $112.887B gross margin = 36.8% (37.2% in FY2024)
- Services: $82.314B = 75.4% (73.9% in FY2024)
- Total: $195.201B = 46.9%
- Apple does NOT break out gross margin per device (no separate iPhone margin).
- 10-K: products margin up on favorable costs and mix, "partially offset by tariff costs".
- SURPRISE FACT: Services = 26.2% of net sales but 42.2% of gross profit (82.314 / 195.201).
- Other FY2025: operating income $133.05B; net income $112.010B; R&D $34.55B.

## Worked examples
- Per $100 of sales, FY2025: Products -> $36.80 gross profit; Services -> $75.40. Services keeps about 2x per dollar.
- App Store (illustrative, ignores taxes): $10 purchase at 30% -> Apple $3, developer $7. At 15% -> Apple $1.50, developer $8.50.
- Capital return FY2025: $89.3B buybacks + $15.4B dividends = $104.7B, about 93% of $112.0B net income.

## Installed base (company-stated)
- More than 2.5 billion active devices, stated Jan 29, 2026 (Q1 FY26 results), up from 2.35 billion stated Jan 2025.

## App Store
- Standard commission 30%; 15% for Small Business Program (developers under $1M proceeds in prior calendar year) and for subscriptions after the first year.
- Apple-commissioned Analysis Group study (June 2026): App Store ecosystem facilitated $1.4 trillion in developer billings and sales in 2025; for more than 90% Apple took no commission (e.g., physical goods, ride/food orders, in-app ads).
- Epic v. Apple: April 2025 federal judge found Apple in contempt over its link-out terms (including a 27% commission on outside purchases); Ninth Circuit affirmed contempt but said Apple may charge a fee tied to genuine costs; Supreme Court agreed June 30, 2026 to hear Apple's appeal (next term). Apple disputes the contempt finding.

## Google search payments (US v. Google)
- Aug 5, 2024: Judge Amit Mehta ruled Google illegally maintained a search monopoly; ruling found Google paid Apple about $20 billion in 2022 for default placement in Safari.
- Trial testimony (Nov 2023): Apple gets 36% of Google search ad revenue from Safari.
- Sept 2, 2025 remedies: payments still allowed; exclusive deals barred; default deals limited to one year.
- Apple's FY2025 10-K quote: remedies "could materially adversely affect the Company's ability to earn revenue from such licensing arrangements."
- Google appealed (notice Jan 16, 2026); DOJ cross-appealed (Feb 2026) seeking stronger remedies; pending at D.C. Circuit as of Aug 2026.

## Capital return
- FY2025: repurchased 402 million shares for $89.3B; dividends $15.4B. May 2025: $100B authorization. Apr 30, 2026: another $100B.
