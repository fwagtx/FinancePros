## Issues and fixes

1. **Line 6: a red flag is attributed to regulators without support (factual; this causes the FAIL).** The other three flags check out. "An agent who won't share records" is not in the CFPB, FinCEN or interagency red-flag lists I could find. Swap it for FinCEN's flag.
   - text: `Regulators' red flags: sudden big withdrawals, new names on accounts, unpaid bills, and a helper who won't let them speak for themselves.`
   - say: `Regulators' red flags: sudden big withdrawals, new names on accounts, unpaid bills, and a helper who won't let them speak for themselves.`
   - scene.items: `["Big withdrawals", "New co-owners", "Unpaid bills", "Controlling helper"]`. If the list limit is under 18 characters, use `"Pushy helper"`.
   - post.ig: change "an agent who won't share records" to "a helper who won't let them speak for themselves".

2. **Cover pairs the wrong number with the title (misleading).** "When Family Takes The Money" next to "$27B" suggests family took $27B. Most of the $27B is elder scams by strangers (about 80% of reported activity). Elder theft by people the victim knows is a minority.
   - cover: `{"title": "When Family Takes The Money", "prop": "tag", "label": "FinCEN 2024", "value": "#1: kids"}`

3. **Line 1: the opening is too slow.** It is true and specific, but at 11 words it runs about 3.5-4 s spoken.
   - text: `Elder theft's most common suspects in bank reports? Adult children (FinCEN, 2024).`
   - say: `Elder theft's most common suspects? Adult children.` (7 words, under 3 s)
   - scene: unchanged.

4. **Line 3: on-screen and spoken numbers don't match, and the wording presumes guilt.** The screen shows $98,863 and $23,762 while the voice says "about ninety-nine thousand" and "about twenty-four thousand." "The thief" also asserts guilt; these are suspicious-activity reports.
   - text: `When the suspect was someone the victim knew, the average report involved about $99,000. The median: about $24,000.`
   - say: `When the suspect was someone the victim knew, the average report involved about ninety-nine thousand dollars. The median, about twenty-four thousand.`
   - scene: left.value `"$99K"`, right.value `"$24K"` (both at most 7 characters). Other fields unchanged.

5. **Line 4: unsupported "common tool" framing that makes POAs sound bad.** None of the sources I found say how often POAs show up in elder-theft reports. The line also needs a neutral note so the video doesn't imply POAs or families are bad.
   - text: `A power of attorney lets an agent handle someone's money. Most agents are honest; some misuse it.`
   - say: `A power of attorney lets an agent handle someone's money. Most agents are honest; some misuse it.`
   - scene: unchanged.

6. **Line 2: minor accuracy and tightening.** These are BSA reports from all financial institutions, not only banks (banks filed 72%).
   - text: `FinCEN reviewed 155,415 reports filed from June 2022 to June 2023: about $27 billion in suspected elder exploitation.`
   - say: `FinCEN reviewed a hundred fifty-five thousand reports filed from June twenty twenty-two to June twenty twenty-three: about twenty-seven billion dollars in suspected elder exploitation.`
   - scene: unchanged.

7. **Line 5: optional precision fix.** The current wording is acceptable. A tighter version, if word budget allows: `...agents must act in good faith, within their authority, and in the person's best interest.` This adds 3 words. Not required.

8. **Post text.**

   - post.ig: change "Theft by a trusted person averaged" to "Elder-theft reports (victim knew the suspect) averaged".
