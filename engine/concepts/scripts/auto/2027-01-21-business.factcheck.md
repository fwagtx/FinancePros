## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Hook was misleading (line 0).** "sells its goods at a loss" (present tense, general) suggests that every sale loses money. In fact the line is mostly early-buyout payoffs, booked against each item's remaining book value after rent-funded depreciation. New hook (11 words, true and specific): "Rent-A-Center's owner lost money on merchandise sales. Rent paid the bills." Hook title is now "Sales at a [loss]" and the cover is now "Sales at a Loss?" (16 chars).
2. **Missing context (new line 2).** Added: "Those include early buyouts, already partly paid for by rent." (list: Early buyouts / Rent paid part).
3. **$40 "costs" implied $60 profit (line 4).** Changed to "minus about $40 in product costs, leaves $60."
4. **Unconfirmed "often to several customers" (old line 5).** Removed. Merged into the ownership line as "Returns rent again."
5. **Ownership stat year.** Kept the FY2024 label. It is the latest confirmed figure.
6. **Chart line 8 was under 15 spoken words (10).** Now 15: "Acima added two hundred fifty-one million of twenty twenty-five's roughly three hundred seventy-five million jump." This also fixes the vague "of that growth" (growth from what?).
7. **Compare subs (charge-offs).** "Rent-A-Center" / "other stores" changed to "of revenue" on both sides, since these are lease charge-offs as a % of revenue.
8. **Header** "makes money on a TV" did not match once the TV list was removed. It is now "How rent-to-own / really makes money".
9. **Word trims** to stay at 140: "Its real money ... about 77%" became "Real money ... 77%" (77.3%); the revenue chart line was tightened.
10. **Post title, IG, TikTok and YT captions** were rewritten to match: the hook is now in past tense, the early-buyout context was added, the Acima $251M of about $375M was added, "about 40%" cost.
