# Research: How to save your first $10,000 (The Guide, long-form, posts Oct 14, 2026)

## Angle / hook
- No breaking news needed. Timely context: Fed raised rates Sept 16, 2026 to 3.75%-4.00% (first hike since 2023), so top savings yields are about 4%-4.5% APY in late Sept 2026 while the FDIC national average savings rate is 0.37% APY (Sept 21, 2026).
- Hook (worked example below): same plan ($416.67 a month for 24 months) earns $385.68 at a 4.00% APY vs $0.96 at 0.01% APY. "Same savings plan: $386 of interest, or 96 cents."

## Rules and limits
- FDIC insurance: $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category. Covers checking, savings, money market deposit accounts, CDs (FDIC).
- Money market FUNDS are not bank deposits and not FDIC insured; can lose value ("break the buck") (SEC Investor.gov bulletin).
- Fintech apps: Synapse (banking-as-a-service middleman) filed Chapter 11 April 2024, freezing over $265 million across 100,000+ accounts; estimated $60M-$90M shortfall; CFPB allocated $46.2M from its Civil Penalty Fund (approved Nov 28, 2025). FDIC insurance covers bank failure, not a fintech's failure. (Banking Dive, American Banker, CFPB.)
- Regulation D: April 24, 2020 Fed interim final rule removed the six-per-month limit on savings withdrawals/transfers; banks may still set their own limits (Federal Reserve).
- Interest is taxable income; banks send Form 1099-INT when interest is $10 or more; all interest is reportable even below $10 (IRS).
- I bonds: $10,000 per person per calendar year electronic limit; can't cash in first 12 months; lose last 3 months of interest if cashed before 5 years; composite rate 4.26% for bonds issued May-Oct 2026 (0.90% fixed) (TreasuryDirect, May 1, 2026). New rate set Nov 1.
- Treasury bills: mature in one year or less; interest federally taxable but exempt from state and local income tax (31 USC 3124).

## Worked examples (FinancePros math; assumptions: steady APY, deposits at month end, monthly compounding equivalent to APY)
- $10,000 / 12 months = $833.33/mo; / 24 = $416.67/mo; / 36 = $277.78/mo.
- Over 2 years: per week $10,000/104 = $96.15; per biweekly paycheck $10,000/52 = $192.31.
- $416.67 a month for 24 months (deposits $10,000.08):
  - 4.00% APY: $10,385.76 (interest $385.68)
  - 0.37% APY: $10,035.56 (interest $35.48)
  - 0.01% APY: $10,001.04 (interest $0.96)
- $10,000 for one year: 4% = $400; 0.37% = $37; prices up 3.4% (CPI Aug 2026) = $340 of purchasing power.
- Savings rate example: $416.67 from a $4,000 monthly take-home = 10.4% (vs 4.1% national personal saving rate, Aug 2026).
- Windfall example: a $3,275 refund (IRS avg as of Apr 17, 2026) covers 32.75% of $10,000; remaining $6,725 / 24 = $280.21/mo.
- Tax example: $385.68 interest in a 12% bracket = $46.28 federal tax.

## Surprising fact
- Where the money sits can matter more than the habit: the same 24 deposits earn $385.68 or 96 cents. And the typical (median) family's checking+savings total was $8,000 in 2022, so $10,000 is above the typical balance.
