## Issues found and fixed (applied to raw.json)
1. **Line 0 (hook).** The old line, "Netflix ended free password sharing. Then it added about thirty million members.", had no date, and "Then" implied the crackdown caused the growth.
   - New text: "2023: Netflix started charging sharers and gained nearly 30 million members."
   - New say: "Twenty twenty-three: Netflix started charging sharers and gained nearly thirty million members." (12 words)
   - It is literally true: paid sharing rolled out through 2023, and 2023 net adds were 29.53M, which is nearly 30M. "And" claims no causation, and the line-8 caveat stays.
2. **Line 2.** "US sharers had to ... pay $7.99 extra" was wrong about who pays. The account owner pays for an Extra Member; the sharer does not.
   - New text: "In May 2023, US sharers needed their own plan, or owners paid $7.99 monthly."
   - New say: "In May twenty twenty-three, US sharers needed their own plan, or owners paid seven ninety-nine monthly."
3. **Line 5.** "Pre-launch levels" is unclear to viewers (launch of what?). The quote card keeps Netflix's exact wording.
   - New text: "By July 2023, Netflix said every region's revenue beat pre-crackdown levels."
   - New say: "By July twenty twenty-three, Netflix said every region's revenue beat pre-crackdown levels."
4. **Line 7 (evergreen).** $7.99 is no longer the price. The US ad-free Extra Member went to $8.99 in Jan 2025 (TVLine, NBC, Jan 21, 2025), and the brief says $9.99 after March 2026. The line needed a date anchor.
   - New text: "Our math: at launch price, one extra member was $95.88 a year."
   - New say: "Our math: at launch price, one extra member was about ninety-six dollars a year."
5. **post.ig.**
   - The first line was dated and the causal "then" removed: "In 2023, Netflix started charging password sharers and added 29.5 million paid members that year."
   - The math line now reads "at the May 2023 US price ... (that price has since gone up)".
6. **post.tiktok.** Rewritten with the same non-causal, dated framing: "In 2023 Netflix began charging password sharers and added 29.5M paid members. Not financial advice. ..." It is exactly 150 characters.
