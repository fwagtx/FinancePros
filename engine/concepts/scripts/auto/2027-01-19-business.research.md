# Research brief: How medical labs make money on blood tests (posts Jan 19, 2027)

Researched Nov 20, 2026. Note: sec.gov, cms.gov and q4cdn.com were not reachable from this session (network), so filings were confirmed via search snippets of the company releases / reprints. Items marked UNCERTAIN need a second look.

## Key facts (with dates)
- Quest Diagnostics FY2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025) net revenues: $11.035B ($11.04B), up 11.8% from $9.872B in 2024. Source: Quest Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 10, 2026.
- Quest Diagnostic Information Services (DIS) revenue FY2025: $10.785B vs $9.614B in 2024 (+12.2%). DIS was ~98% of revenue.
- Quest FY2025 requisition volume +12.3% (organic +3.4%; much of the rest from acquisitions e.g. LifeLabs); revenue per requisition roughly flat (+0.1%). Source: same release.
- Quest processed ~244 million test requisitions (2025 Corporate Responsibility Report executive summary, ~8,000 patient access points, ~57,000 employees). Earlier reports: 208M (2022 report), ~206M (2023 report). UNCERTAIN: "~" company approximate figure; assumed to be FY2025 (secondary EDGAR summary says FY2025).
- Quest 10-K (MD&A): "Revenue per requisition is impacted by various factors, including ... fee schedule changes (i.e., unit price), test mix, payer mix, business mix and the number of tests per requisition."
- Quest 10-K FY2024: Medicaid + managed Medicaid ~8% of consolidated revenues; ACA exchange plans <5%. Medicare share not confirmed.
- Labcorp FY2025 revenue: $13.95B vs $13.01B in 2024 (Labcorp Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 2026). Includes a large biopharma services arm, so not used for per-order math.
- Medicare pays lab tests under the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule (CLFS): one national rate per test code (not geographic), set under PAMA from private-payer data (Section 1834A).
- CLFS 2026 national rate CPT 85025 (CBC with automated differential): $7.77. UNCERTAIN: confirmed only by a third-party rate site (PayerPrice, "2026 national lab fee schedule rate"); another site says $7.59. Consistent with PAMA math: ~$10.66 (2017) cut 10%/yr 2018-2020 -> $7.77, then frozen 2021-2026 (no reduction in CY2026 per CMS MM14312).
- CPT 80048 (BMP): third-party site says ~$8.27. UNCERTAIN, not used.
- Policy context (NOT used on screen, could change before posting): CMS preliminary 2027 CLFS rates (Sep 2026) average ~16% lower; cuts capped at 15%/yr 2027-2029; Congress may delay again. Medicare Part B lab spending $8.4B in 2024 (HHS OIG).

## Worked example (our math)
Revenue per requisition = DIS revenue / requisitions = $10.785B / 244M = $44.20, about $44 per order (FY2025).
Versus one CBC at $7.77 from Medicare: an order typically bundles several tests and payers differ.

## Surprising fact
Medicare pays under $8 for a full blood count, yet Quest booked $11B in 2025: the model is enormous volume of cheap, automated tests, with price set by payer mix.
