# Fact-check: 2026-12-06-business (How Six Flags makes money from passes)
Checked Oct 7, 2026, for posting Sun Dec 6, 2026. Line indexes are 0-based.

## Verified claims
- **L1/L2, FY2025 per capita:** total $61.90, admissions $33.41, in-park $28.49. Confirmed in the Q4/FY2025 release (Feb 19, 2026; 8-K Ex. 99.1) and the FY2025 ARS/10-K. Math: 33.41 + 28.49 = 61.90, and 28.49 / 61.90 = 46.03%, so "46%" is correct.
- **L3/L4, revenue recognition:** the 10-K says multi-use products, "including season-long products for admission, dining, beverage and other products and the first 12-month non-cancelable period for membership products, are recognized over the estimated number of uses expected for each type of product." "Books each one over the visits it expects" is a fair paraphrase. In the example, $120 / 6 = $20.
- **L6, Q2 2026:** the release says "The increase in Same-Park Basis net revenues was driven primarily by 4% higher attendance, including a 10% increase in season-pass visitation." So "same parks" / "same-park" is correct. The quarter ended June 28, 2026.
- **L7, deferred revenue:** "$431 million" as of June 28, 2026 is confirmed by the Q2 2026 release. The 10-Q says season-long products, including memberships, "represent most of the deferred revenue balance in any given period." At Jan 1, 2026, 89% of current deferred revenue was season-long products. The rest was online transaction fees, advance and group tickets, game and gift cards, sponsorships, resort reservations and other items.
- **L5, in-park items:** the company lists the major per capita categories as "admission, food and beverage, merchandise, games and extra-charge products." Food & drinks, Games and Upcharge rides all fit.
- Cross-check: Q2 2026 in-park share is 29.27 / 62.89 = 46.5%, so the 46% framing still holds this year.

It is in-park revenue (park and water-park revenue plus related parking and online transaction fees) divided by attendance. It excludes out-of-park revenue such as resorts and out-of-park food and merchandise. Parking and online fees also sit inside the "non-admission" share.
