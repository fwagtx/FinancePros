# Research brief: Where the money goes in an MLM (income disclosures)
Slot 2026-11-29-business (How They Make Money). Researched October 2, 2026. Fetching official PDFs (amway.com, herbalife, youngliving.com, ftc.gov) was blocked by the egress proxy; figures below are confirmed via multiple search results quoting the official documents.

## Timely angle
- No news this week. Evergreen angle: companies' own income disclosure statements (latest: Amway 2025 U.S. Income Disclosure).
- Related 2026 context: April 2026 FTC actions settling allegations against high-level MLM participants over deceptive earnings claims (Stormy Wellington re: Total Life Changes/Farmasi; Steven and Gina Merritt). Allegations, settled by order. (FTC press releases, April 2026)

## Amway, U.S. Income Disclosure (company's own figures, before expenses)
- 2025: average annual earnings for all U.S. IBOs at Founders Platinum level and below = $750 before expenses (includes IBOs who reported no product sales).
- 2025: 38% of U.S. IBOs had no reported product sales, did not sponsor another IBO, and received no payments from Amway.
- 2025: 59% of U.S. IBOs received a payment from Amway in at least one month.
- 2025, among IBOs who received a payment: top 10% averaged $15,691 (median $4,854); top 50% averaged $3,598 (median $551).
- Cost: registration $0; renewal after first contract year $55 (plus optional $16 IBOAI membership). (amway.com Amway Insider "Is there an Amway registration fee?", via search)

## WORKED EXAMPLE (labeled example)
- Amway 2025 average gross: $750. Minus $55 renewal fee (applies after the first contract year) = $695.
- $695 / 12 = $57.92 a month, before product purchases, travel, phone or other costs (not counted in the disclosure).
- Without the fee: $750 / 12 = $62.50 a month.

## FTC staff report (September 4, 2024): "Multi-Level Marketing Income Disclosure Statements"
- Staff reviewed income disclosure statements from 70 MLMs (as posted in February 2023).
- Most participants received $1,000 or less per year, i.e. less than $84 a month on average.
- In at least 17 MLMs, most participants made no money at all.
- Many statements leave out low/no earners and do not account for participant expenses, which can outstrip income.
(Note: the requested title "Selling an empty dream" could not be confirmed; the report's title is as above.)

## FTC rulemaking
- January 13, 2025: FTC proposed changes to the Business Opportunity Rule (expand to "money-making opportunities," retitle "Business and Money-Making Opportunity Rule") and a proposed Earnings Claim Rule for MLMs (ban deceptive earnings claims, require written substantiation, keep records 3 years). Proposed, not final; reports say the fate is uncertain (Chairman Ferguson voted against as commissioner).

## Herbalife U.S. Statement of Typical Distributor Earnings 2024 (published 05/09/2025)
- About 113,000 U.S. distributors ordered products for resale.
- In a typical month about 50,600 earned money.
- First-year distributors: 50% earned more than $154 in a month (top 10%: more than $1,144). All others: 50% earned more than $268 a month (top 10%: more than $4,982). Before expenses.
