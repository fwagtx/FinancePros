# Research brief: How homeowners associations get and spend money (2026-11-14-business)

Researched Oct 2, 2026. Posts Nov 14, 2026. Note: foundation.caionline.org, census.gov, globenewswire and natlawreview were blocked for direct fetch in this session; figures below are confirmed through search results quoting those sources (several independent results each) and checked by arithmetic.

## Timely angle
- The Foundation for Community Association Research (CAI's research arm) released its 2025 U.S. National and State Statistical Review on April 1, 2026 (press release "U.S. Surpasses 373,000 Community Associations As Housing Model Reaches New Heights"). These are the newest national totals.
- The 2025 ACS 1-year estimates (which would update the Census HOA-fee median) had NOT been released as of early Oct 2026 (Census says release date being determined). Latest official median fee is therefore the 2024 ACS (released Sept 2025).

## Key facts (with dates)
- Community associations (HOAs + condominiums + cooperatives) in the U.S., end of 2025: about 373,000. Projected up to 377,000 in 2026. (Foundation, 2025 Statistical Review, Apr 2026; Dec 22, 2025 "2026 HOA Outlook" release)
- Residents, 2025: about 78.1 million Americans; 29.6 million housing units; 35.2% of U.S. housing stock. (same)
- HOAs are roughly 58-63% of associations, condos 35-40%, co-ops 2-4%. (Foundation)
- Money in, 2025: owners paid $124.2 billion in assessments (dues). (Foundation 2025 Statistical Review)
- Reserves, 2025: $31.1 billion of that went to reserve funds ("one dollar in four") for long-term repair/replacement of common property. 31.1 / 124.2 = 25.04%.
- Remainder (derived): 124.2 - 31.1 = $93.1 billion for everything else (operating costs such as insurance, landscaping, utilities, management, routine maintenance). Category split nationally is NOT published in what I could confirm; describe categories only as examples.
- Prior year, 2024: 370,000 associations, 77.1 million residents, $120.9 billion assessments, $30.2 billion to reserves (24.98%). (Foundation 2024 Statistical Review)
- Median monthly condo/HOA fee, 2024: $135 among the 21.6 million (of 86.6 million) owner households that pay one. With a mortgage: $120 median; without: $184. About 3 million paid more than $500/month; about 5.6 million (26%) paid less than $50. (U.S. Census Bureau, 2024 ACS 1-year; Census story "Condo or Homeowners Association Fees Topped $500 Monthly for About 3 Million Households", Sept 2025)
- Caution: realtor.com separately reports a median HOA fee of $135 on homes FOR SALE in 2025 (different measure). Do not mix.
- Reserve health (weakly sourced, secondary summaries only): Association Reserves' April 2026 Industry Insights Report (100,000+ reserve studies, 1986-2025) is summarized as only ~25.7% of associations "strong" (70%+ funded) and ~34% "weak" (<30% funded), where special assessments are "highly likely". ## Surprising fact
- About one dollar in four of HOA/condo dues ($31.1B of $124.2B in 2025) is not spent that year at all: it goes into savings (reserves) for roofs, roads, pools that wear out later. When reserves fall short, associations can levy a one-time "special assessment" on owners.

## Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL)
- Hypothetical HOA: 200 homes, each paying the 2024 median $135/month.
- Annual budget: 200 x $135 x 12 = $324,000.
- If it saves the national 2025 share (~25%) in reserves: 0.25 x $324,000 = $81,000 a year to reserves; $243,000 for operating costs.
- Special assessment: a hypothetical $500,000 roof/road project with $300,000 in reserves leaves a $200,000 gap; $200,000 / 200 homes = $1,000 per home.
