## Remaining minor notes (non-blocking)

1. **L7 "was sold".** Technically, substantially all of its assets were sold to a Sleep Country Canada subsidiary. Optional wording: "its business was sold to Sleep Country Canada."
2. **L9 "$40 an order".** Class materials describe it as $40 per qualifying mattress, in cash or store credit, for California MattressFirm.com orders from 2020 to 2024. Optional wording: "$40 per mattress". The post says "per online order", which is acceptable.
3. **L10 read-aloud.** "more of the price paid for selling than for making" is slightly stiff. Optional: "At Sleep Number, more of your money went to selling the bed than making it."
4. **Header "Where your mattress money goes".** It is a little broader than one company, but every body line names Sleep Number, so this is acceptable.
5. **Mattress Firm stat card.** The card shows "$40" with no year. The spoken line gives "2025", so this is fine.
