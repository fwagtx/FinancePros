# Research brief: How movie theaters make money (it's the popcorn). Posts Oct 31, 2026

Company used: Cinemark Holdings (NYSE: CNK), third-largest US chain. Fiscal year 2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025), from its Q4/full-year 2025 earnings release (Feb 2026) and FY2025 10-K.

## FY2025 key numbers (Cinemark, worldwide unless noted)
- Total revenue: $3,115.0 million (+2.1%)
- Admissions revenue: $1,544.7 million
- Concession revenue: $1,227.2 million -> 39.4% of total revenue (1227.2 / 3115.0)
- Attendance: 193.0 million patrons
- Average ticket price: $8.00; concession revenue per patron: $6.36
- US segment: film rentals and advertising = 58.0% of admissions revenue in 2025 (57.9% in 2024)
- US segment: concession supplies = 18.9% of concession revenue in 2025 (18.0% in 2024)
- International: film rentals 51.4% of admissions; concession supplies 22.8% of concession revenue

## Worked example (US segment, 2025 percentages)
- $10 of ticket sales: film rentals and advertising 58.0% = $5.80 -> theater keeps $4.20 before rent, staff and other costs.
- $10 of concession sales: supplies 18.9% = $1.89 -> theater keeps $8.11 before other costs.
- 8.11 / 4.20 = 1.93 -> each concession dollar leaves the theater nearly twice as much as each ticket dollar.
- Surprise: more than half of every US ticket dollar goes out to film rentals and advertising (mostly studio film rent).
