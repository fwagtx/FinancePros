## The story: Synchrony Q3 2026 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026; released Oct 20, 2026)
Sources: Synchrony Q3 2026 results as reported by Investing.com (results + slides + call transcript), Motley Fool call transcript (Oct 20, 2026), GuruFocus, Benzinga transcript, Seeking Alpha via TradingView. Official 8-K/press release not directly reachable from this environment (sec.gov blocked).
- Net earnings: $892 million, $2.68 per diluted share. Analyst estimate ~$2.42 (Investing.com). Beat.
- Purchase volume: "approaching $50 billion," up 8% year over year (record Q3). Exact figure reported as $49.7B or $49.8B by different summaries (note: Q2 2026 was $49.8B, +8%) -> use "nearly $50 billion" / "~$50B", +8%.
- Net charge-off rate (annualized, share of average loan receivables written off): 4.79% in Q3 2026, down 37 bps from 5.16% in Q3 2025; down from 5.43% in Q2 2026. Net charge-offs about $1.2 billion.
- 30+ day delinquency rate: 4.34% of period-end loan receivables at Sept 30, 2026, vs 4.39% a year earlier (Q3 2025) and 4.16% at June 30, 2026 (seasonal rise; delinquencies typically rise in Q3/Q4).
- Loan receivables: $103.3 billion, up 3%; "higher purchase volume was partially offset by elevated payment rates" (customers paying down balances faster).
- Net interest income ~$4.8B, +2% (one summary only).
- Guidance: full-year 2026 net charge-off rate below 5.5% (long-term framework 5.5%-6%); full-year EPS outlook raised to about $9.50 (summaries; "reaffirmed at top of range" per Seeking Alpha headline). Treat EPS guidance as weakly sourced; not used in script.
- Shares slipped after the report (Investing.com headline) - intraday, NOT used.
- Partners: Synchrony issues store/co-brand cards for retailers including Amazon and Lowe's (long-standing; Synchrony 10-K partner list).

## Second data point: Ally Financial Q3 2026 (Oct 20, 2026)
- Retail auto net charge-off rate 1.89% vs 1.88% in Q3 2025 (roughly flat) and 1.57% in Q2 2026 (Investing.com, Motley Fool transcript, Yahoo/StockStory). Consolidated NCO 1.17%. 2026 retail auto NCO guidance kept at 1.8%-2.0%.

## What it means for a normal person
- A charge-off is debt a lender gives up collecting (typically after ~180 days unpaid on cards); it still hurts the borrower's credit. Lower charge-off rates at Synchrony mean a smaller share of its card balances went unpaid than a year ago.
- Delinquencies are seasonal and roughly flat year over year; customers are paying balances faster (company comment).
- This is one lender's customers (store cards), not all US card borrowers.

## What to watch next
- Other card lenders' Q3 results (Capital One, American Express) and the NY Fed household debt report (early November).
- Synchrony's monthly credit statistics (8-K) for October/November seasonal trends.
