# Research brief: Why GameStop pays so little for your trade-ins
Show: How They Make Money. Posts Mon Jan 11, 2027 (evergreen). Researched Nov 12, 2026.
GameStop fiscal years end around Jan 31 (fiscal 2025 = 52 weeks ended Jan 31, 2026).

## Core fact (the surprise): used games were GameStop's high-margin product
GameStop 10-K for fiscal 2018 (year ended Feb 2, 2019), gross profit percent by category:
| Category | FY2016 | FY2017 | FY2018 |
|---|---|---|---|
| New video game hardware | 11.0% | 9.1% | 8.5% |
| New video game software | 24.1% | 22.9% | 21.5% |
| Pre-owned and value video game products | 46.3% | 45.5% | 43.4% |
| Accessories | 34.8% | 32.5% | 32.7% |
| Collectibles | 34.7% | 32.7% | 33.0% |
| Digital | 85.9% | 85.8% | 88.5% |
- Ratio FY2018: 43.4 / 8.5 = 5.1, about five times (our math).
- GameStop no longer discloses gross margin by category: neither the FY2025 results release (Mar 24, 2026) nor the Q2 FY2026 release (Sep 2026) gives category margins. Do NOT claim a current pre-owned margin. Always date the 43.4% as fiscal 2018.

## Worked example (our math, from the FY2018 disclosed margin, illustrative)
- $100 of pre-owned sales x 43.4% gross margin = $43.40 gross profit; cost of goods = $100 - $43.40 = $56.60.
- $100 of new hardware sales x 8.5% = $8.50 gross profit; cost = $91.50.
- Gross profit is before store rent, wages and other operating costs (SG&A); the spread also must cover those.
- Real dated trade-in vs GameStop resale prices for one item: NOT confirmable from reputable sources (only promo-tied figures, e.g. Switch 2 launch credits of $175 OLED / $125 standard / $100 Lite from April 2025, Tom's Guide/Gizmodo; GameStop resale prices not confirmed). So no specific item example is used.

## Trade-in mechanics (reputable outlets)
- Cash offers are often about two-thirds or less of the store-credit offer (BGR).
- Pro members get extra trade credit (10% on Switch trade per Gizmodo/Tom's Guide coverage, 2025).
- Traded devices are inspected, repaired if needed, and resold as pre-owned / refurbished (GameStop's own category definition includes refurbishment and warranties).
