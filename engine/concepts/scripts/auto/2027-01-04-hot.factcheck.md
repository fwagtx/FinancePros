## Issues and replacements
1. **Line 4 (bracket framing), recommended.** "In a 22% bracket" implies the whole prize is taxed at 22%. That holds only within a narrow income band, and the prize itself can push income into 24%.
   - TEXT: `Our math: a $30,000 car, all taxed at 22%, is $6,600 in federal tax.`
   - SAY: `Our math: a thirty-thousand-dollar car, all taxed at twenty-two percent, means sixty-six hundred dollars in federal tax.` (17 words, unchanged count)
   - Scene `to.label` stays "22% federal tax".
   - Post ig/yt_desc: replace "in a 22% bracket" with "all taxed at 22%".
2. **Line 0 (hook), minor strengthening.** The hook is true and specific: "could" is a fair hedge, and the 22% case is shown in L4. But spoken "sixty-six hundred" has no unit, so "owe" is vague.
   - TEXT: `Win a $30,000 car, and you could owe $6,600 in tax.`
   - SAY: `Win a thirty-thousand-dollar car, and you could owe sixty-six hundred in tax.` (12 words, at the hook cap)
3. **Line 6 (split, left items), clarity.** "Pay the tax / Or sell it" reads as if selling avoids the tax. It does not: you still owe it and sell to fund it.
   - left.items: `["Pay the tax", "Or sell to pay"]` (14 chars)
4. **Line 3 (on-screen vs spoken), minor.** TEXT says "Form 1099-MISC" but SAY says "a ten ninety-nine form". The difference is acceptable, but to align them:
   - TEXT: `And the show reports it to the IRS on a Form 1099.` (the sub keeps "Form 1099-MISC")
5. **Post sources (time word), minor.** "IRS 2026 tax brackets": a prize won after the Jan 4, 2027 posting is 2027 income. Use `IRS tax brackets` (or "IRS 2026-27 tax brackets").
6. **Line 7 (word budget), optional.** With fix 2 the spoken total becomes 140, which is the cap. To leave margin, drop "remember":
   - SAY: `So a free car still has a price tag. Rules vary, so check a tax pro.` (total 137 with fixes 1 and 2)
