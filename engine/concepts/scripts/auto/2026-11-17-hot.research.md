# 2026-11-17-hot research: Money myth "Black Friday always has the best prices"

Researched Oct 2, 2026. Black Friday 2026 is Nov 27, 2026 (posting date Nov 17, 2026). Keep evergreen; every number dated.
Note: news.adobe.com / business.adobe.com / which.co.uk are blocked for direct fetch here; figures confirmed via search results quoting the releases and reputable outlets.

## Myth
"Black Friday always has the best prices of the year."

## Fact 1: price-tracking study (Which?, UK consumer group, published Nov 2025)
- Tracked 175 home, tech and health appliances at 8 UK retailers (Amazon, AO, Argos, Boots, Currys, John Lewis, Richer Sounds, Very) for a year around Black Friday 2024 (six months before and after).
- 83% were the same price or cheaper on at least one occasion outside the four-week Black Friday sales period ("eight in 10").
- 100% were the same price or cheaper at some point in the six months either side.
- On Black Friday itself, none of the deals were at their cheapest price of that year.
- Earlier Which? study (2023): 98% of Black Friday "deals" weren't the cheapest price of the year.
- Caveat: UK retailers and products; it is the most rigorous public price-tracking study found. US equivalents (Keepa/camelcamelcamel roundups, BestBlackFriday estimate ~30% of items same or cheaper otherwise) are weaker/unsourced methodology; not used on screen.

## Fact 2: Black Friday is still a real discount day (Adobe Analytics, US online, 2025)
- Black Friday 2025 (Nov 28, 2025): $11.8 billion online, up 9.1% year over year.
- Cyber Monday 2025 (Dec 1, 2025): $14.25 billion, up 7.1%, biggest online day ever.
- Cyber Week 2025 (Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday): $44.2 billion, up 7.7% (vs $41.4B in 2024).
- Full season Nov 1-Dec 31, 2025: $257.8 billion online, up 6.8%.
- Black Friday 2025 was the season's most discounted day: average discount across measured categories 23.7% off listed price (vs 22.2% in 2024); toys, televisions, sporting goods and appliances recorded their deepest single-day discounts of the season on Black Friday (Adobe 2025 holiday recap, Jan 2026).
- 2025 season peak discounts: electronics 30.9% (vs 30.1% in 2024), toys 29.6% (vs 28%), TVs 24.3% (vs 24.2%) (Adobe, Jan 7, 2026 release).
- Adobe Cyber Monday recap (Dec 2, 2025): after Cyber Week, discounts came down but stayed notable in the first week of December: toys up to 23%, electronics 19%, TVs 19%.
- Adobe 2025 forecast (Oct 2025): Black Friday best day for TVs, toys, appliances; Cyber Monday best for electronics, apparel, computers; Thanksgiving for sporting goods. (Forecast only; not used.)

## Worked example (hypothetical)
A $500 TV at 2025's peak TV discount of 24.3%: 500 x 0.243 = $121.50 off; 500 - 121.50 = $378.50.

## The surprising fact
In Which?'s year of tracking around Black Friday 2024, not one deal was at its lowest price of the year on Black Friday itself, yet Adobe found Black Friday 2025 was the most discounted single day of the US online season. Both are true: big average discounts, but not the guaranteed lowest price for a given item.

## Framing (description, not advice)
Black Friday is often a good price, not always the best one. Price history is what separates the two.
