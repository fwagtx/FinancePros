# Research brief: How gyms make money from members who never go (How They Make Money, posts Oct 23, 2026)

## Planet Fitness, FY2025 (Q4/year-end release, Feb 2026; 10-K and annual report)
- About 20.8M members and 2,896 clubs at Dec 31, 2025. 20.8M / 2,896 = about 7,182 members per club.
- Total revenue $1.3B (+12.1%). System-wide sales $5.3B (from $4.8B), made up of monthly dues and annual fees billed by the company and franchisees.
- Same-club sales +6.7%; 181 new clubs opened in 2025.
- A typical club is about 20,000 sq ft (Franchise Times). The model signs far more members than a club can hold at once (Franchise Times; Sherwood News).
- Pricing: Classic card $15 a month (raised from $10 in 2024); Black Card about $24.99; annual fee typically $49 (company pages and press).

## Research: "Paying Not to Go to the Gym" (DellaVigna & Malmendier, American Economic Review, 2006)
- 7,752 members of three US health clubs, tracked over 3 years.
- Members on flat monthly contracts over $70 went 4.3 times a month on average, paying over $17 per visit, when a 10-visit pass cost $10 a visit.
- On average they gave up about $600 in savings over the membership. The explanation: overconfidence about future attendance.

- Q2 2026 (Aug 6, 2026 release): 21.5M members, 2,930 clubs at June 30, 2026, so 21.5M / 2,930 = about 7,338 per club.

## Worked example (hypothetical usage)
- $15 x 12 + $49 = $229 a year. At 10 visits a year, $229 / 10 = $22.90 per visit.
