## Facts (exact values, dates)
### Stocks
- S&P 500 closed Thu Nov 19, 2026 at 7,175.83, down 147.80 pts (-2.0%); 6th straight loss, longest losing streak since 2024 (AP via Times Free Press/WSVN/DTN; ABC/WaPo index tables).
- Record close 7,818.93 on Tue Oct 6, 2026 (first close above 7,800). 7,175.83 is 8.2% below record (AP; our math -8.22%).
- Dow -1,101.11 (-2.2%) to 49,097.06; Nasdaq -550.69 (-2.3%) to 23,879.20 (AP). More than 90% of S&P 500 stocks fell Thursday.
- S&P 500 2025 close: 6,845.50 (Dec 31, 2025; Kiplinger, Business Journal), +16.4% price in 2025. YTD through Nov 19: +4.8% (our math 7,175.83/6,845.50 = +4.83%).
- A "correction" is commonly defined as a 10% drop from a record; the S&P is not there as of Thursday.
- Drivers (AP, CNBC): doubts that massive AI spending will pay off; Nvidia leadership change.
### Nvidia
- Q3 FY2027 (reported after close Tue Nov 17, 2026): record revenue $115.8B, up 103% y/y; Data Center $107.6B; analysts (LSEG) expected $109.1B. Q4 guide: $130B +/-3%, assumes no China data center compute revenue (Nvidia release; Investing.com; StockAnalysis).
- First report since Jensen Huang's exit as CEO (CNBC/Yahoo/stockanalysis).
- Stock: rose ~5% after hours, then fell ~2% Wed; Thu erased an early 4.7% gain and closed down 3.3% (AP-based reports). Exact Thursday close varies by source (2.7%-3.3%).
### The Fed
- Sept 16, 2026: Fed raised rates 0.25 pt to 3.75%-4.00%, first hike since July 2023, unanimous; first big decision under Chair Kevin Warsh (Axios, ABC, Yahoo). Had held at 3.50%-3.75% since Dec 2025.
- Oct 27-28 meeting: held at 3.75%-4.00%, vote 10-2; Cook and Hammack dissented for a hike (Bloomberg, CNBC).
- Minutes released Wed Nov 18, 2026: "almost all participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks for inflation and the labor market, further action to raise the target range for the federal funds rate would likely be appropriate." A couple said a move larger than 25 bp could warrant consideration in December if inflation data were unfavorable. "many participants viewed the AI buildout as a material source of inflation risk." Staff flagged circular financing among AI firms as a stability risk (CNBC, Bloomberg, US News, Korea Times).
- Next FOMC: Dec 8-9, 2026. Futures traders price a December hike as very likely (CME FedWatch via secondary sources ~95%+; not used on screen).
- Inflation: Sept 2026 CPI 3.7% y/y (BLS Oct 14, 2026; CNBC, CNN), driven by gasoline; Fed target 2%.
- Fed funds target upper bound, end of year (chart): 2021 0.25%, 2022 4.50%, 2023 5.50%, 2024 4.50%, 2025 3.75%, 2026 (now, Nov) 4.00%. Source: Federal Reserve.
### Bonds and mortgages
- 10-year Treasury closed 5.04% Nov 19 (AP; BondStats); 2-year 4.76%, 30-year 5.40%. 10-year first topped 5% since 2007 in Sept 2026 (Fortune Sept 22, CNN Sept 23).
- 2-year (4.76%) above the Fed's 4.00% top: traders expect higher short rates.
- Freddie Mac PMMS Nov 19, 2026: 30-yr 7.28% (from 7.11% prior week; 6.26% a year ago); 15-yr 6.66% (from 6.45%). Ended four straight weekly declines from 7.40% peak on Oct 8 (Freddie Mac via GlobeNewswire, Yahoo, MLQ).
- Chartable series: Freddie Mac 30-yr, third Thursday of November: 2021 (Nov 18) 3.10%; 2022 (Nov 17) 6.61%; 2023 (Nov 16) 7.44%; 2024 (Nov 21) 6.84%; 2025 (Nov 20) 6.26%; 2026 (Nov 19) 7.28%. (Freddie Mac PMMS; AP Nov 21, 2024; Freddie Mac Nov 14 2024 release yr-ago 7.44).
- Our math: $400,000 30-yr loan, principal+interest: 7.28% = $2,737/mo; 6.26% = $2,465/mo; difference about $271/mo. 7.11% = $2,691 -> one week's rise adds about $46/mo.
- NAHB/Wells Fargo HMI Nov 2026 (released Nov 17): 25, down from 29; lowest since April 2012. 46% of builders cut prices; average cut 6%; 68% used incentives (NAHB, HousingWire, Inman).
### Shoppers / tariff refunds
- Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs Feb 20, 2026 (Learning Resources); importers get refunds.
- Walmart Q3 (Nov 19): revenue +5.2% to $188.8B; raised full-year outlook for 3rd time (AP, CNBC). Walmart previously said it received ~$2.9B in IEEPA refunds (Aug 2026), using them for price rollbacks.
- Target Q3 (Nov 18): comparable sales +5.6%; results include $301M pretax tariff refund benefit (Target call / Investing.com).
- Home Depot Q3 (Nov 17): sales $43.4B, +4.9%; comps +2.2% (SEC 8-K / company release).
### History / context
- 2022: Fed hiked fast; S&P 500 fell from 4,766.18 (end 2021) to 3,839.50 (end 2022), about -19.4%, and bonds fell too - stocks and bonds both lost.
- S&P year-end chart: 2021 4,766; 2022 3,840; 2023 4,770; 2024 5,882; 2025 6,846; 2026 (Thu Nov 19) 7,176.
- Usually when stocks fall, investors buy Treasuries and yields drop (Thursday the 10-yr did slip 3 bp). But inflation + expected hikes keep yields high (~5%), so mortgages rose for the week.

### What it means (neutral, education)
- 401(k): down from October peak but still up for the year as of Thursday. Our math: $50,000 x 8.2% = $4,100 paper drop.
- Borrowers: card rates track the prime rate, which moves with the Fed. Our math: a 0.25-pt hike on a $5,000 balance = about $12.50 a year more interest.
- Savers: higher rates can mean higher yields on savings/CDs.
- Home buyers: rates 7.28%, builders cutting prices (46%).
### What to watch
- Fed meeting Dec 8-9; next inflation report; Freddie Mac weekly survey; whether S&P drop reaches 10%; Nvidia's $130B guide.
