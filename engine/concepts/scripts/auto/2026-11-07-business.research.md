## Worked math
1. Price per stall (company figures): $16,500,000 / 308 = $53,571.43 per stall -> "$53,571", spoken "over fifty-three thousand dollars".
2. 2% cap rate: cap rate = annual net operating income / price. 2% -> $2 of parking profit per year per $100 of price. (Definition; math only.)
3. EXAMPLE (hypothetical, labeled): land assessed at $2,000,000, combined property tax rate 2% (assumption).
   - Lot only: $2,000,000 x 2% = $40,000 per year.
   - Add a $20,000,000 building (assessed at cost, assumption): ($2,000,000 + $20,000,000) x 2% = $440,000 per year.
   - Point: under a standard US property tax, which taxes buildings as well as land, building raises the bill 11x in this example; a surface lot keeps carrying cost low while the owner waits.

## Definitions / history
- "Taxpayer" (building): US real-estate/planning term coined in the early 1900s for a cheap one- or two-story building put up to produce income to pay property taxes on land while the owner waits to redevelop. (Wikipedia "Taxpayer (building)"; Firehouse Vigilance "The Fireman's Guide to Main Street: The Taxpayer"; Fire Engineering "Taxpayers - An Overview"). Surface parking is a common modern interim use of the same kind (Seattle OPCD interim parking report; Colonial Parking development page describe surface lots as interim use).
- Split-rate tax (Lincoln Institute of Land Policy): taxes land at a higher rate than buildings. A 1913 Pennsylvania law let Pittsburgh and Scranton set different rates for land and buildings, intended to encourage development of large land holdings and discourage land speculation; Pittsburgh rescinded its split-rate tax in 2001. Lincoln research tentatively linked it to 1980s downtown construction in Pittsburgh.
- Detroit: city's Land Value Tax Plan (proposed 2023) would raise taxes on parking lots, vacant land, scrapyards; needs Michigan legislative approval; stalled in the legislature (Outlier Media, BridgeDetroit, Crain's Detroit; Detroit News Apr 1, 2026 on new mayor's tax plan). Status in flux; NOT used in script.
