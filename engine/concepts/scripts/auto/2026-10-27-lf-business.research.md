# Research brief: How Nvidia became the world's most valuable chip company (How They Make Money, posts Oct 27, 2026)

## Financials (Nvidia filings)
- FY2026 (ended Jan 25, 2026; results Feb 25, 2026): revenue $215.9B (+65%); data center $197.3B (+68% from $115.2B); gaming $16.0B (+41%); GAAP gross margin 71.1%; net income $120.1B. A $4.5B H20 charge (China export rules, excess inventory and purchase obligations).
- Q2 FY2027 (ended July 26, 2026): revenue $96.2B (+106% y/y); data center $89.0B (+117%); gross margin 75.0%.
- Revenue by fiscal year ($B): FY2020 10.92; FY2021 16.68; FY2022 26.91; FY2023 26.97; FY2024 60.92; FY2025 130.5; FY2026 215.9.
- FY2021 segments: gaming $7.76B, data center $6.7B.
- FY2026 10-K: one direct customer was 22% of revenue and another 14%.
- Fabless: TSMC makes most advanced chips; first Blackwell wafer at TSMC Arizona (Oct 2025).
- Mellanox acquisition closed April 27, 2020 (~$6.9-7.1B).

## History
- Founded 1993 at a Denny's near San Jose by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, with about $40K.
- NV1 (1995) struggled; "30 days from going out of business"; RIVA 128 (1997); IPO Jan 1999.
- GeForce 256 (Oct 1999), "the world's first GPU". CUDA released Nov 2006.
- AlexNet (2012) trained on two GTX 580 GPUs; ImageNet top-5 error 15.3% vs 26.2%.
- First public company worth $5T: Oct 29, 2025 (CNBC). About $5.4-5.6T in Sept 2026.

## Comparisons
- Apple FY2025 net income about $112.0B on about $416.2B revenue (~27%). Costco FY2025 about 2.9%.

## Worked example
- Per $100 of FY2026 sales: about $71 gross profit (71.1% margin); about $56 net income (120.1 / 215.9 = 55.6%).
