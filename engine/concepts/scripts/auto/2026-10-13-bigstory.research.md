### JPMorgan Chase (3Q26, released Oct 13, 2026; 8-K/financial supplement filed Oct 13, 2026)
- Net income: $15,305 million (~$15.3B) vs $14,393 million in 3Q25 -> up ~6%. (SEC supplement; Yahoo Finance; Fortune)
- Diluted EPS: $5.57 vs $5.07 in 3Q25. (Yahoo Finance, CNBC, Quiver)
- Analysts expected $5.90 (LSEG, per CNBC/Yahoo); some outlets cite $5.93. A rare miss.
- Excluding $658 million of net investment securities losses (amid the quarter's bond rout), EPS $5.75. (CNBC, Yahoo)
- Total net revenue (reported): $50,172 million (~$50.2B), up ~8% y/y. (Managed-basis figure reported as $50.9B by some outlets; we use reported $50.2B.)
- Card Services net charge-off rate: 2.91% in 3Q26 vs 3.34% in 2Q26 and 3.15% in 3Q25. (JPM 3Q26 results slides; PYMNTS)
- Earlier 2026 quarters: 1Q26 3.47%, 2Q26 3.34% (JPM 1Q26/2Q26 releases).
- Full-year 2026 card NCO guidance ~3.2% (lowered from ~3.4% given in Q1). (Benzinga call transcript; Q1 10-Q)
- Debit and credit card spend up 10% year over year (gas and travel strong). (PYMNTS; call coverage)
- Consumer & Community Banking net income up 13% to $5.67B.
- CFO Jeremy Barnum on call: consumer currently healthy on a strong labor market; an extended period of high rates would likely hurt consumers (exact wording varies between outlets; NOT used as a quote).
- Dimon (release, as quoted by Yahoo Finance Oct 13, 2026): "The U.S. economy continued to be fueled by strong business investment and ongoing consumer spending." He cited large fiscal deficits, sticky inflation, elevated asset prices, rising interest rates and geopolitical conditions as forces shifting beneath the surface: "These forces may be manageable, but we remain prepared for a wide range of outcomes." ## Context and meaning
- Net charge-off rate = share of card balances a bank writes off as unlikely to be repaid. Falling = fewer borrowers defaulting.
- Card NCOs rose through 2023-2025 as pandemic savings ran out ("normalization"); 3Q26 is the lowest rate in this set.
- For a normal person: big banks see most people still paying bills and spending (+10%), but bank chiefs flag sticky inflation and rising rates as the risk. Bank profits also show where money is going: card interest, trading.
- What to watch: card charge-offs next quarter, interest rates, inflation; Bank of America/Morgan Stanley report later this week (typical schedule; not verified).
