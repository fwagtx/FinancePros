# Research brief: How Apple makes money from the App Store (posts Oct 18, 2026)

## Apple FY2025 Form 10-K (fiscal year ended Sep 27, 2025)
- Total net sales: $416,161M ($416.2B). Services net sales: $109,158M ($109.2B), 26% of the total. Products net sales: $307.0B.
- Gross margin %: Services 75.4%, Products 36.8%, total 46.9% (total gross margin about $195.2B).
- Services = App Store, advertising, AppleCare, cloud, digital content, payments, licensing and other. Apple does not report App Store revenue on its own.
- Math: Services gross profit = 109.2 x 0.754 = $82.3B. Products gross profit = 307.0 x 0.368 = $113.0B. Services share of gross profit = 82.3 / 195.3 = 42%.

## Commission
- Standard App Store commission: 30% on paid apps and in-app purchases of digital goods. App Store Small Business Program (since Jan 2021): 15% for developers earning under $1M a year in proceeds.
- The commission on US link-out purchases is being litigated (Epic v. Apple; Ninth Circuit Dec 2025; remand ongoing in 2026). Not covered in the video, to avoid stale legal status.

## Worked example
- $9.99 in-app purchase at 30%: 0.30 x 9.99 = $3.00 to Apple; the developer keeps $6.99.
- At 15%: 0.15 x 9.99 = $1.50; the developer keeps $8.49.

## Surprising fact
- An Apple-funded study (Analysis Group, June 2025) found the App Store ecosystem facilitated $1.3 trillion in billings and sales in 2024, and about 90% of it paid Apple no commission (physical goods and services such as rides and food, plus in-app ads).
