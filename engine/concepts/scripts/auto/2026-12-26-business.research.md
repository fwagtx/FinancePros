# Research brief: How Five Below makes money on a $5 price point (posts Dec 26, 2026)

Note on fiscal years: Five Below's fiscal year ends on the Saturday closest to Jan 31.
- Fiscal 2025 = year ended January 31, 2026 (results released March 18, 2026).
- Fiscal 2024 = year ended February 1, 2025. Fiscal 2023 = year ended February 3, 2024 (53 weeks).
- Fiscal 2026 Q2 = quarter ended August 1, 2026 (released September 2, 2026). Q3 fiscal 2026 results are due in early December 2026 (before posting), so all quarter figures below are labeled by period and not described as "latest".

## Timely angle
No breaking news needed (evergreen buffer). The holiday season (Dec 26 posting) is Five Below's biggest quarter; the story is the $5 model plus the move to pricier items.

## Business model (company filings)
- Specialty value retailer of "trend-right, high-quality products loved by tweens, teens and beyond" (Form 10-K, fiscal 2024, filed March 2025).
- "Most of our products remain at $5 and below" (10-K wording, fiscal 2022 and later 10-Ks; same framing in the fiscal 2025 10-K).
- New store model: about 9,500 square feet, in power, community and lifestyle centers (10-K fiscal 2024). Small box, low rent, high volume.
- Founded 2002 with everything at $5 and below. Fall 2019: rolled out new pricing chainwide, raising prices on certain products above $5 (10-K fiscal 2022 wording: "in fall 2019, we rolled out new pricing to our full chain, increasing prices on certain products over $5").
- "Ten Below" test section began in late 2019 after 17 years selling only items at $5 or less (Retail Dive / RetailTouchPoints, March 2021).
- "Five Beyond" store-within-a-store: permanent sections added to 140 stores in 2020; plan for 170-180 more in 2021, about 30% of the fleet by end of 2021 (Retail Dive, March 2021, citing CEO Joel Anderson). So Five Beyond dates from 2020, NOT 2022 (correcting the task's assumption).
- Five Beyond price ceiling: third-party pages say $6 to $25 (some say $6-$10). NOT confirmed in company filings -> do not state a ceiling on screen. WEAK.
- Under CEO Winnie Park (CEO since late 2024): company said it was returning focus to core whole-dollar $1 to $5 price points while keeping curated items above $5; simplified to whole-dollar prices in 2025 (Q4 fiscal 2024 call, March 2025; Q2 fiscal 2025 release, Aug 2025).
- Q4 fiscal 2025 call (March 2026): about 80% of units still sold at $5 or less; higher price points helped holiday results (Motley Fool/AOL call summary). WEAKLY SOURCED (summary, not verbatim transcript) -> not used on screen.

## Key numbers (company releases / 10-K)
Fiscal 2025 (year ended Jan 31, 2026), release of March 18, 2026:
- Net sales $4.76 billion, up 22.9% from $3.88 billion in fiscal 2024.
- Comparable sales +12.8%.
- 150 net new stores; 1,921 stores in 46 states at year end (+8.5%).
- Gross profit $1,714.7 million; gross margin 36.0% (vs 34.9% in fiscal 2024) (10-K fiscal 2025).
- Operating income $457.4 million vs $323.8 million (mychesco summary of release; consistent with search snippets). Operating margin computed: 457.4 / 4,760 = about 9.6%.
- Net income about $358.6 million; diluted EPS $6.47; adjusted diluted EPS $6.67 (vs $5.04).
- Q4 fiscal 2025: net sales $1.73B (+24%), comps +15.4% = about +8% comparable ticket and +7% comparable transactions. Adjusted gross margin 40.3%, down ~20 bps, mainly from about 160 bps of transitory tariff costs (company release). Full-year fiscal 2025 tariff headwind about 90 bps (call summary, weaker).

Q2 fiscal 2026 (quarter ended Aug 1, 2026), release Sept 2, 2026:
- Net sales $1.26B, +22.9%; comps +14.1% (about 13.6% from more transactions, 0.4% from average ticket).
- 52 net new stores; 2,022 stores in 46 states (+8.8%).
- GAAP diluted EPS $3.99 included $2.33 per share from tariff refunds and related interest; adjusted EPS $1.68.
- Full-year fiscal 2026 outlook raised: net sales $5.63-$5.71B, comps +10% to +12%, about 150 net new stores. (Guidance; not used as fact.)

Tariffs (neutral): company reported transitory tariff costs hit Q4 fiscal 2025 gross margin by about 160 bps; in Q2 fiscal 2026 it recorded tariff refunds plus interest worth $2.33 per diluted share.

## Worked example (EXAMPLE, using company-wide fiscal 2025 averages)
- Gross margin 36.0%: a $5 item -> 5 x 0.36 = $1.80 gross profit (before rent, wages, etc.).
- Operating margin ~9.6%: $5 x 0.096 = about $0.48 operating profit per $5 of sales.
- So it takes about 10 five-dollar items to earn about $4.80 operating profit (10 x 0.48).
- A $15 item at the same average margins: 15 x 0.096 = about $1.44 operating profit, three times one $5 item from the same shelf space and one checkout. (Illustration; real margins vary by item.)
- Scale check: $4.76B / $5 = about 952 million five-dollar sales-equivalents in fiscal 2025.

## Surprising fact
For its first 17 years (2002-2019) Five Below sold nothing above $5; now it still keeps most products at $5 and below, yet it earned about 48 cents of operating profit per $5 of sales in fiscal 2025, and its holiday-quarter growth in Q4 fiscal 2025 came about equally from bigger baskets (+8% ticket) and more trips (+7% transactions).
