## Issues
1. **L8 credits the gross margin to the business model, but the 10-K's wording is different (should fix).** "So in 2025, its gross margin was 61.6%" says the 61.6% is caused by the concentrate model. The 10-K says something more general: "Generally, finished product operations produce higher net operating revenues but lower gross profit margins than concentrate operations." It explains the FY2025 increase with pricing and the prior-year refranchising, partly offset by currency and commodity costs. Fix: text "Concentrate earns higher margins than bottling, per its 10-K. 2025 gross margin: 61.6%." Say: "Its filing says concentrate earns higher margins than bottling. Its twenty twenty-five gross margin was sixty-one point six percent." At minimum, drop the "So".
2. **L1 hook: "make" is loose (minor, should fix).** Coca-Cola does make the concentrate, so "doesn't make" overstates the point. Also, 85% is company-wide unit case volume across all brands, not Coke brand alone. The cover already says "bottle". Stronger and accurate: "Coca-Cola doesn't bottle most of the Coke you drink." That is about 9 words, under 3 seconds, and pays off the cover. Apply the same change to the first line of the IG post and the YT description.
3. **L4 right label "Own bottling" (minor).** The 10-K says finished product operations consist "primarily" of consolidated bottling. They also include US fountain syrup sales and finished brands such as fairlife. Use "Finished drinks" instead.
4. **L4 narration is awkward (minor).** "...revenue that year, out of forty-seven point nine billion dollars." Better: "That syrup business made fifty-nine percent of Coca-Cola's forty-seven point nine billion dollars in twenty twenty-five revenue."
5. **L6 spoken vs on-screen (minor).** "Why does it matter?" is spoken but not shown. No number differs, so this is acceptable. "Its largest US bottler" could also read as KO-owned. Coke Consolidated is independent (it bought back KO's stake in 2024), so "The largest US Coke bottler" is clearer.
6. **L2 label length (minor).** The stat label "Coca-Cola volume via concentrate" is 32 characters. All sub-labels are within 28 ("Unit cases, 10-K FY2025" is 23, "Coke Consolidated" is 17, "10-K FY2025" is 11, "FY2025" is 6). If labels share the 28-character limit, use "Volume via concentrate" (22).
7. **L7 generalization (OK).** "Bottlers carry the costly plants, trucks and workers" fits the model. KO still owns some bottlers (Bottling Investments), which "most" in the hook already allows for. No change needed.
