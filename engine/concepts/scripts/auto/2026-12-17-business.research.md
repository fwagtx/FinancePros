# Research brief: How Roku makes more from ads than from selling streaming sticks
Slot: 2026-12-17-business (How They Make Money). Posts Thursday, December 17, 2026. Researched Sunday, October 18, 2026.
Main numbers are full-year FY2025 (Roku Form 10-K, fiscal year ended December 31, 2025), so they stay valid on the posting date. Q3 2026 results are due around late October / early November 2026; nothing in the script depends on them.

## Timely angle
- Fox Corporation agreed on June 15, 2026 to acquire Roku in a cash-and-stock deal valued at about $22 billion ($96 cash + 0.9693 Fox Class A shares per Roku share, about $160/share). Expected to close in the first half of 2027 (pending; not closed as of Oct 2026). Sources: Variety; Fox TV stations (FOX 13, FOX 5 DC); multiple wire pickups. Use only as "agreed in June 2026", never as completed.
- Roku said on April 16, 2026 it passed 100 million streaming households worldwide (Roku press release via Business Wire; Light Reading, TV Technology). Streaming household = distinct user account streaming on Roku in a 30-day period.

## The two segments (FY2025 10-K, in thousands)
| | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Platform revenue | $4,144,886 | $3,522,776 (+18%) |
| Devices revenue | $592,365 | $590,122 (flat) |
| Total net revenue | $4,737,251 (~$4.737B, +15%) | $4,112,898 |
| Platform gross profit | $2,156,444 | $1,885,960 (+14%) |
| Devices gross profit (loss) | $(82,020) | $(80,315) |
| Total gross profit | $2,074,424 (~$2.074B, +15%) | |

Derived (my math from the 10-K figures):
- Platform share of FY2025 revenue: 4,144.9 / 4,737.3 = 87.5%. Devices = 12.5%.
- Platform gross margin FY2025: 2,156.4 / 4,144.9 = 52.0%.
- Devices gross margin FY2025: -82.0 / 592.4 = -13.8% (FY2024: -13.6%; shareholder letter called FY2024 devices GM "(14)%").
- Platform gross profit is 104% of total gross profit; devices subtract from it (2,156.4 - 82.0 = 2,074.4).

What's in Platform (10-K description): digital advertising (direct and programmatic video ads, ads in the Roku interface/Home Screen, The Roku Channel), streaming distribution (subscription and transaction revenue shares, Premium Subscriptions sold through Roku, owned subscription services like Frndly TV, branded remote-control buttons).
What's in Devices: streaming players, Roku-made TVs, smart home, audio, accessories.

Strategy, per the 10-K (paraphrase, not a quote): Roku manages the average selling prices of its streaming devices to sell more of them and grow Streaming Households, accepting lower devices gross profit because it expects that to increase platform revenue and platform gross profit over time.

## Recent quarters (labelled by period)
- Q4 2025: platform revenue $1.224B (+18%), devices $170.9M (+3%); platform GM 52.8%, devices GM -23.3%; net income $80.5M. (TheWrap, The Desk, Zacks)
- Q2 2026 (reported Aug 6, 2026): platform revenue $1.22B (+25%); advertising ~$672.8M, subscriptions ~$548.2M (The Desk); devices $133.7M (-1%), helped by a tariff refund; net income $164.2M. No call/guidance due to pending Fox deal. (Roku 8-K Q2 2026; The Desk; TheWrap)
- Roku stopped quarterly reporting of Streaming Households and ARPU starting Q1 2025. FY2025 streaming hours: 145.6 billion (+15%).

## Worked example (exact math, FY2025 averages)
- Every $100 of Devices revenue: gross margin -13.8% -> about $114 of cost, so Roku lost about $14 before any other expenses.
- Every $100 of Platform revenue: gross margin 52.0% -> Roku kept about $52 of gross profit.
- Label as averages from FY2025 10-K totals, not any single device.

## The surprising fact
Roku's hardware business lost money at the gross-profit level in FY2025 (-$82.0M on $592.4M of sales). All of Roku's gross profit — more than all of it — came from Platform (ads and subscriptions): $2.156B.
