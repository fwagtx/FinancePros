# Research: Face-off: condo vs single-family home (2026-11-23-hot)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- NAR Existing-Home Sales, August 2026 (released Sept 10, 2026): median existing-home price $429,100 (+1.6% y/y); single-family median $434,800 (+1.7%); condo/co-op median $371,600 (+1.5%). Sales 3.98M SAAR; inventory 1.62M.
- Gap: $434,800 - $371,600 = $63,200 (14.5% below single-family).
- Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year fixed: 6.71% (Sep 3), 6.76% (Sep 10), 6.95% (Sep 17), 7.03% (Sep 24, 2026).
- U.S. Census Bureau (Sept 2025 story, 2024 ACS): about 3 million households paid condo/HOA fees above $500 a month; national median fee about $135/month (2024), up from $108 in 2019.
- Florida: post-Surfside milestone inspection / structural reserve laws have driven large special assessments; many buildings face Dec 31, 2026 deadlines.

## Worked example (hypothetical, 20% down, 7% 30-year fixed)
- House $434,800: loan $347,840 -> $2,314.19/mo
- Condo $371,600: loan $297,280 -> $1,977.81/mo
- Difference $336.38/mo (= payment on $50,560)
- A hypothetical $400/mo HOA fee exceeds that difference by $63.62.
