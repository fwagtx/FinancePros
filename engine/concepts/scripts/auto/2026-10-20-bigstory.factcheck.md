## Issues and fixes

1. **L1 hook / post.title: "$50 billion on store cards" is too broad (must fix).** The $49.7B is Synchrony's total purchase volume. That covers private-label store cards plus co-brand and dual cards (for example Lowe's Pro Amex, PayPal and Venmo cards) and CareCredit. Not all of it is store-card spending, and it is one lender, not all store cards.
   - L1 text: `Nearly $50 billion on Synchrony cards, and fewer bills went unpaid.`
   - L1 say: `Nearly fifty billion dollars on Synchrony cards, and fewer bills went unpaid.` (12 words, unchanged)
   - L1 scene.title: `[$50B] on Synchrony cards`
   - post.title (59 chars): `Nearly $50B on Synchrony cards, and fewer bills went unpaid`
   - ig line 1: `Nearly $50 billion on Synchrony cards, and fewer bills went unpaid.`

2. **L2: "reporting Tuesday" (time word).** The video posts on Tuesday, so viewers will hear "Tuesday" said about today, which sounds odd and suggests a different day. "Today" is correct on the posting date. Fixing issue 1 also means the store-card description moves here:
   - L2 text: `That's Synchrony, the bank behind Amazon's and Lowe's store cards, reporting today on how its borrowers are doing.`
   - L2 say: `That's Synchrony, the bank behind Amazon's and Lowe's store cards, reporting today on how its borrowers are doing.` (18 words, unchanged)

3. **L8: "Car loans look steady too" generalizes one lender to all car loans (must fix).** The 1.89% is Ally's retail auto rate only. The line needs to attribute it to Ally.
   - L8 text: `Ally's car loans look steady too, with losses at 1.89%, about flat.`
   - L8 say: `Ally's car loans look steady too, with losses at one point eight nine percent, about flat.` (16 words, down from 17)

4. **L9 / board (optional softening).** The spoken line already says "these lenders", which is good. The board's "= more borrowers keeping up" says more than the data shows. A falling write-off rate means a smaller share of balances went unpaid; it does not count borrowers. Suggested board lines (each 20 characters or fewer): `Fewer write-offs` / `= smaller share` / `going unpaid`.

5. **tiktok (optional, adds attribution).** The current caption (143 chars) is fine. A version that also signals the scope (148 chars): `Synchrony card write-offs fell to 4.79% from 5.16%. One lender, not all borrowers. Not financial advice. #moneytok #creditcards #debt #financeprostv`

6. **Minor.** The cover and header ("Store Card Bad Debt Fell" / "Store card bad debt") are acceptable because the video does pay off the falling write-off rate. "Synchrony Bad Debt Fell" would be more precise but is not required. On L7, the spoken "four point three nine" leaves off "percent". It is fine to say aloud and matches the on-screen 4.39%.
