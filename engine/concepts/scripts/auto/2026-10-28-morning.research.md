Why Visa: it touches almost everyone with a card, has a surprising scale number (281B transactions), a real annual revenue series to chart, and is confirmed by the company's SEC-filed 8-K earnings release plus multiple outlets. Not previously covered (Visa only appeared as a scheduled reporter).

## Facts (all from Visa's fiscal Q4 2026 earnings release, 8-K filed with SEC, released Oct 27, 2026, after the close, unless noted)
- Fiscal year ends September 30. Fiscal Q4 = July-September 2026.
- Q4 net revenue: $12.1 billion, up 13% (nominal and constant-dollar).
- Q4 GAAP net income $6.0B, $3.19/share; non-GAAP net income $6.6B, $3.48/share.
- Q4 GAAP results include a $469 million litigation provision tied to the interchange multidistrict litigation (the long-running case over card "swipe"/interchange fees) and $282M tied to a donation of investments to the Visa Foundation.
- Q4 drivers: payments volume +9% (constant dollars), cross-border volume excl. intra-Europe +14%, processed transactions +10% (call transcripts / earnings summaries).
- Full fiscal 2026 net revenue: record $45.9 billion, up 15% (14% constant-dollar). Outlets round to "$46 billion".
- FY2026 processed transactions: about 281 billion, up 9% (Visa earnings call; cited by Investing.com, Benzinga, Globe and Mail, Motley Fool transcripts).
- Quarterly dividend raised 16% to $0.78/share (payable Dec 1, record Nov 10) (Gotrade, AskTraders, FinanceFeeds).
- Shares slipped after hours Oct 27: CNBC "about 2%"; other reports 1.46%-1.6%. Script says only "slipped" (no figure).
- Consensus comparisons conflict (GuruFocus headline says missed; Investing.com says topped estimates) -> script avoids beat/miss language.

## Simple math (labeled as our division, on average)
- $45.9B / 281B transactions = about $0.163 -> "roughly 16 cents per transaction, on average". Not all revenue is per-transaction, so framed as rough average.

- FY2024: $35.9B ($35,926M, Visa FY2025 10-K)
- FY2025: $40.0B (Visa FY2025 annual report: up 11% to $40B)
- FY2026: $45.9B (Visa Q4 2026 earnings release)

## Context / what it means for a normal person
- Visa is a payment network: it moves the payment and is paid by banks/merchants through fees tied to volume and transactions. It does not lend money or set a cardholder's interest rate; the card-issuing bank does.
- As card spending grows, Visa's revenue grows; merchants' swipe (interchange) fees are the subject of the long-running litigation behind the $469M provision.
