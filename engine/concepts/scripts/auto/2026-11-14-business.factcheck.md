# Fact-check: 2026-11-14-business (How HOAs get and spend money)

Checked Oct 2, 2026 for a Nov 14, 2026 post.

## Verified
- Foundation for Community Association Research (CAI), 2025 Statistical Review: ~373,000 associations at end of 2025; ~78.1M residents; $124.2B in assessments collected in 2025; $31.1B of that went to reserves. All match.
- $31.1B / $124.2B = 25.04%, so "a quarter" is right. $124.2B - $31.1B = $93.1B, which is correct as the non-reserve (operating) share.
- Resident series (Foundation Fact Book / Statistical Reviews): 1970 2.1M, 1980 9.6M, 1990 29.6M, 2000 45.2M, 2010 62.0M, 2015 68.0M, 2020 74.1M, 2024 77.1M, 2025 78.1M. All 9 points match.
- Census Bureau, 2024 ACS (Sept 2025 story): about 21.6M owner households paid a condo or HOA fee, and the median fee was $135 a month. Matches. Note that this is the median among households that pay a fee.
- Hypothetical math: 200 x $135 x 12 = $324,000, and $200,000 / 200 = $1,000. Both correct, and the example is labeled hypothetical ("Picture a hypothetical HOA", "Say...").
- Spoken numbers match on-screen ones (124.2 is spoken as "one hundred twenty-four billion" and 93.1 / 31.1 as "ninety-three" / "thirty-one" billion, which is acceptable rounding).
- Lengths: cover title 28, hook title 20, labels/subs <=28, compare subs 15, board lines <=22, list items 2 words. All within limits.
- No advice, predictions or political opinion. No dated time words. The cover ("Where $124B in HOA Dues Goes") is paid off by line 4.

## Required fixes
1. **Line 2, "a quarter wasn't spent that year" (misleading).** Reserve contributions are set aside for future repairs and replacements, and associations do draw on reserves for projects in the same year. "Wasn't spent" suggests idle or hoarded money, and nobody has measured it.
   - text: `And about a quarter went into savings for future repairs. These groups house 78.1 million Americans.`
   - say: `And about a quarter went into savings for future repairs. These groups house seventy-eight point one million Americans.`
2. **Line 1, "HOAs and condos" (incomplete).** The CAI totals cover HOAs, condominiums AND housing cooperatives (co-ops are about 2-4%). The YT description already says co-ops.
   - text: `In 2025, HOAs, condos and co-ops collected $124.2 billion in dues.`
   - say: `In twenty twenty-five, HOAs, condos and co-ops collected one hundred twenty-four billion dollars in dues.`
   - Post: change the IG first line and the TikTok line from "HOAs and condos" to "HOAs, condos and co-ops".
   - Line 3 chart title (optional): change it to `Americans in associations` (25 chars), because "HOAs, condos, co-ops" is over the 28-character limit.
3. **Line 6, median qualifier.** The $135 is the median among the 21.6M homes that pay a fee, not among all homes.
   - text: `Among homes that pay one, the Census Bureau's median fee was $135 a month in 2024.`
   - say: `Among homes that pay one, the Census Bureau's median fee was one hundred thirty-five dollars a month in twenty twenty-four.`

## Notes (non-blocking)
- Hook: true and specific. It would be stronger if the dollar figure came first: "HOA dues hit $124 billion in 2025." The current version is acceptable.
- Line 8: a $1,000 per home figure assumes the cost is split equally; many associations allocate by unit share. Since the example is hypothetical this is fine. Optionally say "split evenly, that's...".

VERDICT: FAIL
