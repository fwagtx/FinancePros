## Issues and fixes (0-based line indexes)

1. **Line 2: spoken value differs from on-screen value (REQUIRED).** The screen shows `37.5%`, but the narration says "thirty-seven and a half" with no "percent". Replace `say` with:
   `Split fifty-fifty, that's fifteen hundred each: twenty-five percent of one paycheck, thirty-seven point five percent of the other.`
   This is 18 words, the same as now, so the total stays at 138. Leave `text` and `scene` as they are.

2. **Line 5: the Pew 71% is derived (minor, optional).** Pew reports 29% egalitarian, 55% husband-led and 16% wife-led. It does not print "71%". The arithmetic is exact, and "over 60%" correctly includes sole earners, so the line can stay. To be safer, change the stat `sub` from `Pew, 2022 data` to `Pew 2022: 55%+16%` (17 chars). Pew's analysis may also be limited to spouses ages 25-54, which I could not confirm. Nothing on screen has to change for that.

3. **Line 6: 2025 BLS caveat (minor, optional, post only).** BLS's 2025 annual averages cover only 11 months because October 2025 was not collected during the shutdown, and BLS says they are not strictly comparable with other years. You could add "(11-month average)" after "BLS Usual Weekly Earnings (2025 annual averages" in post.ig. No change to the narration is needed.

4. **Line 6 chart, 2024 point (minor).** 82.7 agrees with BLS's rounded 83% for 2024 and stays inside BLS's stated 81-84% band for 2010-2024. Search did not return the exact decimal. Before render, check it against BLS CPS table 37 (2024 annual averages). If the table differs, use the table's value.

5. **Line 0: hook (acceptable, optional upgrade).** "Fifty-fifty bills can take a much bigger bite from one paycheck." It is true and 11 words, though a bit generic, and at roughly 3.3 s it sits right at the 3-second limit. A tighter option of the same length: `Fifty-fifty bills can cost one paycheck way more than the other.` (11 words, so the total stays 138). If you use it, set `text` to `50/50 bills can cost one paycheck way more than the other.`
