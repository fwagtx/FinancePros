## Pick: CrowdStrike to acquire Zscaler (all-stock)
Facts (CrowdStrike SEC 8-K Ex. 99.1 / Zscaler press release, Nov 23, 2026; Help Net Security, Yahoo Finance, ABC News/AP, TheStreet citing Reuters):
- Announced Monday, Nov 23, 2026: CrowdStrike to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock deal valuing Zscaler at approx. $43.9 billion (widely rounded to "$44 billion").
- Exchange ratio: each Zscaler share -> 1.135 CrowdStrike shares. Implied value ~$260.41 per Zscaler share based on Nov 20, 2026 (Friday) close; premium approx. 34% to Zscaler's Nov 20 close.
- Ownership after close: existing CrowdStrike shareholders ~84.1%, Zscaler shareholders ~15.9%.
- Jay Chaudhry (Zscaler founder/CEO) and affiliates (~34% of Zscaler shares) signed voting agreements in support; he joins CrowdStrike's board.
- Expected synergies (company claim): ~$1B annual revenue synergies within 30 months, ~$500M cost savings.
- Closing expected in second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028; needs Zscaler shareholder approval and regulatory approvals.
- Multiple outlets: largest cybersecurity deal on record (prior: Google-Wiz $32B).
- Market reaction (INTRADAY, Monday morning, figures vary by time/source): CrowdStrike shares fell (reports range ~7% to ~12%; -9.67% to $207.24 at 9:55 a.m. ET per GuruFocus); Zscaler shares rose (13% to 21% reports) but stayed below the $260.41 implied value. Commentary (TheStreet citing Reuters): investors focused on dilution.
- Same day: CrowdStrike Q3 FY2027 (quarter ended Oct 31, 2026): revenue $1.58B (+28%), record net new ARR ~$419.2M (+58% y/y), ending ARR $6.26B (+27%).

## Context / what it means
- All-stock deal: buyer pays with newly issued shares instead of cash. Existing owners end up owning a smaller slice (dilution: 100% -> 84.1%).
- Premium: buyers usually pay above market price to win the target's shareholders.
- For a normal person: index funds/401(k)s may hold one or both; the cost of a stock deal is shared by the buyer's own shareholders. Education only.
- Watch next: Zscaler shareholder vote, regulatory review, closing in 2H of CrowdStrike FY2028.
