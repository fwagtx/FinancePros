## Issues

1. **POST title and TikTok caption overclaim (MUST FIX).** "US factories just hit their fastest pace since 2021" describes the whole sector, which is the headline PMI, and July's 55.6 was higher. Only the production index (59.0) is at a high since 2021.
   - title: `US factory output just hit its fastest pace since 2021`
   - tiktok: `Factory output hit its fastest pace since 2021, but costs are hot. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #economy #inflation #financeprostv`

2. **Line 0: the hook is true, but the scene title overclaims, and the body never pays off the hook (MUST FIX).** "Factory output" = the production index (59.0, highest since Nov 2021), so the spoken hook is accurate and strong: 11 spoken words, about 3 s. But the scene title "Fastest factories since [2021]" implies the whole index, and the 59.0 figure never appears. Viewers instead see 55.5 and a chart where July (55.6) is higher than October, which looks like a contradiction.
   - Line 0 scene title: `Fastest output since [2021]`. Keep the text and say unchanged.
   - Line 2 (pay off the hook). text: `Today's ISM report: factory output scored 59, the highest since November 2021.` say: `Today's I S M report: factory output scored fifty-nine, the highest since November twenty twenty-one.` scene: `{"t":"stat","label":"Factory output","value":"59","tone":"up","sub":"Highest since Nov 2021","mood":"up"}`

3. **Line 3: chart x-axis (data issue requested) plus moving the headline 55.5 here.**
   - text: `The overall index hit 55.5, beating forecasts: 10 straight months of growth, after 10 of shrinking.`
   - say: `The overall index hit fifty-five point five, beating forecasts: ten straight months of growth, after ten of shrinking.` (18 words)
   - scene (yearly): `{"t":"chart","title":"ISM index each October","points":[[2019,48.3],[2020,59.3],[2021,60.8],[2022,50.2],[2023,46.7],[2024,46.5],[2025,48.7],[2026,55.5]],"end":"55.5","color":"up","mood":"happy"}`
   - If you keep the monthly chart instead, all 10 points are correct, but label the x-axis with months, and note that July (55.6) is above October.

4. **Line 4: prices framing omits the monthly cooling (SHOULD FIX, for balance).** 75.2 is down from 77.9 in September, and several outlets headline this as "price index cools". As written, viewers may think prices are still accelerating.
   - text: `The catch: input prices scored 75.2, down from September but up from 58.5 last December.`
   - say: `The catch: input prices scored seventy-five point two, down from September but up from fifty-eight point five last December.` (19 words; keep the compare scene)

5. **Line 5: "53% of factories" (minor, optional).** ISM figure: 53.1% of surveyed respondents. Optional text: `About 53% of surveyed factories paid more, for things like copper, aluminum, diesel and electronics.` (say adds "surveyed", 16 words). If applied, the total is 140, the upper limit.

6. **Word budget for the fixes above.** These two trims offset the added words.
   - Line 6 text/say: `For your money, it's mixed: busy factories support jobs, but higher costs can trickle into prices.` (16 words)
   - Line 8 text/say: `Bottom line: factories are busy, but costs are still high.` (10 words); board unchanged.
   - Totals: 138 originally. With fixes 2, 3, 4 and the trims: 11+17+15+18+19+15+16+12+10+6 = **139**. Adding fix 5 gives 140.

7. **Time words.** "Today's" (release Nov 2, posting Nov 2), "Election Day tomorrow" (Nov 3) and "jobs report on Friday" (Nov 6, BLS) are all correct on the posting date. "last December" = Dec 2025, which is correct; the on-screen "Dec 2025" makes it clear.
