# Fact-check: 2026-11-19-explainer (combining finances with a partner)

Checked Oct 2, 2026, for a Nov 19, 2026 post. Sources: the Bankrate couples-finances press release (Feb 2026, YouGov), the Census Bureau story "Almost a Quarter of Married Couples Didn't Have Joint Accounts in 2023, Up From 15% in 1996" (Sept 2025, SIPP) and FDIC joint-account guidance.

## Verified
- Bankrate/YouGov: 2,564 US adults surveyed online Dec 2-8, 2025, of whom 1,208 were married, in a civil partnership or living with a partner. Published Feb 2026. Among those couples, 38% completely combine their finances, 36% use a mix of joint and separate accounts and 26% keep everything separate. Gen Z (ages 18-29): 51% keep finances completely separate (the base is Gen Z respondents in married or live-in relationships). The other generations are 34% (millennials), 23% (Gen X) and 15% (boomers).
- Hook "A 2026 survey": fair. Bankrate brands and published the survey in 2026, and the post copy discloses the Dec 2025 fieldwork. "Couples" matches the base (married, civil partnership or living together). "Fully merge" is a fair paraphrase of "completely combine". The hook is 12 spoken words, true and specific.
- Census SIPP: the share of married couples with no joint account rose from 15% (1996) to 23% (2023). The base is householder married couples who own any assets at financial institutions, so "married couples" is acceptable. The story ran in Sept 2025, so it is not stale.
- FDIC: each co-owner is insured up to $250,000 for their combined interests in all joint accounts at the same insured bank (joint-account ownership category). The script's wording "per co-owner, per bank" is accurate.
- Math: 6,000/10,000 = 60% and 40%. 0.6 x 3,000 = 1,800 and 0.4 x 3,000 = 1,200. 6,000 - 1,800 = 4,200 and 4,000 - 1,200 = 2,800. 4,200/6,000 = 2,800/4,000 = 70%. All correct.
- Tone: neutral. The script describes one method and gives no advice or prediction. The example is labeled hypothetical. The cover is paid off. No time words will be wrong on the posting date.
- On-screen values match the spoken values on every line.
- Lengths are within limits except item 1 below. Hook title "Only [38%] fully merge" is 22 characters. Compare subs are at most 15. Board lines are at most 21. Labels are at most 27.
