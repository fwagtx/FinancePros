## Issues

1. **Hook, L0 and cover: "Nine in ten ... just letters" (minor, wording).** 89.2% is close to 9 in 10, but strictly it's just under. "Just letters" also suggests the audit is trivial, when correspondence audits often end with extra tax. A correspondence audit is one done entirely by mail, so "letters" is accurate. The hook is specific and short (about 2.5 s), so it works.
   - Stronger and exact: say "Nearly nine in ten IRS audits of individuals happen entirely by mail." Caption: "Nearly 9 in 10 IRS audits of individuals happen entirely by mail."

2. **Cover title "9 in 10 Audits Are Letters" leaves out "of individuals" (fix).** Across all return types, only 81% of FY2025 audits were correspondence audits (about 8 in 10). The 89% figure applies only to individual income tax returns.
   - Fix the cover title to "9 in 10 Individual Audits Are By Mail" (or "89% of Tax Audits: By Mail" with the label "Individuals").

3. **L3 label "Under $25K" leaves out zero-income returns, and the post text treats 0.5% to 6.6% as the full range (fix, factual).** The 0.5% rate covers TPI from $1 to under $25,000. Returns with no positive income were audited at 1.8%. The lowest rate is not 0.5% either: every bracket from $25K to $500K was 0.2%. The yt_desc line "0.3% of 2021 returns were audited, from 0.5% under $25,000 of income to 6.6% at $10 million or more" therefore states a wrong range.
   - L3 left label: "$1–$25K", or keep "Under $25K" and change the sub to "pos. income, TY2021".
   - yt_desc: "...0.3% of 2021 returns were audited: 0.2% for most incomes from $25,000 to $500,000, 0.5% under $25,000 and 6.6% at $10 million or more."
   - Optional for L3 narration, for accuracy: "Income matters: half a percent under twenty-five thousand, just point two percent for most middle incomes, six point six percent at ten million plus." Line 3 as written isn't false.

4. **L5 timeline mixes two tracks (minor, clarity).** "Letter → 30 days CP2000 → 90 days Tax Court" reads as one sequence counted from a single letter. In fact a CP2000 is not an audit, and the 90 days run from the notice of deficiency.
   - Fix the events: {when:"CP2000", what:"Reply in 30 days"}, {when:"Audit letter", what:"Says what to prove"}, {when:"Deficiency", what:"90 days: Tax Court"}.

5. **L6 sub "25%+ income missed" (minor, precision).** The law says *more than* 25% of gross income stated on the return, and "25%+" includes exactly 25%.
   - Fix: "Over 25% income left off".

6. **Optional: fraud is not mentioned.** There is no time limit for a fraudulent return or a return never filed (IRC 6501(c)). Adding "no limit for fraud" to the L6 caption would complete the picture. Leaving it out is not an error.

7. **L2 chart fairness (note).** The data is fair: it uses closed years only and is labeled by tax year. Make sure the y-axis starts at 0 so the drop from 0.6 to 0.3 isn't exaggerated. The chart shows a halving; it doesn't need to look like a collapse.
