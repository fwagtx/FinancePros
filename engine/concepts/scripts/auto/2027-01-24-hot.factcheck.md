# Fact-check: 2027-01-24-hot (0% utilization myth), posting January 24, 2027

Firecrawl had no credits left, and direct fetches of myfico.com, experian.com and investors.fico.com were blocked by the session network. I verified the claims with WebSearch result summaries, which link to the primary pages.

## Claims verified
- **Line 2, amounts owed are about 30% of a FICO Score (myFICO/FICO):** true. This is FICO's standard factor breakdown, and Experian repeats it.
- **Line 3, all cards at $0 can score slightly lower than a small balance (myFICO):** true. A myFICO FAQ says reporting no revolving balances is "slightly more risky" than a small reported balance. A myFICO blog post says $0 can read as a lack of credit activity.
- **Line 5, 800+ scorers average about 7% (Experian, 2026):** true. Experian's 800+ post, using June 2026 data, gives 7% utilization against 29% for all consumers. The "myFICO" attribution in the IG caption is not verifiable: myFICO's old "high achievers" 7% figure is about 2012 data and uses a 785+ cutoff. The FICO-review 4% figure is a median for 800+ from a Kiplinger summary. Both were left out.
- **Line 4 math:** $150 / $5,000 = 3%. Correct. A statement balance paid in full in the grace period costs $0 in interest. Correct.
- **Chart:** April 2021 716, April 2022 716, April 2023 718 (record), April 2024 717, April 2025 715 and April 2026 714 match FICO releases, CNBC and the Credit Karma table. 717 is backed by FICO/Motley Fool saying 2025 was down 2 from 2024. FICO's Spring '26 and Aug 25, 2026 releases put the average at 714, down one point from April 2025. No newer FICO average comes out before January 24, 2027; the next edition is due around spring 2027.

## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 3 (compare), "like 3%" read as if myFICO said 3%.** myFICO gives no number. Removed "like 3%" and changed the compare right side from `3%` to label "Balance", value "Small". New SAY: "The twist: myFICO says all cards at zero dollars can score slightly lower than a small balance, because it can look unused." The 3% now appears only in our own worked example, which was moved to directly follow this line.
2. **IG caption and YT description attribution, "(myFICO; Experian 2026 data)".** The myFICO link to the 800+ figure is unverifiable. Changed to "(Experian, 2026 data)".
3. **Chart was stale and unverified.** Its last point was "715 in 2025", but April 2026 is 714, so it would be stale on the posting date. The April 2019 706 and April 2020 708 points could not be verified, and FICO's April 2020 figure may not be 708. New points: 2021 to 2026 = 716, 716, 718, 717, 715, 714, with end "714".
4. **Chart did not connect to the myth.** Moved it to follow the 800+ stat and opened it with "For scale," so the national average gives context for the 800+ / 7% figure. New SAY: "For scale, FICO's US average each April: seven sixteen in twenty twenty-one, a record seven eighteen in twenty twenty-three, seven fourteen in twenty twenty-six."
5. **Line order.** The worked example now comes right after the "small balance" twist and before the 800+ stat, so the 3% example is introduced as our own example.
6. **Money line, "Paid in full: zero dollars interest."** It read awkwardly out loud. Now "Paid in full, interest is zero dollars." The on-screen $0 is still spoken.
7. **List line, "you never need to carry interest."** "Carry interest" is wrong wording. Now "So zero percent versus low single digits is a tiny gap, and you never need to pay interest."
8. **800+ stat line.** Reworded so the source comes first and the subject is clear: "Experian's twenty twenty-six data: people with eight hundred plus scores average about seven percent utilization." The "2026" stays correct on the posting date, since it is a data year.
9. **Post captions.** The IG caption and YT description were rewritten to match: the 3% is framed as our own example, FICO's 714 (April 2026) was added, and the myFICO attribution for 800+ was dropped. The TikTok caption needed no change.
