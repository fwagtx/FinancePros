# 2026-12-09-hot research brief: Face-off, splitting bills 50/50 vs by income

Researched Saturday, October 10, 2026. Posts Wednesday, December 9, 2026, 6 PM Central. Evergreen; no news hook.

## The two methods (definitions)
- 50/50 (equal split): each person pays the same dollar amount of shared bills, regardless of income.
- Proportional (by income): each person pays a share of shared bills equal to their share of combined income
  (e.g. 60% of income -> 60% of bills). Same percentage of each paycheck goes to shared bills.
- Neither is "correct"; script must stay neutral: describe both methods and the math only.

## Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL, label as example; monthly take-home pay)
- Partner A take-home: $6,000/month. Partner B: $4,000/month. Combined: $10,000.
- Shared bills: $3,000/month.
- 50/50: $3,000 / 2 = $1,500 each.
  - A: $1,500 / $6,000 = 25% of pay. B: $1,500 / $4,000 = 37.5% of pay.
  - Left over: A $4,500, B $2,500.
- Proportional: A earns $6,000/$10,000 = 60%; B earns 40%.
  - A pays 0.60 x $3,000 = $1,800 -> $1,800 / $6,000 = 30% of pay.
  - B pays 0.40 x $3,000 = $1,200 -> $1,200 / $4,000 = 30% of pay.
  - Left over: A $4,200, B $2,800.
- Either way the total is $3,000. 50/50 equalizes dollars; proportional equalizes percent of pay.

## Surprising fact
- Under 50/50 in the example, the lower earner spends 37.5% of pay vs 25% for the higher earner: a 12.5-point gap from the "equal" option.
- Survey (UK): more people call proportional fairest, but more couples actually do 50/50.

## Survey data (attribute; single source each)
- YouGov (UK), research published August 2024 (date per IBTimes UK citing YouGov; YouGov page itself not fetched):
  - 48% of Britons say splitting in proportion to income is fairest; 37% say 50/50.
  - In practice, among couples where both partners work: 46% split 50/50, 38% split proportionally to income.
  - When partners earn about the same: 69% split 50/50. When one earns notably more: 49% split proportionally, about a third (33%) still split 50/50.
  - Source: https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/50381-what-is-the-fairest-way-for-couples-to-split-household-bills
- MoneySuperMarket (UK, via secondary coverage, year unconfirmed): 38% of couples split 50/50, 18% by income. NOT used (unconfirmed date).
- No comparable US survey found (Bankrate/Credit Karma/Pew searched).

## Income gaps within couples (US)
- Pew Research Center, April 13, 2023 ("In a growing share of U.S. marriages, husbands and wives earn about the same"), 2022 data, opposite-sex marriages:
  - 55%: husband is primary (>60% of combined earnings) or sole earner.
  - 29%: egalitarian (each earns 40%-60%), up from 11% in 1972.
  - 16%: wife is primary (10%) or sole (6%) earner, up from 5% in 1972.
  - Derived: 55% + 16% = 71% of these marriages have one spouse earning more than 60% of combined pay (includes sole earners).
