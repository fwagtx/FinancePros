It does not, and the $259M figure is back wages only.
   Fix: L3 "In a lawsuit, federal wage law can add an equal amount..." Optionally, L7 "The Labor Department recovered $259 million in back wages alone..."

   Fix: "Say you're hourly and earn twenty dollars an hour..."

Change to "can come with... if you sue". Some outlets report "$295M" for FY2025, which likely includes other amounts. $259M in back wages is the primary WHD figure, so keep it.
6. **Opening (L1):** "Shorted on pay? Your boss can owe you double." It is true, under 3 seconds, and creates curiosity. That is acceptable. It would be stronger if more specific: "Unpaid overtime? Federal law can make your boss pay it twice."
