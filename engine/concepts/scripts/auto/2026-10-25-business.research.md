# Research brief: How online banks pay higher interest (Business, posts Oct 25, 2026)

- FDIC National Rates and Rate Caps: the national savings rate was 0.37% as of Sept 21, 2026 (FRED SNDR). The national rate cap was 4.38%.
- Top high-yield savings rates: up to 4.20% (Bankrate, Sept 2026) and up to 4.50% (Fortune, Sept 30, 2026). These are variable rates.
- Ally Financial 2025 10-K / Q4 2025 results:
  - Ally Bank is a direct bank with no branch network; it gathers deposits online.
  - Retail deposits were $144B at year-end 2025, 92% FDIC insured.
  - Full-year 2025 net interest margin (excluding OID) was 3.47%.
  - Ally is a leading US auto lender.
- Model: lower occupancy and branch costs, plus higher rates to attract deposits, which fund loans. The bank earns the spread between loan yields and deposit costs (NIM).
- Worked example: $10,000 at 0.37% = $37 a year; at 4.00% (illustrative online rate) = $400 a year.
- Surprising fact: the same $10,000 can earn about 10x more depending on where it sits.
