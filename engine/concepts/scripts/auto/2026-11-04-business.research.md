# Research brief: How Walmart makes money on thin margins (posts Nov 4, 2026)

## Fiscal 2026 (year ended Jan 31, 2026; 10-K filed Mar 13, 2026; release Feb 19, 2026)
- Total revenue $713.2B (+4.7%) ($713,163M)
- Operating income $29,825M (+1.6%) -> operating margin 4.18%
- Consolidated net income $22,270M
- Membership and other income $6,750M (membership fees plus rental, recycling, gift card breakage, etc.)
- Global advertising grew 46% to nearly $6.4B (includes VIZIO)
- Revenue per day: 713.2 / 365 = ~$1.95B

## Q2 FY2027 (released Aug 20, 2026)
- Revenue $187.9B (+5.9%); global advertising +38%; membership fee revenue +17%; global eCommerce +23%.

## Chartable series: operating margin by fiscal year (op income / total revenue, 10-Ks)
FY2020 3.93% (20,568/523,964) | FY2021 4.03% (22,548/559,151) | FY2022 4.53% (25,942/572,754) | FY2023 3.34% (20,428/611,289) | FY2024 4.17% (27,012/648,125) | FY2025 4.31% (29,348/680,985) | FY2026 4.18% (29,825/713,163)

## Worked example
$100 of sales x 4.18% = about $4.18 of operating profit (before interest and taxes).
Comparison: Netflix 2025 operating margin 29.5% (Netflix 10-K) -> about $29.50 per $100.
