## The story: Nvidia took in $115.8 billion in three months, and most index-fund owners hold a slice
Source: NVIDIA press release "NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Third Quarter Fiscal 2027," Nov 17, 2026 (quarter ended Oct 25, 2026), nvidianews.nvidia.com; SEC 8-K filed Nov 17, 2026. Confirmed by TechCrunch (Nov 17), GuruFocus, TradingView, Reuters via Investing.com (Nov 18).

Key numbers (Q3 FY27 vs Q3 FY26):
- Revenue: $115.8B ($115,844M), up 20% q/q and up 103% y/y (from $57.0B / $57,006M). First quarter above $100B (BigGo Finance).
- Data Center revenue: $107.6B, up 21% q/q, up 110% y/y.
- Edge Computing revenue: $8.2B, +14% q/q, +42% y/y.
- GAAP net income: $65.3B ($65,287M) vs $31.9B ($31,910M), +105%. Non-GAAP net income $64.9B.
- GAAP diluted EPS $2.70 (vs $1.30); non-GAAP $2.68 (vs $1.27).
- GAAP gross margin 74.8% (non-GAAP 74.9%).
- Q4 FY27 outlook (includes an extra week): revenue $130.0B +/- 3%; assumes no China data center compute revenue; gross margin 71.0%-72.0%. Analysts' average estimate ~$125.25B (Reuters via Investing.com).
- Shareholder returns: $28.1B in Q3 (buybacks + dividends); board added $150B to buyback authorization in Sept 2026; ~$227.3B remaining. Next dividend $0.25/share on Dec 23, 2026.
- Analyst consensus Q3 revenue ~$109.3B (TradingView).
- Quote: "Our momentum is real." - Colette Kress, interim CEO and CFO, Nov 17, 2026 release.
- Premarket Nov 18: Nvidia shares up ~2.8%-3.7% (sources vary; NOT used on screen); Nasdaq-100 futures ~+1%.

Our math:
- Quarter length: Jul 27 - Oct 25, 2026 = 91 days. $115,844M / 91 = $1,273M per day, i.e. $1.27 billion a day.
- $100 in an S&P 500 index fund at ~6% Nvidia weight = about $6 in Nvidia.

Index weight (secondary sources, varies daily; round on screen):
- Nvidia about 6% of the S&P 500 in mid-November: Trefis (Nov 13) 6.1% of SPY citing State Street holdings file 6.14% as of Nov 12; CalculatorAI 6.30% as of Nov 10 (2nd behind Apple); Yahoo Finance/Moneywise "Nvidia is about 6% of the S&P 500". MarketWatch (via Morningstar, Nov 15): "A month ago, Nvidia's stock peaked at nearly 9% of the entire index by value."
- Index funds held nearly half of assets in large private 401(k) plans in 2023 (ICI via Axios) - not used on screen.

## Context
- Revenue has doubled in a year, almost all from data center AI chips sold to cloud and AI companies.
- The company itself flags margin dipping to 71-72% next quarter (Vera Rubin ramp) and no China data center revenue assumed.
- First report under interim CEO Colette Kress (leadership change covered in a previous video; not the focus).

## What it means for a normal person's money
- Most 401(k)s and IRAs hold S&P 500 index funds; Nvidia is about 6% of that index, so about $6 of every $100 rides on one company's results.
- "Diversified" funds are more concentrated than many people assume; knowing what's inside your fund is the takeaway (education, not advice).
