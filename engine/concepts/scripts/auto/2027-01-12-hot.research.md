# 2027-01-12-hot research brief
Topic: Scam alert: fake travel agency / vacation package scams. Posts Tue Jan 12, 2027, 6 PM CT (made Nov 13, 2026; evergreen, no news peg).
Format: Scam Alert. Education only, no advice to buy/sell. Every number dated.

## Key facts (with dates)
1. FTC Consumer Sentinel Network Data Book 2024 (published March 2025; latest annual data book on ftc.gov reports page as of Nov 13, 2026),
   category "Travel, Vacations and Timeshare Plans" (note: category INCLUDES timeshare, not only fake agencies):
   - 58,347 reports in 2024 (0.90% of all Sentinel reports)
   - total reported loss $274M in 2024 (rank 9 by total loss among fraud categories)
   - median reported loss $922 in 2024
   - overall 2024 fraud: $12,537,194,708 lost (about $12.5 billion), 2,600,678 fraud reports, overall median fraud loss $497.
   ($274M also reported by secondary outlets, e.g. This Week Health, Ground News "Travel scams cost Americans $274 million a year".)
2. Same category, earlier FTC Data Books:
   - 2023: 55,063 reports, $122M total loss, median $1,187 (CSN Data Book 2023 PDF)
   - 2022: 62,445 reports, $104M total loss, median $1,259 (CSN Data Book 2022 PDF; an FTC 2023 release/secondary reports say about $105.1M, data-book rounding/revision)
   - 2021: about $95M (secondary outlets citing Sentinel: $95.3M, 53,891 reports, median $1,112). Not read from the FTC PDF directly.
3. 2025 context: FTC says consumers reported losing a record $15.9 billion to fraud in 2025 (up from $12.5B in 2024), ~3 million fraud reports
   (FTC testimony to Joint Economic Committee, March 2026; Spokesman-Review/AOL Apr 2026). A 2025 travel-category total was NOT found;
   the 2025 Data Book is not listed on ftc.gov reports page as of Nov 13, 2026. So 2024 is the latest confirmed travel-category year.
4. FTC guidance (consumer.ftc.gov "Avoid scams when you travel"; consumer alert June 2025 "How to avoid travel website scams"):
   - A travel site demanding payment by wire transfer, gift card or cryptocurrency is a sign of a scam; don't pay anyone who insists on these.
   - Paying by credit card gives you the best protections (FTC rental-scam guidance).
   - Get the terms, cancellation and refund policies before you pay or sign; if you can't, walk away.
5. BBB guidance (bbb.org/travel and BBB local alerts): fake agencies advertise steep package discounts, take payment upfront and disappear;
   pay with a credit card; search company name + "scam"/"complaint"; verify reservations by contacting the hotel, resort or airline directly.
6. Fair Credit Billing Act (FTC "Fair Credit Billing" page, 15 U.S.C. 1666): charges for goods or services "not delivered as agreed" are billing
   errors; write to the card issuer within 60 days after the first statement with the error was sent. Credit cards only, not debit.

## Chartable series (FTC Consumer Sentinel, Travel, Vacations & Timeshare Plans, total reported losses, $ millions)
2021: 95 (secondary citing FTC) | 2022: 104 | 2023: 122 | 2024: 274

## Worked example (our math)
- Growth 2023 -> 2024: 274 / 122 = 2.25, so more than double in one year (+$152M).
- Growth 2021 -> 2024: 274 / 95 = 2.88, nearly triple.
- Median travel-scam loss vs all fraud (2024): 922 / 497 = 1.86, nearly double the typical fraud loss.

## The surprise
Reported travel/vacation/timeshare scam losses more than doubled in a single year ($122M in 2023 to $274M in 2024), even though reports only rose
about 6% (55,063 to 58,347), meaning each scam is taking more money.
(Report growth: 58,347/55,063 = 1.06.)

## Uncertainties
- 2024 "% reporting $ loss" extracted as 67% vs 22% in 2023; unverified, NOT used.
- 2021 $95M figure is secondary only.
- Category includes timeshare scams; script says "travel scams" in the hook and names the full category on line 2.
