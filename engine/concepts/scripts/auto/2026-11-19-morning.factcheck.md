## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 0 (hook), quantifier.** "Almost every" did not match the minutes' "almost all". At 11 words it also ran about 3.7s, over the 3-second limit.
   - Old: "Almost every Fed official said more rate hikes are likely needed."
   - New text/say: "Almost all Fed officials expect more rate hikes." (8 words, about 2.7s; matches the minutes and the CNBC/Bloomberg framing)
2. **Line 3 chart, misleading 2026 point.** The 2026 point is the current rate, not a year-end value.
   - Title changed from "Fed rate (top end)" to "Fed top rate, year-end".
   - Added `chart_note {"x": 2026, "text": "Now"}`. The x-axis stays numeric years.
3. **Line 5, conditional dropped.** The minutes tie the bigger hike to unfavorable inflation data.
   - New text/say: "A couple of officials even floated a bigger December hike if inflation runs hot."
4. **Line 8, overgeneralization.** "It moves with the Fed" is not true for every card.
   - New text: "If you carry a balance, know your APR. Most card rates move with the Fed."
   - New say: "If you carry a balance, know your A P R. Most card rates move with the Fed."
5. **Line 7, word budget and narration.** Trimmed to keep the total spoken words within 115-140.
   - New text/say: "Rising rates make card debt and variable loans cost more, while savings and CDs often pay more."
6. **Post captions** updated to match the script:
   - ig: first line now "Almost all Fed officials expect more rate hikes, minutes show." The December line adds "if inflation runs hot".
   - tiktok: "Almost all Fed officials expect more rate hikes."
   - The title and yt_desc already said "almost all" and were left unchanged.
