## Timely angle (news hook)
- McDonald's Investor Day, September 23, 2026: the company committed about $8.5 billion in "partnering support" to franchisees through 2036, about $5 billion of it through 2030, "through a combination of rent relief and capital support" to fund Restaurant NEXT (new equipment, tech, the AI operating system "ArchIQ"). Estimated cost about $800,000 for a traditional U.S. drive-thru restaurant ($650,000-$700,000 in top international operated markets). Company estimate: about $100,000 more annual cash flow for the average U.S. restaurant, roughly four-year payback for franchisees. (McDonald's Investor Day press release / 8-K Ex. 99.1, Sept 23, 2026; Axios, CNBC, CP24/AP, Fortune Sept 23-24, 2026.)
- Angle: the landlord is cutting rent to get tenants to remodel. Shows the model's power and its dependence on franchisee health.
- Also: net unit growth target of about 4.5% in 2027 (reported as "over", "about" and "nearly" 4.5%; script says "about"), then 3-3.5% a year through 2030 (Investor Day). "More than 46,000 restaurants" (Fortune, Sept 24, 2026).

- Franchised margins = franchised revenues minus franchised occupancy costs (depreciation on owned property, McDonald's own lease costs). Company-operated margins = sales minus food & paper, payroll and occupancy & other (10-K definitions).
- Systemwide sales 2025: "over $139 billion", +7% (+5% constant currency). (Q4 2025 release; Franchise Times)
- Restaurants: 45,356 at Dec 31, 2025, about 95% franchised (10-K).
- Conventional franchise: franchisee pays initial fees plus continuing rent and royalties "based upon a percent of sales with minimum rent payments"; the company "generally owns or holds a long-term lease on the land and building", the franchisee funds equipment, signs, seating and decor; arrangement generally 20 years; at the end McDonald's "maintains control of the underlying real estate and building" and can re-franchise or close (10-K, wording via search excerpt).
- Real estate: "At December 31, 2025, the Company owned approximately 56% of the land and 80% of the buildings for restaurants in its consolidated markets." (10-K)
- Property and equipment at cost, Dec 31, 2025: land $8,169M; buildings and improvements on owned land $22,202M; buildings and improvements on leased land $15,506M; equipment, signs and seating $2,954M; other $459M; total $49,290M (sums correctly). Accumulated depreciation $21,049M; net $28,241M. NOTE: at historical cost, not market value.

## Derived math (exact)
- Rent share of total revenue 2025: 10,442 / 26,885 = 38.8% (about 39%).
- Rent share of franchised revenues: 10,442 / 16,548 = 63.1%; royalties 6,018 / 16,548 = 36.4%.
- Franchised revenues share of total revenue: 16,548 / 26,885 = 61.6%.
- Franchised margin rate: 13,930 / 16,548 = 84.2% (keeps about 84 cents per franchise-revenue dollar after occupancy costs).
- Company-operated margin rate: 1,422 / 9,690 = 14.7% (about 15 cents per dollar of food sold).
- Franchised share of restaurant margin dollars: 13,930 / (13,930 + 1,422) = 13,930 / 15,352 = 90.7% (company says "approximately 90%").
- THE SURPRISE: 2025 rent ($10,442M) was bigger than all sales at McDonald's own restaurants ($9,690M), by $752M.
- Rent growth 2019 to 2025: 10,442 / 7,500.2 = 1.392, about +39%.

## History
- March 2, 1955: Ray Kroc (a milkshake-mixer salesman) and the McDonald brothers reach agreement; McDonald's System incorporated. April 15, 1955: Kroc's first McDonald's opens in Des Plaines, Illinois. (McDonald's history page; TIME)
- 1956: Harry J. Sonneborn, formerly a finance executive at Tastee-Freez, joins Kroc (reportedly $100/week). Franchise Realty Corporation formed in 1956 to lease or buy restaurant sites and act as landlord to franchisees. (Wikipedia: Harry J. Sonneborn / History of McDonald's; UNH Rosenberg pioneers profile; Motley Fool 2016)
- How it worked (per John F. Love's company history as summarized by Motley Fool and others): find sites, sign long leases or mortgages, build the restaurant, sublease to the franchisee at a markup or a percentage of sales, whichever was greater. WEAK on exact numbers (20-40% markup, 5% of sales); script uses only "a markup or a percent of sales, whichever was greater".
- 228 restaurants by 1960 (UNH Rosenberg profile; TIME). The "31 by end of 1957" figure conflicts with other sources (TIME/TheStreet say about 40 by 1957), so it is not used.
- Kroc's original franchise terms: $950 franchise fee plus 1.9% of each restaurant's sales, of which 0.5% went to the McDonald brothers, so Kroc kept 1.4% (Wikipedia, History of McDonald's; Restaurant Finance Monitor, "Ray Kroc, Not the Founder, but a Financial Engineer"). Moderate: secondary but widely reported. Math: 1.4% x $0.15 = $0.0021 = about 0.2 cents (a fifth of a cent) per 15-cent hamburger.
- 1961: Kroc buys out the McDonald brothers for $2.7 million (UNH; widely reported).
- Sonneborn served as president/CEO about 1959-1967 (Wikipedia).
- Quote (WEAK: secondary sources only; attributed to Sonneborn speaking to Wall Street investors, as quoted in John F. Love, "McDonald's: Behind the Arches", 1986): "We are not technically in the food business. We are in the real estate business. The only reason we sell fifteen-cent hamburgers is because they are the greatest producer of revenue, from which our tenants can pay us our rent." Script shows only the first two sentences, attributed "as quoted in Love, Behind the Arches (1986)". Not to be confused with the 2016 film "The Founder", which dramatizes it.

## Fees (U.S.)
- Royalty ("service fee"): 5% of sales for new U.S. restaurants and restaurants bought from McDonald's since Jan 1, 2024, up from 4% (Restaurant Dive; Restaurant Business; McDonald's supplemental info).
- Rent: percent of sales with a minimum; varies by site. Press reports: roughly 8.5% to 15% (Bloomberg 2015 via search), "as high as 16%" (WSJ via search). WEAK; script uses 10% only as a labeled assumption.
- Rent and royalty are charged on sales, not profit (10-K "percent of sales"). Restaurant Business delivery example: on a $50 delivery order, the commission comes out but rent and royalty apply to the full $50.
- Average annual sales, U.S. traditional restaurants open at least one year, 2024: $4,002,000 (median $3,838,000) (2025 Franchise Disclosure Document, via FranchiseChatter / franchimp).

## Worked example (hypothetical, assumptions stated)
- Example restaurant: $4,000,000 annual sales (close to the 2024 U.S. average of $4,002,000).
- Royalty at 5% (rate for new U.S. restaurants since 2024): 0.05 x 4,000,000 = $200,000.
- Rent at an assumed 10% of sales (illustrative; actual rent varies by site): 0.10 x 4,000,000 = $400,000.
- Total to McDonald's: $600,000 = 15% of sales. Left for the franchisee: $3,400,000, before food, paper, wages, advertising, utilities, equipment and debt.
- If sales rise 10% to $4,400,000: rent + royalty = 15% x 4,400,000 = $660,000 (+$60,000), regardless of whether the franchisee's own profit rose.
