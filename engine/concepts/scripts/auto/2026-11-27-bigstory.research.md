## Facts (story: Thanksgiving Day 2026 online spending)
- US consumers spent $7.1 billion online on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday Nov 26, 2026, per Adobe Analytics data reported Friday Nov 27, 2026.
- Up 10.4% year over year.
- Adobe's pre-season forecast (Sept 28, 2026 release) for Thanksgiving was $6.9 billion, up 8.5%. Actual beat it by about $200 million (our math: 7.1 - 6.9).
- Mobile share: a record 63.2% of Thanksgiving online spending (Adobe had forecast 63%).
- Caveat: one outlet noted Adobe's own Thanksgiving release was not yet posted when it checked; figures rest on consistent coverage of Adobe data by several outlets (Seeking Alpha, TheFly/TipRanks, Anadolu Agency, others). A TipRanks headline said $7.3B but its body said $7.1B; treat $7.3B as an error.
- Coverage cited video games and electronics as top categories (not used in script, single-sourced).
- What's next (Adobe forecasts, Sept 2026): Black Friday $12.9 billion (+9.2%), Cyber Monday a record $15.1 billion (+6.2%), Thanksgiving-through-Cyber-Monday $47.5 billion (+7.4%). Full season Nov 1 - Dec 31 forecast $275.1 billion (+6.7%).

## Context
- Thanksgiving used to be a "stores closed, shop Friday" day; online it is now a multibillion-dollar sales day, and deals start weeks before Black Friday (Nov 1-22 was already $82.5B - covered Nov 26, not repeated).
- Market context (not the story): stocks rose in the shortened Black Friday session ending 1 PM ET; Kiplinger: Dow +1.2% to 50,774, S&P 500 +1.6% to 7,297, Nasdaq +2.3% to 24,129.

## What it means for a normal person's money
- Discounts and spending are starting earlier and happening on phones (63.2%), where checkout is one tap. A spending limit set before sales start is the simple guardrail (education, not advice).

## What to watch next
- Adobe's Black Friday actual vs $12.9B forecast (likely reported Saturday), Cyber Monday vs $15.1B.
