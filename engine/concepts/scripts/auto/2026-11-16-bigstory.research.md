## The story: New York factory orders barely grew in November, yet hiring hit its highest since June 2022
Source: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Empire State Manufacturing Survey, November 2026, released Monday Nov 16, 2026, 8:30 AM ET. Survey responses collected Nov 2-9, 2026. Covers manufacturers in New York State.

Key numbers (all diffusion indexes: % of firms reporting increase minus % reporting decrease; above zero = growth):
- General business conditions: 7.8 in November, down about 18 points from 25.5 in October (October was the highest since December 2021). Still positive = still growing, just slower.
- Forecast: economists expected ~17 (consensus varied: 16.7 Dow Jones per CNBC; 17.0-18.0 per other calendars). Missed badly.
- New orders: 3.0, down from 23.2 in October ("only a slight increase").
- Shipments: 8.8.
- Number of employees: 18.1, up from 10.3 - highest since June 2022. Share of firms cutting jobs fell to 3.4% from 9.8%.
- Capital expenditures (spending plans): 23.0, up nine points, highest since December 2022.
- Six-month future business conditions: 28.9, up eight points.
- Prices paid: 45.9; prices received: 27.2 (reported as slower price increases; October values not confirmed, so not used on screen).
- Next release: Dec 15, 2026.

## Context
- The index is a survey, not a count: it asks ~200 New York manufacturers whether things got better or worse. One month is noisy (it has swung between ~6 and ~26 since June).
- The split: demand right now (orders) cooled sharply, but firms are hiring and planning to spend, because they expect better business in six months (outlook 28.9).
- Fed backdrop: the Fed's next meeting is Dec 8-9, 2026; markets largely price a December rate hike (CME FedWatch ~85% per one report); 10-year Treasury ~5.13-5.15% intraday today (CNBC, Schwab). Fed Chair Kevin Warsh speaking today. Used only as "the Fed watches data like this" context.

## What it means for a normal person
- Manufacturing hiring at its strongest reading since mid-2022 in New York is a sign of steady factory paychecks, even as orders cool.
- One survey month is not a trend; the official jobs and factory data come later. Watch whether orders recover in the Dec 15 survey.

## What to watch
