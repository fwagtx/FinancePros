## What it means for a normal person
The 10-year yield is the base lenders price long loans from. When it rises, new mortgage, car-loan quotes tend to follow (not instantly, not one-for-one). Existing fixed-rate loans don't change. Savings yields on short-term accounts follow the Fed rate more than the 10-year.
