- L2 "more than $11 billion in 2025": Correct (Loud & Clear 2026, released March 11, 2026). Coverage says "$11B" and "more than $11 billion".
- L3 "roughly two-thirds of every music dollar to rights holders, split by share of streams": Correct, and it matches Spotify's own wording.

- L6 "since April 2024, 1,000 streams in 12 months" plus a unique-listener minimum: Correct. The rule took effect April 1, 2024 (MBW, Spotify support).

- L8 "13,800+ artists at $100K+ in 2025": The number is correct (Loud & Clear 2026). See issue 1 for the wording.
- The post copy matches the script. It includes a "not financial advice" note and labels the examples as illustrative.

## Issues
1. **L8 wording conflicts with the video's own point (fix needed).** "13,800 artists *made* at least $100,000" is not what Spotify reports. Loud & Clear counts royalties *generated* and paid to rights holders, not artists' take-home pay. L5 just showed that the artist may keep only 20%, so "made" contradicts the video. Fix: on screen, "In 2025, more than 13,800 artists generated at least $100,000 in Spotify royalties." In narration, "...generated at least a hundred thousand dollars in royalties on Spotify alone, before label and other splits." Change the stat label to "Artists generating $100K+, 2025".
2. **L7 chart skips 2022 (minor).** The points jump from 2021 to 2023. If the chart spaces points evenly, the gap misleads. Fix: add [2022, 8]. The 2022 payout was about $8B per Spotify's earlier Loud & Clear. Re-verify it, or drop the point and use years that are evenly spaced. The chart values are also shown as exact numbers when the sources say "$5B+" and "$7B+". That is acceptable.
3. **L4 simplification (minor).** Real streamshare is calculated per country and per month. A short "(simplified)" tag on the example would be more accurate. This is optional.
4. **L9 CTA:** "That's the money. Follow for more." This is fine.
