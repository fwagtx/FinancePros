## What it means for a normal person
- A weaker euro (stronger dollar) means each dollar converts into more euros: European trips cost fewer dollars; euro-priced imports can get cheaper.
- Flip side: stocks priced in euros convert into fewer dollars, so a fund holding European stocks loses value in dollar terms from the currency move alone (all else equal).
