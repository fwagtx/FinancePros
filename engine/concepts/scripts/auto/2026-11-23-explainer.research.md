# Research: Risk vs reward - the tall tree and the safe bush (2026-11-23-explainer)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- Damodaran (NYU Stern) annual returns 1928-2025 (Jan 2026 update, via financewonk / A Wealth of Common Sense): S&P 500 with dividends compounded about 10.0% a year; 3-month T-bills about 3.3% a year. $100 invested at the start of 1928 grew to about $1,157,600 in stocks vs about $2,578 in T-bills by end of 2025 (10-year Treasuries ~$7,750).
- 1928-2024: stocks 9.94%, bonds 4.50%, cash 3.31% (A Wealth of Common Sense, Jan 2025).
- Worst year: 1931, -43.8% (Damodaran -43.84%). 2008: about -37%. 1937: about -35%.
- Down years: 26 of 98 calendar years (1928-2025) were negative for the S&P 500; 72 up.
- S&P 500 total returns (with dividends): 2018 -4.38%; 2019 31.49%; 2020 18.40%; 2021 28.71%; 2022 -18.11%; 2023 26.29%; 2024 25.02%; 2025 17.88% (S&P Dow Jones Indices via Slickcharts; First Trust 2025 recap 17.9%).

## Worked example
$100 x 1.10^98 = ~$1.15M (consistent with $1,157,600). $100 x 1.0337^98 = ~$2,578. Ratio about 449x.

## Surprising fact
The "safe" choice turned $100 into about $2,600; the risky one into over $1 million, but it fell in about 1 of every 4 years along the way.
