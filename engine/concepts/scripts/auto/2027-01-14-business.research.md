# Research brief: Why Netflix's password-sharing crackdown made it money (2027-01-14-business)
Made Nov 15, 2026; posts Thu Jan 14, 2027, 2 PM CT. Evergreen: every number dated. Education only, never a stock tip or price prediction.

## Timely angle
No breaking news needed (evergreen). Latest official numbers: Q4 2025 letter (Jan 20, 2026) and Q3 2026 letter (Oct 2026, 2026 revenue guidance $51.1B). Netflix's Q4 2026 letter is expected around late January 2027, AFTER posting, so the script uses only 2025-and-earlier actuals plus dated guidance.

## The problem Netflix named (its own letters)
- Netflix's shareholder letters (Q4 2022 / early 2023) estimated its service was shared with "100M+" (over 100 million) additional households, including over 30M in UCAN (US and Canada), and said widespread sharing undermines its ability to invest in the service. (Netflix letter, quoted by TechRadar, BGR, CBS News.)

## The crackdown ("paid sharing")
- May 23, 2023: Netflix began emailing US members who shared outside their household. Options: transfer a profile to a new membership the other person pays for, or "buy an extra member" for $7.99/month (US). Extra member = one profile, own login, paid by the account owner, same country. (TechCrunch, CNBC, AppleInsider, 9to5Mac, all May 23, 2023.)
- Q2 2023 letter (July 19, 2023, SEC 8-K Ex. 99.1): paid sharing launched in 100+ countries representing more than 80% of revenue base; Netflix said "revenue in each region is now higher than pre-launch," with sign-ups already exceeding cancellations. Q2 2023 paid net adds 5.89M; 238.39M paid memberships.
- US extra-member price history: $7.99 (May 2023) -> $8.99 ad-free (by Jan 2025 price increase; $6.99 with-ads option added) -> $9.99 ad-free (US price increase announced Mar 26, 2026; new members from Mar 26, existing members over following months). With-ads extra member: sources conflict ($6.99 CNBC/TechCrunch vs $7.99 Netflix help page/Reuters/GMA) -> NOT used on screen. (TechCrunch Mar 26, 2026; CNBC; Netflix Help Center "Plans and Pricing".)
- Note: Netflix's paid-membership counts EXCLUDE Extra Member accounts (Q4 2024 letter: "over 300M paid memberships (which excludes Extra Member accounts)").

## Causation caveat (must respect)
Netflix itself credited paid sharing for higher regional revenue after launch (Q2 2023 letter) and for membership growth, but revenue growth since 2023 also reflects price increases and the ad tier (Netflix's Q3'26 letter cites "growth in memberships, pricing, and ad revenue"). Script must not claim the crackdown alone caused all the growth.

## Worked example (our math)
One US extra member at the May 2023 price: $7.99 x 12 = $95.88 a year.
At the 2026 ad-free price: $9.99 x 12 = $119.88 a year.
Membership jump: 301.63M - 230.75M = 70.88M added in 2023-2024 combined (about 71 million).
Revenue jump: $45.2B - $31.6B = $13.6B more annual revenue from 2022 to 2025 (+43%).

## The surprising fact
People expected the crackdown to backfire (cancellations). Instead, 2023 was Netflix's biggest year of paid net adds at the time: 29.5M, more than triple 2022's about 8.9M. The trick was not new viewers: it was turning existing borrowers into payers.
