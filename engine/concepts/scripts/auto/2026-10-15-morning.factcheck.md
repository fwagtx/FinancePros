## Other checks

- **Labeling of the comparison (required fix).** The 0.51% is the average rate BofA paid across all Consumer Banking deposits, including checking accounts that pay zero or near zero. It is not a savings APY offer. "Pays 0.51% on everyday deposits" reads like an offered rate, and the compare card's "BofA pays / consumer deposits" does not say it is an average. Fixes below. A further nuance: 0.51% is a July-September average, and most of that quarter came before the Sept 16 hike. The compare card should therefore say the Fed range is the current one ("now"), not a Sept 2026 figure set against the Q3 average.
- **Tense.** It is a past-quarter average, so use "paid", not "pays".
- **Opening.** The hook is true, specific and under 3 seconds once it is relabeled. It stays strong.
- **Advice and predictions.** The script has no "move your money" line and no rate predictions. L6's list title "Check your rate" is an instruction-style takeaway, and the rules forbid those. Reword it neutrally.
- **Spoken vs. on-screen numbers.** They match on every line: 0.51, 16.4B, 8%, 3.75%, 949B, 0.61, 0.48. The timeline also shows 0.58% (Q2'25), which is not spoken. That is acceptable because it is chart data and it is confirmed.
- **Compare values are 7 characters or fewer** ("3.75%", "0.51%"): OK.
- **Political opinion:** none. **Stale news:** none, since the results came out Oct 14. **Time words:** "On October fourteenth" and "September hike" are both correct for Oct 15.
- **Industry generalization:** none. The script talks only about BofA.
- **keep:true:** none present.
- **TikTok wording.** "made $16.4B in net interest" suggests profit. Say "net interest income".
