# Fact-check: 2026-10-21-explainer ("Inflation's Hidden Rent")
Checked Oct 1, 2026 for posting Oct 21, 2026. The September 2026 CPI comes out Oct 14, before posting.

## Verified claims
- **Hook / L1-2, cover "1/4":** Owners' equivalent rent (OER) is about a quarter of CPI. BLS December 2025 relative importance: OER of residences is 26.204 and OER of primary residence is 25.230. The BLS factsheet says OER is "nearly one quarter" of the CPI weight. The claim is accurate.
- **L3:** About 80,000 prices a month in 75 urban areas. The BLS CPI technical note and overview confirm this.
- **L4:** Shelter is about a third. BLS December 2025 relative importance for shelter is 35.625. The claim is accurate, though slightly understated (see issue 1).
- **L5 quote:** It matches the BLS rent/OER factsheet word for word: "If someone were to rent your home today, how much do you think it would rent for monthly, unfurnished and without utilities?" The source label (Consumer Expenditure Survey) is correct. "That sets the weight. Real rents set the price changes" matches the BLS explanation that price changes come from the rent sample in the Housing Survey.
- **L6 and post:** CPI-U NSA for August 2026 is 334.980 and for August 2025 is 323.976. Both come from the BLS releases for August 2026 (USDL-26-1496) and August 2025 (USDL-25-1356). The BLS reports a 12-month change of 3.4%. Recomputed: (334.980-323.976)/323.976 = 3.397%, which rounds to 3.4%.
- **L7:** $100 x 1.03397 = $103.397, which rounds to $103.40. The example is labeled as one.
- **L8-9 chart (annual averages):** Recomputed from the BLS annual average indexes (2019 255.657, 2020 258.811, 2021 270.970, 2022 292.655, 2023 304.702, 2024 313.689, 2025 321.943). This gives 1.23, 4.70, 8.00, 4.12, 2.95 and 2.63. The chart's 1.2, 4.7, 8.0, 4.1, 2.9 and 2.6 match the published BLS rounding. 2024's 2.95 is published by BLS as 2.9.

## Issues (none blocking)
1. **L4, minor wording:** Shelter is 35.6%, so "about a third" slightly understates it. Fix: "Shelter alone is over a third." Change both the text and the board line ("Shelter: over 1/3").
2. **Staleness after Oct 14, minor:** By Oct 21 the September 2026 CPI will be the latest figure. August 2026 at 3.4% will not be the newest number. The script never calls it "latest" or "current", and it uses dated past tense, so it is not wrong. To avoid viewers mistaking it for today's rate, consider saying "In the August report, August 2025 to August 2026 gave 3.4%." Another option is to swap in the September 2026 figures after Oct 14. In that case update the board, the money scene ($103.40) and the IG/YT index values together. No other time words ("now", "this month", "latest") appear.
3. **Post IG, minor:** "75 US cities" should say "75 urban areas", to match the BLS wording and L3's sub.
4. **L7, nit:** The on-screen text starts "Example:" but the narration starts "So". The values match. Optionally add "For example" to the narration so it is clearly labeled as a hypothetical.

## Other checks
- **Opening:** It is strong, true and specific, and takes under 3 seconds to say.
- **Cover payoff:** The cover title is paid off by L1-5.
- **Advice and predictions:** There is no advice and no price prediction. "Yours can run hotter or cooler" is descriptive.
- **Politics:** There are no political opinions.
- **On-screen vs spoken:** Every on-screen value matches the spoken one.
- **Math:** All the math is correct.

VERDICT: PASS
