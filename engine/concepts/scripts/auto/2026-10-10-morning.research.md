## Facts (Moderna)
- Nasdaq announced Oct 1, 2026 that Moderna (MRNA) would join the Nasdaq-100, replacing Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD), prior to market open Friday Oct 9, 2026. WBD left because its merger with Paramount Skydance was closing (expected Oct 6). (Nasdaq IR release; Fortuneo/Option Finance Oct 2)
- MRNA closed Friday Oct 9, 2026 at $225.00, up $28.00 or 14.21% from $197.00 (Oct 8). New 52-week high. (Yahoo Finance quote, StockAnalysis, Tokenpost)
- Year-to-date: 24/7 Wall St (Oct 6) said MRNA up 545% YTD at $190.27 -> implies 2025 year-end close of about $29.50. Late-Dec 2025 closes ~$30-31 (Motley Fool AU: $30.40, ~Dec 30). At $225 that is roughly +660% YTD (Yahoo Finance cited 662%; another Yahoo piece 627%). Safe phrasing: "more than 600 percent in 2026", "about $30 at the end of 2025".
- History: Nasdaq announced Dec 13, 2024 that Moderna, Illumina and Super Micro Computer would leave the Nasdaq-100 in the annual reconstitution, effective before open Dec 23, 2024 (replaced by Palantir, MicroStrategy, Axon). Moderna stock was down more than 60% in 2024 at that time (Benzinga). Annual reconstitution drops some of the smallest members and adds large Nasdaq companies not in the index.
- Why the stock ran: Merck/Moderna personalized cancer vaccine intismeran autogene (with Keytruda). Jan 2026: 5-year Phase 2b data showed 49% reduction in risk of recurrence or death in high-risk melanoma (HR 0.510). Aug 2026: Phase 3 INTerpath-001 met its primary endpoint (recurrence-free survival); Moderna shares rose nearly 150% intraday Aug 19 (per Swissquote/Morningstar coverage). Vaccine not yet approved.
- Valuation caution: Citigroup downgraded MRNA to Sell around the inclusion (GuruFocus, Digital Today) - secondary sources, not used in script.

## What it means for a normal person
- Index funds (e.g., funds tracking the Nasdaq-100) follow written rules: shrinking stocks can be dropped, big stocks get added when a slot opens. Funds that track the index were not holding Moderna during most of its 2026 run and add it after it became big again. Trade-off: rules avoid stock-picking guesses, but means buying in after a rise and selling after a drop. No prediction about the stock.

## What to watch
