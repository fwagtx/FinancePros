# Research brief: Why is a plumber so expensive? (posts Oct 18, 2026)

## What customers pay (Angi / HomeAdvisor cost guides, 2025-2026)
- Hourly rates range from about $45 to $200. Master plumbers average about $120 an hour (range $80-$200).
- Service-call or trip fees run about $50-$300. The average plumbing job is about $341 (range $182-$501).
- Emergency plumbers average about $170 an hour.

## What plumbers earn (BLS OEWS / Occupational Outlook Handbook)
- Median wage for plumbers, pipefitters and steamfitters, May 2024: $30.27 an hour ($62,970 a year).
- Chartable series, median hourly wage (May of each year): 2019 $26.52 | 2020 $27.08 | 2021 $28.79 | 2022 $28.89 | 2023 $29.59 | 2024 $30.27.
- Jobs in 2024: 504,500. Projected growth of 4% for 2024-2034. About 44,000 openings a year.
- Training is typically a 4-5 year apprenticeship, and most states require a license (BLS OOH).

## Employer benefit costs (BLS Employer Costs for Employee Compensation, Dec 2025, private industry)
- Total compensation is $46.15 an hour: wages $32.36 (70.1%) and benefits $13.79 (29.9%).

## Worked example (illustrative)
- Wage $30.27 = 70% of total pay cost, so total = 30.27 / 0.70 = $43.24 an hour.
- If 6 of 8 paid hours are billable (the rest goes to driving, estimates and parts runs): 43.24 x 8/6 = $57.65 of labor cost per billed hour.
- At $120 billed: $120 - $57.65 = about $62 an hour left for the truck, fuel, tools, parts stock, insurance, licenses, office and profit.
