# Research brief: Why franchise owners pay the brand a cut of every sale (How They Make Money, posts Dec 3, 2026)

Angle: McDonald's (latest annual report = FY2025 Form 10-K, filed Feb 2026). No breaking news angle; evergreen.
Do NOT generalize McDonald's terms to all franchises.

## Key facts (with dates)
- About 95% of McDonald's restaurants worldwide were franchised at Dec 31, 2025. (MCD FY2025 10-K)
- Conventional franchise arrangements: a lease and a license; franchisee pays an initial fee plus continuing rent and royalties "based upon a percent of sales with minimum rent payments". Term generally 20 years; McDonald's keeps control of the real estate. (MCD FY2025 10-K)
- FY2025 revenues from franchised restaurants: $16,548M = rents $10,442M + royalties $6,018M + initial fees $88M. (MCD FY2025 10-K; confirmed by Q4/FY2025 release)
  - Surprise: McDonald's collects more from rent ($10.4B) than royalties ($6.0B). Rent is also more than its company-operated restaurant sales ($9,690M in 2025).
- Total revenues 2025: $26,885M (2024: $25,920M). (Q4/FY2025 earnings release, Feb 11, 2026)
- US royalty ("service fee"): historically 4% of gross sales; raised to 5% effective Jan 1, 2024 for new US restaurants (new franchisees, buyouts of company-owned stores, relocations); existing restaurants/transfers stay at 4%. First increase in ~30 years. (McDonald's "Supplemental Information on Franchisor Updates in McDonald's U.S. and Canada" Dec 2023; Restaurant Dive; Nation's Restaurant News; Entrepreneur)
- Initial franchise fee: $45,000 for a traditional US restaurant, 20-year term. (McDonald's USA FDD via franchise databases: FDDExchange, Franchise Direct, Biz2Credit)
- Advertising & promotion: not less than 4% of gross sales (local co-ops + national fund OPNAD). (McDonald's USA FDD, NASAA EFD; franchisechatter)
- Rent: base + percentage rent, varies by location; FDD databases report roughly 6%-23% of gross sales (variable; not used on screen as a single number).
- Training: Hamburger University and a 12-18 month pre-opening program. (FDD summaries)
- What the royalty buys (general): the brand/trademark license, the operating system (recipes, procedures, supply chain), training and support. Ad fund is a separate charge.
- Estimated initial investment, traditional McDonald's: $1,471,000-$2,728,000 (FDD summaries; not used on screen).

## FTC Franchise Rule
- 16 CFR Part 436: franchisor must give a prospective franchisee its Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) at least 14 calendar days before the buyer signs a binding agreement or pays any money. FDD has 23 items incl. Item 5 initial fees and Item 6 other fees. (FTC Franchise Rule Compliance Guide, 2008; eCFR)

## Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL)
Store with $3,000,000 annual sales, 5% royalty + 4% ad contribution:
$3,000,000 x 5% = $150,000; $3,000,000 x 4% = $120,000; total = $270,000 a year, before rent.

## Surprise
The biggest piece of McDonald's franchise income isn't the royalty; it's rent: $10.4B of $16.5B in 2025.
