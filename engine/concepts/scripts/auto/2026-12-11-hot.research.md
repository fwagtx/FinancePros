# 2026-12-11-hot research brief: Money Myth: "Money in a credit union isn't insured"

Posts Friday, December 11, 2026 (evergreen). Researched Monday, October 12, 2026. No timely news hook needed; myth/fact format.
Note: ncua.gov and mycreditunion.gov pages could not be fetched directly from this environment (blocked); figures were confirmed via search results quoting NCUA documents and cross-checked against secondary coverage.

## Core fact (the myth buster)
- Deposits ("shares") at **federally insured credit unions** are insured by the **National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund (NCUSIF)**, run by the **NCUA** (National Credit Union Administration). Created by Congress in **1970**. (NCUA, Share Insurance Fund page)
- Standard maximum: **$250,000 per member, per ownership category, per federally insured credit union**. (NCUA, Share Insurance Coverage)
- NCUSIF is **backed by the full faith and credit of the United States**. (NCUA, Share Insurance Fund page)
- Same $250,000 standard limit as FDIC insurance at banks ($250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category).
- Funded by participating credit unions (Wikipedia, secondary; not used on screen).

## Exact NCUA wording (quote)
- NCUA press release, March 2020 ("Deposits are safe in federally insured credit unions"): **"Credit union members have never lost a penny of insured savings at a federally insured credit union."**
  - Note: this is a historical record, not a guarantee of future outcomes.

## Latest system data (NCUA Quarterly Data Summary, 2026 Q2; press release Sept 14, 2026)
- As of **June 30, 2026**: **4,214 federally insured credit unions** with **146.1 million members**.
- Added 2.3 million members over the year; total assets **$2.50 trillion** (+5.0% y/y); loans $1.76 trillion; delinquency 96 bp.
- Q1 2026 (as of March 31, 2026): 4,250 FICUs, 145.8 million members.

## NCUSIF size (context only, not used on screen)
- Q4 2025 NCUSIF board presentation: equity ratio **1.30%** (normal operating level 1.33%); total assets ~$24.1 billion; insured shares ~$1.863 trillion. Q2 2026 equity ratio projected 1.27% (projection, not actual).

## Ownership categories: worked example (hypothetical)
- NCUA: individual accounts insured up to $250,000; a member's interest in all joint accounts combined insured up to $250,000 (per co-owner); retirement accounts (IRAs) insured separately up to $250,000.
- Example (one federally insured credit union, two members who co-own a joint account):
  - Member A individual account: up to $250,000
  - Joint account, A + B: 2 owners x $250,000 = **$500,000**
  - Total for A's individual + the joint account = $250,000 + $500,000 = **$750,000** covered (each category separate).

## Exceptions / what's NOT covered
- **Privately insured credit unions**: a small number of state-chartered credit unions use private share insurance instead of NCUSIF. The only remaining private insurer is **American Share Insurance (ASI)**, Ohio-based, founded 1974.
  - Count: **106 state-chartered credit unions in 10 states** (ASI CEO comment letter to the Federal Reserve, July 2021). A 2023 press release (InvesTex CU conversion) says ASI covers 1.3 million+ members and $22 billion in shares in 10 states. No 2026 count found (WEAKLY DATED).
  - ASI coverage is private, not backed by the full faith and credit of the US.
- **Not insured by NCUSIF** (NCUA share insurance FAQ): stocks, bonds, mutual funds, life insurance, annuities, municipal securities, even if sold at a credit union.
- **Crypto / digital assets**: NCUA's financial technology and digital assets page says the Share Insurance Fund does not cover digital assets or cryptocurrencies, including those held through third-party vendors or in custody at a credit union.

## The surprise
- Credit union deposits carry the same $250,000 federal, full-faith-and-credit protection as bank deposits, and NCUA says members "have never lost a penny of insured savings" at a federally insured credit union. Fewer credit unions (down ~30% since 2015) but more members.
