## Other checks
- **Opening (line 1): true but weak-ish.** "Grocery coffee averages $9.30 a pound. So why?" is specific, verified, and under 3 seconds to say. But "So why?" doesn't create urgency, because a price alone has no comparison. Stronger version: "Your coffee costs almost fifty percent more than two years ago. Here's why." Alternative: "Grocery coffee hit nine thirty a pound, up nearly half in two years."
- **Awkward narration, line 1 `say`:** "nine dollars thirty a pound" reads unnaturally aloud. Use "nine-thirty a pound" or "nine dollars and thirty cents a pound".
- **Spoken vs on-screen:** all spoken values match the on-screen ones (line 8 is "six ninety-seven" and $6.97). The only inconsistency is $9.299 on the line-8 board against $9.30 everywhere else.
- **Time words on Oct 7:** "in two years", "that November" and "the August average" are all correct. Nothing reads "this week" or "just". OK.
- **Stale news:** none. The tariff is framed as history, and August 2026 is the latest BLS month.
- **Advice or price predictions:** none. The post carries a "not financial advice" note.
- **Cover/title payoff:** "Why Is Coffee So Expensive?" and "Why Grocery Coffee Costs $9.30 a Pound" are both paid off.
- **Post text:** all figures ($9.30, $6.31, 47%, $9.72, >99%, 35%, Nov 2025) are verified.
