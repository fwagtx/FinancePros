## Other checks
- **Opening (line 1): weak-ish.** "Grocery coffee averages $9.30 a pound. So why?" is specific and true (if the BLS figure holds) and takes under 3 seconds to say. But "So why?" doesn't create a need to know, because $9.30 on its own isn't shocking without a comparison. Stronger version: "Your coffee costs almost fifty percent more than two years ago. Here's why." Or: "Grocery coffee just hit nine thirty a pound, up almost half in two years." Both put the jump into the hook.
- **Awkward narration, line 1 `say`:** "nine dollars thirty a pound" reads unnaturally. Use "nine dollars and thirty cents a pound" or "nine-thirty a pound".
- **Spoken vs on-screen:** line 8 `say` has "the August average" while `text` has "August 2026". This is acceptable. Line 8's board uses $9.299 while the rest of the video uses $9.30 (see the math mismatch above). All other spoken values match the on-screen ones.
- **Time words on Oct 7, 2026:** "in two years" (Aug 2024 to Aug 2026) is OK. "That November" = Nov 2025 is OK. There are no "this week", "yesterday" or "just" phrasings. OK.
- **Stale news:** the tariff story is about a year old but is framed as history, which is fine. August 2026 is the latest BLS month available on the posting date, which is OK.
- **Advice or price predictions:** none. The post includes "not financial advice". OK.
- **Cover/title payoff:** "Why Is Coffee So Expensive?" and "Why Grocery Coffee Costs $9.30 a Pound" are both paid off by lines 3–9. OK.
