## Chosen: More Americans quit their jobs in September (JOLTS)
Source: BLS "Job Openings and Labor Turnover - September 2026", USDL-26-1630, released 10:00 a.m. ET Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Data are seasonally adjusted; September is preliminary.

Facts (all from BLS release / BLS API series):
- Job openings: 7.5 million (7,523,000) in September 2026; rate 4.5%. BLS calls it "little changed". August revised up 111,000 to 7.2 million (7,190,000). Change: +333,000.
- Hires: 5.4 million, rate 3.4%, changed little. Hires +158,000 in accommodation and food services.
- Total separations: 5.3 million, rate 3.3%.
- Quits: increased to 3.4 million (3,365,000) from 3,044,000 in August (revised) = +321,000. Quits rate 2.1% (BLS: "little changed"), August 1.9%.
- Largest one-month quits increase since May 2023 (May 2023: 3,999K vs April 3,617K = +382K; no monthly gain since then was >=321K; checked against BLS series JTS000000000000000QUL). Also reported by The Tribune/Reuters-style wire and investinglive.
- Quits in accommodation and food services: +197,000 (of the +321,000 total). Arts, entertainment and recreation quits -19,000.
- Over the year, total nonfarm quits +375,000 (Sept 2025: 2,990K).
- Layoffs and discharges: 1.6 million, rate 1.0%, little changed. Health care and social assistance layoffs -70,000.
- BLS: "the quits rate can serve as a measure of workers' willingness or ability to leave jobs."
- Next JOLTS (October 2026) release: Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 10:00 a.m. ET.
- Economists' estimate for openings ~7.1M (investinglive: 7.523M vs 7.100M estimate); topped every estimate in Bloomberg survey (per press).
- Indeed Hiring Lab headline: "September 2026 JOLTS Report: Eerily Quiet" - quits bump "may signal a turnaround, but the signal is faint."

## Context / what it means
- Quits are a confidence signal: people quit when they think they can find something else. After two years near 1.9-2.0%, the rate ticked up to 2.1%.
- But the jump is concentrated: 197K of 321K extra quits were in restaurants and hotels, a high-turnover sector. One month of preliminary data; BLS calls the rate change "little changed".
- Still far below the September 2021 rate of 2.9%.
- Normal person: a slightly more fluid job market; one month is not a trend.
