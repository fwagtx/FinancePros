# Research brief: How Duolingo makes money from a free app (2026-11-19-business)

Researched Oct 2, 2026. Posts Nov 19, 2026. Duolingo's Q3 2026 results are due around early Nov 2026; this brief uses the FY2025 Form 10-K (year ended Dec 31, 2025) and the Q2 2026 shareholder letter / 10-Q (quarter ended June 30, 2026, released Aug 5, 2026). Quarterly figures are labeled "as of June 2026" / "Q2 2026" so they stay accurate on the posting date.

## Timely angle
No single news event to hook on (Q3 2026 results may come out before posting; do not reference them). Evergreen angle: a "free" app crossed $1 billion in annual revenue in 2025.

## Key facts (with dates and sources)

### Full year 2025 (Form 10-K, FY ended Dec 31, 2025)
- Total revenue: $1,037.6M, up 39% from 2024. [10-K FY2025; Q4/FY2025 shareholder letter]
- Revenue by type, FY2025 [10-K FY2025]:
  - Subscription: $873.4M (up $265.9M, +44%) = 84.2% of revenue (873.4 / 1,037.6)
  - Advertising: $79.7M = 7.7%
  - Duolingo English Test: $42.0M = 4.0%
  - In-app purchases: $40.5M = 3.9%
  - Other (remainder): about $2.0M
  - Check: 873.4 + 79.7 + 42.0 + 40.5 = 1,035.6; 1,037.6 - 1,035.6 = 2.0
  - Note: one secondary summary said subscriptions "83.5%"; we use the dollar figures from the 10-K (84.2%, say "about 84 percent").
- Bookings FY2025: $1,158.4M; net income FY2025: $414.1M; adjusted EBITDA $305.9M (company non-GAAP). [Q4/FY2025 letter]
- Gross margin FY2025: 72.2% (vs 72.8% in 2024), lower partly due to higher AI costs. [10-K summary via StockTitan]
- Q4 2025: DAUs 52.7M (+30%), paid subscribers 12.2M (+28%), revenue $282.9M. [Q4/FY2025 letter]
- Over 130M MAUs as of Dec 31, 2025. [10-K summary]

### Q2 2026 (shareholder letter, Aug 5, 2026; 10-Q for quarter ended June 30, 2026)
- Revenue: $298.5M, +18% YoY.
- Total bookings: $289.1M, +8% YoY.
- DAUs: 58.7M, +23% YoY.
- MAUs: 140.6M, +10% YoY.
- Paid subscribers (period end, June 30, 2026): 12.7M, +17% YoY.
- Paid conversion (our calculation, not a company-stated figure): 12.7M / 140.6M MAUs = 9.03%, about 9%. So roughly 91% of monthly users pay nothing. Versus DAUs: 12.7 / 58.7 = 21.6%.
- Gross margin Q2 2026: 72.6%.
- Video Call (a Max feature) was being extended to regular Super subscribers, per coverage of Q2 2026 (Class Central).

### Business model (how the free app pays)
- Free tier: full courses free, supported by ads; the free tier also limits mistakes/energy, nudging upgrades.
- Super: paid subscription, removes ads and limits. Family plans available.
- Duolingo Max: pricier AI tier (Explain My Answer, Roleplay, Video Call). Secondary sources (Class Central, citing CEO remarks) say Max was about 9% of paid subscribers in Q2 2025, up from about 5% at end-2024. Not in an official filing table we could verify: describe Max qualitatively only.
- In-app purchases: gems and similar items ($40.5M FY2025).
- Duolingo English Test: online English proficiency test accepted by universities ($42.0M FY2025).

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
Freemium math using Duolingo's ~9% paid share (Q2 2026) and an assumed $60 per year price (assumption only, not Duolingo's list price):
- 1,000,000 users x 9% = 90,000 paying users
- 90,000 x $60/yr = $5,400,000 a year
- 910,000 users pay $0 (they may still see ads)

## The surprising fact
About 9 in 10 monthly users pay nothing (as of June 2026), yet subscriptions were about 84% of 2025 revenue. A small paying minority funds the free app; ads are under 8%.
