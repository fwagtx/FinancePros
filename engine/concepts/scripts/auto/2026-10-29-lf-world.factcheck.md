## Issues

1. **HOOK (L1, cover, post title/ig/tiktok): the "40%" comes from Saudi Arabia's self-reported figure, and "allowed" implies a choice.** OPEC's secondary sources put Saudi August output at 7.276M b/d. That is 3.14M, or 30.1%, under the target, not 40%. Saudi Arabia also told OPEC that it *supplied* 7.1M b/d to the market, drawing on storage. The shortfall was forced by war damage to export routes, not chosen. "Pumped 40% less than OPEC+ allowed" states a disputed self-reported number as plain fact, and the "allowed" framing reads as a policy choice. Fixes:
   - L1 text/say: **"Saudi Arabia says it pumped 40% less oil than its OPEC+ quota."** As an alternative, use "War just pushed Saudi oil output 40% below its OPEC+ quota, by its own count." Avoid "just" if the line is reused later, because the post date is Oct 29.
   - L2: add "Outside estimates used by OPEC put it at 7.3 million, still 30% short." Optionally add a third compare value, or a sub reading "Saudi-reported; others: 7.28M" (27 characters).
   - L37: "That's where our forty percent comes from, using Saudi Arabia's own figure."
   - L90: "...ended up 40% under its own target, by its own count..."
   - Cover/post: change "pumped 40% under its quota" to "reported pumping 40% under its quota". Change yt_desc "reported pumping" to keep it as is. It already says "reported".
2. **L4: the cause is incomplete and the war wording is vague.** "After fighting with Iran began" does not say who fought. The war began Feb 28, 2026 with US and Israeli strikes on Iran. Saudi Arabia's August drop was also driven by Houthi and Iranian attacks around the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb and on Yanbu, because Saudi Arabia had rerouted exports to Yanbu. Neutral fix: "The cause was war. After US and Israeli strikes on Iran began in late February 2026, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz was badly disrupted, and later attacks hit Red Sea export routes too." Board: "Hormuz + Red Sea / routes disrupted / from March 2026".
3. **L14: "third-biggest producer" is shaky.** The EIA says the UAE held the third-largest *production capacity* in OPEC, behind Saudi Arabia and Iraq. On production, sources differ on the UAE versus Iran. Fix: "The UAE had OPEC's third-largest production capacity, after Saudi Arabia and Iraq, and pumped about 3.4 million barrels a day in 2025." Sub: "No. 3 capacity in OPEC (EIA)" (28 characters).
4. **L13/L16/L18: the UAE left OPEC+ as well as OPEC.** L18 implies this but L13 does not say it. Fix L13: "The United Arab Emirates left OPEC and OPEC+ on May 1, 2026."
5. **L49: ">10M in 1980" is overstated.** EIA and country-study data put Saudi crude output at about 9.9M b/d in 1980, "almost 10 million". Some secondary sources say "more than 10". Fix: "from about 10 million barrels a day in 1980"; left value "~10M".
6. **Time sensitivity (posting Oct 29).** By then OPEC will have published September output, around mid-October. Saudi exports rebounded in September (CNBC, Sept 25), and the Oct 4 meeting will have set November targets. The script already dates everything to August and October, which is good. Make sure the hook always carries "August": the L1 caption has no date and only the scene label shows "Aug 2026". Suggestion: "In August, Saudi Arabia says it pumped..." L34 is fine because it is past tense and an October target.
7. **L77: on-screen and spoken values differ.** The screen shows "100.1M" while the narration says "about one hundred million". Say "one hundred point one million", or show "~100M".
8. **L59 (minor):** Brent's January 2016 low was $26.01 on EIA spot data and about $27.88 for ICE front-month. "About $27" sits between the two. Say "below thirty dollars, about twenty-six" with EIA spot, or keep the line and cite the futures settle.
9. **L68 (minor):** Since 2023, Dated Brent also includes US WTI Midland cargoes. Say "Brent is a benchmark built mostly on North Sea crudes" rather than "a North Sea blend".
10. **L24 (minor):** "Secondary sources" are not all firms. They include agencies such as the EIA and IEA alongside Argus and Platts. Say "outside sources, like energy agencies and price reporters".
11. **L25 (minor, instruction-style):** The board line "Check the date." is mildly imperative. Use "Every figure is dated."
