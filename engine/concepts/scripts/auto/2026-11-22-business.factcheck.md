The hook is true, but it takes about 12 spoken words (about 4-4.5 s) to reach the number. It also opens with a dated frame ("In fiscal twenty twenty-two") instead of the shock.
Stronger: lead with the loss and leave the year for line 2. This is 10 words, about 3 s.
- text: `Disney streaming lost $3.4 billion in one year.`

### 5. Segment definition changed in FY2026 (L8, IG caption, YT description)
Starting in FY2026, Disney's Direct-to-Consumer segment **excludes Hulu Live TV**, which moved to Linear Networks. The $712M and the restated $329M are on that new basis. The Q3 FY2025 figure originally reported was $346M, on the old basis that the chart uses. The comparison in L8 is like-for-like and correct. However, the captions present it right after the FY22-FY25 series as if it continued the same series.
- L8 scene `sub`: `vs $329M restated; excl. Hulu Live` (if the fallback is kept)
- IG caption line, replace `Q3 fiscal 2026 streaming operating income: $712M, vs $329M a year earlier.` with `Q3 fiscal 2026 DTC operating income (new basis, excl. Hulu Live TV): $712M, vs $329M restated a year earlier.` Update this again if L8 is refreshed.
- YT description, replace `and streaming operating income hit $712 million in Q3 fiscal 2026.` with `and streaming operating income hit $712 million in Q3 fiscal 2026 (new segment basis excluding Hulu Live TV).`
