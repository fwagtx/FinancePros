# Research brief: Why Spotify is betting on audiobooks and video podcasts
Slot 2026-12-04-business (posts Fri Dec 4, 2026). Researched Mon Oct 5, 2026. Spotify files 6-K shareholder decks with the SEC (foreign private issuer); fiscal year = calendar year.
Latest quarter available: Q2 2026 (reported Aug 4, 2026). Q3 2026 not yet reported as of Oct 5, 2026 (will likely be out by posting date; video avoids "latest quarter" wording and names the quarter).

## The economics (core angle)
- Music: Spotify says rightsholders (labels and publishers) receive roughly two-thirds of every dollar Spotify generates from music (Loud & Clear FAQ; Forbes Jan 28, 2026; CBC).
- Spotify says it paid more than $11 billion to the music industry in 2025 (Loud & Clear 2026, Forbes Jan 28 2026, Hollywood Reporter, Hypebot).
- Podcasts/audiobooks do NOT carry per-stream label royalties, but they are not free: Spotify's Q4 2025 deck says Premium margin gains were "largely offset by video podcast costs"; Q2 2026 deck cites "audiobooks costs and video podcast costs" in Premium gross margin. Costs are under Spotify's own deals (e.g. Partner Program payouts, audiobook licensing).

## Key numbers (official 6-K shareholder decks)
- FY2025 (Q4 2025 deck, Feb 10, 2026): revenue EUR 17,186M; gross margin 32.0% (2024: 30.1%); operating income EUR 2,198M (2024: EUR 1,365M). Q4 2025: 751M MAUs, 290M Premium subs.
- Q2 2026 (deck Aug 4, 2026): revenue EUR 4,777M; gross margin 33.4% (all-time record); operating income EUR 655M; 777M MAUs; 300M Premium subscribers. Premium gross margin 34.9%, ad-supported 19.1%.
- Q2 2026 deck: Audiobooks+ add-on tiers with higher-hour plans; Audiobooks+ Family and Student plans "later this year"; Narrated Articles count toward Premium monthly audiobook allowance.

## Audiobooks in Premium (current terms)
- spotify.com/us/premium (fetched Oct 2026): Premium Individual $12.99/month; "15 hours/month of listening time from our audiobooks subscriber catalog".
- Audiobooks+ add-on in US: $11.99/month for another 15 hours (Engadget, Publishing Perspectives Aug 2025; Spotify support). Not used in script.

## Video podcasts
- Spotify newsroom, May 21, 2026 (Investor Day): "More than 500 million users have streamed a video podcast."
- Spotify Partner Program paid podcast creators more than $100M in Q1 2025 (Entrepreneur, Inside Radio) - historical.
- Sept 17, 2026 newsroom: Partner Program expanding to more than 35 new markets; total monthly payouts to enrolled shows up more than a third since eligibility expanded earlier in 2026.

## Surprising fact
Spotify lost EUR 659M in 2022 and earned EUR 2.2B operating income in 2025, while still passing about two-thirds of music revenue to rightsholders.
