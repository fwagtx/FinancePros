# Who owns the U.S. national debt (research brief)
- Total public debt outstanding: $40,096,954,633,567 (about $40.10T) on Sept 29, 2026 (Treasury Debt to the Penny). Passed $40T on Aug 18, 2026.
- Sept 22, 2026: debt held by the public $32.38T; intragovernmental holdings $7.72T (trust funds such as Social Security, federal retirement funds).
- Federal Reserve: US Treasury securities held outright $4,557,779 million (about $4.56T), week ended Sept 23, 2026 (H.4.1).
- Foreign holders total: about $9.25T, July 2026 (Treasury TIC, released Sept 16, 2026; lowest since October per Bloomberg).
  - Japan $1,103.9B; United Kingdom $998.3B; China (mainland) $618.0B (TIC Table 5, July 2026). China peaked about $1.32T in 2013.
- Surprising fact: China holds about 1.5% of total US debt (618 / 40,100 = 1.54%).
- Rest: 40.10 - 7.72 - 4.56 - 9.25 = about $18.6T (~46%) held by US investors: mutual funds, banks, pension funds, insurers, state and local governments, individuals (savings bonds). Mixed dates (July/Sept 2026) so rounded.
