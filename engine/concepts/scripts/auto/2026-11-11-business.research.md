# 2026-11-11-business research: How Nvidia makes money

## Fiscal 2026 (year ended Jan 25, 2026), Nvidia Q4/FY2026 release Feb 25, 2026 and 10-K
- Revenue $215.9B (+65%). GAAP operating income $130.4B. Net income $120.1B.
- Data Center revenue $193.7B (+68%) = 193.7/215.9 = 89.7% of revenue.
- Net margin: 120.1 / 215.9 = 55.6% -> about 56 cents of profit per $1 of sales.
- Growth driven by Blackwell; Data Center networking +142%.

## Latest quarter: Q2 fiscal 2027 (ended July 26, 2026), reported Aug 26, 2026
- Revenue $96.2B (+106% y/y). Data Center $89.0B (+117%).
  - Hyperscale $48.7B; AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise $40.3B. 48.7/89.0 = 54.7%.
- GAAP gross margin 75.0% -> cost of revenue about 25 cents per $1 of sales.
- Next report (Q3 FY2027) expected after Nov 11, 2026 (mid/late November).

## Worked example
$100 of Nvidia sales in fiscal 2026: about $90 came from data centers (89.7%); about $56 was left as net profit (55.6%).
FY2026 vs FY2020: 215.9 / 10.92 = 19.8x, about 20 times.

## Surprise
A company famous for gaming graphics cards now gets about 90% of revenue from data centers, and keeps about 56 cents of every dollar as profit.
