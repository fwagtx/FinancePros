So the figures below were confirmed from search-result summaries of several outlets, including the title of the MOTIR press release.

## Verified claims
- Release date: MOTIR released the October 2026 figures on Sunday, Nov 1, 2026. "came out today" is correct for a same-day post.
- Total: $115.28B (rounds to $115.3B), up 93.7% y/y. Confirmed by Korea Times, JoongAng Daily, Seoul Economic Daily, Herald and DEV/VNA reports.
- Chips: $56.44B, up 259.4%, a 48.9% share of total exports. "Nearly half" is correct.
- US: up 185.8% to $24.9B, which is 2.86 times last year. "Nearly tripled" is correct.
- China: up 133.6% to $26.98B. $27.0B and "$27 billion" are correct.
- Workdays: 22 this October against 20 a year earlier, because Chuseok fell in October 2025 and in September 2026. The daily average was $5.24B, up 76.1%. "+76%" and "a big holiday moved out of October" are correct.
- Attribution: the ministry credited "continued facility investment by global big tech firms in the competition for AI" leadership. Memory-chip prices (DDR5) and server demand are also reported. "Big tech's spending on AI data centers" is a fair paraphrase.
- Chart points:
  - Oct 2023: $55.09B (MOTIE). OK.
  - Oct 2024: $57.52B. OK.
  - Oct 2025: $59.57B at first release. OK.
  - Oct 2026: $115.3B. OK.
  - Minor: 115.28 / 59.57 = +93.5%, not +93.7%. The ministry's 93.7% implies a revised Oct 2025 base of about $59.5B. Not material, and no change is needed.
- "Barely budged in years": October grew from $55.1B to $57.5B to $59.6B, about 4% a year. This is acceptable as plain-language shorthand.
