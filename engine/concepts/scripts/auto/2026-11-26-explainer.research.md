# Research: What the S&P 500 actually is, explained with acorns (2026-11-26-explainer, Acorn Economics)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- S&P 500 launched March 4, 1957 (500 stocks; earlier S&P indexes from 1923/1926). Maintained by S&P Dow Jones Indices committee; ~503 share classes from 500 companies.
- Float-adjusted market-cap weighted: bigger companies (by public float value) get larger weights.
- Eligibility for additions: US domicile, positive GAAP earnings (most recent quarter and trailing four quarters sum), liquidity/float, and minimum company market cap (US$22.7B from July 1, 2025; reviewed quarterly). Criteria apply to additions, not continued membership.
- Concentration: top 10 weight about 40% (41.9% Sept 18, 2026 per trackers; record 40.7% in 2025; ~18-23% in 1990-2015). Nvidia ~8%.
- Year-end closes: 2019 3,230.78; 2020 3,756.07; 2021 4,766.18; 2022 3,839.50; 2023 4,769.83; 2024 5,881.63; 2025 6,845.50.
- Index funds track the index by holding the same stocks in the same proportions.
