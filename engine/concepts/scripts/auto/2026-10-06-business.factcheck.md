## Issues

1. **Rounding in the post (ig, line 3 of post text): "$55.7B, minus $15.8B ... = $40.0B."** Viewers who do the subtraction get $39.9B, not $40.0B. Line 4's on-screen scene ($55.7B to $40.0B) has the same apparent gap. Fix: use "$55.75B minus $15.75B = $40.0B", or write "about $15.8B" and "about $40B". This is minor, but it is the one visible math mismatch.
2. **Hook (line 1) is true and specific but a bit long and clunky.** It runs about 13 words, roughly 3.5 seconds spoken, and "profited over twenty billion dollars" reads stiffly. Stronger option in under 3 seconds: say "Visa lends zero dollars. It still made twenty billion in profit." and caption "Visa lends $0. It still made $20B profit." Avoid "last year", because FY2025 ended a year before the posting date.
3. **Cover title "How Visa Profits Lending $0" is ungrammatical.** Fix: "How Visa Profits Without Lending" or "Visa Lends $0, Profits $20B". The video does pay off the cover.
4. **28 cents per $100 could be confused with the swipe fee merchants pay (about 2% or more, mostly interchange that goes to banks).** This is optional but worth fixing. Change the caption to "that's Visa's cut: about 28¢ per $100 spent" so viewers do not think the whole card fee is 0.28%.
5. **$14.2T volume period.** The figure is fiscal-year payments volume and excludes cash. The 10-K's own volume table covers the 12 months to June 2025 and shows $13.9T. The script's wording is defensible, so no change is required. If the team wants the most conservative figure, "about $14 trillion" covers both.
6. **Freshness and time words.** "Fiscal 2025" (the year ended Sept 30, 2025) is still Visa's latest reported full year on Oct 6, 2026. FY2026 results are expected in late October 2026, after the post goes live. The script uses no "this year", "last year" or "recently", so nothing becomes wrong on the posting date. Optional: say "the year ended September 2025" once for clarity. Be aware the figures will be superseded within about 3 weeks of posting.
7. **Text vs spoken wording.** All numbers match between caption, narration and scene ("$40.0 billion" is read as "forty billion", which is fine). Small wording differences ("Tiny slices, sure. But..." on line 7, "the interest on your card goes to your bank, while..." on line 8) are harmless. Narration reads naturally aloud.
8. **Advice, predictions, politics.** None found. The post carries the "not financial advice" line. Neutral context, not an error: FY2025 net income includes a $2.5B litigation provision (interchange MDL). No change is needed.

## Summary

Every factual number and all 5 chart points check out against Visa's filings. The only fixes needed are the rounding in the post text ($55.7B - $15.8B shows as $39.9B), tightening the hook, and the cover grammar. Adding "Visa's cut" to line 6 is optional. Nothing here is a wrong fact, advice or a spoken-vs-screen mismatch.

VERDICT: PASS
