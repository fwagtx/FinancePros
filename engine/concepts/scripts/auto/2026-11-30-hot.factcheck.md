## Issues

1. **Line 1 hook is too slow and mislabels the category.** At 11 words, with a 5-word spoken number, it runs about 3.5-4 seconds. The $929M is the FBI's *confidence/romance* category, so "Romance scams took" is a slight overstatement.
   Fix (9 words, about 3 seconds; "last year" means 2025, which is correct on November 30, 2026):
   - text: `Romance cons took $929 million last year.`
   - say: `Romance cons took nine hundred twenty-nine million last year.`
   - scene.title: `Romance [cons], 2025` (20)
   - Leave label `FBI 2025` and value `$929M` as they are.
   Optional, for more curiosity: lead with the payoff instead of the stat ("That soldier asking for money? Army investigators say it's a lie."). That would mean restructuring lines 1-3, so the stat fix above is the minimum.
2. **Line 3 chart title** says "romance fraud", but the series is confidence/romance.
   - scene.title: `FBI: romance & confidence` (25)
3. **Line 7: the on-screen $2,218 does not match the spoken "about twenty-two hundred".** The hypothetical also doesn't say what each ask is for.
   - text: `Example: leave papers $500 + flight $1,200 + medical $2,500 = $4,200, nearly double the FTC's ~$2,200 median romance loss (Q3 2025).`
   - say: `Example: leave papers five hundred, a flight twelve hundred, medical bills twenty-five hundred. That's forty-two hundred dollars, nearly double the F T C's median romance loss of about twenty-two hundred.`
   - scene.right.value: `~$2,200` (7)
   - scene.right.sub: `FTC median Q3 2025` (18)
   - Keep the left side as it is ($4,200, sub `$500+$1,200+$2,500`, 18).
4. **Line 9: "weeks after meeting online" / "Asks in weeks" is not supported by any source I found.** The FTC documents excuses for never meeting or video chatting, so use that instead.
   - text: `The pattern: a far-off soldier who won't video chat asks for gift cards, wires or crypto.`
   - say: `The pattern: a far-off soldier who won't video chat asks for gift cards, wires or crypto.`
   - scene.left.items: `["Far-off base", "Won't video chat", "Never meets up"]` (12/16/14)
5. **The post text uses the same "Romance scams took $929M" label** (category mislabel).
   - post.title: `Romance cons took $929M in 2025. Meet the fake soldier.` (55)
   - post.tiktok: `Romance cons took $929M in 2025. Meet the fake soldier. Not financial advice. #moneytok #personalfinance #romancescam #scamalert #financeprostv` (143)
   - post.ig, first line: `Confidence and romance cons took $929 million in 2025, per the FBI.`
   - post.ig example line: `Example: $500 + $1,200 + $2,500 = $4,200, nearly double the FTC's $2,218 median romance loss (Q3 2025).` This can stay, because the post text has no spoken counterpart.
   - The yt_desc is accurate ("Confidence and romance fraud... $929.3 million"). The quoted 'I'm in the military far away' matches the FTC spotlight's lie label, so it is OK.
6. **Minor, optional.** Line 5 "follow a script: ... then a flight home" implies a fixed order, which CID does not state. It is acceptable as narration. Line 8 "The surprise:" is a soft label, which is also acceptable.
