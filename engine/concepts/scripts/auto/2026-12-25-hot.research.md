# 2026-12-25-hot research brief: Why is printer ink so expensive when printers are cheap?
Posts Friday, December 25, 2026, 6 PM Central. Evergreen; every number carries its date/period. Researched October 26, 2026.

## Angle
"Why is X so expensive" Hot Take. The surprising fact: for HP, the ink and toner (supplies) bring in almost twice the revenue of the printers themselves (FY2025: $10.9B supplies vs $5.8B printer hardware). The printer is the bait.

## Facts (with sources)
1. Razor-and-blades model: sell the machine cheaply (near or below cost), earn on repeat purchases of consumables (ink cartridges). Widely described business model (Stratrix "HP - The Printer Is the Bait", The Register).
2. HP CEO Enrique Lores told CNBC at Davos in January 2024 that HP loses money on printers it sells and makes money on ink/supplies and subscriptions (BetaNews 2024-01-23; PetaPixel 2024-01-24; The Register 2025-03-19 gives the line as "We lose money on the hardware, we make money on the supplies" - exact wording NOT verified against CNBC footage, so use as paraphrase only). Lores also said a customer who does not use HP supplies is "a bad investment" (EE Journal transcript; PetaPixel).
3. HP FY2025 Form 10-K (fiscal year ended Oct 31, 2025; latest filed 10-K as of Oct 2026 - FY2026 10-K expected Dec 2026):
   - Printing net revenue $16.702B (FY2024: $17.338B)
   - Supplies $10.916B (FY2024: $11.295B), down 3.4%
   - Commercial Printing (hardware) $4.633B; Consumer Printing (hardware) $1.153B -> hardware total $5.786B
   - Printing earnings from operations $3.118B, 18.7% margin
   - Supplies share of Printing revenue: 10.916/16.702 = 65.4%
4. Earlier 10-Ks (Supplies / Printing revenue, $M): FY2021 12,632/20,128; FY2022 11,761/18,902; FY2023 11,452/18,029.
5. Which? (UK consumer group) 2019: branded home printer ink bought direct from the brand cost about £1.89 per ml (about £1,890 per litre), roughly ten times vintage champagne; Dom Perignon at that rate would be £1,417.50 a bottle (The Drinks Business, July 2019, reporting Which?). Later Which? press release (date not confirmed): an Epson WorkForce Pro WF3820DWF cartridge at £1.78/ml, over seven times Dom Perignon (24p/ml); only 4% of surveyed owners had a third-party cartridge fail. Use the 2019 dated figure only.
   Consumer Reports (older, 2013-2017 era): replacement ink roughly $13 to $75-95 per ounce; HP 64XL about $93/oz (article date unclear) - not used in the script.
6. HP "Dynamic Security": firmware that can stop printers from working with certain non-HP (cloned-chip) cartridges. US class action In re HP Printer Firmware Update Litigation (N.D. Cal.): plaintiffs alleged a 2016 firmware update made printers reject some non-HP cartridges; final approval April 25, 2019; $1.5 million cash fund and HP barred from reinstalling/reactivating Dynamic Security on the affected printers (Saveri Law Firm case page; final order PDF; HP 10-Q FY2019).
   Europe: Euroconsumers settlement (Nov 2022) - HP fund of up to $1.35 million for certain OfficeJet Pro / PageWide Pro owners in Belgium, Italy, Spain, Portugal (owned Sept 1, 2016 - Nov 17, 2022) (The Recycler; Silicon.eu). So the "$1.35M" figure is the EU deal, not the US one.
   2025: HP settled a toner-lockout class action (Mobile Emergency Housing v. HP), final approval March 2025, promising to make firmware updates optional; "HP denies that it did anything wrong" (The Register 2025-03-19; Action-Intell 2025-03-19).
7. Alternative models (neutral, no recommendation):
   - Ink-tank printers (e.g., Epson EcoTank, launched in the US in 2015): higher upfront printer price, refilled from cheaper bottles of ink (Boston Globe Aug 2015; Computerworld review).
   - Ink subscriptions (e.g., HP Instant Ink): monthly fee for a set number of pages, with ink shipped automatically; extra pages charged; plan prices change over time (HP raised Instant Ink prices in 2025 per Galaxus) - no specific plan prices used.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled as example)
Assumptions: $60 printer; four $40 ink refills a year; three years.
Ink: 4 x $40 = $160 a year; 3 x $160 = $480. $480 / $60 = 8 -> ink costs eight times the printer.

## The surprising fact
HP's supplies revenue (FY2025 $10.9B) is nearly double its printer hardware revenue ($5.8B), and the supplies share of printing revenue rose from 62.8% (FY2021) to 65.4% (FY2025).
