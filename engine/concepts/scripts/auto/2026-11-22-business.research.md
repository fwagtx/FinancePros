# Research: How Disney+ lost billions before it turned a profit (2026-11-22-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts (Disney filings)
- Entertainment Direct-to-Consumer (Disney+ and Hulu; ESPN+ reported in Sports after the FY2023 recast) operating income (loss): FY2022 -$3,424M; FY2023 -$2,496M (FY2023 10-K / Q4 FY2023 release); FY2024 +$143M; FY2025 +$1,327M (FY2025 Q4 release, Nov 13, 2025).
- Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended ~June 27, 2026; released Aug 5, 2026): DTC operating income $712M vs $329M a year earlier; 12.9% margin. Streaming revenue +11% to $5.5B.
- Older segment (DMED DTC incl. ESPN+) losses: widely reported cumulative loss of more than $11B from Disney+ launch through early 2024 (Forbes, Apr 2024).
- Disney+ subscribers: 131.6M at end of FY2025 (Sept 27, 2025) - the last quarter Disney reported subscriber counts.
- Disney+ launched Nov 12, 2019 at $6.99/month. Ad-free price history (US): $6.99 (2019), $7.99 (Mar 2021), $10.99 (Dec 2022, Premium), $13.99 (Oct 2023), $15.99 (Oct 2024), $18.99 (Oct 21, 2025), $21.49 (Oct 21, 2026; ads plan $12.49). (Variety Sept 2025; CBS/TEGNA Sept 2026; Droid Life Sept 23, 2026.)
- Disney+ Basic with ads launched Dec 8, 2022 at $7.99.

## Chartable series
DTC operating income ($B): FY2022 -3.42; FY2023 -2.50; FY2024 0.14; FY2025 1.33.
Disney+ ad-free monthly price: 2019 6.99; 2021 7.99; 2022 10.99; 2023 13.99; 2024 15.99; 2025 18.99; 2026 21.49.

## Worked example
Ad-free for a year: 2019 $6.99 x 12 = $83.88; Oct 2026 $21.49 x 12 = $257.88. 21.49/6.99 = 3.07x (+207%).
Swing FY2022 -> FY2025: 1,327 - (-3,424) = $4,751M.

## Surprising fact
Same unit went from a $3.4B annual loss (FY2022) to $1.3B profit (FY2025), while the ad-free price roughly tripled.
