## Verified claims
- **SpaceX raised $75B at $135/share, over 555M shares (L13-14, L19, L96).** Correct for the base deal. CBS, CNBC and NPR all report a $75B IPO at $135. $75B / $135 = 555.6M shares.
- **Banks got about 0.7%, about $500M in fees (L13-14, L61-71).** Correct. Bloomberg reported fees under 0.75%. Yahoo Finance, Fortune and Futu report about $500M, "near 0.7%". The exact figure is $500M / $75B = 0.67%. The approximation is fine.
- **Most mid-size IPOs pay exactly 7%, "93% of $30-160M deals" (L26-35).** Correct. Jay Ritter's "IPOs-Underwriting" (data through 2025) shows 93.3% of IPOs in the $30-160M range paid exactly 7% over 2001-2025. The range is in 2025 dollars.
- **0.7% is "a tenth" of 7% (L26-27).** Correct.
- **Gross spread definition (L48-56).** Correct. The underwriter buys at the IPO price minus the spread and resells at the IPO price.
- **Day one: +19%, $135 to $160.95, June 12, 2026 (L77-84).** Correct per CNBC ("closes at $161, jumping 19%"), CBS and Motley Fool. The exact gain is 25.95 / 135 = 19.2%.

## Issues

1. **L13-14, L61, L90-97, post: the greenshoe is left out (factual completeness; fix recommended).**
   - Underwriters exercised the 15% greenshoe (83.3M shares), so the IPO's total take rose to **$85.7B** (CNBC, Jun 15, 2026).
   - The $75B and $14.4B figures describe only the base deal. With the greenshoe, the day-one gain on all 638.9M IPO shares was about **$16.6B** (638.9M x $25.95).
   - The script is internally consistent and matches the headline coverage. Still, on Oct 3 a viewer can easily find "$85.7B".
   - Fix for L13: "SpaceX's $75 billion IPO paid its bankers just 0.7%."
   - Fix for the L96 label: "$25.95 x 555.6M base shares". Alternatively, add "(base deal)" to the L165 and L167 post text.

2. **L90-91 and L118: "the investors banks picked" is imprecise.**
   - SpaceX (the company) directed allocation, and roughly the low-20s percent of the offering went to retail through brokerages (CNBC, Jun 11, 2026).
   - The chosen buyers were mostly, but not only, institutions picked by the banks.
   - Fix: "That handed the IPO buyers, mostly big funds banks picked, about $14.4 billion in one day."

3. **L90 vs L91: the on-screen value differs from the spoken one.** The text says "$14.4 billion" but the narration says "about fourteen billion dollars". Fix: say "about fourteen point four billion dollars."

4. **L13 vs L14: small mismatch between caption and narration.** The caption says "just 0.7%" but the narration drops "just". Fix: "Its bankers got just zero point seven percent." This also sharpens the hook.

5. **L13-14: the opening is true and specific, but a little slow and soft.**
   - It runs about 4 seconds spoken, and "zero point seven percent" lands without contrast. The contrast doesn't arrive until line 2.
   - Stronger option (about 3 seconds): "The biggest IPO ever paid its bankers a tenth of the normal fee." Then reveal $75B and 0.7% vs 7%.
   - Alternative: "SpaceX's bankers took a ninety percent pay cut. Here's why."

6. **The news is getting stale.** The IPO was June 12, 2026, almost 4 months before posting. The script uses no "this week/today" words, and the explainer framing keeps it valid. Keep "June 2026" on screen (L38, L84) so it doesn't read as fresh news. No wrong time words were found.

7. **L35: the sub-label is missing its period.** Fix: "93% of $30-160M deals, 2001-25" (Ritter, 2025 dollars).

8. **L27: "exactly 7%" plus "Most" is fine.** No change needed.
