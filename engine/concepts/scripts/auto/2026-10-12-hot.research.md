# Research brief: 2026-10-12-hot. Money myth: gold always protects you

Format: Money Myth (myth, then fact). Posts Monday, October 12, 2026. Evergreen: every figure carries its date.

## Timely angle (as of late September 2026)
- Gold's record (January 2026): spot gold hit an intraday record of about $5,589 to $5,592 an ounce on January 28-29, 2026 (feeds differ: $5,589.38 on Jan 28; $5,591.61 on Jan 29; LBMA PM fix peaked near $5,405). Use "about $5,590 (intraday spot, January 2026)". Sources: CNBC Jan 26-27, 2026 record coverage; investingnews / StoneX record summaries.
- Gold also rose 64% in 2025, its biggest annual gain since 1979 (reported in January 2026 coverage).
- September 28, 2026: spot gold fell about 3% to $4,155.67 an ounce (10:31 GMT), its lowest since August 5, as higher oil prices fueled inflation fears and raised bets on a Fed rate HIKE, with Treasury yields and the dollar rising. Sources: CNBC, "Gold drops more than 1% on U.S. rate-hike bets" (Sept 28, 2026); MarketScreener/Reuters "Gold hits 7-week low as oil-driven inflation fears boost rate-hike bets" (Sept 28, 2026); Yahoo Finance (Sept 28, 2026).
- Math: $4,155.67 / $5,589.38 = 0.7435 -> about 26% below the January 2026 record (25.6% vs $5,589; 25.7% vs $5,592). Say "about 26%".
- The surprising part: gold fell while inflation fears were RISING, because higher interest rates and a stronger dollar make a metal that pays no interest less attractive. (Education, not a prediction.)

## The historical myth-buster
- On January 21, 1980 the London gold fixing reached $850 an ounce. That nominal record was not surpassed until January 3, 2008 ($865.35). About 28 years. Source: Gold fixing (Wikipedia, citing LBMA fixing history); BullionVault "Gold Spike: January 1980".
- Adjusted for inflation, gold did not exceed its January 1980 peak until 2025 (Bloomberg, Sept 11, 2025: "Gold Surpasses Inflation-Adjusted Record High Set in 1980"; other sources date the crossing to April 2025). About 45 years. Say "until 2025".

## Worked example (exact math, labeled as example)
Example: someone buys one ounce at the January 21, 1980 fix of $850.
- CPI-U January 1980 = 77.8; CPI-U January 2008 = 211.080 (BLS, 1982-84=100).
- Same buying power in January 2008: $850 x 211.080 / 77.8 = $2,306.13 -> about $2,306.
- Gold in January 2008 (Jan 3 record fix): $865.35.
- Real value kept: 865.35 / 2,306.13 = 37.5% -> about 62% of buying power lost over 28 years, even though the price was back at a "record".

## The one surprising fact
Gold bought at the 1980 top took 28 years to get back to its price, and 45 years (until 2025) to beat it after inflation. And in September 2026 it was about 26% below its January 2026 record even as inflation fears rose.

## Balanced context (no advice, no predictions)
- Gold has had big up years (2025: +64%). It pays no interest or dividends. It can swing hard both ways. Whether it "protects" depends heavily on the price paid and the time frame.
