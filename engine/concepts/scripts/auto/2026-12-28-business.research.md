# Research brief: How Canva became profitable with a free design app
Show: How They Make Money | posts Mon Dec 28, 2026, 2 PM Central (evergreen; written Oct 29, 2026)

IMPORTANT: Canva is a private company (no 10-K). All company figures below are what Canva itself announced or told reporters; they are unaudited. Attribute as "Canva says" / "reported".

## Key facts (with dates)
- Founded/launched 2013 (Canva's 10-year anniversary release, Aug 28, 2023, Business Wire).
- Aug 2023 (Canva release, 10th anniversary): more than $1.5B annualized revenue; described a six-year track record of profitability.
- Aug 2025 employee/secondary share sale (Bloomberg, Fortune Aug 22, 2025, Forbes Australia): valued Canva at $42B, $1,646.14 per share; Canva reported $3.3B annualized revenue, 27M paid users, 240M+ monthly users. Canva said it "has been profitable for the last eight years" (Startup Daily). Valuation was $32B in 2024 (reported).
- End of 2025 (co-founder/COO Cliff Obrecht to TechCrunch at Web Summit Qatar, Feb 18, 2026): $4B annual recurring revenue (ARR, not recognized revenue); more than 265M monthly active users (up 20%); more than 31M paid users; accounts with 25+ seats reached $500M ARR, up 100% (doubled).
- Profit definition: Canva says it has been profitable on a free-cash-flow basis every year since 2017 (Fortune; Canva spokesperson "operating free cash flow"). Caveat: Startup Daily noted statutory ASIC accounts (2021, 2022) showed accounting losses; Canva said that reflects non-cash items like stock-based compensation. So say "free cash flow" profitable, attributed to Canva.
- Aug 12, 2026 (Fortune; also B&T, Startup Daily): Canva cut its expected 2026 revenue growth rate by a third, to 20% (from 30%), citing higher-than-expected costs of delivering AI features; Q2 2026 revenue reported at $921.9M, up 25.2% (secondary outlets citing shareholder letter). Backers reportedly marked down valuation by about $10B (AFR, via secondary outlets). IPO reportedly slipping to 2027 (The Information via Capital Brief) -- report only, no predictions.

## Business model (freemium)
- Free plan for everyone -> huge funnel (265M+ monthly users end-2025).
- Paid tiers: Pro (individuals), team plans (Teams, now sold per-seat as "Business" per 2026 pricing reviews), Enterprise for large organizations. Canva raised team prices sharply in 2024 (moved Teams to per-person pricing; reported increases up to 300%). Exact current list prices conflict across sources (Pro $120-$180/yr) -> do NOT quote list prices on screen.
- Growth engine increasingly B2B: 25+ seat accounts $500M ARR end-2025, doubled.

## Worked example (OUR MATH, from Canva-reported end-2025 figures)
- Paying share: 31M / 265M = 11.7% -> about 12%, roughly 1 in 9 pays.
- Non-paying: 265M - 31M = 234M; 234 / 265 = 88.3% -> almost 9 in 10 pay nothing.
- Average ARR per paying user: $4,000M / 31M = $129.03 -> about $129 per paying user per year (blended average incl. business accounts; uses "more than" figures, so approximate).

## Chartable / timeline data (uneven dates -> use timeline)
- 2013: Canva launches
- 2017: free-cash-flow profitable (Canva says, every year since)
- Aug 2023: $1.5B+ annualized revenue (Canva)
- Aug 2025: $3.3B annualized revenue (Canva, share sale)
- End 2025: $4B ARR (Canva/Obrecht)
Paid users series: 27M (Aug 2025) -> 31M+ (end 2025). Monthly users: 100M (Oct 2022, Canva/Business Wire) -> 240M+ (Aug 2025) -> 265M+ (end 2025).

## Surprising fact
Almost 9 in 10 Canva users pay nothing, yet Canva says it has been free-cash-flow profitable every year since 2017.
