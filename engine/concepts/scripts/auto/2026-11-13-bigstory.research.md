## Facts (Applied Materials, fiscal Q4 2026, quarter ended late Oct 2026; released Thu Nov 12, 2026 after the market close)
Source: company press release "Applied Materials Announces Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Results" (Nov 12, 2026) and SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1.
- Q4 revenue: record $10.41 billion, up 53% year over year (year-ago Q4 FY2025: $6.80B; our check 10.41/6.80 = 1.531).
- Non-GAAP EPS: record $4.16, up 92% y/y. GAAP EPS $3.40.
- Non-GAAP operating income: record $3.68B, 35.3% of revenue.
- Semiconductor Systems revenue $8.00B (+64%); Applied Global Services record $1.9B (+26%).
- Fiscal 2026 full-year revenue: record $34.45 billion, up 21%.
- CEO Gary Dickerson: on track to grow Semiconductor Systems "more than 40 percent in our calendar 2026".
- Wall Street estimates (per GuruFocus/Investing.com coverage): $10.30B revenue, $4.06 non-GAAP EPS -> beat on both. Our math: 10.41 vs 10.30 = about 1.1% above.
- Q1 FY2027 guide: revenue ~$11.10B +/- $0.5B (vs ~$10.84B est., per coverage) - reports conflict on whether the guide beat or missed -> NOT used on screen.

## Context / explanation
- Stock prices move on news versus expectations ("priced in"). A beat of about 1% on revenue, after a big run in chip stocks, can trigger profit-taking ("sell the news").
- Friday's whole chip group was weak (Nvidia leadership change, inflation/yields), so the drop was not only about Applied.
- Business results and one day's stock move are different scoreboards.
