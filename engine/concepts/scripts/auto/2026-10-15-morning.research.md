## What it means for a normal person
Banks earn interest on loans and securities and pay depositors less; the difference (net interest income) is a core profit engine. At BofA, consumer deposits earned about half a percent in Q3 2026 while the Fed's target range sits at 3.75%-4.00%. Rates paid differ widely by bank and account type; the rate on any account is disclosed on statements/disclosures. (Education only, no recommendation.)
