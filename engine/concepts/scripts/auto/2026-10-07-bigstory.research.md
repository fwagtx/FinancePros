## Facts (story 1)
- Oct 6, 2026: Google and Constellation Energy announced a 20-year power purchase agreement for 890 MW of NEW nuclear capacity on the PJM grid. (Google Cloud press release; ANS Nuclear Newswire; Utility Dive; Quartz; Yahoo Finance)
- Capacity comes from "uprates": upgrades to turbines, steam generators and digital control systems at 11 existing Constellation nuclear units on six sites in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey. No new plant. (ANS; Utility Dive)
- Constellation (not Google) commits more than $4.3 billion of new investment. Google buys the power for 20 years. (ANS headline: "Constellation to invest $4.3 billion"; Utility Dive; citybiz)
- Separate 15-year agreement: an additional 2,700 MW from Constellation's existing fleet in PJM. Total contracted ~3,590 MW (3.6 GW). (technology.org; RTO Insider)
- Timing: first uprate expected by 2028 (Constellation); all 890 MW before the end of 2032 (Google blog).
- Jobs: sustains ~4,400 existing jobs, ~7,200 construction jobs (company).
- PJM serves ~67 million people across 13 states (Google blog via sustainabilitymag/convergedigest).
- Constellation (CEG) stock Oct 6: +12.25% to close at $300.40 (FinanceFeeds); Zacks: +12.3%.
- Same day (Oct 6): DOE Office of Energy Dominance Financing announced a CONDITIONAL loan commitment of up to $4.2B to Vistra for uprates/modernization at Beaver Valley (PA), Davis-Besse and Perry (OH); preserves ~4 GW, adds 433 MW, extends operation ~20 years beyond current licenses; ~3,000 project jobs. Vistra stock +10.8% (Zacks). (DOE via ANS, Utility Dive, Public Power, Reason)
- Note: some outlets wrongly say "Google signs $4.3B agreement"; the $4.3B is Constellation's investment.
