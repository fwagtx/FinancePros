# Research brief: The 30-day rule (Explainer, posts Oct 28, 2026)

- Slickdeals impulse spending surveys (via Study Finds, Chain Store Age, Talker):
  - 2025: about $254 a month (about $3,045 a year), 9.94 impulse buys a month at about $25.93 each.
  - Monthly averages by year: 2020 $183; 2021 $276; 2022 $314; 2023 $151; 2024 $282; 2025 $254.
- FTC Cooling-Off Rule (16 CFR 429): 3-day right to cancel sales made at home, work or a dorm (covered at $25+), or at a seller's temporary location such as a hotel, convention center or fairground (covered at $130+). The deadline is midnight of the third business day. Exempt: real estate, insurance, securities, and some vehicle and arts/crafts sales (FTC consumer advice).
- The 30-day rule: write down the item, wait 30 days, then decide. Common variants use 24-48 hours for small purchases.
- Worked example: $254 x 12 = $3,048 a year (Slickdeals reports $3,045 from unrounded data).

SOURCES:
