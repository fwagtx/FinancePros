## What it means
Driving costs about $15 more per 12-gallon tank than a year ago. Diesel moves freight, farm and construction goods, so higher diesel can push up prices of goods over time. Fed rate hikes don't directly lower fuel prices. Prices dipped in the last week as crude slipped to the $90s.
