## Issues

1. **On-screen value differs from spoken value (line 2)**
   - Screen and caption say $11.56B and $1.71B.
   - Narration says "about eleven point six billion" and "one point seven".
   - Fix: round the screen to match the narration (see fixes A and B).
2. **"Kept" overstates what adjusted gross profit (AGP) is (hook, line 2, cover, post)**
   - AGP is revenue minus what Robinson pays carriers.
   - Staff, technology and office costs all still come out of it. It is not money kept after operating costs.
   - The cover word "Kept" can stay if line 2 says "after paying truckers".
   - The post copy should add "before its own costs" (fix G).
3. **Hook does not say who "it" is**
   - The caption names C.H. Robinson at once, and line 2 names it in speech, which meets the minimum.
   - The spoken "it" is still vague. "This company" makes the listener need the name, at about 3 seconds (fix A).
4. **Stale news by Nov 27**
   - Robinson's Q3 2026 results are due around late October. By posting day Q3 is the latest quarter, so Q2 2026 (line 8, post) will be old.
   - DAT's September and October 2026 rate reports will also be out by then.
   - Required before posting: re-check Robinson's Q3 2026 NAST AGP margin and truckload cost per mile, and DAT's latest month. Then update line 8, line 9 and the post, or keep Q2 and August clearly dated as they are now.
   - The figures are correct as of today.
5. **2020 chart point only weakly verified**
   - 13.45% sits right on the rounding line, and the revenue figure came from one excerpt that conflicted with others.
   - Confirm it against the 10-K, or drop the 2020 point. If you drop it, also change the line 5 text from "from 2020 to 2025" to "from 2021 to 2025". The range 12.4 to 14.8 still holds.
6. **"Shipper contract prices lock for up to a year" (line 7)** overstates it. Contracts usually run a year or less and can be renegotiated (fix C).
7. **YouTube description reads backwards**
   - "a 14.8% spread ... That margin fell to 12.4% in 2021" implies the margin fell after 2025.
   - Fix the wording (fix G).
8. **Line 8: reason the margin fell is left out (minor)**
   - Costs rose 29% while prices rose about 25.5%, and the gap is what squeezed the margin.
   - Optional: add the price rise to the caption only (fix D).
9. **Narration polish (minor)**
   - Line 9: "two dollars and nineteen cents a mile" is long. "two nineteen a mile" reads more naturally (fix E, which also offsets the word count).
   - Line 2 caption repeats "freight broker C.H. Robinson" right after line 1's caption. Trim it (fix B).
10. **TikTok hashtag #personalfinance** does not fit a freight-business story. Suggest #logistics instead (minor).
11. **House-rule checks:**
   - No advice, no price predictions, no political opinion.
   - "So brokers sell the gap" generalizes the business model, not Robinson's numbers, so it is acceptable.
   - The title and cover are paid off by lines 2 to 4.
