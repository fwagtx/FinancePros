# Research brief: Why UnitedHealth makes so much from Optum, not just insurance
Show: How They Make Money. Posts Sunday Jan 3, 2027, 2 PM Central. Evergreen; every number dated.
Researched Nov 4, 2026.

## Company structure (UnitedHealth Group, UNH)
- Two platforms: UnitedHealthcare (health insurance/benefits) and Optum (health services).
- Optum has three reportable segments: Optum Health (care delivery: doctors, clinics, value-based care), Optum Insight (data, analytics, tech, revenue-cycle services), Optum Rx (pharmacy benefit manager / pharmacy services).

## FY2025 (full year 2025, release Jan 27, 2026, 8-K Ex. 99.1; same segment data in FY2025 10-K)
| Segment | Revenue | Earnings from operations |
|---|---|---|
| UnitedHealthcare | $344.9B ($344,903M) | $9.4B ($9,425M) |
| Optum Health | $102.0B ($101,957M) | -$0.3B (-$278M; includes charges) |
| Optum Insight | $19.4B ($19,417M) | $2.6B ($2,624M) |
| Optum Rx | $154.7B ($154,726M) | $7.2B ($7,193M) |
| Optum total | $270.6B ($270,620M) | $9.5B ($9,539M) |
| Consolidated | $447.6B ($447,567M) | $19.0B ($18,964M) |
- Eliminations FY2025: corporate eliminations of $167,956M (~$168B) of intersegment revenue (sales between UNH's own businesses, mainly Optum services to UnitedHealthcare), plus $5,480M Optum intra-eliminations. Check: 344.9 + 270.6 - 168.0 = 447.6.
- FY2025 earnings from operations included a ~$2.8B charge. Medical care ratio FY2025: 89.1%.
- Our math: Optum share of FY2025 earnings from operations = 9,539 / 18,964 = 50.3%.

## FY2024 (as shown in the FY2025 release)
- UnitedHealthcare revenue $298.2B, EFO $15.6B ($15,584M).
- Optum revenue $253.0B ($252,957M), EFO $16.7B ($16,703M): Optum Health $7.8B, Optum Insight $3.1B, Optum Rx $5.8B.
- Consolidated revenue $400.3B, EFO $32.3B ($32,287M). Corporate eliminations $150,887M.
- Our math: Optum share FY2024 = 16,703 / 32,287 = 51.7%.

## FY2023 (release Jan 12, 2024)
- Optum revenue $226.6B, EFO $15.9B; UnitedHealthcare revenue $281.4B, EFO $16.4B. Consolidated EFO $32.4B. (Insurer was slightly bigger profit-wise in 2023.)

## Why owning providers/PBM matters: medical loss ratio (MLR) rule
- ACA "80/20 rule" (CMS): insurers in individual and small-group markets must spend at least 80% of premiums on medical care and quality improvement; large-group at least 85%; otherwise they owe rebates. Source: CMS Medical Loss Ratio page.
- (Medicare Advantage plans also have an 85% minimum MLR under CMS rules.)
- Mechanism (explanatory, neutral): the rule limits the insurer's share of premiums; it does not limit the profit of doctors, clinics or pharmacy-benefit businesses that are paid for that care. When UnitedHealthcare pays Optum for care or pharmacy services, that is a cost to the insurer and revenue to Optum; it is eliminated in UNH's consolidated totals (~$168B in 2025).

## Regulatory scrutiny (factual only)
- WSJ reported (2025) that the DOJ has an antitrust probe, begun in 2024, examining the relationship between UnitedHealthcare and Optum; separate from civil and criminal DOJ probes into Medicare Advantage billing, which UNH said in a July 24, 2025 filing it is cooperating with. No outcome reported.
