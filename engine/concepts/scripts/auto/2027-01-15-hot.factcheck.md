## Issues found and fixes applied
1. Line 2 (key concern) - "Mostly it's payment for order flow: trading firms pay the app for your orders." Transaction revenue includes crypto, which Robinhood calls "transaction rebates", not PFOF. 10-K: ~$2.33B of $2.63B came from routing user orders to market makers (options, equities, crypto). Fixed: text/say "Mostly, market makers pay the app to route your orders: payment for order flow." Board: ["Market makers pay", "for your orders"]. Captions now say PFOF on stocks/options, rebates on crypto.
2. Line 3 - "dipped in 2023" understated a two-year decline (2022 and 2023). Fixed: "Its trading revenue bottomed in 2023, then more than tripled by 2025." (say matches).
3. Line 4 - spoken "about one point five billion" vs on-screen $1.51B. Fixed say: "one point five one billion".
4. Line 5 - "to settle best-execution charges" omitted the disclosure charge and the no-admission term. Fixed: "In December 2020, Robinhood paid a $65 million SEC penalty, neither admitting nor denying." Stat label "SEC penalty, 2020", sub "PFOF, best exec".
5. Line 6 - spoken "about thirty-four million" vs on-screen $34.1M. Fixed say: "thirty-four point one million, after commission savings."
6. Line 7 - "stricter proposals" was vague. Fixed: "The SEC withdrew its routing proposals in 2025; the EU's ban fully applied mid-2026."
7. Fairness - script only showed the critics' side. Line 8 fixed: "Defenders say it funds free trades; critics see a conflict. Rule 606 reports show who pays." Balance sentence also added to IG and YouTube captions; IG/YT now note neither-admit-nor-deny.
8. IG caption - "EU's PFOF ban took full effect July 1, 2026" reworded to "fully applies after its transition ended June 30, 2026" (both accurate; now matches primary wording).
