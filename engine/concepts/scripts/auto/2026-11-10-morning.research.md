## What it means for a normal person
- Monthly prices kept climbing (0.4% after 0.6%); groceries +0.4%, gas +1.0%.
- If your raise is smaller than price growth, your real pay shrinks (September: prices +3.7% y/y vs average hourly earnings +3.1% y/y in October, BLS jobs report Nov 6).
- CPI feeds the Fed's December 8-9 decision, which flows into credit card APRs, loan rates and savings yields.
