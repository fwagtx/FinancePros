## Facts (chosen story)
- Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs in February 2026 (most sources: Feb 20, 2026; one says Feb 21). Use "February 2026".
- CBP declaration filed with the Court of International Trade on Sept 15, 2026 (Brandon Lord, CBP Executive Director of Trade Programs), figures as of Sept 11, 2026:
  - Universe: ~53 million entries, ~330,000 importers, ~$166 billion in duties.
  - ~$134.7B accepted for processing through CAPE.
  - ~$122B in refunds (duties plus interest) certified and sent to Treasury for disbursement (~73% of the pool).
  - 20,184 refunds (~$1.3B) not sent because importer gave no ACH bank info.
- CAPE Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries, ~$11.4B, only importers that are plaintiffs at the CIT) scheduled to deploy Oct 6, 2026 (CBP confirmed date Sept 15). Launch not independently confirmed.
- Earlier milestones (definitions vary, do not chart together): $100B paid as of July 31 (CBP filing via Supply Chain Dive); $86.3B as of July 10 (tracker); $23.68B completed via CAPE ~June 10.
- Refunds go to the importer of record; officials say most consumers will not get direct payments.
- Levi Strauss Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended Aug 30, 2026), reported Oct 7, 2026 (8-K, 10-Q):
  - $78.6 million of IEEPA tariff refunds booked as reduction of cost of goods sold; $57.1M received by Aug 30; $4.7M interest income on refunds.
  - Refunds added 490 bp to gross margin; ~120 bp redeployed into the business; ~$30M of refund redeployment costs in Q4 SG&A.
  - Revenue ~$1.61B, +4% reported; adjusted EPS $0.48 incl. $0.11 net refund benefit. Raised FY adj EPS outlook to $1.54-$1.56.
  - CFO had earlier estimated close to $80M (Black Enterprise / BoF).
- Walmart: CFO said it is eligible for nearly $3 billion in refunds and plans to use it to lower prices (Aug 2026; NBC Palm Springs, Newsweek, SAN).
- Amazon: received about $600 million in refunds (Q2); will reimburse shoppers automatically only where a specific import charge can be traced; otherwise invest in low prices (Quartz, IADS).
- Costco faces a consumer class action seeking refunded money; no price-cut pledge found.
