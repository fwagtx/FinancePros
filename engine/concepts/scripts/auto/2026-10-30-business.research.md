# How diamond prices work: natural vs lab-grown (research brief)
- CNBC (Sept 26, 2026): a 1-carat natural diamond averages $3,898, 51% below its 2021 average of $8,007. Diamond Standard Index hit a record low 2,490 in early Aug 2026. Glut of mined stones after Covid-era overproduction + lab-grown competition. Lab-grown typically priced 70-85% below comparable naturals.
- De Beers preliminary results for 2025 (Feb 2026): average realised rough price fell 7% to $142/carat (2024: $152); rough price index down 12%.
- Natural diamond pricing: scarcity + supply control by big miners (De Beers controls sorting/sales via sights).
- Lab-grown diamonds: same chemistry/physical properties (FTC treats them as diamonds); price tracks falling production cost. JCK: lab-grown wholesale prices down ~74% since 2020. De Beers Lightbox launched 2018 at $800/carat; closed 2025 as lab prices fell ~90% at wholesale.
- Chart: The Knot Real Weddings Study, share of engagement rings with lab-grown center stones: 2019 12%, 2022 36%, 2023 46%, 2024 52%, 2025 61%.
- Worked math: $8,007 - $3,898 = $4,109 drop; 4,109/8,007 = 51.3%.
