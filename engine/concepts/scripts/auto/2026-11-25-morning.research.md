## Facts (FDIC press release + statement, Nov 24, 2026; Reuters Nov 24, 2026)
- FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile, Q3 2026, released Tuesday, November 24, 2026; covers 4,205 insured banks and savings institutions.
- Total unrealized losses on securities: $486 billion in Q3 2026, up from $326.7 billion in Q2 2026 — "primarily because interest rates rose" (FDIC). Reuters: "jumped 48.8%". (Our check: 486/326.7 = 1.4876 -> +48.8%.)
- FDIC: unrealized losses "remain well below their peak in third quarter 2023."
- Unrealized losses = difference between market value and book value of non-equity securities at quarter end (FDIC note). They become realized only if securities are sold below book value.
- Net income: $84.8 billion, down $5.3B (5.9%) from Q2, up $5.5B (7.0%) from a year earlier. ROA 1.28%.
- FDIC: industry "continued to maintain strong capital and liquidity levels."
- Domestic deposits grew 0.9%, ninth straight quarterly increase. Loans +1.9% q/q, +7.5% y/y.
- Deposit Insurance Fund: $163.5B on Sept 30, 2026; reserve ratio 1.50%.
- Standard FDIC deposit insurance: $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category (FDIC standard rule).
- Context: Silicon Valley Bank failed in March 2023; losses on its bond holdings were central to its collapse (widely reported). The FDIC's series peak was Q3 2023.

## What it means for a normal person
- Banks hold lots of bonds; when rates rise, older lower-paying bonds lose market value. That's a paper loss unless the bank has to sell.
- Deposits are protected by FDIC insurance up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category; good to know where your balances sit vs that limit.
- FDIC says capital and liquidity remain strong; bank profits still up 7% from a year earlier.
