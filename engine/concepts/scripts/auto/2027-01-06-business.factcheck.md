# Fact-check: 2027-01-06 Business (How sneaker resale apps make money)

Checked Nov 7, 2026 for posting Wed Jan 6, 2027, 2 PM CT. Independent web search (stockx.com and goat.com pages seen via search results).

## Verified (no change needed)
- StockX 2020: 7.5M+ trades, $1.8B GMV, GAAP revenue "over $400 million", break-even in H2 2020. The revenue figure comes from StockX's Apr 8, 2021 funding release ($3.8B valuation). The GMV and trades also appear in StockX's Jan 2021 Annual Snapshot (PR Newswire). A 2025 Entrepreneur interview says 2020 was the last revenue figure StockX has shared publicly. Sources: stockx.com/about/press-release-stockx-valuation-surges-to-3-8-billion-with-255-million-financing; prnewswire.com/news-releases/...-301207387.html; benzinga.com (Apr 2021).
- StockX seller fees: 3% payment processing on all sales plus a transaction fee by Seller Level, with Level 1 at 9% (StockX Help "What are StockX's fees for Sellers?"). The Mar 1, 2026 seller-program update kept Level 1 Flex at 9%. It raised the US non-Flex seller shipping fee from $4 to $5 and removed the 1% Flex Quick Sale discount (stockx.com/news/updates-to-the-stockx-seller-program). The US minimum seller fee is $5. Caveat: one cached StockX help version shows 8%, and some blogs say 9.5%. 9% is the best-supported official figure.
- $200 math: 9% = $18, 3% = $6, total $24, leaving $176 (correct). The US seller shipping fee of $5 is not included, so the line needs a qualifier (issue 4).
- GOAT: 9.5% commission plus a seller fee for sellers rated 90+, with higher tiers below that (goat.com/fees, "Last Updated: August 1, 2026"). There is also a 2.9% cash-out fee (third-party).
- eBay: 8% final value fee on eligible sneakers $150+ for non-store and Starter sellers (7% for Basic+ stores). Authenticity Guarantee at no extra cost (pages.ebay.com/sell-sneakers). "eBay 8%" is fair.
- 22%: $400M / $1.8B = 22.2%, so the math is correct. The wording needs a fix (issue 5).
- "A middleman that inspects the shoes" (line 2): mostly fair. StockX's Verified Seller program (Apr 2, 2025) lets invited sellers ship direct without StockX authentication, but reports put it under 1% of orders (StockX news Apr 2, 2025; Complex; Sole Retriever). No change required.
- "Without owning shoes": fair. StockX's core marketplace matches third-party sellers and buyers and does not take title to inventory.
