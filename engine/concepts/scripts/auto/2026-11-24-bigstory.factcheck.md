## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 4: overstated wording.** The Conference Board says below 80 *usually* signals a recession. The script said it plainly "signals" one. FIXED:
   - text: "The Conference Board says below 80 usually signals a recession ahead. 83.6 is the first reading above it this year."
   - say: "The Conference Board says below eighty usually signals a recession ahead. Eighty-three point six is the first reading above it this year."
   - Same word count. Splitting it into two sentences also reads better out loud.
2. **post.yt_desc: factual error.** It said inflation expectations "stayed at 5.7%". They actually eased for a second month, down to 5.7%. FIXED: "eased for a second month but stayed elevated at 5.7%".
3. **post.ig: caption matched to the new script wording.** It now reads "its first reading this year above 80, the level the Conference Board says usually signals a recession ahead."
