# Research brief: How Six Flags makes money from season passes
Slot 2026-12-06-business (posts Sun Dec 6, 2026). Researched Oct 7, 2026. Company: Six Flags Entertainment Corporation (NYSE: FUN), the post-merger company (Cedar Fair + legacy Six Flags merged July 1, 2024).
Note: Q3 2026 results expected early November 2026 -- every number below is tied to its period.

## Full year 2025 (Q4/FY2025 release, Feb 19, 2026, 8-K Ex. 99.1; FY2025 10-K)
- Net revenues: $3.10 billion (FY2025).
- Attendance: 47.4 million guests (FY2025).
- Per capita spending: $61.90 (FY2025) vs $61.31 (FY2024).
  - Admissions per capita: $33.41 (2025) vs $33.70 (2024).
  - In-park product per capita (food, merchandise, games, extras): $28.49 (2025) vs $27.61 (2024).
  - Math: 28.49 / 61.90 = 46.0% of per-guest spending was NOT admission in 2025. 33.41 + 28.49 = 61.90 (checks).
- Q4 2025: attendance down 13% to 9.3M, partly due to "a smaller active season pass base in 2025 compared to 2024"; per capita $66.41.
- Deferred revenue Dec 31, 2025: $310.8M ($311M), vs $308M at Dec 31, 2024.
- FY2025 net loss $1.60B incl. ~$1.5B non-cash impairment (context only; not used).

## Revenue recognition (FY2025 10-K, exact)
"Revenues from multi-use products, including season-long products for admission, dining, beverage and other products and the first 12-month non-cancelable period for membership products, are recognized over the estimated number of uses expected for each type of product. The estimated number of uses is reviewed and may be updated periodically during the operating season prior to the ticket or product expiration. The number of uses is estimated based on historical usage adjusted for current period trends."
- 10-K also: deferred revenue is typically highest right before peak summer and lowest at season end; season-long products incl. memberships are most of the balance.
- Revenue sources (10-K): (1) admission, (2) food, merchandise and games, (3) accommodations, extra-charge products and other.

## Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 28, 2026; release Aug 6, 2026, 8-K Ex. 99.1)
- Net revenues $865M reported (-7%, after divesting seven non-core parks); same-park $864M (+2.4%).
- Attendance 13.1M visits; same-park +4% (~449,000 visits).
- Same-park growth included "a 10% increase in season-pass visitation."
- Per capita $62.89 reported; admissions per capita $33.62; in-park product per capita $29.27 (vs $28.27 reported Q2 2025).
- Admissions per capita declined, "associated with expanded season pass benefits and increased cross-park visitation," offset by "continued strength in guest spending on food, extra-charge attractions and other in-park offerings."
- CEO quote: "season-to-date pass sales increasing 7% and our active pass base growing 6% on a Same-Park Basis."
- Membership: lower upfront commitment, "building the Company's recurring-revenue base"; expanded to six more parks in June 2026.
- Total deferred revenue $431M as of June 28, 2026 (same-park +2%).

## Q1 2026 (release May 7, 2026)
- Net revenues +12% to $225.6M; attendance +4% to 2.9M; per capita $69.26 (+6%); active pass base +6% through end of April (same-park).

## Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL, labeled)
Pass price $120 (assumed), Six Flags estimates 6 uses (assumed) -> $120 / 6 = $20 of admission revenue recognized per visit.
If that holder spends the Q2 2026 average in-park amount of $29.27 per visit (assumption: pass holders spend the average), 6 visits x $29.27 = $175.62 in-park spending, more than the $120 pass.

## Surprising fact
Almost half (46%) of what the average Six Flags guest spent per visit in 2025 was not admission at all, and Q2 2026 admissions per capita fell because of richer pass benefits while pass visits rose 10% and food/extra spending rose.
