## History / context
- CNBC analysis (Deena Zaidi, Nick Wells): S&P 500 has risen in the year after each of the last 11 midterm elections, 1982-2022; average one-year return 13.7%. Across all elections 1980-2024, average +0.2% one day after, +1.1% one month after.
- Corroboration: Yardeni Research (via CNBC, 2022): S&P 500 higher 12 months after every midterm since 1954, avg ~15%. Motley Fool (Shiller data): up in 32 of 38 midterm years since 1874.
- Caveat: small sample; past performance is not a promise.
