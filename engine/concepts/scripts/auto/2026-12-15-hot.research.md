# Research brief: Why is Christmas so expensive? (slot 2026-12-15-hot, posts Tue Dec 15, 2026)

Written Oct 16, 2026. NRF's 2026 holiday forecast (usually early Nov) is NOT out; PNC's 2026 Christmas Price Index (usually late Nov) is NOT out. All figures below are dated; refer to them by year.

## Hook: PNC Christmas Price Index (2025)
- 2025 cost of one set of the 12 "Twelve Days of Christmas" gifts: $51,476.12, up 4.5% from $49,263.47 in 2024 (PNC release Nov 24, 2025; confirmed by PNC page pnc.com/christmaspriceindex, Business Journal Daily, IREI, Finviz).
- Worked math: 51,476.12 - 49,263.47 = 2,212.65; 2,212.65 / 49,263.47 = 4.49% -> 4.5%.
- "True Cost of Christmas" (all 364 gifts as the verses repeat): $218,542.98, up 4.4%.
- Buying online: $55,748.05 (+3.1%), shipping/packaging cited.
- Five Gold Rings +32.5% (gold prices up ~45%). PNC (Amanda Agati) attributed the rise to labor market pressures and economic uncertainty, not tariffs (all gifts domestic).
- PNC's 2025 rise (4.5%) beat the 3% CPI reading.

## Demand (NRF)
- 2025 holiday sales (Nov 1-Dec 31, NRF core definition excludes autos, gas, restaurants) grew 4.1% per CNBC/NRF Retail Monitor (NRF, Jan 2026), in line with NRF's forecast of 3.7%-4.2% growth to $1.01-$1.02 trillion — first time above $1 trillion. 2024: $976.1 billion (+4.3%). Census-based final not confirmed in my searches; say "topped $1 trillion" per NRF.
- NRF/Prosper holiday survey (Oct 16, 2025): average planned spending $890.49 per person on gifts, food, decorations and other seasonal items — second-highest in survey's 23-year history; 2024 record $901.99. Split: $627.93 gifts, $262.56 seasonal items. ~85% expected higher prices due to tariffs.
- Seasonal hiring: NRF (Nov 6, 2025) expected 265,000-365,000 seasonal hires vs 442,000 in 2024.
- Census: November-December share of annual retail sales ~18.4% (Statista citing Census, year not stated) vs 16.7% if spread evenly (2/12); Pew (2014): 18.14% in 2013, 19.41% in 1993. Weakly dated; not used on screen.

## Shipping (peak surcharges, 2025 season)
- UPS Ground Residential demand surcharge: $0.40/package before Nov 23, 2025; $0.60 Nov 23-Dec 27, 2025; $0.40 Dec 28-Jan 17, 2026 (EasyPost, ShipWise, DCL summaries of UPS schedule).
- Worked math: (0.60 - 0.40) / 0.40 = 50% higher per package at peak.
- FedEx residential: $0.40 Oct 27-Nov 23, $0.65 Nov 24-Dec 28, 2025 (Supply Chain Dive). High-volume shippers pay up to $7.50+ per package.

## Travel
- Hopper 2026 Holiday Travel Index (reported Sept 29-30, 2026; Fox stations, WLOX, ADN): Christmas domestic round trip averaging $452, up 23% from a year earlier; Thanksgiving $402 (+31%); highest holiday fares in a decade; Hopper cites prolonged high jet fuel costs. Christmas hotels $197/night, +18%. These are fares tracked as of late Sept 2026, not final prices.

## The surprise
- The 12 Days gifts cost $51,476.12 in 2025; buying every repeated gift in the song: $218,542.98.
- Shipping a box from home stores costs 50% more in surcharge at UPS peak.

## Mechanics (no advice)
Everyone shops, ships and travels in the same ~6 weeks. Demand peaks, carriers and airlines price capacity at the peak (surcharges, peak fares), and retailers staff up.
