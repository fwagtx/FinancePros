## Issues

1. **lines[2] text/say** (also **post.yt_desc**). Problem: the index began in 1976, so "worst year since 1976" suggests 1976 was even worse. It was the worst year in the index's history.
   - text: "Its worst year since the index began in 1976. The old record: a drop of about 3%, in 1994."
   - say: "Its worst year since the index began in nineteen seventy-six. The old record: a drop of about three percent, in nineteen ninety-four."
   - scene.lines[0]: "Worst on record" (or "Worst since 1976 start")
   - yt_desc: replace "its worst year since 1976" with "its worst year since the index began in 1976".

2. **lines[7] text/say** (also **post.ig**, **post.yt_desc**). Problem: "A fund never matures" is too broad. Defined-maturity (target-date) bond ETFs, such as iShares iBonds and Invesco BulletShares, do mature. Most open-ended bond funds don't.
   - text/say: "A high-quality bond held to maturity pays back face value, barring default. Most bond funds never mature."
   - ig: replace "a bond fund has no maturity date." with "most bond funds have no maturity date."
   - yt_desc: replace "while funds never mature" with "while most bond funds never mature".

3. **lines[0] (hook).** Problem: the line is true but generic, and the shock number only appears on screen. A specific number would hold viewers better. It is about 10 words, which is OK for 3 seconds. Suggested replacement:
   - text/say: "Myth: bonds can't lose value. In 2022, they lost 13%." (say: "Myth: bonds can't lose value. In twenty twenty-two, they lost thirteen percent.")
   - If adopted, change lines[1] to avoid repeating it: text "That's the main US bond index: down 13.01%." / say "That's the main US bond index: down thirteen point zero one percent."
   - Optional; this alone is not a reason to fail.

4. **lines[3] text/say.** Problem: "small gains" doesn't fit 2019 (+8.72%), 2020 (+7.51%) or 2025 (+7.3%). There was also a loss in 2021. "Every year since 2016" leaves out 2026, which isn't finished on the post date.
   - text: "Here's 2016 to 2025: mostly gains, then one big cliff in 2022."
   - say: "Here's twenty sixteen to twenty twenty-five: mostly gains, then one big cliff in twenty twenty-two."

5. **lines[6] say/text (minor).** Problem: "rates up 2 points" is less clear than "percentage points". The spoken line also drops "dollars", so it reads awkwardly.
   - say: "Our math, with FINRA's rule of thumb: duration six, rates up two percentage points, and ten thousand dollars drops to about eight thousand eight hundred dollars."

6. **post.yt_desc (minor).** Problem: "fall roughly by their duration per point" is unclear.
   - Replace with "bond prices fall about 1% for each year of duration per 1-point rate rise, per FINRA".
