# Research: How dog daycare and boarding make money (2026-11-25-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026. Most operators are private; numbers come from franchise disclosure documents (FDD Item 19, via franchise-data sites) and APPA.

## Facts
- Dogtopia FDD (Item 19, fiscal 2025): average gross sales $935,548 (all reporting locations); franchised average $928,043, median $905,798. Royalty 7% of gross sales; marketing fee 2%. (franchiseinvestordata, 1851 Franchise, Sharpsheets)
- Camp Bow Wow FDD: royalty 7% of gross/net revenue (3.5% first year), ad fund 1% (can rise to 3%); average gross sales $993,149 (2024, 148 locations); ~$1,089,860 FY2025 (secondary sites).
- Revenue lines: daycare (day passes/packages), overnight boarding, grooming/baths, training, retail.
- Dogtopia's franchise FAQ: labor is the main operating cost.
- APPA State of the Industry 2026 (Pet Age): US pet industry $158B in 2025; grooming, boarding, training and other services $14.3B in 2025 (APPA had projected $13.5B); boarding, salon grooming and mobile grooming use declined in 2025 as owners turned to friends/family. Other services: $11.4B (2022), $12.3B (2023), ~$13.0B (2024).

## Worked example
$935,548 x 7% = $65,488 royalty; x 2% = $18,711 marketing; total $84,199 (9%).
