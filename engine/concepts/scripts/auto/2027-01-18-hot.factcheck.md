## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Weak hook (line 0).** "Half of covered workers were in a PPO in twenty twenty-six." is a flat stat that raises no question, and it uses 11 words to land the number.
   - Fixed. text/say: "Half of covered workers are in a pricier PPO. Why?" (10 words, about 3 s). It is true: 50% are in a PPO, and PPO premiums are above the all-plan average. The question gets answered by the trade-off in lines 2, 3 and 7. The hook scene title "Why [PPO]?" now matches.
2. **The 2026 date moved out of the hook.** To keep the time anchor, line 1 now carries the year.
   - Fixed. text: "In 2026, only 12% were in an HMO, per KFF. So what's the difference?" / say: "In twenty twenty-six, only twelve percent were in an HMO, per KFF. So what's the difference?" This also fixes a mismatch: the text said "per KFF" but the spoken line did not.
3. **Cover not paid off.** "Why Half Use PPO" promises reasons for PPO popularity, such as employer offerings, but the video never gives them. The video explains the HMO-vs-PPO trade-off.
   - Fixed. cover.title: "HMO or PPO?" (11 chars).
4. **Captions.** post.title now says "covered workers", which is accurate. The IG caption now notes KFF's 2026 plan reclassification. The TikTok caption now says "a pricier PPO" and stays within its length limit.
5. **On-screen vs spoken values.** "about ten thousand three hundred" vs $10,272, "about ninety-eight hundred" vs $9,804, and "about twelve hundred" vs +$1,219 are acceptable rounding marked with "about". No change.
6. **Time words.** "In 2026" is still correct on Jan 18, 2027, because the 2026 KFF survey is the latest and the 2027 edition is due around Oct 2027. Nothing is stale.
7. **Advice, predictions, politics.** None. Line 7 ("a PPO sells freedom at a higher price") is a neutral description of the trade-off, not a recommendation. No change.
8. **Narration.** Reads cleanly. No awkward lines remain.
