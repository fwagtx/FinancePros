# Research brief: How Fidelity makes money on zero-fee index funds
(Researched Nov 6, 2026 for posting Jan 5, 2027. Evergreen; every number dated.)

## Angle / hook
Fidelity sells index funds with a 0.00% expense ratio, yet FMR LLC (Fidelity, privately held, so no 10-K) reported record revenue of $37.7 billion for calendar 2025 (Fidelity 2025 Annual Report, released March 2, 2026). No single timely news hook; the annual report is the latest official source.

## The ZERO funds (facts)
- Launched August 2018: Fidelity ZERO Total Market Index Fund (FZROX) and Fidelity ZERO International Index Fund (FZILX), announced Aug 1, 2018, inception Aug 2/3, 2018; expense ratio 0.00%. Two more (ZERO Large Cap FNILX, ZERO Extended Market FZIPX) announced Sept 12, 2018. Sources: Fidelity/Business Wire releases Aug 1 2018 and Sept 12 2018; Mutual Fund Observer launch alert (Aug 2018); StockAnalysis (0.00% ER).
- Why they can be free: they track indexes Fidelity built itself, so no index-licensing fee; Fidelity pays costs itself (Motley Fool, Aug 10, 2018; InvestmentNews 2018).
- Only at Fidelity: 2018 prospectus (SEC 485APOS filing): "Shares of the fund are available only to individual retail investors who purchase their shares through a Fidelity brokerage account." Widely reported that they can't be transferred in kind to another broker (you'd have to sell) -- blogs/forums only, not an official statement; do not state as hard fact on screen.
- Purpose: Fidelity's CEO said the no-fee lineup is aimed at attracting new customers (Financial Advisor magazine / InvestmentNews, 2018).
- Securities lending: an analyst suggested Fidelity would profit from lending the funds' shares; Fidelity said it is "not receiving any revenue from the Fidelity ZERO Index funds for securities lending" (InvestmentNews, 2018). So securities lending on ZERO funds is NOT the money source per Fidelity.
- FZROX size: about $32.1B net assets as of Nov 28, 2025 (YCharts); $32.4B as of Feb 3, 2026 (StockAnalysis). Third-party data; other sites differ -> UNCERTAIN, not used on screen.

## How Fidelity still earns (mechanisms, described by Motley Fool Aug 2018 and InvestmentNews 2018)
- Loss leader: free funds bring customers and assets onto the platform.
- Cash: uninvested cash in brokerage accounts is swept/invested; firm keeps part of the interest. CEO cited "higher interest rates" as helping 2025 results (Fidelity 2025 Annual Report via Bloomberg/Financial Planning).
- Paid products: other Fidelity funds with fees; managed accounts and advice.
- Managed assets (Fidelity's own funds + managed accounts): $7.1 trillion at Dec 31, 2025, up from $5.9T at end 2024 (Fidelity 2025 Annual Report; Bloomberg Mar 2, 2026).
- Assets under administration: $18.0 trillion at Dec 31, 2025, up 19% (Fidelity 2025 Annual Report); $15.1T end 2024.

## Worked example (OUR MATH)
Annual fee = balance x expense ratio.
- $10,000 x 0.00% = $0 per year
- $10,000 x 0.03% = $3 per year
- $10,000 x 1.00% = $100 per year
(Illustrative ratios; ignores growth.)

## Surprising fact
The fund itself makes Fidelity $0 in fees, and Fidelity says it takes no securities-lending revenue from ZERO funds; the money comes from the customer relationship (cash, paid products, managed money) -- and the funds only live inside Fidelity brokerage accounts.
