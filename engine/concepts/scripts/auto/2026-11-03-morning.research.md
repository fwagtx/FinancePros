## Facts (Apple fiscal Q4 2026, quarter ended Sept 26, 2026; reported Mon Nov 2, 2026 after close)
- Revenue: about $114.5 billion, up 12% year over year; record for a September quarter. (CNBC, MacRumors, Apple 8-K)
  - Year-ago (fiscal Q4 2025): $102.47B. 114.5 / 102.47 = +11.7% (our math, matches reported 12%).
- EPS: $2.10 vs about $1.99 expected. Net profit about $30.7B. (MacRumors, Yahoo)
- iPhone revenue: $57.58B vs $56.54B estimated; September-quarter record (Forbes Nov 2, 2026). Only ~8 days of iPhone 18 Pro sales.
- Mac revenue up 24%. iPad missed. Greater China $17.54B vs $18.75B estimated.
- Gross margin Sept quarter: 47.9%, incl. about 1 point from tariff refunds (Apple).
- December-quarter guidance: gross margin 45% to 46%, below ~46.3% to ~47% expected (CNBC/Reuters); revenue growth 7% to 10%; Services growth 8% to 9%.
- Cause: higher memory chip costs; CEO John Ternus called memory pricing "unprecedented" and expects even higher memory costs in the December quarter; also Siri AI ramp costs. CFO Kevan Parekh: margin down about 1 point sequentially mainly from memory. Memory squeeze tied to demand from AI infrastructure builders (Reuters via Investing.com, Stocktwits).
- Stock: fell about 4.5% in premarket trading Tuesday Nov 3 (reports range 4.4%-4.7%, ~ $322-323). (CNBC Nov 3 "Apple and Palantir stocks fall in premarket trading", Reuters via Investing.com, TradingKey)
- First earnings report under CEO John Ternus (succeeded Tim Cook in September 2026).
- Market context: Monday Nov 2 Nasdaq -1.07%, S&P 500 -0.51%; Tuesday futures mixed/slightly lower ahead of midterm results (Yahoo Finance live, Reuters).
