# Research brief: How to buy your first home (The Guide, posts Oct 28, 2026)

- NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers (July 2024-June 2025):
  - First-time buyer share 21% (record low); median first-time buyer age 40 (record high).
  - First-time median down payment 10% (repeat buyers 23%).
  - Down payment sources: savings 59%, financial assets 26%, gifts or loans from family/friends 22%.
  - Prior shares: 2021 34%, 2022 26%, 2023 32%, 2024 24%.
- NAR Existing-Home Sales, August 2026: median existing-home price $429,100 (+1.6% y/y).
- Freddie Mac PMMS, Sept 24, 2026: 30-year fixed 7.03%, the first time above 7% since January 2025.
- FHA: 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score; 500-579 needs 10% down. Upfront MIP 1.75%; annual MIP 0.55% for most borrowers (since March 2023).
- Conventional loans from 3% down (Fannie/Freddie programs); VA and USDA allow 0% for eligible buyers.
- FHFA 2026 conforming loan limit: $832,750 for one-unit homes in most areas.
- Homeowners Protection Act: borrowers can request PMI cancellation at 80% LTV; automatic termination at 78% of original value.
- CFPB/TRID: Loan Estimate within 3 business days of application; Closing Disclosure at least 3 business days before consummation.
- Free weekly credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com.
- Closing costs typically 2%-5% of the purchase price (Freddie Mac and others).
- Buyer-agent written agreements before touring, required by NAR practice changes since Aug 17, 2024.
- Worked example: $429,100 with 10% down = $42,910 down; loan $386,190.
  - 30-year at 7.03%: $2,577.12 a month; total paid $927,762; interest $541,572. First-month interest $2,262, principal $315.
  - 15-year at 7.03%: $3,477.67 a month; interest $239,790.
  - At 6%: $2,315.40.
  - Closing costs 2%-5% = $8,582 to $21,455.
  - 28% rule: $80K salary = $1,867 a month; $2,577 / 0.28 x 12 = $110,443.
