# Fact-check: 2026-12-30-business (How Fortnite makes money)
Checked Oct 31, 2026 for posting Dec 30, 2026 (evergreen). I checked each claim myself with web search and the court PDF. The writer's brief was used only as leads.

## What checks out
- **2018 $5.48B and 2019 $3.71B Fortnite revenue (lines[1], cover, post).** These come from an Epic internal presentation dated Jan 2020. It was made public in Epic v. Apple and reported in May 2021 by The Verge, TechSpot, Complex and Twinfinite. They add up to about $9.19B, so "$9B+", "about $9 billion" and "about $5.5B / $3.7B" are all correct. (PC Gamer printed $5.8B for 2018, which looks like a typo. Most outlets give $5.48B.) "Epic's own numbers, via court" is fair wording.
- **The quote (lines[2]).** "V-Bucks themselves don't have a marginal cost." appears word for word in the Sept 10, 2021 Rule 52 order (regmedia.co.uk copy, footnote 74). AppleInsider (May 3, 2021) confirms Sweeney gave this answer under Apple's cross-examination. "Epic's CEO testified" and the source "Tim Sweeney, Epic v. Apple trial, May 2021" are correct.
- **Skins, dances (emotes) and the Battle Pass (lines[3]).** This matches the court's findings.
- **March 2026 V-Bucks change (lines[4], lines[5]).** Epic announced it on Mar 10, 2026, and it took effect Mar 19, 2026. The $8.99 pack went from 1,000 to 800 V-Bucks, and the Battle Pass went from 1,000 to 800 V-Bucks. Several outlets confirm this: Shacknews, Insider Gaming, Nintendo Wire, Mein-MMO and Gamesmarket. (fortnite.com could not be reached from this session.) Math: 1,000 / 800 = 1.25, and $0.00899 to $0.01124 per V-Buck is +25%. Correct.
- **Over $1B paid to creators since 2023 (lines[6]).** Epic said this at State of Unreal, June 17, 2026. GamesBeat, Tubefilter, PocketGamer.biz and 80.lv all report it. Epic said all of that money went to third-party creators.
- **FTC, Dec 19, 2022 (lines[7]).** The total was $520M: a $275M COPPA penalty plus $245M in refunds over alleged dark patterns and billing practices (FTC, AP/PBS, CBS, NBC). The line attributes the claims to the FTC ("FTC claims"), which is correct. "Tricky buy buttons" fairly sums up the FTC's allegation about confusing button setups that led to unwanted charges.
- There is no advice and no price prediction. The hook is true, and the cover and title are paid off in the video.
