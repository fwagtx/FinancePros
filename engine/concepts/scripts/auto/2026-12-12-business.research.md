## 1. Tuition: sticker vs net price (College Board, Trends in College Pricing and Student Aid 2025)
- Public 4-year, in-state, published tuition & fees 2025-26: **$11,950**, up $340 (2.9% before inflation) from $11,610 in 2024-25. (College Board highlights + newsroom release)
- Private nonprofit 4-year, published tuition & fees 2025-26: **$45,000**, up $1,750 (4.0% before inflation). (College Board highlights)
- Public 4-year out-of-state: about $31,880 (2025-26; Yahoo Finance citing College Board) - secondary only.
- Public 2-year in-district: $4,150 (2025-26).
- Net tuition & fees (after grant aid and federal tax credits/deductions), first-time full-time students, 2025-26 ESTIMATES:
  - Public 4-year in-state: **about $2,300** (peaked at $4,450 in 2012-13, in 2025 dollars). (College Board report; Higher Ed Dive)
  - Private nonprofit 4-year: **$16,910** (down from $19,810 in 2006-07, in 2025 dollars). (College Board report; Higher Ed Dive)
- Total grant aid to students, 2024-25: $173.7 billion (+5.4% after inflation). (College Board)
- Average total budgets 2025-26: public 4-yr in-state $30,990; private nonprofit 4-yr $65,470. (College Board highlights)
- Inflation-adjusted public 4-year in-state tuition fell about 7% from 2015-16 to 2025-26. (College Board newsroom)

## 2. Housing and food (auxiliary enterprises)
- College Board 2025-26 average on-campus housing & food: **public 4-year $13,900**, **private nonprofit 4-year $15,920** (each up 4.4%). Source: release text reproducing College Board 2025 data (Stuart Martin Chamber news release 11/19/2025) + multiple College Board-citing guides. Official PDF not fetched directly; one low-quality site gives $13,420/$15,630 (rejected).
- Housing, dining, bookstores = "auxiliary enterprises" in IPEDS: self-supporting businesses run by the college (definition per Utah Board of Higher Education report).
- National auxiliary share of revenue: NOT CONFIRMED for a recent year (Hartwick College, one private college, ~13-16% of revenue, mostly room & board - single institution, do not generalize).

## 3. Revenue mix (NCES Condition of Education / IPEDS)
- Latest year confirmed via search: 2020-21 (pandemic-distorted). Total revenue at degree-granting institutions ~$993B (constant 2021-22 dollars).
  - Public: government grants/contracts/appropriations ~40% of revenue (2020-21).
  - Private nonprofit: investments ~46% of revenue in 2020-21 (up 36 points from 2019-20) - a market-boom year, not typical.
  - Private for-profit: tuition & fees 93%.
- 2021-22 and 2022-23 shares: NOT CONFIRMED (couldn't reach NCES tables). Not used in script.

## 4. Donors and endowments (2025 NACUBO-Commonfund Study of Endowments, FY2025 = July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025; released Feb 2026)
- 657 U.S. institutions, **$944.3 billion** in endowment assets.
- Average one-year return FY2025: **10.9%** net of fees (FY2024: 11.2%). 10-year annualized: 7.7%.
- Median endowment: **$253.6 million**.
- Average effective spending rate: **4.9%** (FY2024: 4.8%).
- Withdrawals for spending: $33.4 billion in FY2025, up ~11% from $30.1B in FY2024.
Sources: NACUBO 2025 NCSE summary PDF (edge.sitecorecloud.io NACUBO media), AI-CIO, Institutional Investor, TIFF.

## 5. Endowment excise tax (2025 reconciliation law, Pub. L. 119-21)
- Old rule (2017 law): flat 1.4% on net investment income for private colleges with 500+ tuition-paying students and $500,000+ endowment per student.
- New rule, tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2025: tiered by "student adjusted endowment" (assets per student):
  - $500,000-$750,000 per student: 1.4%
  - $750,000-$2 million: 4%
  - over $2 million: **8%**
- Student threshold raised from 500 to **3,000** tuition-paying students (smaller colleges drop out).
- Sources: Aon, O'Melveny, CASRAI guide; consistent. (Instead.com's "21%" four-tier = House draft, not enacted; rejected.) Open: per-student denominator details pending Treasury/IRS guidance.

## WORKED EXAMPLES (exact math)
1. Sticker vs net, average private nonprofit freshman, 2025-26 (College Board averages, net is an estimate):
   $45,000 sticker - $16,910 net = **$28,090** covered by grants and tax benefits. $16,910 / $45,000 = 0.3758 = **about 38%** of sticker paid.
2. Endowment payout example, median endowment FY2025:
   $253.6M x 4.9% = $12.4264M = **about $12.4 million** a year for the budget.

## SURPRISE FACT
- The average first-year private nonprofit student pays about 38% of the $45,000 sticker in tuition and fees (2025-26 estimate). At public 4-year in-state, net tuition averages about $2,300 vs an $11,950 sticker. Colleges' "price" is a list price that grants discount heavily - while housing and food ($13,900 / $15,920) gets far less discounting.
