## How Trump Accounts work (530A accounts)

- Open to any US child **under 18** with a Social Security number.
- Parents, grandparents and others can contribute up to **$5,000 per child per year** combined, after-tax; limit indexed for inflation after 2027.
- Children born **2025 through 2028** may get a one-time **$1,000** Treasury deposit (seed).
- Account must be **claimed** to get the $1,000 seed and family/employer contributions; **auto-enrolled children can receive philanthropic gifts without a parent claiming** (TrumpAccounts.gov via CNBC).
- Treasury temporary regulations (end of September 2026) let the accounts hold **stock donations**.
- **The catch (Treasury guidelines via CNBC):** families **cannot refuse** stock gifted to a Trump Account and **generally cannot sell it until the earlier of five years or the end of the calendar year the child turns 17.** CNBC notes this ties part of some kids' wealth to the performance of individual companies.
