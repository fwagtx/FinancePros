# Fact-check: 2027-01-22-business (Keurig razor-and-blades)
Checked Nov 23, 2026, for posting Fri Jan 22, 2027, 2 PM CT. This is an evergreen post.

## Verified claims
- **FY2025 U.S. Coffee net sales: $3,990M** (2024: $3,967M, +0.6%). Source: KDP FY2025 10-K, Q4/FY2025 release.
- **U.S. Coffee K-Cup pod net sales: $3,213M in 2025.** That is 80.5% of $3,990M, so "about 80%" is correct. Our math gives $3,990M - $3,213M = $777M for everything else ($582M appliances plus other). Source: FY2025 10-K revenue disaggregation.
- **Comparable segment series, 2021-2025:** $4.09B (recast $4,089M), $4.30B ($4,302M), $4.07B ($4,071M), $3.97B ($3,967M), $3.99B ($3,990M). The 2021 and 2022 figures come from the FY2023 10-K, which recast the segments. The 2023-2025 figures come from the FY2025 10-K. The pre-recast "Coffee Systems" figures ($4.72B, $4.98B) do NOT appear, so the chart is comparable.
- **Adjusted operating margin of 30.9% in 2025.** U.S. Coffee adjusted operating income fell 4.4% to $1,233M, which is 30.9% of net sales (2024: 32.5%). This is a non-GAAP figure and is labeled "adjusted" on screen and in the narration. The GAAP segment operating income was $962M (24.1%). One search summary put $1,233M under U.S. Refreshment Beverages. That cannot be right: $1,233M / $10.4B is about 11.9%, while $1,233M / $3,990M is 30.9%. A second search of the release confirmed the figure is U.S. Coffee.
- **BLS ground coffee average of $9.46/lb.** The BLS series APU0000717311 (100% ground roast, all sizes, U.S. city average) shows $9.459 for **February 2026**. The month and year are now on screen.
- **Pod price math:** $1.97/oz x 16 = $31.52/lb, which is correct. There is no dated retail source for this price, so it is labeled "example" in the narration, on screen ("Pods, example") and in the captions.
- **Keurig 2.0 (2014) rejected unlicensed pods.** This is true and widely reported.
- **Brewer and accessory sales fell 23% in fiscal Q2 2015** (quarter ended March 28, 2015). Keurig mainly blamed a 22% drop in brewer volume, high retail inventory and a tough comparison with the prior year. In coverage of the May 2015 call, the CEO also pointed to consumer confusion about which pods were compatible. The source does not support plain causation, so the wording now states only the timing.
- **JDE Peet's deal:** KDP's acquisition closed April 1, 2026, with 96.22% of shares tendered. The planned tax-free spin-off of "Global Coffee Co." is expected in **early 2027**, so it could happen before or right after Jan 22, 2027. The wording has to stay true in both cases.

## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 6, stale-news risk:** "...bought JDE Peet's, planning a coffee spinoff" would be wrong if the spin-off finishes in early 2027. The line now reads: "In 2026, Keurig's parent closed its JDE Peet's deal, part of a plan to spin coffee off." (spoken: "In twenty twenty-six, Keurig's parent closed its JDE Peet's deal, part of a plan to spin coffee off."). This is true whether or not the split has happened by then.
2. **Line 5, implied causation:** "By early 2015, brewer and accessory sales fell 23%" read as cause and effect. It now reads: "In one early-2015 quarter, brewer and accessory sales fell 23%." Captions add "fiscal Q2 2015 ... from a year earlier."
3. **Line 3, labels and awkward reading:** The on-screen labels did not show the BLS month or that the pod price is an example. They are now "Ground, Feb '26" and "Pods, example". The narration ended on "thirty-one fifty-two" with no unit. It now ends "Thirty-one fifty-two a pound." Spoken: "Ground coffee averaged nine forty-six a pound in February twenty twenty-six. Example pods at a dollar ninety-seven an ounce? Thirty-one fifty-two a pound."
4. **Line 1, segment clarity:** The on-screen text now says "U.S. Coffee segment sales hovered near $4B a year..." Spoken: "US coffee sales hovered near four billion a year, hitting three point nine nine billion in twenty twenty-five." I cut "have" to save words.
5. **Line 2, trimmed:** "Razor and blades: pods were three point two one billion. Brewers and the rest, seven hundred seventy-seven million." The on-screen values match the spoken ones.
6. **Captions (IG and YT):** Added "(non-GAAP)" to the margin, "fiscal Q2 2015, from a year earlier" to the 23% figure, the BLS month, the "example" pod label, the JDE line in its time-proof form, and the Q4 2025 release as a source.

## Other checks (no change needed)
- **Hook:** "About eighty percent of Keurig's US coffee sales are pods, not machines." It is 12 words, under 3 seconds, true (80.5%) and specific, and it makes the viewer want to know why. Acceptable. A punchier alternative if wanted: "Keurig's real money isn't the machine. Eighty percent is pods."
- **Cover and title:** "Pods Pay Keurig" / ~80% is paid off by lines 0-4.
- No stock commentary, price predictions or political opinion. The takeaway ("price a year of refills") is a consumer-shopping tip, not investment advice.
- **Time words:** "in 2025", "Feb 2026", "2014", "early-2015" and "In 2026" are all fixed dates, so they will still be correct on Jan 22, 2027.
- Every on-screen value matches the spoken value. The math checks out: 3.99 - 3.21 = 0.78 (exactly $777M), 3,213/3,990 = 80.5%, and 1.97 x 16 = 31.52.
- **Checker:** OK, 9 lines, 140 spoken words, 12-word hook, valid JSON. The last line is unchanged.
- **Minor:** The chart color is "down" for a roughly flat series ($4.09B to $3.99B). That is acceptable but could be "neutral".
