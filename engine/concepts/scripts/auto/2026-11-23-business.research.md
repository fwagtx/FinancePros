# Research: How robo-advisors make money (2026-11-23-business)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- Wealthfront (Nasdaq: WLTH; IPO Dec 2025). Fiscal 2026 (year ended Jan 31, 2026; release Mar 11, 2026): revenue $365.0M; cash management revenue $271.7M (+18%); investment advisory revenue $91.9M (+26%); other $1.4M. Platform assets $94.1B. Cash share = 271.7/365.0 = 74.4%.
- S-1 (2025): cash management revenue was ~71% (FY2024), ~75% (FY2025), ~76% (six months to Jul 31, 2025) of total revenue. FY2025 revenue $308.9M; FY2024 $216.7M. Wealthfront charges 0.25% a year on managed investment accounts. Annualized cash management fee ~0.60% as of July 31, 2025 (program banks pay Wealthfront a fee on swept deposits; clients receive the rest of the rate).
- Betterment: 0.25% a year (digital) on qualifying balances; flat monthly fee for small balances.
- Schwab Intelligent Portfolios: no advisory fee; SEC settlement June 13, 2022: $187M ($135M penalty + $52M disgorgement/interest) over disclosures about cash allocations (2015-2018); Schwab neither admitted nor denied.

## Worked example
$10,000 managed at 0.25% = $25/yr. $10,000 in cash at ~0.60% fee = $60/yr kept by the platform.
