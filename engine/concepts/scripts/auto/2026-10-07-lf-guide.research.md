## Rules (official)
- CARD Act minimum payment warning (Reg Z): each statement must show how long payoff takes at the minimum, total cost, and the monthly payment to pay off in 36 months; plus a credit counseling phone number. Wording: "If you make only the minimum payment each period, you will pay more in interest and it will take you longer to pay off your balance."
- Payment allocation, 12 CFR 1026.53: any amount above the minimum must go first to the balance with the highest APR (exception: last two billing cycles of a deferred-interest promo).
- Payment late if not received by 5 p.m. on the due date (CFPB). Reported to bureaus once 30+ days late; can stay up to 7 years (CFPB via Experian).
- Penalty APR on the existing balance allowed only once 60 days late (Reg Z 1026.55(b)(4)).
- Balance transfer fee: typically 3%-5% of the amount moved, and it can be charged on a 0% offer (CFPB Ask CFPB).
- Debt management plans (CFPB/NFCC): nonprofit credit counseling; one monthly payment to the agency, which pays creditors; creditors may lower rates or waive fees. Typical fees: setup $75 or less, monthly about $25-$50 (NCOA/NFCC).
- Debt settlement: FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule (effective Oct 27, 2010) bans debt relief companies from charging fees before they settle or reduce a debt.
- FICO: amounts owed = 30% of score; credit utilization is a big part (myFICO). Closing a card can raise utilization.

## Worked examples (computed in python)
1. Minimum-only trap: $5,000 at 22% APR, minimum = 1% of balance + interest, $25 floor, no new charges: 230 months (19 years 2 months), $8,099.77 interest (~$8,100). First minimum = $50 + $91.67 interest = $141.67.
   - Fix the payment at $141.67 instead: 58 months, $3,121.28 interest.
   - 36-month payoff: $190.95/month, $1,874.28 interest.
   - First month interest on $5,000 at 22%: $91.67.
2. Avalanche vs snowball, $8,000 over three cards, $400/month budget, minimums fixed:
   - Card A $900 at 19% (min $35); Card B $2,600 at 29% (min $80); Card C $4,500 at 22% (min $110). Minimums $225, extra $175.
   - Avalanche (highest APR first: B, C, A): debt-free month 26, interest $2,155.51. First card gone month 12.
   - Snowball (smallest balance first: A, B, C): debt-free month 26, interest $2,263.62. First card gone month 5.
   - Difference: $108.11.
3. Balance transfer: $5,000 moved, 3% fee = $150; 0% for 18 months -> $286.11/month to clear it. Same $286.11/month at 22%: 22 months, $1,083.18 interest.
4. Personal loan at 11.86% for 24 months: $235.04/month, $640.97 interest. Card at 22% over 24 months: $259.39/month, $1,225.38 interest.

## Surprising fact
Paying only the minimum on $5,000 at 22% takes about 19 years and costs about $8,100 in interest, more than the original balance. Freezing the first minimum payment ($141.67) cuts that to under 5 years and about $3,121.
