RBI pledged 46.91 t, sent by air in four consignments starting July 4, 1991, to the Bank of England (London), which handled the bars. 46.91 rounds to 47. It is 11 spoken words (limit 12) and specific, and it makes the viewer ask why. It runs about 3.5 s, slightly over 3 s. Optional tighter version: TEXT "In 1991, India pawned 47 tonnes of gold." / SAY "In nineteen ninety-one, India pawned forty-seven tonnes of gold." Line 1 would then become "It flew it to London. Rangarajan, "1991's golden transaction" (mse.ac.in PDF); MoF Rajya/Lok Sabha reply AU858 (sansad.in): "RBI in July 1991 pledged 46.91 tonnes of gold with the Bank of Japan and the Bank of England and raised a loan of US$ 405.0 million".
