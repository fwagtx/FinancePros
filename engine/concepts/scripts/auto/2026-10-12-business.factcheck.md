## Issues

1. **Line 9 (fact, must fix): "Over 60% of new sign-ups pick ads" is missing its scope.** Netflix's Q1 2026 letter says the ads plan was over 60% of sign-ups *in countries where the ads plan is offered* (12 markets), not all sign-ups globally. As written it overgeneralizes.
   Fix: text "In ad-plan countries, over 60% of new sign-ups pick ads..." / say "In countries with the ad plan, over sixty percent of new sign-ups pick it..." Apply the same fix to the IG caption.

2. **Line 5 (attribution): the news-card headline is not CNBC's real headline.** CNBC's Apr 19, 2022 article was titled "Netflix shares crater 25% after company reports it lost subscribers for the first time in more than 10 years". The wording shown ("...loses 200,000 subscribers in its first quarter...") matches a CNBC *video* from Apr 20, 2022. Fix: use the actual headline, or keep the text and change the source to "CNBC, April 2022" and present it as a summary rather than a headline.

3. **Line 8 (framing): "an $11 gap ... for ads to fill"** reads as fact that ads must make up $11 per member. Netflix does not disclose ad revenue per member. Fix: frame it as a hypothetical, e.g. "To match Standard, ads would need to bring in about $11 a month per member", or put "if" in the line.

4. **Line 4 (minor, attribution plus spoken/on-screen mismatch).** "Fees took revenue from..." gives fees all the credit, but 2025 includes about $1.5B of ads. Suggest "Mostly fees: revenue went from...". The spoken line also leaves out "in 2025" at the end, which the on-screen text has. Add "in twenty twenty-five" to the say line.

5. **Line 7 (minor, time words):** the scene title "US prices, March 2026" can look out of date in October. Change it to "US prices since March 2026". "Now" is accurate on the posting date (no US price change since March 26, 2026).

6. **Opening (line 1):** it is true and specific, and the contrast works. But the narration runs about 14 words, roughly 4 to 5 seconds, which is over 3 seconds. The 2025 timeframe only arrives in line 2. Stronger: "Netflix made forty-five billion last year. Ads? Just three percent." Or lead with the tension: "Netflix pushes ads everywhere, but they're just three percent of its money."

7. **Staleness risk:** all figures are FY2025 plus 2026 guidance. Netflix's Q3 2026 earnings are expected around mid-October, likely after Oct 12. If the posting slips past that report, recheck the $3B target and the 60% figure.

8. **Post sources (minor):** the 60% sign-up stat comes from the Q1 2026 letter. Add "Q1 2026" to the sources list in the IG caption.
