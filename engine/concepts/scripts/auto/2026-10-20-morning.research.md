## The story: GM Q3 2026 (GM release via PR Newswire, Oct 20, 2026; Investing.com, NewsBytes, Stocktitan summaries)
- Q3 2026 (July-September) revenue: $50.0 billion ($50.02B). Consensus ~$48.7B.
- Q3 2026 net income attributable to stockholders: $3.3 billion.
- Q3 2026 EBIT-adjusted: $4.1 billion ($4.11B; one source says consensus $4.24B, i.e. slightly below).
- Q3 2026 EPS-diluted-adjusted: $3.70 vs consensus ~$3.63 (another source cites $3.69 consensus).
- Year ago, Q3 2025 (GM release Oct 21, 2025): revenue $48.6B ($48,591M), net income attributable to stockholders $1.3B ($1,327M), EBIT-adjusted $3.4B, EPS-adjusted $2.80.
- Full-year 2026 guidance: EBIT-adjusted narrowed to $14.5-15.5B (from $14.0-16.0B); adjusted automotive free cash flow raised to $11.5-12.5B (from $9.5-11.5B); EPS-diluted $9.72-$10.72.
- Buyback: board approved a new multi-year $20.0 billion share repurchase authorization with no expiration date; GM completed the $6.0B program announced January 2026. Release says the program may be suspended or discontinued at any time at the company's discretion.
- Dividend: $0.18 per share quarterly, payable Dec 17.
- Context: GM 2026 gross tariff cost guidance (July) $2.5-3.5B, lowered after a Supreme Court tariff ruling (~$0.5B favorable adjustment). Q3 tariff update not confirmed.

## Simple math (derived)
- $20B / $3.3B Q3 net income = about 6.1 -> "about six times last quarter's profit."
- Shares outstanding fell from ~1.2B (end 2023) to 849M (Sep 30, 2026): about 29% fewer (rounded 2023 base).
- Fewer shares -> each share's claim on profit rises; earnings per share can rise even if total profit is flat (definition).

## What it means for a normal person
- GM is an S&P 500 company, so S&P 500 index funds (common in 401(k)s) hold a small piece of it.
- A buyback sends cash to selling shareholders and shrinks the share count; remaining owners each hold a bigger slice. The cash leaves the company; the program can be paused.
