How scratch-off tickets are priced: odds, prizes and the state's cut

## Worked example (real, official): Texas Lottery Game No. 2764, "Million Dollar Loteria", $20 ticket
Source: Texas Lottery game page (details.html_252698586) and game procedures PDF (2764procedures.pdf), as surfaced in search, September 2026. Game was active as of September 2026.
- Price: $20.00 per ticket
- Tickets in game: approximately 25,701,775
- Overall odds of winning any prize: 1 in 3.29
- "Over $385.3 million in total prizes"
- Prize table (amount: number of prizes): $1,000,000: 10; $20,000: 43; $5,000: 343; $1,000: 2,581; $500: 12,035; $250: 66,748; $200: 185,051; $150: 205,886; $100: 919,945; $50: 1,028,071; $40: 514,059; $30: 1,799,106; $20: 3,084,150

Math (my calculation from the official table):
- Sales if every ticket sells: 25,701,775 x $20 = $514,035,500 (about $514.0 million)
- Total prizes: sum of table = $385,370,190 (matches "over $385.3 million"; about $385.4 million)
- Payout: 385,370,190 / 514,035,500 = 74.97% (about 75%)
- Average prize per $20 ticket: 385,370,190 / 25,701,775 = $14.99 (about $15). Average loss per ticket about $5.
- Winning tickets: 7,818,028. 25,701,775 / 7,818,028 = 3.29 (matches printed odds)
- $20 "wins" (just your money back): 3,084,150 = 39.4% of all winning tickets (about 39%)
- Winning MORE than $20: 4,733,878 tickets, odds 1 in 5.43
- Top prize: 10 x $1,000,000 in 25.7 million tickets, about 1 in 2.57 million

SURPRISING FACT: The "1 in 3.29" odds on the ticket count breaking even as a win. About 39% of all "winning" tickets in this game just hand back the $20. The odds of getting more than your money back are 1 in 5.43.

Source: Minnesota Lottery official blog, "Scratch Game Payouts by Price Point" (mnlottery.com/blog/scratch-game-payouts-by-price-point-2). Post date not confirmed (search metadata suggests about 2022). Percent of sales expected to be paid out in prizes over the life of a game:

Their example: $10 million in $5 tickets sold pays about $6.8 million in prizes (68%). Pattern: pricier tickets pay back a bigger share.

## Where a lottery dollar goes: Texas Lottery, FY2025 (Sept 1, 2024 to Aug 31, 2025), all games
Source: Texas Lottery news release October 27, 2025, "Texas Lottery Delivers $1.8 Billion to the State" (also NASPL Insights, Public Gaming Research Institute, Dallas Morning News Oct 30, 2025).
- Total sales: $7.91 billion
- Scratch ticket sales: $6.434 billion, more than 81% of sales; ranked third nationally
- $20 price point led scratch sales: $1.505 billion (more than 23% of scratch)
- Prizes: $5.38 billion = 68.03% of sales (release calls it a record)
- Transferred to the state: $1.8 billion, including $1.77 billion to the Foundation School Fund (public education) plus Fund for Veterans' Assistance and other programs; 22nd straight year over $1 billion
- Retailer commissions and bonuses: more than $426.6 million (about 5.4% of sales)
- Administrative expenses: over $289 million = 3.65% of sales
- Per $1 (my math): prizes 68.0 cents; state 1.8/7.91 = 22.8 cents (about 23); retailers 5.4 cents (about 5); admin 3.65 cents (about 4). Sum about 100.
