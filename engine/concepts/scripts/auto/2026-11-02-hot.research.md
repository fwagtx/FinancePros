# Research brief: Face-off, leasing vs buying a car (posts Nov 2, 2026)

## Data (Experian, State of the Automotive Finance Market, Q2 2026)
- Average new-vehicle lease payment: $617/month (+0.7% YoY).
- Average new-vehicle loan payment: $765/month (+2.1% YoY).
- Average amount financed for a new vehicle: $43,610.
- Gap: $765 - $617 = $148/month.

## Lease terms (industry norms: CarsDirect, Toyota Financial glossary, others)
- Mileage limits commonly 10,000, 12,000 or 15,000 miles a year.
- Excess mileage typically $0.15-$0.25 per mile (range $0.10-$0.30).
- Disposition (turn-in) fee typically about $300-$500.
- At lease end you return the car (or buy it at the residual price); no ownership equity.

## Worked example (illustrative, using Q2 2026 averages)
- 36 months of lease payments: 36 x $617 = $22,212 (before fees); you own nothing at the end.
- 36 months of loan payments: 36 x $765 = $27,540; you keep the car and any equity (value minus loan balance).
- Payment difference over 3 years: $27,540 - $22,212 = $5,328.
- Mileage risk: 36,000 allowed (12k/yr); drive 39,000 -> 3,000 x $0.25 = $750.
- Surprise: the lease's lower payment buys use, not ownership; the cheaper monthly number isn't the same as cheaper overall.
