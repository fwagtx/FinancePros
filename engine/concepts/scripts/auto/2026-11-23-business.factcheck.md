## Newer results that weaken the current framing

- **Q1 FY2027** (quarter ended Apr 30, 2026; released Jun 4, 2026): revenue $90.5M, cash $63.4M (down 1% from a year earlier), advisory $26.2M (up 32%). Cash share was 70%. The cash fee rate was 58 bps.
- **Q2 FY2027** (quarter ended Jul 31, 2026; released Sep 9, 2026): revenue $91.9M, cash $61.8M (down 10%), advisory $28.8M (up 31%). Cash share was 67%. The cash fee rate was **55 bps, down 6 bps** from a year earlier. Management blamed Fed rate cuts and promotional incentives.
- Q3 FY2027 results are expected around January 11, 2027, after this video posts. So on November 23, FY2026 is still the latest full year and Q2 FY2027 is the latest quarter.

**What this means for the script:** The FY2026 numbers are accurate and clearly dated, so using them is fine. But citing the July 2025 fee of 0.60% when a July 2026 rate of 0.55% has been published is stale. Saying nothing about the shift toward advisory revenue, while calling cash a "secret," leaves an outdated picture. The post title's present tense ("74% of revenue is cash") is wrong for the latest quarter, where cash was 67%.
