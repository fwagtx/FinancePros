## Hook (true as stated)
"The molecule behind Zepbound earned $16 billion in three months."
- Tirzepatide is sold as Mounjaro (type 2 diabetes) and Zepbound (obesity). Q3 2026 (Jul-Sep): $10,723M + $5,352M = $16,075M (our math) = $16.1B. (Lilly Q3 2026 release.)

## Surprise facts
1. Same molecule, two brands, two prices: semaglutide = Ozempic (diabetes, 2017) + Wegovy (obesity, 2021); tirzepatide = Mounjaro (diabetes, May 2022) + Zepbound (obesity, Nov 2023). Approvals: month/year per FDA tracker summaries (Prime Therapeutics GLP-1 pipeline tables; TechTarget).
2. The drug class traces to Gila monster venom: John Eng (VA) isolated exendin-4 in 1992; it became exenatide (Byetta), the first GLP-1 drug, FDA-approved 2005 (VA research coverage via Military.com; UNC Endeavors; Biotech Academy). Semaglutide and tirzepatide are engineered versions of human gut hormones, not lizard venom: say "the class traces back to".
3. Eli Lilly became the first drugmaker worth $1 trillion on Nov 21, 2025, driven by obesity/diabetes drugs (Reuters via Yahoo Finance; Al Jazeera, Nov 21, 2025). Mention only as context, not a stock comment.
4. Tirzepatide (Mounjaro + Zepbound) passed Merck's Keytruda in Q3 2025 as the world's top-selling drug by quarterly sales (STAT, Oct 30, 2025; BioCentury). FY2025: ~$36.5B vs Keytruda ~$31.7B (pharmacompass; secondary). Not used as hook because semaglutide total (all brands) is close in size.
5. Novo Nordisk is controlled by a charity: the Novo Nordisk Foundation (via Novo Holdings) holds ~28% of share capital but ~77% of votes, through A shares with 10x votes (Novo corporate governance page, Nov 2024; Novo Nordisk Foundation ownership page).

## How the drugs work (attributed, no medical advice)
- GLP-1 is a gut hormone released after eating; it helps regulate blood sugar, slows stomach emptying and signals fullness to the brain (UAB research spotlight; medical explainers). Natural GLP-1 lasts minutes; semaglutide/tirzepatide are modified to last about a week (weekly shot).
- Tirzepatide hits two hormone receptors (GLP-1 and GIP); semaglutide hits one (GLP-1).
- Trials (not head-to-head): STEP 1 (Wilding et al., NEJM 2021): semaglutide 2.4 mg, mean weight loss 14.9% vs 2.4% placebo at 68 weeks. SURMOUNT-1 (Jastreboff et al., NEJM 2022): tirzepatide 15 mg, 20.9% vs 3.1% placebo at 72 weeks.
- Limits: most common side effects on Zepbound label: nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal pain (Zepbound prescribing information, FDA/Lilly; Drugs.com summary Aug 2026); Wegovy label similar GI profile. STEP 1 extension (Wilding et al., 2022): one year after stopping semaglutide, participants regained about two-thirds of the weight they had lost (Epocrates summary; Pharmacy Magazine).

## Prices (US, monthly; list price is before rebates; most patients do not pay list)
- Wegovy list $1,349.02/month; Ozempic list about $1,028 (Novo release / coverage, Feb 2026). From Jan 1, 2027: $675 list for both (Novo).
- Zepbound list about $1,060-$1,086/month (sources vary; Noom, SingleCare 2026). Lilly self-pay Zepbound vials (LillyDirect) from Dec 1, 2025: $299 (2.5 mg), $399 (5 mg), $449 (7.5-15 mg) (Fox Business; Lilly).
- Nov 6, 2025 agreements between the Trump administration and Lilly/Novo: Medicare/Medicaid price $245/month for Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound; $50 copay for eligible Medicare patients (mid-2026 pilot); TrumpRx cash price $350 or less trending to $245 over two years; starting doses of pills $149 if approved (Al Jazeera, Axios, CBS, Nov 6, 2025). Neutral framing only.
- Medicare negotiated "maximum fair price" for Ozempic/Wegovy/Rybelsus effective Jan 1, 2027: $274/month (vs 2024 list $959) (CMS via HealthDay/Becker's, Feb 2026).
- Estimated cost-based price to make semaglutide: $0.89-$4.73 per month (Barber et al., JAMA Network Open, March 2024; Yale; Fortune Mar 28, 2024). Estimate built from public data; Novo did not provide production costs. Excludes R&D, trials, factories.

## Shortages and compounding
- Demand outran supply 2022-2024. FDA declared tirzepatide shortage over (Oct 2024; confirmed Dec 19, 2024) and semaglutide shortage resolved Feb 21, 2025, ending broad copycat compounding after grace periods (FDA via BioPharma Dive, Fierce Healthcare, McDermott, 2024-2025).

## Patents / competition
- Semaglutide core patents expired/lapsed in 2026 in Canada (lapsed after missed fee; generics from mid-2026), China (March 20, 2026, ~17 generics in phase 3), India (March 2026), Brazil (~March 2026) (Reddie & Grose, Feb 2026; GeneOnline; Benefits and Pensions Monitor, 2026). Novo cut India prices after generics.
- US: main semaglutide patent ~Dec 2031-2032 (RxIndex, I-MAK); tirzepatide US compound patent 2036 (I-MAK; RxIndex).
