# Fact-check: 2026-10-31 Big Story (ACA 2027 open enrollment)

Checked Sat Oct 31, 2026, ~1 PM CT, using web search excerpts (several sources agree unless noted). Posting 4 PM CT today.

## Verified (no change needed)
- Open enrollment for 2027 coverage starts Sun Nov 1, 2026 in most states (HealthCare.gov guides, Bakersfield Now/Sinclair citing KFF, healthcareinsider). "Tomorrow" is correct for a Saturday Oct 31 post.
- KFF: finalized 2027 benchmark silver premiums up about 13% on average; 11% in HealthCare.gov states, 16% in state-based platforms; second straight double-digit year (2026 was about +26%). (KFF "9 Things to Watch for the 2027 ACA Open Enrollment Period"; Peterson-KFF tracker; Sinclair stations.) Insurers had proposed a 15% median.
- Average benchmark for a 40-year-old: $625/mo in 2026, $497 in 2025 (KFF state indicator via Becker's, Axis; +26%). "This year" = 2026 is correct.
- Our math: $625 x 1.13 = $706.25, so "about $706" is right and labeled "our math" on screen and in speech. It is an illustration (national average increase applied to the 2026 average), not a forecast; acceptable as labeled.
- Premium tax credits are tied to the benchmark, so subsidies generally rise with it; unsubsidized buyers pay the full price (KFF).
- Enhanced credits expired after 2025; the 400% FPL eligibility cap applies again for 2027 (KFF, Medical Daily "Enhanced Subsidies Stay Expired"). The script doesn't comment on politics or say who's to blame, so it's neutral.
- Dec 15, 2026 is the HealthCare.gov deadline for Jan 1, 2027 coverage (multiple guides). Some state exchanges run later (for example CA, MD and WA list Dec 31 for Jan 1 coverage). The final end date (Jan 15) is disputed in court. The script correctly avoids stating it.
- Header, cover (+13%, 2027), post title: paid off by the video.
