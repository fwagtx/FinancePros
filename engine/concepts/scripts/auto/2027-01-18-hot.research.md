# Research brief: Face-off, HMO vs PPO health plans (posts Jan 18, 2027; evergreen)

Note: kff.org, files.kff.org and healthcare.gov were blocked from direct fetch in this session; figures below were confirmed via web search results quoting KFF pages and KFF-based summaries. Flagged items are marked UNCERTAIN.

## Definitions (HealthCare.gov plan-types page / glossary)
- HMO (Health Maintenance Organization): usually limits coverage to doctors who work for or contract with the HMO; generally won't cover out-of-network care except in an emergency. Referrals to specialists are often required (secondary glossaries; plans vary).
- PPO (Preferred Provider Organization): you pay less if you use providers in the plan's network; you can use doctors, hospitals and providers outside the network without a referral for an additional cost (HealthCare.gov plan-types page wording, confirmed via search).
- KFF counts EPOs inside its HMO category.

## Enrollment share of covered workers (KFF Employer Health Benefits Survey)
| Year | PPO | HMO | HDHP/SO | POS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 48% | 15% | 29% | 9% |
| 2019 | 44% | 19% | 30% | 7% |
| 2022 | 49% | 12% | 29% | 9% |
| 2025 | 46% | 12% | 33% | 9% |
| 2026 | 50% | 12% | 29% (HSA-qualified) | 8% |
- 2026 caution: KFF changed its classification in 2026 (HSA-qualified plans separate; HDHPs with an HRA now counted as PPO/HMO/POS), so 2026 shares are NOT directly comparable to prior years.
- CHARTABLE SERIES (evenly spaced, consistent method): HMO share 2016 15%, 2019 19%, 2022 12%, 2025 12%. PPO share same years: 48, 44, 49, 46.

## Premiums (KFF 2026 EHBS, published Oct 22, 2026)
- Average annual premium, all plans: $9,804 single, $28,303 family (each up 5% from 2025).
- PPO average premium: $10,272 single, $29,522 family (KFF: "higher than the overall average premiums for both single and family coverage").
- Worker contribution, all plans: $1,672 single, $7,471 family (family up 9%).
- 2025 (KFF 2025 EHBS): all plans $9,325 single / $26,993 family; PPO $9,818 / $28,272; HDHP/SO $8,620 / $25,379.
- UNCERTAIN: HMO-specific premium. A KFF 2025 Figure 1.1 extract shows an HMO row of $9,229 / $27,277 but column labels unclear; no 2026 HMO premium confirmed. NOT used in script.

## Deductibles (KFF 2026)
- Average single deductible (workers in plans with a deductible): $1,955 in 2026 vs $1,886 in 2025; up 18% over 5 years.
- UNCERTAIN: by plan type. Secondary tables show PPO ~$1,337-1,446 and HMO ~$1,562-1,649 single deductibles, labels unclear. NOT used in script. Many HMOs have no general deductible.

## Worked example (our math, KFF 2026 figures, total premium = employer + worker share)
- Single: PPO $10,272 - all-plan average $9,804 = $468 more per year.
- Family: PPO $29,522 - all-plan average $28,303 = $1,219 more per year.
(Compared against the all-plan average, which itself includes PPOs; not PPO vs HMO directly.)

## The surprising fact
- Even though PPOs cost more than average, about half of covered workers are in one (50% in 2026), while only about 1 in 8 (12%) are in an HMO, down from 19% in 2019.

## Trade-off framing (neutral)
- HMO: narrower network, referrals often required, no out-of-network coverage except emergencies.
- PPO: out-of-network coverage and no referrals, at a higher average premium.
- Education only; no recommendation.
