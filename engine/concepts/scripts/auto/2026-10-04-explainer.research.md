# Research brief: Index funds vs picking stocks, explained with acorns (posts Sun Oct 4, 2026)

Note: direct page fetches to spglobal.com / ici.org were blocked by the network proxy; every number below was confirmed through web search results quoting the official report, and cross-checked in at least two results where possible.

## Timely angle
- S&P Dow Jones Indices published the SPIVA U.S. Mid-Year 2026 scorecard in September 2026 (coverage dated Sept 23 and Sept 28, 2026).
- First half of 2026: 67% of active large-cap U.S. equity funds underperformed the S&P 500, even though conditions favored stock pickers: stock dispersion was the highest since 2008, market breadth widened, and the Magnificent 7 weight in the index slipped from 34.9% to 31.6%. S&P 500 was up about 10% in H1 2026.
- Same report: 74% of mid-cap and 69% of small-cap active funds underperformed in H1 2026.

## Key facts
- 2025 (full year): 79% of all active large-cap U.S. equity funds underperformed the S&P 500. Worse than 65% in 2024; the fourth-worst year in the 25-year history of SPIVA. (SPIVA U.S. Year-End 2025.)
- Longer horizons (year-end 2025): underperformance rates rise with time; over 15 years, more than 90% of large-cap active funds lagged the S&P 500 (reported ~92-93% over 20 years; exact figure not directly confirmed, so NOT used in the script).
- S&P 500 total return 2025: about 17.9% including dividends (First Trust recap, Jan 8 2026; other sources 17.4-17.9%). Not used on screen.

## Fees (ICI, "Trends in the Expenses and Fees of Funds, 2025", released March 25, 2026; asset-weighted)
- Actively managed equity mutual funds: 0.64% average expense ratio in 2025.
- Index equity mutual funds: 0.05% in 2025.
- Index equity ETFs: 0.14%. All equity mutual funds: 0.40%.

## Worked example (exact math)
Fee on $10,000 invested for one year:
- Active: $10,000 x 0.0064 = $64 per year
- Index: $10,000 x 0.0005 = $5 per year
- Difference: $64 - $5 = $59 per year, every year, before any performance difference. Active fund costs 64 / 5 = 12.8x as much.

## Surprising fact
Even in the first half of 2026, when conditions were about as good for stock pickers as they get (highest dispersion since 2008), 67% of big active funds still trailed the plain index.

## Acorn metaphor
- Picking stocks = hunting for the one perfect acorn (and paying a guide to hunt).
- Index fund = grab a little of every acorn in the forest (the whole basket), cheaply.
- Education only: some active funds do win in a given year; the data shows most do not, and the odds get worse over longer periods.
