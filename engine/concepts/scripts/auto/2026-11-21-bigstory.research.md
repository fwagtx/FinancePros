## Facts (all prices = AAA national average, regular unleaded)
- Nov 19, 2026 (Thursday, AAA weekly release "Gas Prices Keep Dropping as Thanksgiving Approaches"): $3.72/gal, down 18 cents in a week (from ~$3.90; AAA page lists $3.9081), and down from $4.30 a month earlier. Lowest since mid-March (March 16 per CNN).
- Year ago (Nov 2025): $3.09 (AAA via WRNJ). Gas "more than 60 cents higher than a year ago". Our math: $3.72 - $3.09 = 63 cents.
- Nov 20, 2026 (Friday) AAA daily national average: $3.6898 (24/7 Wall St, Nov 21; AAA daily). Prices lower in all 50 states vs a month earlier.
- Nov 5, 2026 AAA: $4.10, down 12 cents from the prior week -> Oct 29 approx $4.22.
- CNN (Matt Egan, Nov 19) analysis of AAA data: national average fell 50.5 cents between Oct 29 and Nov 19; outside the 2008 financial crisis it is the biggest three-week drop on record; ~12%, biggest percentage drop since early-2020 lockdowns. Prices fell 26 straight days after peaking Oct 24.
- 24/7 Wall St (Nov 21): EIA weekly series fell 46.4 cents in three weeks to Nov 16, biggest 3-week drop since 2008.
- Thanksgiving Day record (AAA): $3.58, set Nov 24, 2022. GasBuddy's own 2022 record: $3.558. EIA Thanksgiving-week high: $3.65 (2022).
- Gap: $3.69 (Nov 20) vs $3.58 record = about 11 cents (our math; 24/7 Wall St also says ~11 cents).
- AAA (Nov 19): the national average could drop another 15 cents by Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26), which would avoid setting a new record.
- GasBuddy forecast: $3.62 on Thanksgiving Day (vs $2.97 last year per GasBuddy) - would top its 2022 record of $3.558.
- WCVB (Nov 21) quoting AAA: if current prices hold, gas would be higher this Thanksgiving Day than any previous.
- Why falling: crude oil fell to the low-$80s per barrel (AAA), more production, less demand, switch to cheaper-to-make winter blend.
- Why high in the first place: national average was $2.98 on Feb 28, 2026 when US strikes on Iran began; above $4 from April to early November (AAA); 2026 peak week ~$4.56 (May 21).
- AAA Thanksgiving travel: 81.5M travelers Nov 24-30; ~72.9M by car ("nearly 73 million" road trips).
- Our math: 15-gallon tank at $3.72 = $55.80; at $3.09 = $46.35; difference $9.45.

## What it means
Fill-ups are getting cheaper every day, but still about $9 more per 15-gallon tank than last year. Whether Thanksgiving sets a record is down to ~11 cents.

## Watch next
AAA skips its weekly report on Thanksgiving Day (Nov 26). Risk: any re-escalation in the Iran conflict could reverse the slide (GasBuddy's De Haan).
