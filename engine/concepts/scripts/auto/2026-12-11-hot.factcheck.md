## Hook (line 0)
"Think your credit union money isn't insured? That's a myth." Literally defensible: ~98% of CUs are federally insured, and the ASI-insured minority are still insured (privately). But it reads as a blanket claim, and money above limits or in investments isn't insured. It's acceptable as written, because lines 6-7 add the caveats. Optional sharper and more precise wording (9 words, saves 1 spoken word): "Think credit union money isn't insured like bank money? Myth." The speech must match the on-screen text.

## Issues and fixes (0-based line indexes)

2. **Line 2, TTS risk:** a narrator can read "the US" as "us".
