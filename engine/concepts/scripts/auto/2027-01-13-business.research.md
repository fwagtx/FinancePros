# Research brief: How house flippers make money (2027-01-13-business)
Made Nov 14, 2026; posts Wed Jan 13, 2027, 2 PM CT. Evergreen: every number dated. Education only, never advice to flip or invest.

## Definitions (ATTOM, the standard source on US flipping)
- A "flip" = a single-family home or condo sold in an arms-length sale within 12 months of the previous sale (ATTOM methodology).
- Gross flipping profit = median resale price minus median purchase price paid by the investor. It does NOT include renovation (rehab) costs, mortgage interest, property taxes, insurance, selling costs or other holding expenses (ATTOM methodology, quoted in ATTOM releases and MPA Mag / HousingWire coverage). Real (net) profit is lower.
- Gross ROI / "profit margin" = gross profit divided by the purchase price.

## Key numbers (full year 2025; ATTOM Year-End 2025 Home Flipping Report, released March 19, 2026)
- Flips: 297,045 single-family homes and condos in 2025, down 3.9% from 309,050 in 2024; fewest since 2020.
- Flipping rate: 7.4% of all home sales in 2025 (7.6% in 2024).
- Median purchase price of flipped homes: $259,019. Median resale price: $325,000.
- Typical gross profit: $65,981 (2024: $77,000 per the 2025 report).
- Gross ROI: 25.5%, lowest since 2008 (2024: 32.1% as restated in the 2025 report).
- Average holding period: 163 days from purchase to resale (one day longer than 2024; 176 days in 2020). Q4 2025: 160 days.
- 37.7% of flips bought with financing (36.9% in 2024). Median flipped home built in 1978, oldest since ATTOM began tracking.
- Profit margins fell in 150 of 215 metros analyzed.
Sources: ATTOM release; HousingWire; RISMedia; The MortgagePoint (Mar 24, 2026); MyChesCo; MBA Newslink; MPA Mag; InvestmentNews.

## Latest quarter (ATTOM Q2 2026 Home Flipping Report, released Oct 1, 2026)
- Typical gross profit $60,526; gross margin (ROI) 21.5%. Q1 2026: $66,932 / 25.7%. Q2 2025: $71,000 / 27.6%.
- 77,991 flips = 6.2% of home sales (Q1 2026: 64,760, 8.0%; Q2 2025: 80,477, 7.3%).
- Typical flip took 161 days. Sweet spot: homes bought for $100K-$200K had typical margins of about 28%.
Sources: ATTOM release; MPA Mag; InvestmentNews; Wealth Professional; Webull/ATTOM wire copy.
- Q3 2026 flipping report NOT yet out as of Nov 14, 2026 (Q3 reports usually come in December). Q3 2025 for reference: margin 23.1%, 72,217 flips, 6.8% of sales.

## Worked example (exact math, ATTOM 2025 medians)
$325,000 resale - $259,019 purchase = $65,981 gross profit.
$65,981 / $259,019 = 0.2547 = 25.5% gross ROI.
Not yet subtracted: renovation, loan interest, property taxes, insurance, selling fees, over an average 163-day hold. (ATTOM 2020 coverage noted flippers commonly estimate these at 20-33% of after-repair value; secondary source only, NOT used on screen.)

## The surprising fact
The famous "flip profit" number is not profit: the typical 2025 flip's $66K gross excludes the renovation itself, plus loan interest, taxes and fees over about 5 months. And the gross return itself fell to its lowest since 2008 even though home prices were near records: investors pay more to buy, so the spread shrinks (ATTOM CEO Rob Barber: "Flippers are having to get more creative to maintain profitability").
