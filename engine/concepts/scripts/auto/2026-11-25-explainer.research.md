# Research: What money actually is: why squirrels agree acorns have value (2026-11-25-explainer, Acorn Economics)
Researched Oct 2, 2026.

## Facts
- Federal Reserve H.6 (Sept 22, 2026 release): M2 $23,342.8B (Aug 2026, seasonally adjusted), +5.7% y/y; currency in circulation $2,475.6B (Aug 2026). Cash share = 2,475.6/23,342.8 = 10.6%. Non-currency part = $20,867.2B.
- M2 December values (FRED M2SL): 2022 $21.27T; 2023 $20.70T; 2024 $21.49T; 2025 $22.36T. M2 fell in 2022-23, first sustained decline since the 1930s.
- Functions of money (Federal Reserve education materials): medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value.
- History: Executive Order 6102 (April 5, 1933) required Americans to turn in most gold coin/bullion; Aug 15, 1971 Nixon suspended dollar convertibility into gold for foreign governments ("closing the gold window"). The dollar is fiat money: legal tender backed by the government, not a commodity.
