## Facts (ADP National Employment Report, released Wednesday Nov 4, 2026)
- Private employers added 86,000 jobs in October 2026. Forecasts cited: about 65,000 (InvestingLive) to 75,000 (MarketWatch via Quartz). "Slightly higher than expected" (CNBC).
- September revised to 86,000 from 90,000.
- Service-providing +82,000; goods-producing +4,000. Trade/transportation/utilities +39,000. Professional and business services -27,000 (third straight monthly decline).
- By size: small +28,000, medium +33,000, large +24,000.
- ADP Pay Insights (year over year, median):
  - All workers: base pay +3.2%, gross pay +4.7%.
  - Job-stayers: base pay +3.0%, gross pay +4.4%.
  - Job-changers: base pay +4.8%, gross pay +7.6% (gross: fastest since March 2024).
- ADP chief economist Nela Richardson (paraphrase, not exact quote): job-changers were lured by a combination of bonuses, overtime and more hours in addition to higher base pay.
- Next ADP report: Dec 2, 2026.

## Inflation context (BLS CPI, released Oct 14, 2026)
- CPI-U rose 3.7% over the 12 months through September 2026 (up from 3.4% in August); +0.6% in the month, seasonally adjusted. Core 2.6%. Gasoline +9.1% in the month.
- Next CPI (October data): Tuesday Nov 10, 2026, 8:30 AM ET.
- Our math: job-stayer base pay 3.0% minus 3.7% inflation = about -0.7 point (base pay behind prices). With bonuses/overtime (gross 4.4%) stayers were +0.7 ahead. Job-changers base 4.8% = +1.1 ahead. (Note: ADP pay is through October, CPI through September - latest available of each.)

## Meaning for a normal person
- Hiring is slow but positive. The pay gap between staying and switching widened: switchers' gross pay growth is the fastest since March 2024.
- A 3% base raise with 3.7% inflation means base pay buys a bit less than a year ago; bonuses and overtime made up the difference for the typical stayer.
- Takeaway (education, not advice): compare your own raise with the inflation rate to see what your pay is really doing.
