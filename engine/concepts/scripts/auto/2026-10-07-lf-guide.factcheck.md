## Issues

1. **Advice phrasing (fix before publishing).** These lines speak to the viewer as direct instructions rather than education:
   - Line 37: "Just keep paying that first minimum... month after month." Fix: "Now imagine that first minimum, $141.67, is paid every month instead."
   - Lines 92-93: "List every card. Set autopay so nothing is late. Then freeze one fixed payment. Pick your order..." Fix: "Step one lists every card. Step two puts every minimum on autopay. Step three freezes one fixed payment. Step four picks an order..."
   - Line 94: "...and do the math on each." Fix: "...and the math on each one decides which helps."
   - Line 76 scene title: "Don't [double] the debt." Fix: "The [double] debt risk."
   - Lines 22 and 36 and the "Do" column on line 27 are milder. They describe steps of a method; optionally rephrase ("Step one is a list of every card"; "Method" instead of "Do").

   The rest of the script hedges well ("many people", "people often").
2. **Line 26: the example minimums don't follow the rule taught on line 11.** Under 1% + interest, the minimums would be about $25 (floor), $88.83 and $127.50, a total of about $242, not $35 + $80 + $110 = $225. The simulation also keeps the minimums fixed. The results are internally correct, but a sharp viewer may notice. Fix: add to the line 24 sub text "made-up example, fixed minimums", or say "Say their minimums add up to $225."
3. **Line 29: "usually late after 5 p.m." is overstated.** Five p.m. is the earliest cutoff the law allows. Fix: "A payment can count as late if it arrives after the card's cutoff time, which can't be earlier than 5 p.m. on the due date."
4. **On-screen and spoken values differ (all are rounding, so minor).**
   - Line 10: "22.15%" vs "about twenty-two percent."
   - Line 43: $190.95 vs "about a hundred ninety-one."
   - Lines 52 and 53: $2,155.51, $2,263.62 and $108.11 vs "about..."
   - Line 68: $286.11.
   - Line 69: $1,083.18.
   - Line 73: 11.86% vs "about twelve percent."
   - Lines 74 and 75: exact cents vs "about."
   - Line 95: $3,121 vs "about thirty-one hundred."

   Fix: round the captions to match (for example "about $2,156", "~22%", "~12%") and keep the exact cents only in scene sub text. Alternatively, speak the exact figures.
5. **Unverified numbers (the web was blocked).** Recheck against the primary sources before publishing:
   - Line 4: $1.26T and the release date of Aug 11, 2026.
   - Line 73: 11.86%.
   - Line 79: the debt management plan fee ranges.
6. **Awkward to read aloud (minor).**
   - Line 5: "a range of three point seven five to four percent." Fix: "to a range of three and three-quarters to four percent."
   - Line 61: "section ten twenty-six point fifty-three of Regulation Z." Fix: say "a federal rule, Regulation Z" and keep 1026.53 on screen only.
7. **Opening (passes).** "Pay the minimum on $5,000, and it takes 19 years." It is true under the stated assumptions, specific, about 3 seconds long, and creates a need to know. An optional slight strengthening: "Paying the minimum on $5,000 takes 19 years." Line 3 discloses the assumptions promptly.
8. **Other checks (no issues).**
   - Time words: they are absolute dates, and the Q2 2026 data is still the latest on Oct 7. The next FOMC meeting is Oct 27-28, after posting. Nothing is stale.
   - No price or rate predictions.

   - The cover ("The 19-Year Minimum", $5,000 / 19 yrs) and the title are paid off by lines 1-16 and 95. The yt_desc figures match the script.
