## 3. Issues

1. **Line 1 (opening), minor.** The hook is strong: it's specific, true under the stated assumptions, makes you need the answer, and takes about 2.7 s to say. But "it takes" is stated as a universal before the assumptions come in line 3, and real minimum formulas vary. Fix: "Pay only the minimum on $5,000, and it can take 19 years." Same length, and "only" makes it land harder.
2. **Line 3 vs line 15, minor.** The spoken assumptions leave out the $25 minimum floor, which the 230-month result depends on: without a floor, 1% + interest never really finishes. Fix: add it to line 3 ("...a minimum of 1% plus interest or $25, and no new charges") and to the board scene.
3. **Line 29, factual precision.** "A payment is usually late if it arrives after 5 p.m." misstates the rule. Reg Z bars issuers from setting a cut-off time earlier than 5 p.m. on the due date, and many issuers set it later. Fix: "Card companies can't set the cut-off earlier than 5 p.m. on the due date." Also "And late has real rules" is awkward to say out loud. Try "And 'late' has exact rules."
4. **Lines 22, 36-37, 47, 91-95, and line 76 scene "Don't [double] the debt": directive phrasing.** None of these says "you should", but these lines give commands ("write down every card", "you pick one fixed dollar amount", "Just keep paying", "List every card. Set autopay... freeze one fixed payment... Pick your order... do the math", "Remember"). Lines 28 and 32 are already framed as education ("many people...", "people often..."). Fix: frame the checklist as description, e.g. line 92: "The steps: every card listed, autopay so nothing is late, then one fixed payment." Line 37: "Take our $5,000 card, and keep paying that first minimum, $141.67, every month." Scene 76: "Loan + new card debt".
5. **Line 81, minor precision.** The FTC rule covers debt-relief companies that sell by phone (telemarketing, including inbound calls prompted by ads). It lets them collect fees once at least one debt is settled and the customer has paid under that settlement. Fix: "...the FTC has banned debt relief companies that sell by phone from charging fees before they settle a debt."
6. **Lines 26 and 45-57, clarity of a worked example.** The example minimums ($35/$80/$110) don't follow the "1% + interest" formula taught in line 11 (that formula gives about $35 with a floor, $88.83 and $127.50). The simulation also holds them fixed. The math is internally correct, but an alert viewer may notice. Fix: say "fixed minimums" or "Their minimums, held fixed, add up to $225 a month." (Line 45's fixed $400 budget already implies this.)
7. **Line 16 scene subtitle, minor.** "$8,099.77 exact" isn't reproduced ($8,099.70 with cent rounding, $8,099.79 unrounded). Fix: "about $8,100, FinancePros math" or "$8,099.70".
8. **Line 85 scene, minor label.** "+0.25%" should read "+0.25 pt" to match the spoken "quarter point" (a percentage-point change, not a percent change).
9. **Line 54, minor.** "smaller than most people expect" is an unsupported generalization. Fix: "smaller than you might expect."
