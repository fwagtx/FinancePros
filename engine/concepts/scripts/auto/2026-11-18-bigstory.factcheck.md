# Fact-check: 2026-11-18-bigstory (Target tariff refunds), posting Wed Nov 18, 2026 4:00 PM CT

## Verified facts
- Target fiscal Q3 2026 (released Nov 18, 2026): Q3 operating income included a $301M benefit from tariff refunds; refunds added $0.50 to EPS; GAAP and adjusted EPS both $2.65; net sales about $27.1B (Quartz/Panabee: $27.07B), up 7.1%; comparable sales +5.6%. Guidance $10.65-$11.40 includes ~$2.14 of refunds recognized to date and excludes future refunds. CONFIRMED.
- Target Q2 (reported Aug 19-20, 2026): $994M pretax tariff refund benefit (recorded as a reduction of cost of sales), $1.65 of $4.11 EPS. CONFIRMED.
- $994M + $301M = $1,295M, about $1.3B. Math correct, but it is OUR sum of two quarters, not a Target figure, and it is an accounting benefit recognized ("booked"), not necessarily cash received "just" now.
- ~$166B: CBP court filing says ~330,000 importers PAID about $166B in IEEPA duties. It is the amount paid in, not the amount "owed back": CBP reported ~$100B refunded through end of July (~60%); Al Jazeera cites "more than three-quarters" of $128.68B in potential and certified refunds; Cato estimates refunds may total ~$130B. CONFIRMED as "paid", REJECTED as "owed back".
- Only importers of record (or their licensed customs brokers on their behalf) can file through CBP's CAPE portal; consumers cannot. CONFIRMED.
- UPS: "cannot issue IEEPA tariff refunds to payors until we receive the funds from CBP"; FedEx will refund customers once CBP pays it (DHL similar). CONFIRMED.

2. $1.3B is our math with no label. FIX line 3: "That $1.3 billion is our math: $994 million last quarter, plus $301 million now." Captions also say "our math".

6. Line 4 "today's earnings of $2.65": "today's" goes stale; ambiguous. FIX: "That added 50 cents to Target's $2.65 per share." Stat sub "of $2.65 EPS, Q3".
7. Line 5 "sales still rose 7.1%": it is net (total) sales, not comparable (+5.6%). FIX: "To be fair, Target's total sales still rose 7.1%." Stat sub "Net sales, Q3".

FIX: "One bright spot: if you paid a duty fee on a package, FedEx and UPS say they'll refund you once customs pays them." (names the carriers, matches the board).

10. Post captions said "Target got/just got $1.3B ... this year" and "$166B ... owed back". FIX: all captions rewritten to "booked", "our math", "importers paid about $166B, refunds flowing back", source list updated.
