# Research brief: How casinos always win: the house edge (posts Oct 22, 2026)

## The math (standard probability; see e.g. Wizard of Odds and UNLV Center for Gaming Research)
- American roulette: 38 pockets (1-36, 0, 00), 18 red and 18 black. A single number pays 35 to 1; the true odds are 37 to 1.
- House edge: 2/38 = 5.26%. European (single-zero) roulette: 37 pockets, 1/37 = 2.70%.
- Worked example: $10 on red. Win 18 of 38, lose 20 of 38. EV = (18 x 10 - 20 x 10) / 38 = -20/38 = -$0.526 per spin.
- Single number at 35:1: EV = (1 x 350 - 37 x 10) / 38 = -20/38 = -$0.526. Same edge.
- 100 spins of $10 = $1,000 wagered x 5.26% = about $52.63 expected loss.
