# Fact-check: 2027-01-16-hot (Scam Alert: course upsell funnels)
Checked 2026-11-17 for posting Sat Jan 16, 2027, 6 PM CT. Verified independently via web search (ftc.gov direct fetch was blocked by the proxy, so FTC pages were verified through search snippets and mirrors).

## Claims verified
- **$750.6M reported losses to business and job opportunities in 2024.** CONFIRMED. The FTC's March 2025 release and the 2024 Data Book give $750.6M, up nearly $250M from 2023, ranked third by losses.
- **Newer data check.** FTC put total 2025 fraud losses at about $15.9B (JEC testimony, Mar 25, 2026) and imposter losses at $3.5B (June 2026). I could not find a published full-year 2025 figure for the "business and job opportunities" category in any search, and no 2025 Data Book showed up. The 2024 figure stays, clearly dated "in 2024", so it is still accurate on the posting date. Optional: before posting, check ftc.gov/exploredata for a 2025 category total. If one exists, swap it in on line 1, in the stat value, and in the captions.
- **Growth Cave $30,000–$50,000 done-for-you upsell.** CONFIRMED as an allegation. The FTC press release (Mar 2025) says Growth Cave pitched an upgraded program, "Digital Freedom Mastermind", in which it would build the course for buyers at an added cost of $30,000 to $50,000. The complaint says buyers paid $3,500 to $50,000 in total.
- **"Most buyers, even upsell buyers, never made one sale."** NOT VERIFIED. The FTC's own language is that promised gains "rarely, if ever, materialize" and that many consumers could not earn the promised income or generate sales. "Most made zero sales" appears only in a secondary blog. I replaced it with the FTC's wording.
- **January 2026 settlement.** CONFIRMED. FTC press release dated Jan 27, 2026: under two court orders, all defendants settled, including co-CEOs Lucas Lee-Tyson and Osmany Batte and ops manager Jordan Marksberry. They are permanently banned from marketing and selling business opportunities and credit repair programs, and the co-CEOs must liquidate assets for redress. The script says "banned from selling business opportunities", which is accurate and not overstated.
- **Funnel and red flags (lines 2, 3, 6, 7).** These describe the general pattern, consistent with FTC business-opportunity guidance (income claims, pressure tactics, upsell calls). They are consumer-protection tips, not financial advice.

## Issues found and fixes applied
1. **Line 0 hook overgeneralized.** "That cheap make-money-online course is the bait, not the product" asserts this of every cheap course. Growth Cave's entry program was not cheap either ($3,500+). Fixed by hedging, both text and say: "That cheap make-money-online course is often just the bait." (9 words, under 3 s). It is still specific and still makes the viewer want the answer.
2. **Line 1: spoken number did not match the screen.** The narration said "about seven hundred fifty million" while the screen showed $750.6M. The narration now says "seven hundred fifty point six million dollars"; text and screen stay $750.6M.
3. **Line 5 overstated the allegation.** "Most buyers, even upsell buyers, never made one sale" is not supported by FTC materials. It now reads: "The FTC alleged the promised profits rarely, if ever, showed up. In January 2026, the defendants settled and were banned from selling business opportunities." (Say: "...In January twenty twenty-six...".)
4. **Line 5 news headline stated an allegation as fact.** "FTC: most never sold" became "Banned in FTC deal", which is the settled fact and fits the 20-character limit. The source line now reads "FTC complaint 2025; settlement Jan 2026".
5. **Line 4 spoken range was awkward and ambiguous.** "thirty to fifty thousand dollar" became "thirty thousand to fifty thousand dollar" so it matches $30K-50K exactly.
6. **Time words.** "In January 2026" is still correct on Jan 16, 2027. The script contains no relative words like "this year" or "last month".
7. **Captions.** IG and YT now use the hedged hook, drop "most buyers never made one sale", add "done-for-you upgrade" and "settled ... banned from selling business opportunities", and date the sources. TikTok now says "alleged $50K upsell".
8. **Cover "The Upsell Trap / $50K".** Pays off on line 4 (the alleged upsell of up to $50,000). No change.
9. **Advice, politics, price predictions:** none. **Math:** no worked examples.

Checker: OK, 9 lines, 139 spoken words, hook 9 words.
