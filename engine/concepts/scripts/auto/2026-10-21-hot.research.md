# Research brief: Scam alert, job offer scams that ask you to pay (slot 2026-10-21-hot)

Researched October 1, 2026. Posts October 21, 2026. Evergreen: no "this week". Separate from the Oct 15 short (fake bank-fraud calls) and Oct 20 short (Medicare scam calls).

## Timely angle
- FTC consumer warning (covered by WKNO/Protecting Your Money, August 11, 2026): job scams are on the rise. Task-scam reports in 2025 roughly doubled versus 2024 (per news summaries of FTC data; not used on screen, exact figure not confirmed).
- FTC Consumer Sentinel: job opportunity-related scams, Q4 2025: $150.4 million reported lost, 25,002 reports, median loss $2,000 (CW33 citing FTC). Category label is ambiguous (job vs business-and-job opportunities); NOT used on screen.
- Full-year 2025 job-scam loss figure NOT confirmed in search results; do not state one.

## Key numbers
- All fraud, 2025: record $15.9 billion reported lost, up from $12.5 billion in 2024 (FTC testimony to Joint Economic Committee, March 25, 2026; Crowdfund Insider, KHQ, Detroit Free Press, March 2026). Full-year 2025 job-scam subcategory figure could not be confirmed (Data Book blocked; 5 searches).
- Job scams and employment agencies, reported losses: $90 million in 2020 -> $501 million in 2024; reports nearly tripled 2020-2024. [FTC press release March 2025; FTC on X March 2025]
- FTC Data Spotlight Dec 2024 ("Paying to get paid: gamified job scams drive record losses"): losses more than threefold from 2020 to 2023; topped $220 million in first half of 2024 ($223M through June). Task scams: reports from zero in 2020 to ~5,000 in 2023, ~20,000 in first half 2024; estimated nearly 40 percent of 2024 job scam reports (first half 2024 data). Crypto losses to job scams $41 million in first half 2024.
- FTC: fewer than 1 in 10 victims report (context only; not used).

## How the "pay to get the job" scams work (FTC consumer guidance)
- Upfront fees for equipment, starter kits, "training" or certifications. (FTC work-from-home alert, June 2025)
- Fake check: "employer" sends a check, asks you to send part of it back or to a "vendor"/buy gift cards; check later bounces and you owe the bank. (FTC)
- Task scams: simple app tasks (liking videos, rating products), fake "earnings" shown, then you are asked to deposit money, often crypto, to unlock earnings. (FTC Data Spotlight Dec 2024)
- Honest placement firms typically are paid by the hiring company, not candidates. (FTC)

## Exact quote (confirmed word-for-word in two FTC-indexed search results)
"Honest employers, including the federal government, will never ask you to pay to get a job." - Federal Trade Commission (full sentence per fact-check of the FTC job-scams page) (FTC consumer job-scam guidance, consumer.ftc.gov)

## Surprising fact
Reported job-scam losses grew more than five times in four years ($90M in 2020 to $501M in 2024), and the tell is simple: the money flows the wrong way - you are asked to pay the employer.

## Worked example (hypothetical, labeled)
Example: a new "employer" mails a $2,000 check for home-office equipment and says to send $1,500 to its "approved vendor".
Person deposits $2,000, sends $1,500. Check bounces: the bank reverses the $2,000. Net: -$1,500 out of pocket (the $500 "kept" vanishes with the bounced check).
Calculation: +2,000 - 1,500 - 2,000 (reversal) = -1,500.
