# Research brief: How Publix is owned by its employees (How They Make Money)
Posts Tuesday, December 22, 2026. Evergreen: every figure carries its date/fiscal year. Researched October 23, 2026.
Note: sec.gov / publix.com could not be fetched directly from this session; figures were confirmed via search excerpts quoting the FY2025 10-K and Publix/Business Wire releases.

## Core facts
- Publix common stock "is not traded on an established securities market." It is "made available for sale by the Company only to its current employees and members of its Board of Directors" through the Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) and Non-Employee Directors plan, and to 401(k) (SMART Plan) participants. Common stock is also provided to employees through the Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). (Publix FY2025 10-K, fiscal year ended Dec. 27, 2025)
- Price: set by the Board of Directors, based on an independent (outside) valuation that includes comparing Publix's results with publicly traded peers. (10-K; Publix releases) Price is updated quarterly with each results release (effective Mar 1, May 1, Aug 1, Nov 1).
- Selling: no exchange. ESOP shares distributed to participants carry a put option (holder can require Publix to buy them at the then-current price); Publix currently repurchases shares offered by holders, subject to terms. Fair value of distributed shares subject to the put option: $815M (Dec 27, 2025) vs $661M (Dec 28, 2024). (10-K FY2025)
- 2025 (FY ended Dec. 27, 2025): sales $62.7B (+5% from $59.7B in 2024); net earnings $4.7B (vs $4.6B); EPS $1.46 (vs $1.41); comparable-store sales +3.5%. (Publix Q4/annual 2025 release, Business Wire, Mar 2, 2026; 10-K)
- Shares outstanding: about 3.216 billion (end 2025) vs 3.258 billion (end 2024). (10-K balance sheet)
- ESOP: compensation expense for contributions $561M (2025), $516M (2024), $491M (2023). Fair value of shares held by the ESOP: $13.9B (Dec 27, 2025) vs $12.9B (Dec 28, 2024); cost basis $4.1B. (10-K FY2025)
- Eligibility: associates automatically become ESOP participants after a year of employment with 1,000+ work credit hours; board sets the annual contribution, generally paid in Publix stock, at no cost to the employee. (Publix PROFIT plan page publix.org; Publix release) -- contribution % of pay NOT confirmed; vesting schedule sources conflict -> do not use.
- 401(k) SMART Plan match (2023 plan filing): 50% of contributions up to 3% of eligible pay, max $750; discretionary. (Publix 11-K, plan year 2023) -- not used in script.
- People/stores: about 260,000 associates (CEO Kevin Murphy, Fortune Best Companies release, April 2026); 1,431 stores in 8 states (same release). Publix describes itself as the largest employee-owned company in the US.
- 5-for-1 stock split effective close of business April 14, 2022 ($68.80 -> $13.76 split-adjusted).

## Worked example (hypothetical, price only, ignores dividends and taxes)
100 shares x $13.76 (Mar 1, 2022 price) = $1,376
100 shares x $19.65 (Mar 1, 2026 price) = $1,965
Gain = $589; $589 / $1,376 = 42.8% (about 43%).

## Surprising fact
A $62.7B-a-year grocer (2025) whose stock price is set by its own board from an outside appraisal, which can and does go down (Mar 2026: $20.40 -> $19.65; Aug 2026: $20.45 -> $19.60).
