## Chartable series: US Netflix Standard (ad-free) plan monthly price
- 2013: $7.99 (Tom's Guide pricing table, April 2013)
- 2020: $13.99 (Oct 2020 increase; AFTV News: Standard went from $13.99 to $15.49, last increase "October of 2020")
- 2022: $15.49 (January 2022; Looper, AFTV News, NBC News)
- 2025: $17.99 (January 2025, from $15.49; NBC News, Tom's Guide, PCWorld)
- 2026: $19.99 (March 26, 2026, from $17.99; CBS News, Hollywood Reporter)
Note: intermediate steps between 2013 and 2020 ($8.99/$9.99/$10.99/$12.99) were not confirmed in this search and are left off; chart is "selected years". Three increases since Oct 2020: Jan 2022, Jan 2025, Mar 2026.
Math: $19.99 / $7.99 = 2.50x. Ad plan vs Standard: $19.99 - $8.99 = $11.00 per month; x 12 = $132 a year.

## What it means for a normal person
- Streaming is a recurring bill; Netflix itself lists pricing as one of three growth drivers, so Tuesday's report shows how much of the business rests on price hikes and ads.
- The cheaper ad-supported plan is $11 a month less than Standard ($132 a year) - pure arithmetic, not a recommendation.
