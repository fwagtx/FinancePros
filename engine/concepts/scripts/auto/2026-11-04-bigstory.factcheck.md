## Issues

### 1. Hook (line 0) is misleading. BLOCKING
"The typical raise for people who stayed put just lost to prices" is true only for BASE pay (3.0% vs 3.7%). On ADP's gross-pay measure (bonuses and overtime included), stayers got 4.4%, which BEATS 3.7%. The video shows that number itself on line 5, so the hook contradicts the video. The hook needs "base". It also runs 12 words, about 3.5 seconds at a normal pace. A shorter question opener comes in under 3 seconds and pulls the viewer in more.
- Replacement text: "Stayed at your job? The typical base raise just lost to prices."
- Replacement say: "Stayed at your job? The typical base raise just lost to prices." (11 words)
- Scene: label "RAISE" -> "BASE RAISE"; title "Your Raise [Lost]" -> "Base Raise [Lost]". COVER label "RAISE" -> "BASE RAISE".
- POST ig line 1 and the hook copy: use the same sentence. (The POST title "Stayed at your job? Your base raise trailed inflation" is already accurate.)

### 2. Line 5: the stayers-beat-inflation point is left out, and "fastest since" is ambiguous. BLOCKING (goes with #1)
"stayers 4.4%, switchers 7.6%, the fastest since March 2024" reads as if both figures were the fastest. The fastest-since claim applies only to switchers. The line also never says that 4.4% beats prices, so a viewer comes away with a one-sided story.
- Replacement text: "With bonuses and overtime, stayers got 4.4%, beating prices. Switchers got 7.6%, their fastest since March 2024."
- Replacement say: "Add bonuses and overtime, and stayers got four point four, beating prices. Switchers got seven point six, their fastest since March twenty twenty-four." (23 words)
- Scene: left tone "neutral" -> "up"; left sub "same job" -> "beats 3.7%" (or keep "same job").
- POST ig: "With bonuses and overtime: stayers +4.4% (ahead of inflation), switchers +7.6%, switchers' fastest since March 2024."

### 3. Line 4 board: wrong unit (minor)
"= -0.7% (our math)" should be points, not percent. Also note in the post that ADP is year-over-year through October and CPI is through September (different months). That is acceptable as "our math".
- Replacement board lines: ["3.0% - 3.7%", "= -0.7 pts (our math)"]

### 4. Line 7: trim to keep the word count in range (narration)
- Replacement text: "The simple check: your own raise versus 3.7% inflation." (unchanged)
- Replacement say: "The simple check: your own raise versus three point seven percent." (11 words, was 14)
- Not advice. OK.

### 5. Line 8 timeline: date precision (minor)
"Tuesday" for October CPI rests on third-party calendars (Nov 10). Keep it, but have a human confirm on bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm before posting. You could add dates to the timeline: when "Fri Nov 6" / "Tue Nov 10".
