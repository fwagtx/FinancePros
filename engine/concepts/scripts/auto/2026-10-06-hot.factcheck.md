## Issues

1. **On-screen value differs from the spoken one (line 5).** The caption and scene show "22.15%", but the narration says "about twenty-two percent". **Fix:** say "twenty-two point one five percent" so the audio matches the caption and card. The alternative is to show "22%" everywhere, but the post copy uses 22.15%, so matching the spoken line to 22.15% is cleaner.
2. **On-screen value differs from the spoken one (line 6).** The scene "to" value is "$36.92", while the caption and narration say "about $37". **Fix:** change the scene value to "~$37" (or "$37"), or say "thirty-six ninety-two". "~$37" is better.
3. **Weak opening (line 1).** The line is true and short (10 words, about 3 s), but it gives the answer away. It has no number or stakes, so the viewer does not need to keep watching. **Fix (stronger hook, under 3 s):** "Half of people believe this credit card myth." Another option: "Paying card interest to build credit? You're paying for nothing." Then state the myth on line 2 with the 48% stat.
4. **Line 7 relevance and timing (minor).** The Sept 16, 2026 hike is accurate and will still be recent on Oct 6. "Card rates tend to follow" is fine. Do not imply that card APRs have already risen by a specific amount. The script does not do this, so it is OK as written.
5. **Stale stat (minor, disclosed).** The 48% figure comes from a February 2023 survey that is about 3.5 years old. It is clearly dated on screen and in the post, so this is acceptable.
6. **Awkward read-aloud (minor).** In line 4, "Your card usually reports your statement balance anyway, so paying it in full still shows usage, with zero dollars in interest" is long for one breath. **Fix:** "Your card reports your statement balance anyway. Pay it in full, and it still shows usage, with zero interest."
7. **CTA (line 10).** "That's the money." reads oddly out of context. Keep it only if it is the series catchphrase.
