# Research brief: Why is car repair so expensive? (slot 2026-10-27-hot, posts Oct 27, 2026)

Research date: October 1, 2026. Direct fetches of bls.gov, fred.stlouisfed.org, ycharts.com and officialdata.org were blocked by the network proxy; figures below were confirmed through search results quoting those sources, cross-checked where possible.

## Timely angle (August 2026 CPI, released September 2026)
- BLS CPI, 12 months through August 2026: motor vehicle maintenance and repair +5.2%; motor vehicle insurance -5.1%; all items +3.4%; new vehicles +0.6%; used cars and trucks -2.3%. (BLS CPI August 2026 release, as summarized by The Money Overview, "Car repair bills climbed 5.2% in a year while auto insurance fell 5.1%".)
- Cross-check: BLS seasonally adjusted index CUSR0000SETD (1982-84=100): August 2025 = 441.987; August 2026 = 465.136. 465.136 / 441.987 = 1.0524 -> +5.2%. Matches.
- Recent months (same series, SA): June 2026 = 457.313; July 2026 = 460.185; August 2026 = 465.136 (YCharts/FRED, sourced from BLS).

## The surprising fact
- Since January 2020, the BLS motor vehicle maintenance and repair index rose about 55%: January 2020 = 300.112, August 2026 = 465.136 (SA). 465.136 / 300.112 = 1.5499 -> +55.0%.
- Meanwhile in the 12 months to August 2026 repair rose (+5.2%) while car insurance fell (-5.1%).

## Worked example (labeled hypothetical)
- A repair priced $100 in January 2020, rising exactly with the BLS motor vehicle maintenance and repair index, would cost: $100 x (465.136 / 300.112) = $100 x 1.5499 = about $155 in August 2026.
- CCC collision repair: $4,818 (2025, preliminary) - $3,421 (2020) = $1,397 more per repair; $4,818 / $3,421 = 1.408 -> about +41% in five years.

## Collision repair costs (CCC Intelligent Solutions)
- CCC Crash Course 2026: preliminary average total cost of repair (TCOR) for repairable vehicles = $4,818 in 2025, +1.7% vs 2024, the lowest percentage increase since 2017.
- Average repairable vehicle appraisal was $3,421 in 2020 (CCC data as cited by Freedom For All Americans and search summaries; medium confidence).
- 2024 finished at "over $4,730" (CCC Q4 2024 Crash Course / Repairer Driven News).
- Labor hours per repair (national non-comprehensive repairable appraisals): 23.0 hours in 2012, peak 27.6 in 2023, 26.7 in 2025 (CCC Crash Course 2026).
- Average hourly labor rates: +4.5% 2023->2024, +2.9% 2024->2025 (CCC Crash Course 2026; dollar figure not confirmed).
- Scans and calibrations of driver-assist systems are now "routine" at DRP (insurer direct-repair) shops (CCC report, Repairer Driven News, Sept 24, 2025).
- Drivers of cost named by CCC/industry: crash-avoidance systems (cameras, sensors), complex components like headlights, more SUVs and pickups, luxury features in mainstream models, AWD/4WD and advanced powertrains.

## Labor data
- BLS OEWS, May 2025: median wage for automotive service technicians and mechanics (SOC 49-3023) = $24.34/hour, $50,620/year ($50,620 / 2,080 = $24.34). May 2024: $23.88/hour, $49,670/year (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook).
- PartsTech "State of the Automotive Aftermarket" survey of 752 general repair shops (fall 2024, 2025 report): 49% charge $120-$159 per hour of labor; only 10% charge under $100; only 2% above $200. The report devotes 10 of 44 pages to the technician shortage.
- Shop billed rate covers more than wages (facility, equipment, diagnostic software); we describe this generally only.

## Weakly sourced / caveats
- All-items 3.4% and the new/used vehicle numbers for August 2026 come from one secondary article citing BLS.
- CCC 2020 $3,421 figure comes via secondary sources.
- BLS 12-month change in releases uses the not-seasonally-adjusted index; the SA cross-check gives the same 5.2%.
