### Manheim series check (claim 8)
Each point matches the value Cox first reported:
- Sept 2020: 161.2 (+15.2% YoY)
- Sept 2021: 204.8 (+27.1%)
- Sept 2022: 204.5 (-0.1%)
- Sept 2023: 214.3 (**-3.9% YoY**)
- Sept 2024: 203.0 (-5.3%)
- Sept 2025: 207.0 (+2.0%)
- Sept 2026: 205.9 (-0.6%)

In January 2023 Cox rebased the index (base moved from Jan 1995 to Jan 1997 = 100) and said all earlier index levels "should be considered obsolete". That lifted pre-2023 levels by about 9%:
- Sept 2023's -3.9% YoY implies a rebased Sept 2022 of 214.3 / 0.961 = 223.0, against 204.5 as first reported. That is a factor of 1.0905.
- Dec 2022 (219.3, -14.9% YoY) gives the same factor.

What this means for the chart:
- The 2020-2022 points are on the old base and the 2023-2026 points are on the current base.
- So the chart shows a **+4.8% jump from 2022 to 2023**, but Cox reported a **3.9% decline** for that year. It also makes 2021 look lower than today, when on the current base Sept 2021 is about 223, higher than 205.9.
- The year-to-year changes within each base are consistent: 2020→21 +27.0% (reported +27.1%), 2021→22 -0.1%, 2023→24 -5.3%, 2024→25 +2.0%, 2025→26 -0.5% (reported -0.6%, a rounding difference).

Relabeling the chart "as first reported" does not fix it, because the 2022→2023 step would still point the wrong way.
