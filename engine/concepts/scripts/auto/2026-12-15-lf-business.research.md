# 2026-12-15-lf-business research brief: How Starbucks makes money

So every annual number is labeled "fiscal 2025" (52 weeks ended Sept 28, 2025, 10-K filed Nov 2025). Later dated facts (China JV closing spring 2026, Rewards revamp March 10, 2026, Q3 FY2026 quarter ended June 28, 2026) are labeled with their dates. No predictions, no guidance.

## Hook / surprising fact
Starbucks' highest-margin segment has no cafes: Channel Development (packaged coffee, pods, ready-to-drink, foodservice, mostly via Nestle's Global Coffee Alliance) had a 47.3% operating margin in fiscal 2025, versus 11.5% for North America and 12.1% for International. It is 5% of revenue but about 17.7% of segment operating income (worked example below).

## Key numbers (fiscal 2025 10-K unless noted)
- Total net revenues: $37,184.4M (about $37.2B), up 3% from $36,176.2M (FY2024).
- By type: company-operated stores $30,744.8M (83% of revenue; 10-K); licensed stores $4,350.4M (12%; down 3.4% from $4,505.1M); other $2,089.2M (our subtraction; about 6%).
- Segments: North America $27,373.1M (74%); International $7,819.9M (21%); Channel Development $1,871.7M (5%); Corporate and other about $119.7M (our subtraction).
- Segment operating income / margin FY2025 (FY2024):
  - North America $3,156.7M, 11.5% (FY2024 $5,355.3M, 19.8%). Included $653.2M restructuring and impairment charges.
  - International $950.0M, 12.1% (FY2024 $1,045.7M, 14.2%).
  - Channel Development $885.1M, 47.3% (FY2024 $925.9M, about 52.3%; margin down 500 bps).
- Consolidated GAAP operating income $2,936.6M, margin 7.9% (FY2024 $5,408.8M, 15.0%; down 710 bps). Non-GAAP margin FY2025 9.9%.
- Product mix (company-operated store revenue, FY2025): beverages 73%, food 23%, other 4%.
- Stores at Sept 28, 2025: 40,990 total = 21,514 company-operated + 19,476 licensed. U.S. 16,864; China 8,011 (U.S. + China = about 61%). North America 18,311 at Q4 end.
- Restructuring: plan announced Sept 25, 2025, about $1B, including about 900 corporate (non-retail) job cuts and closing underperforming coffeehouses; 627 stores closed under the plan in Q4 FY2025 (584 in North America). Sources: Q4 FY2025 release; AP/Sinclair coverage Sept 25, 2025.
- Q4 FY2025 (quarter ended Sept 28, 2025): global comparable store sales +1%, the first growth in seven quarters; U.S. flat; China +2%. GAAP Q4 operating margin 2.9%.
- Starbucks Rewards U.S. 90-day active members: 34.2M in Q4 FY2025 (up 1%; earnings call / CNBC Oct 29, 2025). 35.8M in Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended June 28, 2026; Starbucks Q3 FY26 Digital IR Dashboard).
- Mobile Order and Pay: 31% of transactions at U.S. company-operated stores as of Dec 31, 2023 (Starbucks via GeekWire, Jan 2024). No FY2025 figure found; label "Dec 2023".
- Gift cards (covered in Dec 8 short; mention briefly): FY2025 breakage $222.4M (company-op $200.4M + licensed $22.0M); stored value cards + Stars deferred revenue $1,751.7M at Sept 28, 2025.

## Licensed vs company-operated (10-K wording)
- "Licensed stores generally have a lower gross margin and a higher operating margin than company-operated stores."
- "Under the licensed model, Starbucks receives a margin on branded products and supplies sold to the licensed store operator along with a royalty on retail sales. Licensees are responsible for operating costs and capital investments..."
- Licensees "provide improved, and at times the only, access to desirable retail space" (airports, campuses, grocery etc.).
- Company-operated: Starbucks books the full sale but pays rent, wages, equipment.

## Nestle Global Coffee Alliance
- Closed Aug 26, 2018. Nestle paid about $7B up front, mostly prepaid royalties, for rights to sell Starbucks packaged coffee/tea and foodservice products globally (outside cafes).
- Recorded as deferred revenue, recognized straight-line over an estimated 40-year economic life: about $176.5M a year since FY2021; at Sept 28, 2025 remaining balance $5.6B long-term + $177.0M current.
- Most Channel Development revenue = product sales to and royalties from Nestle. Also North American Coffee Partnership (ready-to-drink JV with PepsiCo); lower JV income cut CD margin ~350 bps in FY2025.

## Back to Starbucks (neutral, dated)
- Aug 13, 2024: Starbucks names Brian Niccol (Chipotle CEO) chairman and CEO; started Sept 9, 2024. Open letter Sept 10, 2024: "We're getting back to Starbucks." (Axios, Sept 10, 2024)
- Nov 7, 2024: extra charge for non-dairy milk removed in U.S./Canada company-operated stores (announced Oct 30, 2024).
- Jan 2025: plan to cut about 30% of food and drink menu by late 2025 (NPR, Jan 28, 2025). Condiment bars returned. Goal: about four-minute waits. "Green Apron Service" staffing model.
- Sept 25, 2025: ~$1B restructuring (above).
- China: Nov 2025 deal; closed by April 2026 (Starbucks/Business Wire, Reuters Apr 2, 2026): Boyu Capital funds hold 60% of Starbucks China retail operations, Starbucks keeps 40% and licenses the brand. About $4B enterprise value. ~8,000 company-operated stores move to licensed model; long-term aspiration up to 20,000.
- Q3 FY2026 (ended June 28, 2026; released July 29, 2026): global comps +7.9% (transactions +4.2%, ticket +3.5%); North America +8.1%; consolidated operating margin 14.4%, up about 430 bps; revenue $9.3B, down 1% due to China transaction.
- Rewards relaunched March 10, 2026 with three levels: Green, Gold (500 Stars in 12 months), Reserve (2,500 Stars).

## History
- 1971: first store, Pike Place Market, Seattle (Baldwin, Siegl, Bowker).
- 1987: Howard Schultz buys the small chain (about $4M per Fox Business; unverified exact, so we don't say a price).
- June 26, 1992: IPO on Nasdaq at $17/share.

- Coffee costs: ICE arabica futures record $4.3795/lb on Oct 23, 2025 (Bloomberg).

## Worked examples (exact math)
A) Channel Development's share of segment profit, FY2025: 885.1 / (3,156.7 + 950.0 + 885.1 = 4,991.8) = 17.7%. Revenue share: 1,871.7 / 37,184.4 = 5.0%.
B) Nestle prepayment: $7B / 40 years = $175M per year (simple math); filings show about $176.5M recognized per year.
C) Margin per $100 of sales, FY2025: North America $11.50, International $12.10, Channel Development $47.30 (segment operating margins x $100).
D) Licensed vs company-operated store count FY2025: 19,476 / 40,990 = 47.5% of stores licensed, but licensed revenue only 12% of total (Starbucks books royalties and product sales, not the full drink price).
