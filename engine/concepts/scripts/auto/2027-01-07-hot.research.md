# Research brief: 2027-01-07-hot (Money myth: scholarships are only for straight-A students and athletes)

Researched Nov 8, 2026. Evergreen; every number dated.

## Angle
Myth: scholarships/grant money only go to straight-A students and athletes.
Fact: most grant money comes from colleges themselves and the federal government, much of it need-based and tied to the FAFSA. Athletic scholarships are a tiny slice.

## Key facts (with dates)
- Total grant aid to undergraduate + graduate students in 2024-25: $173.7 billion (College Board, Trends in College Pricing and Student Aid 2025, released Oct/Nov 2025). Up 5.4% after inflation vs. prior year.
- Institutional grants (from colleges themselves) 2024-25: $85.1 billion, 49% of all grant aid (College Board 2025 newsroom release; Higher Ed Dive).
- Federal grants 2024-25: $53.7 billion, incl. $38.6 billion in Pell Grants. State grants $16.6B; other (private/employer) grants $18.2B (College Board 2025 chart).
- Pell recipients rose about 14%, from 6.4 million to 7.3 million (College Board 2025 release).
- NCAA: "$4B+ in athletics scholarships awarded annually", 196,000+ student-athletes receive athletics aid each year (NCAA.org scholarships pages, as of 2026). Older third-party figures were ~$2.9-3B.
- OUR MATH: $4B / $173.7B = 2.3% of total grant aid (2024-25 total vs. NCAA annual figure; NCAA says "$4B+", so treat as "about 2%").
- Sallie Mae / Ipsos "How America Pays for College 2026" (survey Apr 22-May 26, 2026; released Aug 12, 2026): in 2025-26, 61% of students received a scholarship, average award $8,291; scholarships covered 15% of reported college spending. 2025 edition (2024-25): ~60%, average ~$8,000. Sallie Mae also reports some families didn't apply because they thought they wouldn't qualify for non-financial reasons (16%, year unclear - not used).
- FAFSA: the 2027-28 FAFSA opened before the Oct 1, 2026 statutory date (Education Department, reported by Sinclair stations/ABC affiliates and The College Investor, Sept 2026; one outlet says Sept 23, 2026 - exact day not double-confirmed, so not used on screen). Federal deadline June 30, 2028; state and college deadlines often much earlier; some aid first-come, first-served. Many colleges use FAFSA data to award their own need-based grants.
- Pell Grant maximum 2026-27: $7,395; minimum $740 (ED Dear Colleague Letter GEN-26-01, Jan 30, 2026; Mapping Your Future). Flat for multiple years.
- NCAN unclaimed Pell estimate: high school class of 2024 left about $4.4 billion in Pell Grants unclaimed (about 830,000 Pell-eligible FAFSA non-completers x 2024-25 average Pell $5,339). Only 51.4% of class of 2024 completed FAFSA by Aug 30, 2024, vs 57.8% for class of 2023. Caveat: an estimate (critics question assuming all non-filers eligible).

## Worked example (OUR MATH)
Max Pell Grant 2026-27 = $7,395. If the max stayed the same for four years: $7,395 x 4 = $29,580.
Check of NCAN math: 830,000 x $5,339 = $4,431,370,000 = about $4.4 billion.

## Surprising fact
Athletic scholarships are only about 2% of all grant aid (our math: $4B / $173.7B), while colleges' own grants are about 49% ($85.1B).
