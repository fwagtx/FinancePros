## Business model (what Pinterest sells)
- Pinterest is free for users. It makes money by selling ads (promoted pins, shopping ads, video ads) that appear in the
  feed and search results, sold by auction and billed by clicks / impressions / actions.
- Its 10-K describes revenue as generated (substantially all) from advertising. (Secondary confirmations: stratrix.com
  business-model summary; autobound.ai FY2025 10-K summary. Exact sentence not opened in the filing itself; wording
  "almost all from ads" is safe.)
- Why advertisers pay: Pinterest says about 96% of searches on Pinterest are unbranded (people search "cozy living room",
  not a brand name), so shoppers have not yet chosen a store. Source: Pinterest exec at Advertising Week NY 2023 via
  eMarketer; repeated by Pinterest in later materials (Branvas cites the Feb 2026 release; some versions say "top searches";
  a 2023 figure of 97% also circulates). Attribute as "Pinterest says".
- CEO Bill Ready, Q3 2026 release (Oct 29, 2026): "Our differentiated AI-driven visual search and shopping experiences are
  making Pinterest increasingly valuable for users and advertisers alike."

## FY2025 (official, Q4/FY2025 press release, Feb 12, 2026)
- Revenue FY2025: $4,222M (up 16% from $3,646M in FY2024). Net income FY2025: about $417M.
- Revenue by region FY2025: US & Canada $3,173M; Europe $775M; Rest of World $274M.
- MAUs, Q4 2025: global 619M; US & Canada 105M; Europe 158M; Rest of World 356M.
- ARPU FY2025 (revenue per monthly user, full year): Global $7.21 (2024: $6.94); US & Canada $30.84 (2024: $29.15);
  Europe $5.12 (2024: $4.24); Rest of World $0.83 (2024: $0.59).
- Q4 2025: ad impressions +41%, ad pricing -19% y/y (mix shift toward under-monetized international markets).

## Q3 2026 (official, Q3 2026 press release, Oct 29, 2026, SEC 8-K exhibit)
- Revenue Q3 2026: $1,174M (Q3 2025: $1,049M), +12%.
- Revenue by region Q3 2026: US & Canada $894M; Europe $193M; Rest of World $87M.
- MAUs: global 661M (record, +10%); US & Canada 107M (+4%); Europe 161M (+7%); Rest of World 394M (+13%).
- ARPU Q3 2026 (quarter): Global $1.80 (Q3 2025 $1.78); US & Canada $8.38 ($7.64); Europe $1.21 ($1.31); RoW $0.23 ($0.21).
- Q4 2026 guidance: $1,425M-$1,475M (8%-12% growth). (Company guidance, not a prediction from us; do not use on screen.)

## Worked example (our math, FY2025 / Q4 2025 data)
- Share of users in US & Canada: 105M / 619M = 0.1696 = about 17% (about one in six).
- Share of revenue from US & Canada: $3,173M / $4,222M = 0.7515 = about 75% (three-quarters).
- Gap: $30.84 / $0.83 = 37.2, so a US/Canada user was worth about 37 times a Rest of World user in 2025.
- Europe vs US & Canada: $30.84 / $5.12 = 6.0x.

## The surprising fact
About one in six Pinterest users (US & Canada) brought in about three-quarters of its 2025 revenue: each was worth about
$31 a year vs about 83 cents in the Rest of World. And user growth is fastest where ARPU is lowest (RoW +13% vs
US & Canada +4% in Q3 2026).
