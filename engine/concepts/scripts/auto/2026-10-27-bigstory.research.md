## Pick: October 2026 consumer confidence (Conference Board, released Tuesday Oct 27, 2026)
Facts (Conference Board release via PR Newswire "US Consumer Confidence Increased in October", Oct 27, 2026; Bloomberg; Quartz; FXStreet; TheStreet):
- Consumer Confidence Index (1985=100): 91.7 in October 2026, up 7.5 points.
- September 2026 revised up to 84.2 (from 81.9 initially reported; 81.9 was the lowest since 2014 per CNBC Sept 29, 2026).
- Highest reading since June 2026; first increase in four months (Bloomberg, Quartz headlines); ended three straight monthly declines.
- Expected: 81.0 (Bloomberg survey of economists); reading topped every estimate in that survey (Bloomberg).
- Present Situation Index: 120.4, up 8.6 from 111.8.
- Expectations Index (consumers' 6-month outlook for income, business, jobs): 72.6, up 6.9 from 65.7; remains below 80, the level the Conference Board says has historically signaled a recession within the next year.
- Average 12-month inflation expectations declined in October but remained elevated at 5.8%.
- Plans to buy autos and homes declined slightly (six-month moving average basis); consumers planned less spending on services and durable goods.
- Conference Board's economist: confidence remains on a long-term downward trend of the past few years. Gain tied mainly to better views of current and expected business conditions (and easing retail gas prices Sept->Oct; not used, gas covered elsewhere).

Chart/compare data: Sept 2026 84.2 (revised) -> Oct 2026 91.7. Monthly series June-Aug has inconsistent revisions across sources (June 91.2/92.2, July 90.8/90.2, Aug 89.4/88.6), so use a two-point compare rather than a chart.

Context: The Michigan sentiment index (covered elsewhere) moved the other way, a reminder the two surveys measure different things. Conference Board survey leans on jobs/business conditions.

What it means: Mood improved, but the forward-looking part is still in the zone the Conference Board ties to recession risk, inflation expectations are high, and big-purchase plans slipped.

What to watch next: November Conference Board release (last Tuesday of November, Nov 24, 2026).
