## The model
- Amazon's 10-K line "Advertising services" = sales of advertising to sellers, vendors, publishers, authors and others, through programs such as sponsored ads, display, and video advertising (Amazon FY2024 10-K definition).
- **Sponsored Products**: cost-per-click ads that promote individual product listings; they sit inside Amazon search results and on product pages, look like normal listings, and carry a small "Sponsored" label. The advertiser pays only when a shopper clicks. (Amazon Ads, advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-products; Jungle Scout, Ad Badger seller guides.)
- Jassy (Q2 2026 call): Sponsored Products remains Amazon's largest ad offering and a central source of growth (coverage via ppc.land, Nasdaq call highlights).
- Why it works: shoppers on Amazon are already searching to buy (high intent), and Amazon sees what was purchased after the ad (closed-loop). (Descriptive; Amazon Ads materials.)
- **Prime Video ads**: Amazon announced in Sept 2023 that Prime Video would include limited ads starting early 2024 (US, UK, Germany, Canada first), with an ad-free option for an extra **$2.99/month** in the US. Rollout reported as Jan 29, 2024 (Variety, BGR). Amazon cited Prime Video ad engagement and live sports as Q2 2026 ad drivers (coverage of Q2 2026 call).
- **Amazon DSP**: Amazon's demand-side platform lets advertisers buy display/video ads on and off Amazon (Amazon Ads site). No separate revenue disclosed. Not used as a number.
- Amazon does NOT disclose ad profit/operating income separately. Do not claim margins.

## Quarterly
- Q1 2025 $13.9B (+19%), Q2 2025 $15.7B (+22%), Q3 2025 $17.7B (+22%), Q4 2025 $21.3B (+23%).
- Q1 2026 $17.2B (+24%) (secondary).
- **Q2 2026: $19.8B, up 26% from $15.7B in Q2 2025** (Amazon Q2 2026 earnings release 8-K, July 2026; AdExchanger, ppc.land, ecomcrew, pulse2). Total Q2 2026 net sales **$200.6B** (+20% from $167.7B).

## Worked examples (math)
- Ad share of revenue, Q2 2026: $19.8B / $200.6B = 0.0987 -> **about 9.9%, roughly 1 in every 10 dollars** Amazon brought in.
- Ad share, FY2025: $68.6B / $716.9B (2025 net sales) = 9.6%.
- Growth 2019 -> 2025: $68.6B / $12.6B = **5.4x** (more than five times in six years).
- Q2 2026 growth check: $19.8B / $15.7B = 1.261 -> 26%.
- vs Netflix: Amazon ads 2025 $68.6B vs Netflix total 2025 revenue $45.2B -> $68.6 / $45.2 = 1.52, ads alone were about **$23.4B (about 50%) bigger** than all of Netflix's revenue.

## Scale comparison, FY2025 ad revenue
- Google (Alphabet) advertising 2025: **$294.7B** ($294.69B per Reuters via ppc.land; sum of quarters 66.9+71.3+74.2+82.3 = ~294.7). Q4 2025 Google ads $82.3B (Alphabet Q4 release via Storyboard18, Verdict). WEAKER: full-year figure from secondary source.
- Meta advertising 2025: **$196.2B** ($196,175M), Meta FY2025 10-K; total revenue $201.0B.
- Amazon 2025: $68.6B -> third behind Google and Meta among US-listed ad sellers (commonly described as "the big three" in digital ads, MediaPost).

## The surprise
- Many of the first results you see in an Amazon search are paid "Sponsored" listings that look like regular results.
- Amazon's ad sales alone in 2025 ($68.6B) were bigger than all of Netflix's revenue in 2025 ($45.2B, Netflix Q4 2025 shareholder letter, Jan 2026).
